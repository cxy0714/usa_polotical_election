Research conventions / 研究规范

1. One source per row / 每行一个来源

English: Every CSV row carries exactly one SRC-####|https://… pair in source_urls. Where an assertion rests on two pages, the row cites the page that carries the assertion and the second page appears in reliability_notes or notes. Joining two ids with a semicolon is forbidden because the validator cannot resolve it.

中文：每个 CSV 行的 source_urls 恰好一组 SRC-####|https://…。若某判断依赖两个页面，该行引用承载该判断的页面， 另一个页面写入 reliability_notes 或 notes。禁止用分号拼接两个编号，因为校验器无法解析。

2. unknown is a value / unknown 是一个值

English: A cell never goes blank and never gets a plausible guess. unknown means "no retrieved source establishes this". It appears in 100+ cells, concentrated in crowd sizes, venue detail, ad lengths, image framing and finance sub-totals. None, N/A, - and TBD are banned by the validator and were removed wherever they appeared.

中文：单元格不得留空，也不得填入看似合理的猜测。unknown 表示"已抓取来源无法确认"。它在 100 多个单元格中出现， 集中于人数、场地细节、广告时长、图片构图与资金分项。None、N/A、-、TBD 被校验器禁止，已全部清除。

3. Verification ladder / 核实阶梯

Status / 状态Meaning / 含义
verifiedAt least one T1/T2 source states the fact directly and it is not contradicted / 至少一个 T1/T2 来源直接陈述且无矛盾
partially verifiedStated by a T3 source, or stated at headline level behind a paywall, or the quotation is truncated / 由 T3 来源陈述，或付费墙后的标题级陈述，或引文被截断
unverifiedRecorded from a single weak or headline-only source with no corroboration / 仅有单一薄弱或仅标题来源，无旁证
conflicting sourcesTwo sources state incompatible facts; both are recorded / 两个来源陈述互不相容的事实，二者均记录

English: Wikipedia-only evidence is never upgraded past partially verified. That rule is why the finance rows (reproduced from an FEC table via the article) and the four link-only Turek portraits are marked as they are.

中文：仅有维基百科支撑的证据绝不升级到 partially verified 以上。资金行（经条目转载的 FEC 表格）与四张仅链接的 Turek 肖像之所以如此标注，正是这条规则的结果。

4. Dates and times / 日期与时间

English: Dates are ISO YYYY-MM-DD or unknown; year-only or month-only evidence becomes unknown with the raw string in notes. event_time is local HH:MM or unknown — publication timestamps were never copied into event_time, because a story filed at 6:05 pm does not mean the event began then.

中文：日期一律 ISO 或 unknown；只有年份或月份的証据写 unknown，原始字符串放入 notes。event_time 为当地 HH:MM 或 unknown——绝不把稿件发布时间当作活动开始时间（下午 6:05 发稿不等于活动 6:05 开始）。

5. Interpretation is marked / 解读必须标注

English: Any sentence that reads the record rather than reports it begins INTERPRETATION:. This applies in analyst_interpretation, in the comparison memo and in the communication analyses. Unmarked text asserts only what a source states.

中文：凡对记录作出解读而非陈述的句子，一律以 INTERPRETATION: 开头。该规则适用于 analyst_interpretation、 比较报告与传播分析。未标注的文字只陈述来源所述内容。

6. No grades, no rankings / 不做评分与排序

English: No row scores a candidate, rates a performance or ranks the two. framing_of_candidate describes tone in words ("sympathetic, biography-led"); it does not assign a number. The NRA-PVF ratings page was retrieved but its candidate grades did not render, so no grade is recorded anywhere.

中文：任何行都不为候选人评分、评级或排序。framing_of_candidate 用文字描述语气（"同情式、传记主导"），不给数字。 NRA-PVF 评分页虽已抓取，但候选人评级未渲染，因此档案中不记录任何评级。

7. Disability as fact / 残疾作为事实

English: Turek's spina bifida, wheelchair use and surgical history are recorded exactly as sources state them — including that he attributes the condition to his father's Agent Orange exposure, which is his attribution, not an established cause. The archive never uses the disability as an argument, a metaphor or a proxy for policy positions, and never infers physical capability at any event.

中文：Turek 的脊柱裂、轮椅使用与手术史严格按来源陈述记录——包括他将病因归于父亲在越南接触橙剂，这是他的自述， 而非已确立的因果。档案绝不把残疾当作论据、隐喻或政策立场的代理，也绝不推断其在任何活动中的体能。

8. Images / 图片

English: Only public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA files are downloaded, and each downloaded file is kept under 2 MB by requesting a scaled thumbnail. Licence, photographer and Commons file page are recorded per row. "Copyrighted free use" is not on the list, which is why Turek's Legislature portraits are link-only.

中文：仅下载公有领域、CC0、CC BY 或 CC BY-SA 文件，并通过请求缩放缩略图把每个文件控制在 2 MB 以下。逐行记录授权、 摄影师与共享资源文件页。"Copyrighted free use" 不在允许清单内，因此 Turek 的州议会肖像仅记录链接。

9. Money 口径 / 资金口径

English: Campaign receipts (FEC) and outside spending (press-reported) are separate rows and are never summed. The finance rows reproduce an FEC summary table; the outside-spending figures are headline-level and marked unverified.

中文：竞选收支（FEC）与外部支出（媒体报道）分列不同行，从不相加。资金行转载 FEC 汇总表；外部支出数字为标题级， 标注 unverified。

10. Arithmetic / 算术

English: Percentages are either published values or recomputed from raw votes, and the row says which. Reconciliation failures are recorded, not repaired: the six-vote statewide difference between the NBC feed and the article's election box is written down in results_notes.md and both numbers survive.

中文：百分比要么是来源公布值，要么由原始票数重算，行内注明属于哪一种。核对失败只记录、不修补：NBC 数据与条目 选举信息框之间六票的全州差异写入 results_notes.md，两个数字都保留。

11. Naming and ids / 命名与编号

English: Row ids follow contract §5: TUR-, HIN- and JNT- prefixes with a four-digit sequence, and COV-#### / SRC-#### / POLL-#### sequences. candidate_id uses the case_meta ids TUREK and HINSON. Finance ids use three-letter candidate prefixes (TUR-FIN-0001), extended to non-nominee committees (CAR-FIN-0001 for Carlin) because those rows document the primary.

中文：行编号遵循合同第 5 节：TUR-、HIN-、JNT- 前缀加四位序号，以及 COV-####／SRC-####／POLL-#### 序列。 candidate_id 使用 case_meta 中的 TUREK 与 HINSON。资金编号用三字母前缀（TUR-FIN-0001），并扩展到非提名人的 委员会（Carlin 用 CAR-FIN-0001），因为这些行记录的是初选。

12. Fetch logging / 抓取记录

English: Every URL requested — including the ones that failed — is in _staging/fetch_log.csv with status and byte count. 140 of 200 requested URLs returned HTTP 200; the rest are 402/403/429 or JavaScript shells, and each one that mattered is named in research_gaps.md.

中文：每一个被请求的网址（包括失败的）都记录在 _staging/fetch_log.csv，含状态码与字节数。200 个请求网址中 140 个返回 200，其余为 402/403/429 或 JavaScript 壳；凡重要的失败均已在 research_gaps.md 点名。

Every CSV row carries SRC-####|https://...; unknown is a value, not a gap.

每个 CSV 行均带 SRC-####|https://...；unknown 是取值，不是缺失。
