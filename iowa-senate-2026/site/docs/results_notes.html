Results notes — reconciliation and variance / 结果说明

1. General election / 大选

English: The figures used in election_results.csv and county_results.csv are the NBC News results feed: Turek 688,746 (50.56%), Hinson 631,482 (46.35%), Laehn 42,109 (3.09%), total 1,362,337. The three candidate figures sum exactly to the published total, and each percentage recomputes from the votes to two decimals (50.558, 46.353, 3.091). County check: the 99 rows of county_results.csv sum to 688,746 / 631,482 / 42,109 and to 1,362,337 — an exact match to the statewide totals, which is the strongest internal check in this case.

中文：election_results.csv 与 county_results.csv 采用 NBC 新闻的计票数据：Turek 688,746（50.56%）、Hinson 631,482（46.35%）、Laehn 42,109（3.09%），合计 1,362,337。三名候选人票数精确相加等于公布总数，各百分比按票数重算到两位小数亦一致（50.558、46.353、3.091）。县级核对：county_results.csv 的 99 行合计为 688,746 / 631,482 / 42,109，总计 1,362,337，与全州总数完全一致——这是本案最强的内部一致性检验。

2. Documented variance: six votes / 已记录的差异：六票

English: The race article's election box gives Turek 688,741, Hinson 631,481, Laehn 42,109 and a total of 1,362,331 — six votes fewer than the NBC feed, and internally consistent on its own terms. The archive uses the NBC figures because they are the county-level source, and records the six-vote difference here rather than adjusting either figure. A six-vote gap does not change the winner or the margin at two decimals (4.21 points).

中文：竞赛条目的选举信息框给出 Turek 688,741、Hinson 631,481、Laehn 42,109，总计 1,362,331——比 NBC 数据少六票，但其自身内部自洽。档案采用 NBC 数据（因其为县级数据来源），并在此记录六票差异，不改动任何一方数字。六票之差不会改变胜者，也不会改变保留两位小数的差距（4.21 个百分点）。

3. Primary percentages / 初选百分比

English: The Republican primary election box gives 74.2 / 25.8 (153,059 / 53,196, total 206,255) and the Democratic box 62.7 / 37.3 (120,287 / 71,663, total 191,950). Night-of reporting used slightly different numbers: 74% to 26% for Hinson (SRC-0040) and 62.6% to 37.4% for Turek with 99% of precincts counted (SRC-0054). Both sets are recorded; the archive keeps the election-box versions in election_results.csv and notes the night-of versions in notes on each row. Recomputation from the votes gives 74.21 / 25.79 and 62.66 / 37.34, which is why the rounded pairs differ by a tenth.

中文：共和党初选信息框为 74.2 / 25.8（153,059 / 53,196，合计 206,255），民主党为 62.7 / 37.3（120,287 / 71,663，合计 191,950）。当晚报道用的是略有不同的数字：Hinson 74% 对 26%（SRC-0040），Turek 在 99% 选区计票时为 62.6% 对 37.4%（SRC-0054）。两套数字均予记录：election_results.csv 采用信息框版本，并在每行 notes 注明当晚版本。按票数重算得 74.21 / 25.79 与 62.66 / 37.34，这正是四舍五入后相差零点一个百分点的原因。

4. The Libertarian column / 自由意志党一栏

English: Thomas Laehn qualified by petition after submitting nearly 10,000 signatures on 2 June 2026 against a 3,500 requirement. His 3.09% is smaller than the 4.21-point margin, so he did not decide the outcome arithmetically; the archive states that as arithmetic, not as a claim about his voters.

中文：Thomas Laehn 于 2026 年 6 月 2 日提交近 1 万份签名（门槛 3,500 份）后以请愿方式取得资格。其 3.09% 小于 4.21 个百分点的差距，因此从算术上并未决定结果；档案只陈述这一算术事实，不对其选民做任何推断。

5. What could not be reconciled / 无法核对的部分

  • No official county canvass from the Iowa Secretary of State could be read: sos.iowa.gov returned HTTP 403

    • and the results application served a JavaScript shell only. The NBC feed is therefore the county authority here, not the state.

  • No precinct-level file was retrieved, so the county rows cannot be checked against a second county source.
  • The FEC's own pages returned HTTP 403; finance figures come from the summary table reproduced by the race

    • article and are marked partially verified.

Every CSV row carries SRC-####|https://...; unknown is a value, not a gap.

每个 CSV 行均带 SRC-####|https://...；unknown 是取值，不是缺失。
