Add seven verified news photographs, and record why the pool is this small

The visual layer had run out of places to look, so this pass probed the channels beyond Wikimedia
Commons and kept only what a licence actually allows.

- Seven photographs from SC Daily Gazette articles (States Newsroom, CC BY-ND 4.0): five of Darline
  Graham, two of Annie Andrews. Stored unmodified because CC BY-ND forbids derivatives.
- Two gates before storage: the article must be about the candidate, and the face must clear a 0.55
  SFace cosine with a 0.05 margin. The first harvest accepted a photograph of Lindsey Graham beside
  Tim Scott as "Annie Andrews" at 0.367, so the floor was raised and every appended row re-checked.
- 04_visual_archive/image_sources_and_licences.md documents what was probed and why it failed:
  Commons exhausted (20 files each, all archived), Openverse zero, senate.gov exposes no portrait
  files, graham.senate.gov unreachable, congress.gov 403, the campaign site is JavaScript and all
  rights reserved, and AP/Reuters/Post and Courier/The State are link-and-credit only.
- The visual hub now states the split (48 Commons + 7 news) and links to that note.
- _staging/_lead_sources_images.csv holds the new source registrations, because
  _lead_merge_sources.py rebuilds sources.csv from the staging files and would otherwise drop them.
- _research_conventions.md and the data dictionary no longer write a real SRC id when describing the
  numbering blocks, which the validator had counted as a dangling citation.

Counts: image_archive.csv grows from 48 to 55 rows; sources.csv from 372 to 379; validation back to
0 hard failures.
