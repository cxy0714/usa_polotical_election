Where the two cases differ. Texas documents a contested primary inside a party that holds the seat; South Carolina documents a vacancy - a death, an appointment, a statutory three-week special primary and a runoff - which is why its events table carries an actor_note and vacancy_context column that Texas does not need.

两个案例的差别（中文）. 德州记录的是在野党内部一场有竞争的初选；南卡记录的是一次空缺——死亡、任命、 法定三周特别初选与决选——因此其活动表带有德州不需要的 actor_note 与 vacancy_context 列。
