0. Non-negotiable rules / 0. 不可谈判的规则

  1. Never invent a date, venue, quotation, dollar figure, endorsement, photo URL or poll number. If it is not in a source you actually retrieved, it does not go in the archive. 绝不虚构任何日期、场地、引语、金额、背书、照片 URL 或民调数字。若某项内容不在你实际检索到的来源中，它就不能进入本档案。
  2. Unknown is a value. Use unknown (lowercase) in CSV cells and ⚠ unknown in Markdown. Never leave a cell blank when a value is expected; never substitute a guess. unknown 是一个取值。 在 CSV 单元格中使用 unknown（小写），在 Markdown 中使用 ⚠ unknown。在应有取值时绝不将单元格留空；绝不以猜测替代。
  3. Every row carries at least one source. The source_urls / source_url cell holds one or more SRC-#### ids from 09_sources/sources.csv, plus the raw URL for the most important one. Format: SRC-0007|https://.... 每一行至少携带一个来源。 source_urls / source_url 单元格存放一个或多个来自 09_sources/sources.csv 的 SRC-#### 编号，并附上其中最重要一条的原始 URL。格式：SRC-0007|https://...。
  4. Separate what was said from what an analyst infers. Stated purpose, observable function and analyst interpretation are three distinct columns/sections. 把「所述」与「分析者所推断」区分开来。 陈述目的（stated purpose）、可观察功能（observable function）与分析者解读（analyst interpretation）是三个彼此独立的列／章节。
  5. Announcement ≠ event. A press release announcing a rally is an announcement row (event_status = announced). Only mark held when there is evidence it took place. Verified cancellations go in with event_status = cancelled and the cancellation source. 宣布不等于活动。 宣布一场集会的新闻稿是一行*宣布*记录（event_status = announced）。只有在有证据表明活动确实发生时，才标记为 held。经核实的取消以 event_status = cancelled 记录，并附上取消的来源。
  6. Distinguish campaign-produced material from independent media. Ads/videos made by the campaign, by an outside PAC, or by a news outlet get different sponsor_type values. 区分竞选团队制作的材料与独立媒体。 由竞选团队、外部政治行动委员会（outside PAC）或新闻机构制作的广告／视频，分别使用不同的 sponsor_type 取值。
  7. Copyright: do not download copyrighted news photographs into the repo. Record metadata + source URL only. Download only assets that are public domain (e.g. official U.S. government / Texas Legislature portraits, federal works) or explicitly licensed. When in doubt: link, don't copy. 版权： 不要将受版权保护的新闻照片下载到仓库中。只记录元数据＋来源 URL。仅下载属于公有领域（例如美国政府／得克萨斯州议会的官方肖像、联邦作品）或已获明确许可的素材。存疑时：只链接，不复制。
  8. No premature conclusions. Do not rank the candidates, score their branding, or declare a winner of the "communication contest". Descriptive observation only; strategic interpretation must be labelled as interpretation. 不作过早结论。 不得为候选人排名、为其品牌打分，也不得宣布 "communication contest"（译：传播竞赛）的胜者。只作描述性观察；策略性解读必须标注为解读。
  9. Do not write the blog post or video script. This is an evidence archive. 不要撰写博客文章或视频脚本。这是一份证据档案。

5. Event purpose: the three-layer rule / 5. 活动目的：三层规则

For each event, fill all three and never blur them:

  • stated_purpose — what the campaign/candidate explicitly said it was for (quote the press release/announcement or write unknown).
  • observable_function — what the activity actually was (e.g. "45-minute stump speech to ~120 people in a union hall, then 10 minutes of press questions").
  • analyst_interpretation — labelled inference, prefixed INTERPRETATION:.

对每一项活动，三层都必须填写，且绝不可混淆：

  • stated_purpose（陈述目的）——竞选团队／候选人*明确表示*该活动所为何事（引用新闻稿／公告，或写 unknown）。
  • observable_function（可观察功能）——该活动*实际是什么*（例如 "45-minute stump speech to ~120 people in a union hall, then 10 minutes of press questions"，译：在工会大厅向约 120 人作 45 分钟竞选演说，随后 10 分钟媒体提问）。
  • analyst_interpretation（分析者解读）——带标注的推断，以 INTERPRETATION: 为前缀。

In Markdown, use a three-row table instead:

在 Markdown 中，改用三行表格：

Layer / 层级Content / 内容Evidence / 证据
Stated purpose / 陈述目的
Observable function / 可观察功能
Analyst interpretation / 分析者解读

6. Verification workflow / 6. 核实流程

For each important claim: find ≥1 Tier-1 or Tier-2 source. Where two sources conflict, record both and set verification_status = conflicting sources, then describe the conflict in research_gaps.md (agents append to their own staging gaps file; Lead merges).

对每一项重要主张：找到至少一个 Tier-1 或 Tier-2 来源。若两个来源相互冲突，两者都记录，并设置 verification_status = conflicting sources，然后在 research_gaps.md 中描述该冲突（分析员将内容追加到各自的过渡空缺文件，由负责人合并）。

Known friction to expect and how to handle:

  • Paywalled articles (NYT, WaPo): the search-result snippet and headline may be all you can retrieve. Record what the snippet supports, mark partially verified, and note the paywall in reliability_notes. Do not fabricate body text.
  • web_fetch may fail or return nav junk. Retry once; if it still fails, rely on the search-result summary and mark accordingly.
  • Social posts are frequently deleted or unretrievable → unverified, note "post not retrievable".

可能遇到的已知摩擦及处理方式：

  • 付费墙文章（NYT、WaPo）：你可能只能取到搜索结果摘要与标题。记录摘要所能支持的内容，标记为 partially verified，并在 reliability_notes 中注明付费墙。不得编造正文内容。
