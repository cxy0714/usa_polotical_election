2. Method / 方法

  1. Decode — ffmpeg → 16 kHz mono PCM; the working WAV is deleted after measurement, the kept copy is AAC 64 kbps mono.
  2. Speech/silence — 25 ms frames, 10 ms hop, RMS in dBFS. The noise floor is the 10th percentile; the speech threshold is max(noise+9 dB, min(p95−6 dB, −32 dB)), then 150 ms gaps are closed and runs under 120 ms dropped.
  3. Pauses — silence runs ≥ 0.25 s inside the first–last speech span, reported as count, mean and maximum length, and per minute of speech.
  4. Loudness — mean, SD and p10/p90 of dBFS over speech frames (dynamics, not LUFS).
  5. F0 — normalised autocorrelation, 40 ms window / 20 ms hop, 55–400 Hz; the first lag within 85 % of the frame maximum is taken as the period (this avoids the usual octave-up error from a strong second harmonic), then median-filtered over 5 frames. Indicative only on rally audio that contains music, applause and PA.
  6. Spectral centroid — mean over speech frames (a rough brightness/timbre proxy).
  7. Speech rate — words from the English captions yt-dlp saved (auto or manual). wpm divides by the caption span; articulation_wpm divides by measured speech time.
  8. Rhetoric counters — pronoun classes, "Texas" mentions, vocabulary richness, mean sentence length and the 12 most frequent content words, all from the caption text.

Scripts: ../_staging/vision/analyze_speech.py (measure) and ../_staging/vision/gen_audio_readme.py (this file + figures).

5. What can and cannot be claimed / 可以说与不可以说

  • Can: speech share, pause counts, loudness dynamics, caption-based speaking rate, and indicative pitch ranges, each per video and per 60 s segment.
  • Cannot: infer emotion, sincerity, confidence or "tone" from these numbers; infer audience reaction from loudness (applause contaminates the VAD); compare venues as if microphone and PA were equal — they are not. Rally audio is a mix, not a studio feed.
  • Do not average across kinds. A 20 s news soundbite and a 20 min rally speech are not the same measurement; the comparison table already restricts to speech-type videos.

5.1 Bilingual reading / 双语阅读

Each transcript is split into lines (about 28 words, or a natural pause) and each line is machine-translated to Chinese, so the site can show timecode | English | Chinese in one table. The Chinese column is a reading aid only: the source is automatic caption text that already mishears names, and the translation adds its own errors. Quote the English line against the video, never the Chinese text.

逐字稿被切成约 28 词一行（或自然停顿），每行再机器翻译成中文， 网站因此能以「时间码 | 英文 | 中文」三列并排。中文列只是阅读辅助： 原文本身是自动字幕，已经会误听人名，机翻又会叠加它自己的错误。 引用时一律以英文行对照视频，不得把中文当作候选人的原话。

Every transcript page links to its source video, and each line's timecode is a YouTube deep link (?t= seconds), so a row can be checked against the footage in one click. 每份逐字稿页都链到原视频，每行时间码就是 YouTube 深链接（?t=秒） ，点一下就能回到录像里核对那一句。

7. Limits and rights / 局限与版权

  1. Caption coverage is partial. Where no English captions exist, words, wpm and the rhetoric counters are empty — the delivery measures are still valid.
  2. Auto captions are not transcripts. They drop punctuation and mishear names; counts are approximate (±few %) and sentence statistics are rough.
  3. F0 on mixed audio can lock onto music or crowd noise. voiced_ratio and the wide p10–p90 spread show how much of each file is genuinely trackable.
  4. Loudness is dBFS, not LUFS, and no microphone calibration exists — never compare absolute levels across venues.
  5. Rights: audio is cut from YouTube uploads owned by the campaigns and stations. Internal analysis only; not licensed for republication or for re-upload.
