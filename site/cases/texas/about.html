Case Study 001 — 2026 Texas U.S. Senate: James Talarico (D) vs. Ken Paxton (R)

案例研究 001 —— 2026 年德州联邦参议员选举：詹姆斯·塔拉里科（民主党）对阵肯·帕克斯顿（共和党）

A reconstruction of the publicly documented campaign process, built for a human analyst who will decide which events, images and arguments deserve emphasis.

本档案是对公开可查的竞选过程的一次重构，服务于一位人类研究者——由他来决定哪些事件、哪些影像、哪些论点值得强调。

This archive collects and organises evidence. It does not conclude which campaign was better, whose branding was more effective, or whose communication was more persuasive. No strategy-effectiveness judgement, no visual-quality ranking and no sentiment scoring appears anywhere in these files.

本档案只收集与整理证据，不对哪一方竞选更成功、哪一方的形象包装更有效、哪一方的传播更有说服力下结论。 全部文件中不含任何竞选效果评判、视觉质量排序或情感倾向打分。

1. What this is / 这是什么

Ten datasets, 158 research files, a 145-video frame-and-audio layer (face-recognised frames, scene keyframes, measured speech delivery) and a bilingual offline website reconstructing the campaign across five parallel research streams, every row carrying its own sources:

十个数据集、158 份研究材料、一套覆盖 145 段视频的画面与音频层（人脸识别帧、镜头关键帧、演讲交付测量），外加一个离线双语站点，按五条并行的研究线索重构整场竞选；每一行数据都自带来源。

2. What this is not / 这不是什么

  • Not a biography. The biographies exist to support event interpretation, not as a life story. 不是传记。传记的存在是为解读事件提供背景，而非讲述完整人生。
  • Not an election summary. Results are context, not the object of study. 不是选举综述。选举结果是背景信息，不是研究对象本身。
  • Not a blog post or video script. No final narrative was written — that is the human researcher's decision. 不是博客文章，也不是视频脚本。本档案没有撰写任何最终叙事——那是人类研究者的决定。
  • Not a verdict. There is no ranking of candidates, campaigns, ads or images. 不是判决书。档案中没有对候选人、竞选活动、广告或影像的任何排序。
  • Not padded. Where evidence was thin, files say so and counts are honest (see §7). 没有注水。凡是证据薄弱之处，文件如实标明，计数保持诚实（见第 7 节）。

5. Documented findings the analyst can rely on / 研究者可以依赖的有据发现

These are stated as documented facts with sources, not as judgements. 以下内容是以来源为据的事实陈述，不是判断。

  1. No debate occurred. Five debate_invitation rows document the chronology: Talarico accepted ≥4 invitations (including FOX/Bret Baier); Paxton's camp said negotiations failed because Talarico "always wants something we can't do"; Talarico's spokesperson called that "lying" and cited "a personal call to his cellphone"; Talarico: "literally any time, any place, any network." The CNN–Jubilee event was a solo voter Q&A, mislabelled a debate by several outlets.

没有举行任何辩论。 五条 debate_invitation 记录还原了时间线：塔拉里科接受了至少四场邀请（含 FOX／Bret Baier）；帕克斯顿阵营称谈判失败是因为塔拉里科"总是要一些我们做不到的东西"（译）；塔拉里科的发言人指此为"撒谎"（译），并称帕克斯顿"无视了每一次尝试，包括打到他手机的一通私人电话"（译）；塔拉里科本人则表示"任何时间、任何地点、任何电视台"（译）。CNN–Jubilee 那场活动是单人选民问答，被多家媒体误标为辩论。

  1. Paxton was nominated twelve weeks after Talarico, after a runoff described as "vile" and "brutal," with the incumbent he beat declining to campaign for him.

帕克斯顿获得提名的时间比塔拉里科晚十二周，且此前经历了一场被形容为"卑劣"与"残酷"（译）的决选；被他击败的现任参议员拒绝为他助选。

  1. The two closing tours are a controlled comparison. Announced 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-16; launched Rockwall airport (10-17, ~200 people) and Plano (10-18); both advertised to finish 2026-11-02 — Talarico's Austin finale held, Paxton's Alamo finale cancelled two days out.

两条收官巡回宣传构成一组可控比较。 分别于 2026-10-14 与 10-16 公布；起点为 Rockwall 机场（10-17，约 200 人）与 Plano（10-18）；双方都宣称将于 2026-11-02 收官——塔拉里科的奥斯汀收官集会如期举行，帕克斯顿的阿拉莫收官集会在两天前取消。

  1. The paid air war was mostly not the candidate's. Documented ads: Talarico campaign 10, outside PACs 4; Paxton campaign 1, outside PACs 8. AdImpact puts outside groups at 92% of Paxton's ad support vs 23% of Talarico's.

付费广告战主要并非候选人自身所为。 有据可查的广告：塔拉里科竞选团队 10 支、外部政治行动委员会 4 支；帕克斯顿竞选团队仅 1 支、外部政治行动委员会 8 支。AdImpact 数据显示，外部团体占帕克斯顿广告投放支持的 92%，而塔拉里科一方为 23%。

  1. Money, as filed: through 2026-10-14 Talarico's committee reported $146,349,912 receipts / $9,922,857 cash on hand; Paxton's $22,319,359 / $4,784,408. Both reported $0 debt.

按申报口径的资金情况： 截至 2026-10-14，塔拉里科委员会报告收入 146,349,912 美元、手头现金 9,922,857 美元；帕克斯顿为 22,319,359 美元、4,784,408 美元。双方均申报零负债。

  1. The visual identities quote different cultures. Talarico named his tour after a television motto (Friday Night Lights) and later cast the series' actors in an ad; Paxton named his after the 1835 Gonzales "Come and Take It" flag and built the tour's imagery around a replica of it.

双方的视觉识别引用的是不同的文化资源。 塔拉里科以一句电视剧台词（Friday Night Lights）为巡回宣传命名，随后又请该剧演员出演广告；帕克斯顿以 1835 年冈萨雷斯"Come and Take It"旗命名，并围绕该旗复制品构建巡回宣传的视觉意象。

  1. Three named Talarico tours, one named Paxton tour: "Frontera" (July), "New American Dream" (August), "Clear Eyes, Full Hearts" (October) vs "Come and Take It."

塔拉里科有三条具名巡回路线，帕克斯顿有一条： "Frontera"（7 月）、"New American Dream"（8 月）、"Clear Eyes, Full Hearts"（10 月），对应帕克斯顿的 "Come and Take It"。

  1. A documented activity gap, not fabricated events: Talarico was off the trail for about two weeks in early October (flu). No events were invented to fill it.

这是有据可查的活动空窗，而非编造事件： 塔拉里科在 10 月初因流感离开竞选活动约两周。档案没有为填补这段空白而虚构任何活动。

6.1 Static website / 静态站点

site/index.html is a derived reading view of the deliverables, generated by _staging/_lead_build_site.py. It needs no server, no network connection and no external assets: open the file directly in a browser.

site/index.html 是上述交付物的派生阅读视图，由 _staging/_lead_build_site.py 生成。不需要服务器、不需要联网、不加载任何外部资源，直接在浏览器中打开该文件即可。

  • Bilingual reading modes / 双语阅读模式 — every page switches between side-by-side EN/ZH, English only and Chinese only. English sits on the left and Chinese on the right, so the two versions can be compared paragraph by paragraph. 每个页面可在「并排双语 / 仅英文 / 仅中文」三档之间切换；英文在左、中文在右，可逐段对照。
  • Client-side search / 站内检索 — the home page indexes the headings and text of all 45 bilingual documents. 首页对全部 45 份双语文档的标题与正文建立索引，输入即搜，不上传任何内容。
  • Datasets as tables / 数据集表格化 — each CSV becomes a filterable table with a download link to the raw file, and every table links back to the bilingual data dictionary. 每个 CSV 渲染为可筛选表格，并提供原始文件下载链接；每张表都回链到双语数据字典。
  • Images load in page / 图片在页面内加载 — the two visual analyses open with an image index strip, and the gallery lists all 116 records split by candidate. Locally stored licensed copies render inline with photographer, date and licence; the 40 rights-reserved records show a "link only" card that jumps to the source page instead of hotlinking an unlicensed copy. 两篇视觉分析页首各有一条影像索引条，图库按候选人分区列出全部 116 条记录。本地许可副本直接显示，注明摄影者、日期与许可；40 条保留所有权利的记录显示「仅链接」卡片，跳转来源页，不做未授权外链嵌入。
  • Video frames and shot structure / 视频帧与镜头结构 — the gallery shows 172 de-duplicated, face-matched frames with similarity, shot scale and timestamp; a companion page indexes 1,073 scene keyframes and per-video cut rate, shot length, brightness and motion. 图库陈列 172 帧去重后的识别画面（含相似度、景别与时间码）；另有一页列出 1,073 个镜头关键帧，以及每段视频的剪辑率、镜头长度、亮度与运动量。
  • Speech audio / 演讲音频 — 145 videos measured for speech share, pauses, loudness, F0 and caption-derived rate; 78 timed transcripts and 93 waveform/F0 plots are readable in-page. The 64 kbps audio copies are a local working set and are not committed. 145 段视频的说话占比、停顿、响度、基频与语速均已测量；78 份逐字稿（每行英文配一句机翻中文）与 93 张波形／F0 图可在页内阅读。64 kbps 音频副本属本地工作集，未纳入版本库。
  • Every source is clickable / 来源全可点击 — each SRC-#### id anywhere in the prose becomes a chip that opens the original source in a new tab (with the publisher as tooltip), and bare URLs in the CSV tables are clickable too. 2,066 source chips and 1,371 outbound links across the site; the source registry table links both the id and the URL. 正文中出现的每个 SRC-#### 都变成可点击的编号标签，在新标签页打开原始来源（悬停显示媒体名）；CSV 表格中的网址同样可点击。全站共 2,066 个来源标签、1,371 个外链；来源总表中的编号与网址都带链接。

7. Honest limits — read before using / 使用前必读的诚实局限

Full register: research_gaps.md. The four that matter most: 完整登记见 research_gaps.md。最重要的四项如下：

  1. County-level election data is absent. county_results.csv is intentionally header-only; county tabulation was published via JavaScript interactives that could not be executed, and the Wikipedia article carries only county maps. No county or regional analysis is possible from this archive.

县级选举数据缺失。 county_results.csv 有意只保留表头；县级统计通过 JavaScript 交互页面发布，无法抓取执行，而维基百科条目只有县级*地图*，没有数据表。因此本档案无法支撑任何县级或区域分析。

  1. Facial geometry, body proportions and clothing are still not measured. Landmark detection was never available, so no distances, proportions or garment classification exist. What the frame layer does provide: 172 curated frames with face boxes in source pixels, shot scale, sharpness, brightness and a crude torso-colour proxy. Both visual analysis files state the limit and supply a measurement protocol for the human analyst. Do not write appearance claims from this archive.

面部几何、身体比例与着装仍未测量。 始终没有可用的关键点检测，因此不存在距离、比例或服装分类数据。画面层提供的是：172 帧精选画面，附原始像素中的人脸框、景别、锐度、亮度，以及一个粗糙的躯干颜色代理值。两篇视觉分析文件均如实写明这一局限，并为人类研究者提供了完整的测量规程。请勿依据本档案撰写任何外貌类论断。

  1. The image target was not met for Talarico (18 vs a 60–100 target), and Paxton's pool is context-skewed (68 of 98 from AmericaFest). Both facts are documented rather than padded; any comparative image use must carry them.

塔拉里科的影像数量未达目标（18 张，目标为每位候选人 60–100 张），帕克斯顿的图库则在情境上严重偏斜（98 张中 68 张来自 AmericaFest）。 这两项事实均如实记录而非注水；任何对比性使用影像的场合都必须同时说明它们。

  1. No quotation was verified word-by-word against the audio, though delivery is now measured. Every quotation still comes from printed text. What changed: 145 videos were decoded and measured in 11_speech_audio/ (speech share, pause structure, caption-based rate, indicative F0), and 78 timed caption transcripts can be used to spot-check wording. Emotion, sincerity, emphasis and audience reaction remain unmeasured and unclaimable.

引语尚未逐字与音频核对，但交付方式已有测量。 每一句引语仍来自文字报道。变化在于：11_speech_audio/ 已解码并测量 145 段视频（说话占比、停顿结构、由字幕推算的语速、仅作指示的基频），另有 78 份带时间码的字幕逐字稿可用于抽查措辞。情绪、真诚度、重音与观众反应仍不可测、不可断言。

8. Data conventions / 数据规范

_research_conventions.md is the binding contract. Key points: _research_conventions.md 是具约束力的契约。要点如下：

  • IDs: TAL-EVT-####, PAX-EVT-####, JNT-EVT-####, TAL-SPC-####, PAX-SPC-####, TAL-AD-####, PAX-AD-####, EXT-AD-####, TAL-IMG-####, PAX-IMG-####, SRC-####. 编号体系： 同上，保持英文原样，不得改写。
  • unknown is a value. It means "not established," never "zero" and never "no." unknown 是有效取值。 它表示"未能查明"，绝不是"零"，也绝不是"否"。
  • Three-layer purpose: stated_purpose (what they said) / observable_function (what it was) / analyst_interpretation (inference, prefixed INTERPRETATION:). 三层目的规则： stated_purpose（宣称目的）／observable_function（实际观察到的功能）／analyst_interpretation（推断，前缀 INTERPRETATION:）。
