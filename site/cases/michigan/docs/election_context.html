The office and the calendar

Michigan's Class 2 U.S. Senate seat was up on November 3, 2026. The seat became open when Gary Peters, the two-term Democratic incumbent, declined to seek re-election SRC-0042. The Democratic primary was held on August 4, 2026, the Republican primary the same day, and the general election on November 3, 2026 SRC-0001. Filing was by nominating petition: El-Sayed filed on April 14, 2026 and Rogers on April 17, 2026, both recorded in the Bureau of Elections candidate listing SRC-0005.

中文：密歇根州第二类联邦参议院席位于 2026 年 11 月 3 日改选。该席位因两届民主党现任参议员加里·彼得斯不再寻求连任而空出 SRC-0042。民主党初选于 2026 年 8 月 4 日举行，共和党初选同日，大选于 2026 年 11 月 3 日举行 SRC-0001。提名方式为联署请愿：赛义德于 2026 年 4 月 14 日提交，罗杰斯于 4 月 17 日提交，均见于州选举局候选人名录 SRC-0005。

The Democratic primary field

Three candidates were on the ballot. Haley Stevens, a congresswoman, launched in January 2025 and was the establishment favourite SRC-0040 SRC-0023. Mallory McMorrow, a state senator, launched in January 2025 and suspended her campaign on July 5, 2026, remaining on the ballot SRC-0038 SRC-0033. Abdul El-Sayed, a former Wayne County health director, launched in April 2025 with Bernie Sanders' endorsement SRC-0070 SRC-0078. The primary was the most expensive Senate primary in Michigan history and was dominated by AIPAC's spending against El-Sayed SRC-0010 SRC-0034.

中文：选票上有三位候选人。众议员海莉·史蒂文斯于 2025 年 1 月启动竞选，是建制派热门 SRC-0040 SRC-0023。州参议员马洛里·麦克莫罗亦于 2025 年 1 月启动，2026 年 7 月 5 日暂停竞选但仍留在选票上 SRC-0038 SRC-0033。前韦恩县卫生主任阿卜杜勒·赛义德于 2025 年 4 月启动，获伯尼·桑德斯背书 SRC-0070 SRC-0078。该初选是密歇根州历史上最昂贵的参议院初选，并被 AIPAC 针对赛义德的支出主导 SRC-0010 SRC-0034。

Endorsements and party consolidation

The primary's endorsements tracked the party's internal division. Gretchen Whitmer endorsed Stevens in late July 2026 SRC-0076, and after the primary she endorsed El-Sayed SRC-0055. Senate Democrats met El-Sayed on September 16 and rallied around him SRC-0035, but not every Michigan Democrat followed: Representative Hillary Scholten declined to endorse him after the primary SRC-0065. On the left, the Metro Times and the Michigan Daily editorial board endorsed El-Sayed before the primary SRC-0067 SRC-0077, while Crooked Media's podcast hosts had already planned a fundraiser for him in January SRC-0066.

Three procedural details explain why the field looked fixed early. McMorrow announced more than 30,000 volunteer-gathered signatures in March 2026 SRC-0026; Bernadette Smith entered the Republican primary against Rogers in November 2025 and was later disqualified SRC-0028; and the state party's spring convention in April 2026 was itself covered as a test of whether Democrats would follow their activist base SRC-0012 SRC-0017. NBC declared El-Sayed the winner overnight on August 4-5, which is what prompted McMorrow's endorsement SRC-0039; the AP's own account of McMorrow's July 5 suspension describes a two-person contest a month before the primary SRC-0058.

中文：初选期间的背书反映了政党的内部分歧。格雷琴·惠特默于 2026 年 7 月下旬背书史蒂文斯 SRC-0076，初选之后转而背书赛义德 SRC-0055。参议院民主党人于 9 月 16 日与赛义德会面并表示团结 SRC-0035，但并非所有密歇根民主党人紧随其后：众议员希拉里·肖尔滕在初选后拒绝背书他 SRC-0065。在左翼，《地铁时报》与《密歇根日报》编辑委员会在初选前背书赛义德 SRC-0067 SRC-0077，而 Crooked Media 的播客主持人早在 1 月就为他筹划过筹款会 SRC-0066。

三个程序性细节解释了为何阵容很早便显得固定。麦克莫罗于 2026 年 3 月宣布征集到逾 3 万份志愿者签名 SRC-0026；伯纳黛特·史密斯于 2025 年 11 月加入共和党初选挑战罗杰斯，后被取消资格 SRC-0028；州党 2026 年 4 月的春季代表大会本身即被报道为“民主党人是否跟随其激进基层”的检验 SRC-0012 SRC-0017。NBC 于 8 月 4 至 5 日夜间判定赛义德胜选，正是这促使麦克莫罗表态背书 SRC-0039；美联社关于麦克莫罗 7 月 5 日暂停竞选的报道则描述了初选前一个月的两人对决 SRC-0058。

The Republican side

Rogers was effectively unopposed. Bernadette Smith was removed from the ballot by the Board of State Canvassers for insufficient valid signatures, leaving Rogers as the only Republican candidate; he received 883,328 votes, reported as 100.0% SRC-0027 SRC-0001. He had been endorsed by Donald Trump in July 2025 SRC-0003.

中文：罗杰斯实际上没有对手。伯纳黛特·史密斯因有效签名不足被州选举审查委员会从选票上除名，罗杰斯成为唯一的共和党候选人，获得 883,328 票，报道为 100.0% SRC-0027 SRC-0001。他于 2025 年 7 月获唐纳德·特朗普背书 SRC-0003。

The general-election ballot

Six candidates appeared: El-Sayed (Democratic), Rogers (Republican), Lydia Christensen (Libertarian), Tim Long (U.S. Taxpayers), Douglas P. Marsh (Green) and Walter P. Kristy (Natural Law) SRC-0005 SRC-0001. The minor-party nominations were made at conventions rather than primaries. Together the four minor candidates took 2.00% of the vote.

Turnout and the size of the electorate

The Democratic primary drew 1,537,628 votes across three candidates, and the Republican primary 883,328 for a single candidate SRC-0001. The general election drew 4,798,086 votes as published for the six candidates SRC-0001. No prior-cycle comparison is offered here because no Michigan Senate primary baseline was retrievable in this pass; the turnout-share claim in the primary analysis is qualitative and comes from Bridge Michigan SRC-0008.

中文：民主党初选三位候选人合计 1,537,628 票，共和党初选单一候选人 883,328 票 SRC-0001。大选六位候选人公布合计 4,798,086 票 SRC-0001。此处不提供与往届的对比，因为本轮未取得密歇根州参议院初选的基线数据；初选分析中关于投票率的判断是定性的，来自 Bridge Michigan SRC-0008。

How the result was called

The Associated Press called the Democratic primary for El-Sayed at 9:56 a.m. on August 5, 2026, with the count at 99% and his lead at about one point SRC-0009. Stevens conceded by telephone that morning and McMorrow endorsed El-Sayed shortly after the call SRC-0009. The AP called the general election at 2:27 a.m. on November 4, with 85% counted and El-Sayed leading 51.9% to 46.1% SRC-0029 SRC-0001.

中文：美联社于 2026 年 8 月 5 日上午 9:56、计票 99%、领先约一个百分点时判定赛义德赢得民主党初选 SRC-0009。史蒂文斯当日上午电话认输，麦克莫罗在判定后不久背书赛义德 SRC-0009。美联社于 11 月 4 日凌晨 2:27、计票 85%、赛义德以 51.9% 对 46.1% 领先时判定大选结果 SRC-0029 SRC-0001。

What made the seat consequential

Three things. First, the open seat was one of the few plausible Democratic holds in a Republican-trending presidential year, and national Democrats consolidated behind El-Sayed within days of the primary SRC-0060. Second, the primary became the most expensive single-race test of AIPAC's influence in Democratic politics, with roughly $33 million spent by AIPAC and its affiliated super PAC SRC-0002 SRC-0034. Third, the result made El-Sayed the first Muslim American elected to the Senate SRC-0002.

中文：三点。第一，该空缺席位是民主党在共和党势头上升的总统年中所能守住的少数席位之一，全国民主党人在初选后数日内便团结到赛义德身后 SRC-0060。第二，这场初选成为 AIPAC 对民主党政治影响力最昂贵的一次单一选举检验，AIPAC 及其关联超级政治行动委员会投入约 3300 万美元 SRC-0002 SRC-0034。第三，这一结果使赛义德成为美国参议院首位穆斯林议员 SRC-0002。
