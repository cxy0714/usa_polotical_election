Method and confidence

This document summarises the nineteen position rows held in 01_candidates/policy_positions.csv (ROG-POS-0001 to ROG-POS-0019). Rogers has a fourteen-year House voting record, which makes his positions unusually checkable — but only where a retrieved source itemises a vote. Seven rows quote him in his own words from the October debates; the rest rest on his congressional record as summarised in his Wikipedia biography and are marked partially verified SRC-0003.

中文：本文档汇总 01_candidates/policy_positions.csv 中的十九条立场记录（ROG-POS-0001 至 ROG-POS-0019）。罗杰斯有十四年众议院投票记录，使其立场异常便于核对——但仅限于已取得的材料逐项列出投票之处。七条记录直接引自他在 10 月辩论中的原话；其余依据其维基百科生平所概括的国会记录，并标为 partially verified SRC-0003。

Health care

Rogers opposes Medicare for All, arguing it would end private health insurance and raise taxes; at the October 8 debate he condensed the attack into "We can't afford Abdul" SRC-0019 [ROG-POS-0001]. His House record is consistent with that posture: he voted to end key Affordable Care Act provisions and opposed extended ACA subsidies SRC-0003 [ROG-POS-0002]. No positive Republican alternative was retrieved from the campaign — the campaign website's health message is limited to "protecting seniors' social security & medicare" SRC-0082.

中文：罗杰斯反对“全民医保”，认为它将终结私营医疗保险并加税；在 10 月 8 日的辩论中他把这一攻击凝练为“我们养不起阿卜杜勒”SRC-0019 [ROG-POS-0001]。他在众议院的记录与这一立场一致：投票取消《平价医疗法案》关键条款，反对延长 ACA 补贴 SRC-0003 [ROG-POS-0002]。本档案未从竞选方取得任何积极的共和党替代方案——竞选网站的医疗表述仅限于“保护老年人的社会保障与联邦医疗保险”SRC-0082。

Abortion

He opposes abortion and holds a 100% National Right to Life Committee rating with a 0% NARAL rating from his House years, where he also voted to bar federal funding for Planned Parenthood SRC-0003 [ROG-POS-0003]. In the 2026 campaign he said he supports access in cases of rape, incest and to save the mother's life, that abortion should be left to the states, and that he would not support a federal ban — a marked shift from a federal-issue posture, recorded as position_changed: yes [ROG-POS-0003].

中文：他反对堕胎，在众议院期间获全国生命权委员会 100% 评分、NARAL 0% 评分，并投票禁止联邦资金资助计划生育协会 SRC-0003 [ROG-POS-0003]。在 2026 年竞选中他表示支持在强奸、乱伦以及为挽救母亲生命的情况下允许堕胎，认为该议题应交给各州，且他不会支持联邦禁令——这与他此前把堕胎视为联邦议题的姿态明显不同，故记为 position_changed: yes [ROG-POS-0003]。

Immigration and ICE

Rogers has a long record of supporting strict immigration laws, a border wall and increased enforcement [ROG-POS-0004] SRC-0003. The distinctive move in 2026 was tactical rather than substantive: he criticised how ICE raids were being conducted in public, saying his time in the FBI taught him that raids of that nature should be quick, decisive and without public fanfare, while opposing abolition of the agency SRC-0019 [ROG-POS-0005]. That let him put distance between himself and the administration's visible enforcement without endorsing El-Sayed's position.

中文：罗杰斯长期支持严格的移民法、边境墙与加强执法 [ROG-POS-0004] SRC-0003。2026 年他的独特动作是策略性的而非实质性的：他批评 ICE 突袭行动的公开方式，称联邦调查局的经历让他认为此类行动应当迅速、果断且不事张扬，同时反对废除该机构 SRC-0019 [ROG-POS-0005]。这使他得以与政府的公开执法保持距离，又不必认同赛义德的立场。

Foreign policy: Israel, Iran and trade

He is a longtime supporter of Israel and AIPAC, and in 2026 he reversed himself on accepting AIPAC's help. In late August he asked AIPAC's board chair not to intervene, warning it could hurt more than help; by October 8 he said "Anybody that wants to help my campaign ... I'll take all of that help" SRC-0019 SRC-0073 [ROG-POS-0006]. AIPAC-linked United Democracy Project began spending against El-Sayed less than 24 hours later SRC-0073.

On Iran he supported the 2026 war, said in March that the U.S. would be "fine" on gas prices because "we got plenty of oil", and by September was calling for the war to end quickly because of energy costs, while opposing immediate withdrawal; at the final debate he called Iran "absolutely" a threat SRC-0036 SRC-0003 [ROG-POS-0007]. On Canada he broke with the administration: "When they went to Canada, I thought that was not the right direction ... they didn't account for how integrated we are in the automotive business" SRC-0020 [ROG-POS-0008].

中文：他长期支持以色列与 AIPAC，2026 年却在是否接受 AIPAC 协助上自我逆转。8 月下旬他要求 AIPAC 董事会主席不要介入，警告这可能弊大于利；到 10 月 8 日他表示“任何愿意帮我的竞选的人……我都会接受这一切帮助”SRC-0019 SRC-0073 [ROG-POS-0006]。不到 24 小时后，与 AIPAC 关联的“联合民主项目”便开始投放攻击赛义德的广告 SRC-0073。

关于伊朗，他支持 2026 年的战争，3 月称美国在油价上会“没事”，因为“我们有充足的石油”；到 9 月因能源成本改口呼吁尽快结束战争，同时反对立即撤军；在最后一场辩论中他称伊朗“绝对是”威胁 SRC-0036 SRC-0003 [ROG-POS-0007]。关于加拿大，他与政府分道扬镳：“当他们把矛头指向加拿大时，我认为方向不对……他们没有考虑到我们在汽车产业上的高度一体化”SRC-0020 [ROG-POS-0008]。

Economy, trade and affordability

His tariff position evolved in three steps: blanket support, then case-by-case adjustment, then a public call to end the Canada trade war. At the Detroit Economic Club he defended tariffs as correcting "about 40 years of bad trade policy" and cited GM moving Silverado production to the Orion plant as evidence they worked SRC-0020 [ROG-POS-0009]. His affordability offer included pausing Michigan's gas tax — a proposal that misdescribed the 2019 increase's authorship, since Republicans sponsored and helped approve it, and that a Republican legislative leader rejected the same day SRC-0013 [ROG-POS-0010]. His stated diagnosis of housing and consumer costs is high interest rates, to be reduced by restraining federal spending [ROG-POS-0011] SRC-0003, and his standing message is "bringing manufacturing jobs back home, lowering the cost of gas, groceries, & prescription drugs" SRC-0082 [ROG-POS-0012].

中文：他的关税立场分三步演变：全面支持、逐案调整、公开呼吁结束对加拿大的贸易战。在底特律经济俱乐部，他为关税辩护，称其纠正了“约 40 年的糟糕贸易政策”，并举通用汽车把 Silverado 生产线迁至 Orion 工厂为证 SRC-0020 [ROG-POS-0009]。他的生活成本方案包括暂停密歇根州汽油税——但该提案错述了 2019 年加税的提出者（共和党人提出并协助通过），且当日即遭共和党议会领袖否决 SRC-0013 [ROG-POS-0010]。他对住房与消费成本的诊断是高利率，应以约束联邦支出加以缓解 [ROG-POS-0011] SRC-0003；其固定信息是“把制造业岗位带回本土，降低汽油、食品杂货与处方药的价格”SRC-0082 [ROG-POS-0012]。

Democracy and the 2020 election

He supports the SAVE Act, which would require documentary proof of citizenship to register for federal elections and photo ID at the polls [ROG-POS-0013] SRC-0003. His record on 2020 is the most ambiguous item in the file: in November 2020 he wrote that Trump was "violating norms of behavior and setting a dangerous precedent" and that Biden should be certified; by 2024 he declined to say who won and said he had not called Trump "clearly responsible" for January 6 SRC-0003 [ROG-POS-0014]. The archive records the change rather than resolving it.

中文：他支持《SAVE 法案》，该法案要求登记参加联邦选举时提供公民身份证明文件，并在投票站出示带照片的身份证件 [ROG-POS-0013] SRC-0003。他在 2020 年大选上的记录是本档案中最模糊的一项：2020 年 11 月他撰文称特朗普“违反行为规范、开创危险先例”，并认为应当认证拜登胜选；到 2024 年他拒绝说明谁获胜，并表示自己未曾称特朗普对 1 月 6 日事件“明显负有责任”SRC-0003 [ROG-POS-0014]。本档案记录这一变化，而不对其下定论。

Technology, education, guns and LGBTQ rights

On technology he calls for a one-year state moratorium on data centres pending a "fair, transparent approval process" [ROG-POS-0015] SRC-0003, and says federal guardrails are needed for AI, drawing on a briefing he received as Intelligence Committee chair about an early AI-guided missile; he is open to a narrow antitrust exemption so AI firms can cooperate on safety, while saying of the pending bill "I still need to go through the details" SRC-0020 [ROG-POS-0016]. On education his promise is to "get kids reading at grade level again" and his framing is "education not indoctrination" SRC-0022 SRC-0082 [ROG-POS-0019]. He was endorsed by the NRA Political Victory Fund in the general election [ROG-POS-0018] SRC-0001. On LGBTQ rights he voted for the 2004 Federal Marriage Amendment and against repealing "don't ask, don't tell", but said in 2023 that his view on same-sex marriage had evolved and supported state anti-discrimination legislation, while opposing the 2024 Title IX rules and transgender athletes in women's sports [ROG-POS-0017] SRC-0003.

中文：技术方面，他呼吁对数据中心实行为期一年的州级暂停，以待“公平、透明的审批程序”[ROG-POS-0015] SRC-0003；他认为人工智能需要联邦层面的护栏，依据是他在担任情报委员会主席时听取的关于早期人工智能制导导弹的简报；他对给予人工智能企业有限反垄断豁免以便合作开展安全研究持开放态度，但对在审法案表示“我还需要细看细节”SRC-0020 [ROG-POS-0016]。教育方面，他的承诺是“让孩子们重新达到年级阅读水平”，其表述框架是“教育而非灌输”SRC-0022 SRC-0082 [ROG-POS-0019]。大选中他获得全国步枪协会政治胜利基金背书 [ROG-POS-0018] SRC-0001。LGBTQ 权利方面，他投票支持 2004 年联邦婚姻修正案、反对废除“不问不说”，但 2023 年表示自己对同性婚姻的看法已经演变，并支持州级反歧视立法，同时反对 2024 年的第九条新规与跨性别运动员参加女子项目 [ROG-POS-0017] SRC-0003。

Where the record is thin

Four rows are thin by construction. Gun policy rests on an endorsement with no first-person statement. Spending and interest-rate positions come from a summary rather than from him. The 2024 shift on 2020 certification is described but not explained. And no Rogers campaign policy paper was retrievable, so his positive agenda is known almost entirely through debate answers and the campaign homepage SRC-0082.

中文：四条记录在结构上即较为单薄。枪支政策仅依据一份背书，无本人表述。支出与利率立场来自摘要而非其本人。2024 年关于 2020 年认证问题的转向有描述而无解释。且未能取得罗杰斯竞选方的任何政策文件，因此其积极议程几乎只能通过辩论回答与竞选主页了解 SRC-0082。
