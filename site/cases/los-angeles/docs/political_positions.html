Nithya Raman — positions / Nithya Raman — 立场

Sixteen rows in ../policy_positions.csv as RAM-POL-0001 … RAM-POL-0016. Her site is unusually document-dense: eight issue pages (housing, homelessness, public safety, renters, Palisades recovery, transportation, plus biography and "why I'm running") were retrieved in full, so most positions are quoted from the candidate's own text rather than paraphrased from coverage.

../policy_positions.csv 中十六行，编号 RAM-POL-0001…RAM-POL-0016。她的网站文件密度异常高： 八个议题页面（住房、无家可归、公共安全、租客、帕利塞兹重建、交通，加传记与"我为何参选"）均完整 取得，故多数立场直接引自候选人原文，而非转述报道。

1. Housing: build more, permit faster / 住房：多建、快批

The core claim is arithmetic: the city has "the fewest homes per adult of major US cities" because land use policy limited where housing can be built, so the plan will "triple annual housing construction" across deed-restricted, market-rate, social housing and shelters (RAM-POL-0001) SRC-0007. The permitting promise is that it should take "weeks, not months", delivered through a mayoral office devoted to expediting housing (RAM-POL-0002) SRC-0007 SRC-0062. In the final debate she added that apartment construction is now falling, which is her causal link between the incumbent's tenure and the affordability crisis (SPE-RAM-0006) SRC-0062.

核心主张是算术性的：由于土地用途管制限制了可建位置，洛杉矶"人均住房量为美国大城市最少"，因此计划 把年度住房建设"提高两倍"，覆盖限制租金住房、市场价住房、社会住房与收容所（RAM-POL-0001） SRC-0007。许可承诺是"以周计而非以月计"，通过一个市长直属的加速审批办公室实现（RAM-POL-0002） SRC-0007 SRC-0062。在最后一场辩论中她补充说公寓建设正在下降，这是她把现任任期与可负担性危机 联系起来的因果链（SPE-RAM-0006）SRC-0062。

2. Renters and the RSO credit collision / 租客与租金条例归属冲突

The renters page is the archive's densest statistical argument: 245,599 eviction notices 2023–2025, 93% for non-payment, average arrears under $4,000, and a city where nearly two-thirds of residents rent and nearly 60% are rent-burdened (RAM-POL-0003) SRC-0010. She also claims credit for "revising the Rent Stabilization Ordinance to reduce rental increases for the first time in 40 years" (RAM-POL-0004) SRC-0014 — the identical achievement Bass claims (BAS-POL-0002). Both rows are conflicting sources; this is the archive's clearest evidence that the runoff was fought over ownership of a shared record rather than over divergent policy.

租客页面是档案中统计论证最密集的一处：2023–2025 年 245,599 份驱逐通知，93% 为欠租，平均欠款不足 4,000 美元；全市近三分之二居民租房、近 60% 承受租金负担（RAM-POL-0003）SRC-0010。她还把 "四十年来首次修订租金稳定条例以降低涨幅"算作自己的功劳（RAM-POL-0004）SRC-0014——与 Bass 主张的 完全同一政绩（BAS-POL-0002）。两行皆标 conflicting sources；这是档案中"决选争夺的是同一政绩的 归属、而非不同政策"的最清晰证据。

3. Homelessness: a target, a cost critique, a moral frame / 无家可归：目标、成本批评、道德框架

Three commitments: cut tent and encampment sleeping "by at least 50% by the 2028 Olympics" (RAM-POL-0005); build capacity to "effectively eliminate long-term encampments" in the areas people live, work, shop and go to school (RAM-POL-0005); and replace Inside Safe's economics — she says it costs "an average of $85,000 a year for a motel room and services", with some rooms near $100,000, and that "40% of people moved indoors through the Mayor's signature program have returned to the streets" (RAM-POL-0006) SRC-0008. Both statistics are the campaign's; the archive records them as claims and notes that no city audit was retrieved. Her moral vocabulary — "a moral failing and a stain on any leadership that would call itself progressive" — is quoted verbatim on the same page.

三项承诺：到 2028 年奥运会把睡在帐篷与营地里的人数"至少减少 50%"（RAM-POL-0005）；在人们居住、工作、 购物、上学的区域建立"有效清除长期营地"的能力（RAM-POL-0005）；并以另一套经济逻辑取代 Inside Safe—— 她称其"每间汽车旅馆房加服务年均 8.5 万美元"，部分房间接近 10 万美元，且"经由市长标志性项目安置 室内的人中有 40% 重返街头"（RAM-POL-0006）SRC-0008。两项统计均属竞选方；档案按主张记录，并注明 未取得任何市府审计。她的道德语汇——"一种道德失败，是任何自称进步的领导层的污点"——同页逐字引用。

4. Public safety: neither defund nor business as usual / 公共安全：既非削警，也非照旧

The framing sentence is explicit: "We will not achieve the public safety Angelenos need through the binary choice of defunding the police or defending business as usual" (RAM-POL-0007) SRC-0009. The accountability argument rests on a number — LAPD is "the single largest source of liability payouts in the City budget, accounting for $152 million in fiscal year 2025" (RAM-POL-0008) SRC-0009 — and on a personnel judgement: Chief Jim McDonnell has "lost the confidence" of the city (RAM-POL-0009) SRC-0060. The $152 million figure and the paraphrase of the chief judgement are campaign or commentary material, both flagged partially verified.

框架句很明确："我们无法通过削警或维护现状这种二元选择，实现洛杉矶人需要的公共安全"（RAM-POL-0007） SRC-0009。问责论证依赖一个数字——洛市警局是"市预算中最大的赔偿责任来源，2025 财年 1.52 亿美元" （RAM-POL-0008）SRC-0009——以及一项人事判断：警长 Jim McDonnell 已"失去这座城市的信任" （RAM-POL-0009）SRC-0060。1.52 亿美元与对警长判断的转述分别属竞选材料与评论材料，均标 partially verified。

5. Fire recovery and the budget / 火灾重建与预算

Her recovery plan proposes a single accountable "Recovery District" because rebuilding is "spread across more than twelve agencies, with no single accountable entity responsible for getting families home" (RAM-POL-0010) SRC-0011, with state law cited as the enabling authority. The accompanying accusations — a mayor "out of the country while the fire raged", a chief recovery officer who left after 90 days, and a city that "tried to cover up responsibility by altering the after-action report" (RAM-POL-0011) — are recorded, and the alteration allegation is explicitly unverified. The budget argument is that "City Hall made fiscal decisions that weakened our budget and our ability to function" (RAM-POL-0013) SRC-0013, with the Convention Center financing as the concrete case: "no responsible mayor would pursue them through a financing plan that drains essential services for decades" (RAM-POL-0014) SRC-0071.

她的重建计划主张设立单一问责的"重建区"，因为重建"分散在十二个以上机构，没有一个对让家庭返家负责 的实体"（RAM-POL-0010）SRC-0011，并援引州法作为授权依据。随之而来的指控——市长"在火势蔓延时 身在国外"、首席恢复官 90 天后离职、市府"试图通过修改事后报告来掩盖责任"（RAM-POL-0011）—— 均予记录，其中"修改报告"的指控明确标 unverified。预算论证是"市府做出的财政决定削弱了我们的预算 与运作能力"（RAM-POL-0013）SRC-0013，具体案例为会议中心融资："任何负责任的市长都不会用一项 数十年抽取基本服务资金的融资方案去推进它们"（RAM-POL-0014）SRC-0071。

6. Transit, immigration, foreign policy / 交通、移民、外交

Transportation is a delivery argument: Measures R and M "committed $120B" and Measure HLA (2024) mandated street-safety work at repaving, so the Sepulveda and Vermont corridors and the K Line Northern Extension must be accelerated (RAM-POL-0012) SRC-0012. On immigration she argues that "an adversarial federal government is sending ICE agents into our communities, and too many residents feel unprotected" (RAM-POL-0015) SRC-0013. The one foreign-policy row is deliberately thin: LAist reports that she and Bass "gave different descriptions of the Gaza war" at the Sept. 29 forum, but no verbatim quote was retrievable from the audio, so RAM-POL-0016 is partially verified SRC-0033.

交通是"交付"论证：R 号与 M 号措施"承诺 1,200 亿美元"，2024 年 HLA 号措施要求重铺路面时同步实施 街道安全改造，因此 Sepulveda、Vermont 走廊与 K 线北延必须加速（RAM-POL-0012）SRC-0012。移民方面 她主张"一个对抗性的联邦政府正把 ICE 探员送进我们的社区，太多居民感到不受保护"（RAM-POL-0015） SRC-0013。唯一的对外政策行有意写得单薄：LAist 报道她与 Bass 在 9 月 29 日论坛上"对加沙战争给出 不同描述"，但音频中未能取得逐字引语，故 RAM-POL-0016 为 partially verified SRC-0033。

7. Where the record is strongest and weakest / 记录最强与最弱之处

Strongest: housing, renters, homelessness and public safety — four domains with verbatim candidate text and named numbers. Weakest: Gaza (no transcript), the Recovery District's legal mechanism (state law cited but not identified), and every self-reported statistic, which the archive could not audit.

最强：住房、租客、无家可归与公共安全——四个领域有候选人原文与具名数字。最弱：加沙（无文字稿）、 重建区的法律机制（援引州法但未指明条文），以及所有自报统计——档案无法审计。
