Election context / 选举背景

1. The rules that shaped the race / 决定赛制的规则

Los Angeles elects its mayor in a nonpartisan contest: no party appears on the ballot, and if no candidate wins a majority in the June primary the top two advance to a November runoff. In 2026 the primary fell on 2026-06-02 and the runoff on 2026-11-03 SRC-0001. Because the office is nonpartisan, this archive records party = Nonpartisan for every candidate and reconstructs ideology only from endorsements, issue positions and coalition behaviour — never from a ballot label.

洛杉矶以无党派方式选举市长：选票上不列党派，若无人在 6 月初选获得多数，前两名进入 11 月决选。 2026 年初选为 2026-06-02，决选为 2026-11-03 SRC-0001。由于该职位无党派，本档案对每位 候选人记 party = Nonpartisan，并只从背书、议题立场与联盟行为重建意识形态——绝不用选票标签。

The practical consequences are visible in the data. Fourteen candidates appeared on the primary ballot SRC-0021 SRC-0002; the top three took 88.8% of the vote between them, so the primary functioned as a three-way contest with a long tail. The winner of the primary (Bass, 34.3%) did not win the runoff. The $1,800 per-donor contribution cap for mayor SRC-0034 pushed much of the race's money into independent expenditure committees, which is why campaign_finance.csv needs a report_type column.

这些后果在数据中可见。初选选票上有十四位候选人 SRC-0021 SRC-0002；前三名合计得票 88.8%， 故初选实质是三人之争加一条长尾。初选第一名（Bass，34.3%）并未赢得决选。市长选举单人捐款上限 1,800 美元 SRC-0034 把大量资金推向独立支出委员会，这正是 campaign_finance.csv 需要 report_type 列的原因。

2. The field / 候选人阵容

The field was set on the filing deadline of 2026-02-07, when Raman entered "on the last day" SRC-0013 SRC-0039 and Austin Beutner withdrew after his daughter's death SRC-0031. Beyond the three who mattered, the ballot carried ten candidates with published totals ranging from 30,008 (Adam Miller, 3.5%) down to 881 (Nelson Cheng, 0.1%) SRC-0021. Two write-in candidates were certified separately and have no published totals SRC-0002. Pratt's 217,977 votes (25.5%) are the single most consequential number in the primary: they are roughly twice the runoff's final margin.

阵容在 2026-02-07 提交截止日定型：Raman "在最后一天"入场 SRC-0013 SRC-0039，Austin Beutner 在 女儿去世后退出 SRC-0031。除三位关键人物外，选票上还有十位候选人，公布票数从 30,008（Adam Miller， 3.5%）到 881（Nelson Cheng，0.1%）SRC-0021。另有两名补写候选人获单独认证，无公布票数 SRC-0002。 Pratt 的 217,977 票（25.5%）是初选中最重要的单一数字：约为决选最终差额的两倍。

4. The counting environment / 计票环境

California counts mail ballots incrementally and releases updates in daily batches, so election-night leads are provisional for days. The Wikipedia account adds that this practice "triggered election conspiracists, including President Donald Trump" in 2026 SRC-0001 — a claim that rests on the encyclopedia alone and is therefore partially verified; no wire story retrieved here covers it. This is also why primary-night "Bass leads" reporting is recorded in results_notes.md as a count-order statement rather than a result.

加州分批计票、按日公布更新，故选举夜的领先数日在数天内都是暂定的。维基百科的叙述补充说，这一做法 在 2026 年"引发电举阴谋论者，包括特朗普总统" SRC-0001——该说法仅依赖百科，故为 partially verified；本档案未取得任何通讯社报道涉及此事。这也是 results_notes.md 把初选夜 "Bass 领先"的报道记为计票顺序陈述而非结果的原因。

5. The polling trajectory / 民调轨迹

Eight reconcilable runoff polls are in polling.csv. Read as a sequence, they show the incumbent starting ahead, the challenger taking a consistent mid-runoff lead, and the final poll returning to a tie: Tulchin (August) Bass +6; Hart (Sept. 1–9) Raman +5; Berkeley IGS (Sept. 15–20) Raman +11; Manhattan (Sept. 24–Oct. 1) Raman +3 leaned; FM3 and Impact (early October) Raman +2 and +10; McLaughlin (Oct. 15–18) Raman +8; Berkeley IGS (Oct. 19–25) tie at 40–40 with 19% undecided SRC-0028 SRC-0045 SRC-0019 SRC-0020. The final result (Raman +9.8) landed between the last two readings, which is a reminder that a 19% undecided share is not noise.

polling.csv 收录八项可对账的决选民调。按序阅读可见：现任者起步领先，挑战者在决选中段稳定领先， 最后一项民调回到平手：Tulchin（8 月）Bass +6；Hart（9/1–9）Raman +5；Berkeley IGS（9/15–20） Raman +11；Manhattan（9/24–10/1）含倾向 Raman +3；FM3 与 Impact（10 月初）Raman +2 与 +10； McLaughlin（10/15–18）Raman +8；Berkeley IGS（10/19–25）40–40 平手，19% 未决 SRC-0028 SRC-0045 SRC-0019 SRC-0020。最终结果（Raman +9.8）落在最后两次读数之间，提醒我们 19% 的 未决比例不是噪声。

6. The fabricated poll / 伪造民调

One widely circulated runoff poll showing Bass far ahead was retracted by the company that published it SRC-0047, and two outlets traced the operation behind it SRC-0080 SRC-0081. The archive excludes it from polling.csv and mentions it here because its existence is part of the election's information environment: in the final month, a number that was never a measurement circulated as one.

一份广为流传、显示 Bass 大幅领先的决选民调被其发布公司撤回 SRC-0047，两家媒体追查了其背后的 操作 SRC-0080 SRC-0081。档案将其排除在 polling.csv 之外，并在此提及，因为它的存在本身是这场 选举信息环境的一部分：在最后一个月，一个从未是测量的数字以测量的身份流通。

7. What overlaid the race / 叠加在选举之上的议题

Three exogenous frames ran through every retrieved source. The January 2025 fires and the slow rebuild SRC-0052 SRC-0011; federal immigration enforcement, which produced the runoff's most contested advertisement SRC-0044; and the 2028 Olympics, the deadline against which Raman set her encampment target SRC-0008 and against which Bass argued the city could not afford a change of management SRC-0062. A fourth, quieter frame — a roughly $1 billion budget gap SRC-0073 — supplied the challenger's fiscal argument and the city controller's endorsement SRC-0072.

三条外生框架贯穿所有已获取来源。2025 年 1 月大火与迟缓的重建 SRC-0052 SRC-0011； 联邦移民执法，催生了决选中最具争议的广告 SRC-0044；以及 2028 奥运会——Raman 以它为营地 目标设定时限 SRC-0008，Bass 以它论证城市承受不起管理层更替 SRC-0062。第四条更安静的框架—— 约 10 亿美元的预算缺口 SRC-0073——提供了挑战者的财政论点与市主计长的背书 SRC-0072。
