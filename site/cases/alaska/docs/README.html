2026 U.S. Senate election in Alaska / 2026 年阿拉斯加州联邦参议员选举

What this archive covers

This case documents the 2026 Alaska U.S. Senate race from the incumbent's March 2025 launch to the 17 November 2026 call. Republican Sen. Dan S. Sullivan sought a third term; Democrat Mary Peltola, a former at-large U.S. representative, challenged him and won with an outright first-round majority. The archive holds 124 sources, 30 campaign events, 31 policy positions, 12 archived speeches, 10 video-index entries, 12 images (6 downloaded portraits), 6 advertisements, 7 social/email items, 24 media-coverage records, 8 finance rows, 12 election-result rows and 8 polls.

本案例记录 2026 年阿拉斯加联邦参议员选举，从 2025 年 3 月现任议员宣布竞选，到 2026 年 11 月 17 日 媒体判定胜负。共和党参议员丹·沙利文寻求第三任期；民主党人、前全州联邦众议员玛丽·佩尔托拉挑战他， 并在第一轮即获过半票数获胜。档案含 124 个来源、30 场竞选活动、31 条政策立场、12 篇演讲存档、 10 条视频索引、12 张图像（其中 6 张已下载的肖像）、6 则广告、7 条社交/邮件条目、24 条媒体报道、 8 条财务记录、12 条选举结果与 8 项民调。

Five structural facts this archive establishes

  1. The ranked-choice machinery was never used. Peltola's 50.12% first-round share made the

    2. scheduled 2026-11-18 elimination round moot; her margin over Sullivan was 4.32 points and 12,458 votes (144,302 − 131,844). Every "second choice" claim in the campaign is therefore counterfactual, not tabulated.

  2. The same-name challenger did not decide the race. Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. took 4,760 votes

    3. (1.65%). Even if every one of those ballots had gone to the incumbent, Sullivan would have had 136,604 (47.45%) against Peltola's 144,302 (50.12%) — still a 7,698-vote loss, still short of 50%.

  3. The challenger out-raised the incumbent roughly two and a half to one. NBC reported

    4. $37 million for Peltola against $14 million for Sullivan through mid-October (SRC-0056); Sullivan's own campaign reported $13.9 million in Q3 receipts and about $5 million cash on hand on 2026-10-16 (SRC-0024).

  4. Sullivan ran behind his party's House candidate; Peltola ran far ahead of hers. In the same

    5. election, Republican House candidate Nick Begich III took 136,215 votes (47.54%) — 4,371 more than Sullivan — while independent House candidate Bill Hill took 119,689 (41.77%), 24,613 fewer than Peltola (SRC-0001). Peltola's vote was within 966 of the 145,268 drawn by the governor's-race leader, Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins (SRC-0001).

  5. The primary was crowded and deliberately distorted. The primary results table lists sixteen

    6. Senate candidates; 166,004 votes were cast, Peltola took 49.5% and Sullivan 41.4% (SRC-0006, SRC-0064). Outside groups spent more than $2 million promoting fringe Republican Gerald Heikes, and about $180,000 each promoting Green Richard Grayson and Democrat Carol Hafner (SRC-0017, SRC-0041, SRC-0045).

  1. 排序复选机制从未启用。 佩尔托拉首轮得票 50.12%，使原定 2026-11-18 的淘汰轮失去意义；

    2. 她对沙利文的优势为 4.32 个百分点、12,458 票（144,302 − 131,844）。因此竞选中的一切"第二选择" 说法都只是反事实推演，而非实际计票结果。

  2. 同名挑战者并未决定选举。 小丹尼尔·沙利文获 4,760 票（1.65%）。即使这些票全部归现任议员，

    3. 沙利文也只有 136,604 票（47.45%），对手 144,302 票（50.12%）——仍少 7,698 票，仍未过半。

  3. 挑战者筹款约为现任议员的两倍半。 NBC 报道截至 10 月中旬佩尔托拉筹得 3,700 万美元，

    4. 沙利文 1,400 万美元（SRC-0056）；沙利文竞选团队 2026-10-16 自报第三季度收入 1,390 万美元、 手头现金约 500 万美元（SRC-0024）。

  4. 沙利文得票低于本党众议员候选人，佩尔托拉远高于众议员候选人。 同一场选举中，共和党众议员

    5. 候选人尼克·贝吉奇获 136,215 票（47.54%），比沙利文多 4,371 票；独立众议员候选人比尔·希尔获 119,689 票（41.77%），比佩尔托拉少 24,613 票（SRC-0001）。佩尔托拉与州长选战领先者 乔纳森·克莱斯-汤姆金斯所得 145,268 票仅差 966 票（SRC-0001）。

  5. 初选候选人拥挤且被人为扭曲。 初选结果表列出 16 名参议员候选人，共 166,004 票，佩尔托拉 49.5%、

    6. 沙利文 41.4%（SRC-0006、SRC-0064）。外部团体花费逾 200 万美元推举边缘共和党人海克斯， 并各花约 18 万美元推举绿党人格雷森与民主党人哈夫纳（SRC-0017、SRC-0041、SRC-0045）。

What is strong, what is thin

Strong: the official results arithmetic and turnout; the same-name ballot saga (nine ADN/APM/Beacon stories plus two court rulings); the three joint debates with verbatim quotes; endorsement and finance reporting; six licence-cleared portraits.

Thin: crowd sizes and rally venues (almost never reported); any documented Peltola or Sullivan appearance at the October AFN convention; paid-ad creatives (no ad video or script retrieved); Sullivan's concession — no concession speech or event was found; precinct-level results (Alaska publishes by state house district, and no machine-readable district table was retrieved).

强项：官方结果算术与投票率；同名选票争议（九篇 ADN/APM/Beacon 报道加两次法院裁决）；三场联合辩论 及逐字引语； endorsement 与财务报道；六张授权清晰的肖像。 薄弱：集会人数与场地（几乎无报道）；10 月 AFN 大会上两位候选人的露面均无记载；付费广告素材 （未取得任何广告视频或脚本）；沙利文的认输——未找到认输演讲或活动； precinct 级结果 （阿拉斯加按州众议院选区公布，未取得可机读的选区表）。
