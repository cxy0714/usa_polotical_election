Candidate comparison / 候选人比较

The short version

An incumbent with a national-security brand, an institutional endorsement coalition and a resource-development message lost to a former congresswoman who ran on prices, fisheries and "Alaska First", raised two and a half times his money, and cleared 50% in the first round. The same-name ballot dispute dominated six weeks of coverage and moved 4,760 votes — not the outcome.

一位以国家安全为品牌、拥有建制派背书联盟与资源开发论述的现任议员，败给了一位以物价、渔业与 "阿拉斯加优先"为纲领、筹款为其两倍半、并在首轮即过半的前众议员。同名选票争议占据了六周报道， 但只涉及 4,760 票——并未改变结果。

Where they actually agree

The most under-reported fact of the race is issue convergence. Both candidates attacked trawl bycatch at Kodiak and promised to curb factory trawlers (SRC-0036). Both claim credit for blocking an Endangered Species Act listing for Chinook salmon (SRC-0045). Both backed large-scale oil development, including ANWR or NPR-A work, and both attacked "Lower 48" regulators — Peltola from the left flank of the development consensus, Sullivan from the right (SRC-0040, SRC-0045). Both were co-endorsed by Sealaska's board (SRC-0102). Neither campaigned on the Jones Act, and neither has a retrieved position on it (PEL-POS-12, SUL-POS-15).

The election was therefore not a referendum on resource development. It was a referendum on affordability, on Trump, and on which candidate Alaskans believed would defend the state's interests in Washington.

本场选举最被低估的事实是议题趋同：两位候选人都在科迪亚克抨击拖网兼捕、都承诺约束工厂拖网船 （SRC-0036）；都声称阻止了帝王鲑被列入《濒危物种法》（SRC-0045）；都支持大规模石油开发 （含 ANWR 或 NPR-A），都攻击"下四十八州"监管者——佩尔托拉站在开发共识的左翼，沙利文站在右翼 （SRC-0040、SRC-0045）；两人均获 Sealaska 董事会共同背书（SRC-0102）；无人以《琼斯法案》竞选， 也均无已取得的立场（PEL-POS-12、SUL-POS-15）。因此这场选举并非对资源开发的公投，而是对 可负担性、对特朗普、以及对"谁能在华盛顿捍卫本州利益"的公投。

Where the race was decided

Three measurable asymmetries stand out.

  1. Money. $37M to $14M through mid-October is a 2.6:1 advantage for the challenger (SRC-0056).

    2. Incumbents rarely lose that column in Alaska; when they do, the incumbent's message is being out-supplied.

  2. Party drag. Sullivan ran 4,371 votes behind Begich, the Republican House candidate, while

    3. Peltola ran 24,613 ahead of the independent House candidate (SRC-0001). A Republican incumbent underperforming his own ticket by that margin, in a state Trump carried three times, is the clearest aggregate signal in the results.

  3. The affordability frame. Peltola's ads and speeches tied the Iran war to fuel prices and

    4. corporate concentration to grocery prices (SRC-0043, SRC-0036). Sullivan's counter — that she is a "Lower 48 liberal" — was repeated sixteen times in one forum but never answered the price claim on its own terms (SRC-0045).

三项可测量的不对称为主：其一，资金——截至 10 月中旬 3,700 万对 1,400 万，挑战者领先 2.6 倍 （SRC-0056）；其二，政党拖累——沙利文比共和党众议员候选人贝吉奇少 4,371 票，佩尔托拉比独立 众议员候选人希尔多 24,613 票（SRC-0001），在特朗普三度拿下的州，现任共和党人低于本党得票如此 之多，是最清晰的总体信号；其三，可负担性框架——佩尔托拉把对伊战争与油价、企业集中与食品价格 相连（SRC-0043、SRC-0036），而沙利文"下四十八州自由派"的反击在一场论坛重复十六次，却未正面回应 物价主张（SRC-0045）。

The same-name episode, weighed

Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. received 4,760 votes (1.65%). The maximal counterfactual — every minor vote to Sullivan — still leaves him 708 votes short (see results_notes.md). The episode's real effect was agenda-setting: it produced at least nine news stories, two court rulings, a DOJ suit and a state investigation, and it forced the incumbent to spend the campaign's final weeks litigating ballot mechanics rather than prices (SRC-0009 to SRC-0016, SRC-0038, SRC-0047).

小丹尼尔·沙利文获 4,760 票（1.65%）。极限反事实——全部小党票归沙利文——仍少 708 票 （见 results_notes.md）。该事件真正的效应是议程设置：至少九篇报道、两次法院裁决、一次司法部 诉讼与一次州级调查，并迫使现任议员在选战最后数周纠缠选票机制而非物价 （SRC-0009 至 SRC-0016、SRC-0038、SRC-0047）。
