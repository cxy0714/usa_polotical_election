What the timeline shows

Of 14 entries, 9 are documented appearances, 3 are endorsement announcements and 2 are unverified gap markers; 3 are joint debates. The cluster of endorsement rows is a property of the sources, not of the campaign: Alaska outlets report endorsement releases far more reliably than rally logistics.

14 条记录中，9 条为有据可查的露面，3 条为背书公告，2 条为未核实缺口标记，3 场为联合辩论。背书记录密集源于信息来源而非竞选本身：阿拉斯加媒体对背书新闻稿的报道远比对集会后勤可靠。
