Annie Andrews — Biography / 安妮·安德鲁斯——生平

Overview / 概览

Annie Elizabeth Andrews (born August 9, 1980) is a pediatrician who was elected to the United States Senate from South Carolina in 2026. A Democrat, she defeated Republican appointee Darline Graham on November 3, 2026, becoming the first woman elected to the Senate from South Carolina and the first Democrat to win a South Carolina Senate election since 1998. She is scheduled to take office on January 3, 2027 [SRC-0400]. Before entering politics she spent fourteen years as a pediatric hospitalist at the Medical University of South Carolina and taught pediatrics there; her research and advocacy concerned firearm injuries among children [SRC-0400]. Her only previous campaign was the 2022 race for South Carolina's 1st congressional district, which she lost to incumbent Nancy Mace, 42.5% to 56.5% [SRC-0400][SRC-0416].

安妮·伊丽莎白·安德鲁斯（1980 年 8 月 9 日生）是一名儿科医生，2026 年在南卡罗来纳州当选联邦参议员。作为民主党人，她于 2026 年 11 月 3 日击败共和党受任者达琳·格雷厄姆，成为南卡首位经选举产生的女性联邦参议员，也是 1998 年以来首位在南卡赢下参议员选举的民主党人。她定于 2027 年 1 月 3 日就职 [SRC-0400]。进入政界前，她在南卡罗来纳医科大学（MUSC）做了十四年儿科住院医师（pediatric hospitalist）并任教，其研究与倡议聚焦儿童枪伤问题 [SRC-0400]。她此前唯一一次竞选是 2022 年南卡第一国会选区的选举，以 42.5% 对 56.5% 败给现任议员南希·梅斯 [SRC-0400][SRC-0416]。

Early life and education / 早年生活与教育

Annie Elizabeth Lintzenich was born on August 9, 1980 in Paducah, Kentucky, and grew up in Indianapolis, Indiana, where she graduated from Cathedral High School [SRC-0400]. She earned a bachelor's degree at the University of Dayton, where she was a member of Chi Omega sorority, then a master's degree in clinical research from the Medical University of South Carolina and a Doctor of Medicine from the University of Cincinnati [SRC-0400]. The retrieved source does not state the years of any of these degrees, or the year of her pediatrics residency in Cincinnati.

安妮·伊丽莎白·林岑尼希（Annie Elizabeth Lintzenich）1980 年 8 月 9 日生于肯塔基州帕迪尤卡，在印第安纳州印第安纳波利斯长大，毕业于 Cathedral High School [SRC-0400]。她于代顿大学取得学士学位（在校期间为 Chi Omega 姊妹会成员），随后在 MUSC 取得临床研究硕士学位、在辛辛那提大学取得医学博士学位 [SRC-0400]。已检索来源未载明上述学位的具体年份，也未载明她在辛辛那提儿科住院医师培训的年份。

Medical career / 医疗职业生涯

Andrews completed her pediatrics residency in Cincinnati before moving to the South Carolina Lowcountry in 2009. She then worked fourteen years at MUSC's children's hospital in Charleston as a pediatric hospitalist and held a professorship in pediatrics at MUSC [SRC-0400]. In September 2022, while running for Congress, she announced she was taking an unpaid leave "to protect myself and my colleagues", and she resigned in June 2023 [SRC-0400][SRC-0413]. As of mid-2025 she worked part-time as a pediatrician at Children's National Hospital in Washington, D.C., while continuing to live in Charleston [SRC-0400].

安德鲁斯在辛辛那提完成儿科住院医师培训后，于 2009 年迁往南卡低地地区（Lowcountry）。此后她在查尔斯顿的 MUSC 儿童医院做了十四年儿科住院医师，并在 MUSC 担任儿科学教授 [SRC-0400]。2022 年 9 月，正在竞选国会的她宣布休无薪假，"以保护自己与同事"，并于 2023 年 6 月辞职 [SRC-0400][SRC-0413]。截至 2025 年年中，她在华盛顿特区儿童国家医院（Children's National Hospital）兼职行医，同时仍居住在查尔斯顿 [SRC-0400]。

Gun-violence research and advocacy / 枪支暴力研究与倡议

Andrews dates her political activism to the 2018 Parkland high school shooting, after which she joined the gun-safety group Moms Demand Action and began clinical research into gun-violence prevention aimed at reducing pediatric firearm injuries [SRC-0400]. The retrieved source records her own framing of the subject as directly related to her work as a pediatrician; it does not quote her at length on the research itself.

安德鲁斯将自己的政治行动起点追溯到 2018 年帕克兰高中枪击案；此后她加入了控枪组织 Moms Demand Action，并开始从事以减少儿童枪伤为目标的枪支暴力预防临床研究 [SRC-0400]。已检索来源记录了她本人的表述——该议题与她的儿科医生工作直接相关——但未就研究本身给出长篇引语。

2022 U.S. House campaign (SC-01) / 2022 年国会众议员竞选（SC-01）

She announced her candidacy for South Carolina's 1st congressional district in November 2021 — her first run for political office — and won the Democratic nomination in June 2022 after running unopposed [SRC-0400]. In September 2022 the incumbent, Nancy Mace, began running ads accusing Andrews of "child abuse" for providing gender-affirming care to minors; Andrews responded with a statement rejecting surgery for anyone under 18 while defending evidence-based care with parental consent, condemned the attacks as putting LGBTQ children at risk, and said she would take unpaid leave and increase her personal security [SRC-0400][SRC-0433]. The Charleston City Paper editorial board endorsed her in October 2022 [SRC-0400][SRC-0418]. She lost on November 8, 2022 with 42.5% to Mace's 56.5%, conceded by telephone, and said she was unsure where her political career would go next but that her candidacy had encouraged other medical professionals to run for office [SRC-0400][SRC-0416].

她于 2021 年 11 月宣布竞选南卡第一国会选区——这是她首次竞选公职——并在无人竞争的情况下于 2022 年 6 月赢得民主党提名 [SRC-0400]。2022 年 9 月，现任议员南希·梅斯开始投放广告，指称安德鲁斯为未成年人提供性别肯定医疗构成"虐待儿童"；安德鲁斯发表声明回应，表示不支持对 18 岁以下者实施手术、但支持在父母同意下的循证医疗，谴责该攻击使 LGBTQ 儿童面临更大风险，并称将休无薪假、加强人身安保 [SRC-0400][SRC-0433]。2022 年 10 月，查尔斯顿 City Paper 编辑委员会为她背书 [SRC-0400][SRC-0418]。她于 2022 年 11 月 8 日以 42.5% 对梅斯 56.5% 落败，随后致电承认败选，并表示不确定自己政治生涯的下一步，但她的参选鼓励了更多医疗专业人士投身选举 [SRC-0400][SRC-0416]。

Their Future. Our Vote / "Their Future. Our Vote" 政治行动委员会

In April 2023 Andrews announced the creation of a political action committee called "Their Future. Our Vote", described in press coverage as focused on electing candidates who advocate for children [SRC-0400][SRC-0411][SRC-0412].

2023 年 4 月，安德鲁斯宣布成立名为 "Their Future. Our Vote" 的政治行动委员会；据媒体报道，该委员会致力于选出为儿童权益发声的候选人 [SRC-0400][SRC-0411][SRC-0412]。

2026 Senate campaign / 2026 年参议员竞选

On May 29, 2025 Andrews announced her candidacy for the United States Senate, aiming to unseat Republican incumbent Lindsey Graham; she said she decided to run after President Donald Trump appointed Robert F. Kennedy Jr. as Secretary of Health [SRC-0400][SRC-0405]. She was endorsed by former Democratic National Committee chair Jaime Harrison and by the 314 Action Fund, EMILYs List, the JAC and Vote Mama [SRC-0400][SRC-0419][SRC-0420][SRC-0421][SRC-0422][SRC-0423]. She won the Democratic nomination on June 9, 2026 [SRC-0400][SRC-0438]. Lindsey Graham died on July 11, 2026 and his sister Darline was appointed two days later; Darline Graham won the Republican nomination in an August 25 special primary runoff, so Andrews faced Graham, Constitution Party nominee Mark Hackett, Libertarian Kasie Whitener and write-in candidate Catherine Fleming Bruce in the general election [SRC-0400][SRC-0401].

2025 年 5 月 29 日，安德鲁斯宣布竞选联邦参议员，目标是把共和党现任议员林赛·格雷厄姆拉下马；她表示，促使她参选的是特朗普总统任命小罗伯特·肯尼迪为卫生部长 [SRC-0400][SRC-0405]。她获得前民主党全国委员会主席海梅·哈里森以及 314 Action Fund、EMILYs List、JAC、Vote Mama 的背书 [SRC-0400][SRC-0419][SRC-0420][SRC-0421][SRC-0422][SRC-0423]。她于 2026 年 6 月 9 日赢得民主党提名 [SRC-0400][SRC-0438]。林赛·格雷厄姆于 2026 年 7 月 11 日去世，其妹达琳两天后受任命；达琳·格雷厄姆在 8 月 25 日的特别初选决选中赢得共和党提名，因此安德鲁斯在大选中面对格雷厄姆、宪法党提名人马克·哈克特、自由党提名人凯西·惠特纳以及写名候选人凯瑟琳·弗莱明·布鲁斯 [SRC-0400][SRC-0401]。

Result and transition / 结果与过渡

Andrews won on November 3, 2026, becoming the first woman elected to the Senate from South Carolina; the seat had been in Republican hands without interruption since 1964, when Strom Thurmond switched parties [SRC-0400]. The state-level vote totals for this race are held by Stream F and were not present in the local mirror used for this biography, so they are not restated here. She is to assume office on January 3, 2027 [SRC-0400].

安德鲁斯于 2026 年 11 月 3 日胜选，成为南卡首位经选举产生的女性联邦参议员；该席位自 1964 年斯特罗姆·瑟蒙德转党以来一直由共和党连续持有 [SRC-0400]。本场选举的州级得票总数由 F 线索负责，且不在本传记所使用的本地镜像中，故此处不予重述。她将于 2027 年 1 月 3 日就职 [SRC-0400]。

Personal life / 个人生活

In 2011 Andrews married Charlie Andrews, a neurological critical care doctor at MUSC; they have three children. She filed for divorce in August 2023 and the divorce was finalized in 2024 [SRC-0400].

2011 年，安德鲁斯与 MUSC 神经重症监护医生查理·安德鲁斯（Charlie Andrews）结婚，育有三名子女。她于 2023 年 8 月提出离婚，2024 年离婚生效 [SRC-0400]。
