Darline Graham — Biography / 达琳·格雷厄姆——生平

Overview / 概览

Darline Lettie Graham Nordone (born Darlene Lettie Graham, June 12, 1964) is a Republican who served as the junior United States senator from South Carolina from July 2026, appointed to fill the vacancy created by the death of her older brother, Lindsey Graham [SRC-0450][SRC-0451]. She was the Republican nominee in the 2026 Senate election and lost to Democrat Annie Andrews [SRC-0450][SRC-0482]. She is the first woman to represent South Carolina in the Senate and, per the retrieved biography, the first sister to succeed her brother in that office [SRC-0450]. Her incumbency is dated: appointed 2026-07-13, sworn in 2026-07-14, which means she had been a sitting senator for 112 days when the election was held on 2026-11-03 [SRC-0450][SRC-0457]. She had never run for or held elected office before the appointment [SRC-0459].

达琳·莱蒂·格雷厄姆·诺多内（Darline Lettie Graham Nordone，原名 Darlene Lettie Graham，1964 年 6 月 12 日生）是共和党人，自 2026 年 7 月起任南卡罗来纳州联邦参议员，受任命填补其兄林赛·格雷厄姆去世留下的空缺 [SRC-0450][SRC-0451]。她是 2026 年参议员选举的共和党提名人，败给民主党人安妮·安德鲁斯 [SRC-0450][SRC-0482]。她是南卡首位在参议院代表该州的女性，据已检索传记亦为美国参议院史上首位接替兄长席位的妹妹 [SRC-0450]。其"现任"身份有明确日期：2026-07-13 受任命、2026-07-14 宣誓就职，故在 2026-11-03 投票日时她仅已担任参议员 112 天 [SRC-0450][SRC-0457]。受任命之前，她从未竞选或担任民选公职 [SRC-0459]。

Early life and family / 早年生活与家庭

Graham was born in Central, South Carolina, to Florence James ("F. J.") Graham and Millie Graham (née Walters). Her parents operated a restaurant, bar, liquor store and pool hall, and the family lived in rooms behind the business [SRC-0450]. Her mother died of cancer when Graham was 11, and her father died of a heart attack 15 months later; after their deaths her brother Lindsey arranged for her to live with an aunt and uncle in Seneca, South Carolina [SRC-0450]. Lindsey, nine years her senior, became her legal guardian before joining the Air Force in 1982, and he taught her to spell her first name "Darline", the spelling she uses as an adult [SRC-0450][SRC-0486].

格雷厄姆生于南卡罗来纳州森特勒尔（Central），父母为弗洛伦斯·詹姆斯·"F. J."·格雷厄姆与米莉·格雷厄姆（娘家姓 Walters）。父母经营餐馆、酒吧、酒类商店与台球厅，全家住在店铺后方的房间里 [SRC-0450]。母亲在她 11 岁时因癌症去世，父亲在 15 个月后因心脏病去世；父母双亡后，其兄林赛安排她住到南卡塞尼卡（Seneca）的姑父母家中 [SRC-0450]。长她九岁的林赛在 1982 年加入空军前成为她的法定监护人，并教她把名字拼作"Darline"——这也是她成年后一直使用的拼法 [SRC-0450][SRC-0486]。

Education / 教育

She graduated from the College of Charleston with a Bachelor of Science in sociology in 1989, earned a Master of Arts in rehabilitation counseling from South Carolina State University in 2009, and is a certified public manager [SRC-0450][SRC-0466].

她于 1989 年在查尔斯顿学院取得社会学理学士学位，2009 年在南卡罗来纳州立大学取得康复咨询文学硕士学位，并持有注册公共管理者（certified public manager）资格 [SRC-0450][SRC-0466]。

Public administration career / 公共行政职业生涯

In 2007 Graham began working for the South Carolina Vocational Rehabilitation Department, where she helped coordinate a remote work program for adults with severe disabilities for Time Warner; by 2015 she was the department's public information director [SRC-0450]. In September 2019 she became commissioner and agency head of the South Carolina Commission for the Blind, which administers vocational rehabilitation, independent living and blindness-prevention programs; she held that post until July 14, 2026 [SRC-0450][SRC-0464]. She also serves on the South Carolina State Workforce Development Board and its works management committee, and in 2026 became president-elect of the National Council of State Agencies for the Blind, with a term scheduled to begin in January 2027 [SRC-0450][SRC-0467]. Disability-policy coverage describes her career as centred on vocational rehabilitation — publicly funded programs that help people with disabilities overcome barriers to employment — and notes that because she had never held elected office it was difficult to know what her priorities would be [SRC-0459].

2007 年，格雷厄姆进入南卡罗来纳州职业康复部工作，其间协助为时代华纳（Time Warner）协调一项面向重度残疾成年人的远程工作计划；至 2015 年，她已担任该部公共信息主任 [SRC-0450]。2019 年 9 月，她出任南卡盲人委员会委员兼机构负责人，该机构负责职业康复、独立生活与防盲项目；她任该职至 2026 年 7 月 14 日 [SRC-0450][SRC-0464]。她还任职于南卡州劳动力发展委员会及其工程管理委员会，并于 2026 年当选全国州级盲人机构理事会（National Council of State Agencies for the Blind）候任主席，任期定于 2027 年 1 月开始 [SRC-0450][SRC-0467]。残障政策领域的报道称其职业生涯以职业康复为核心——即帮助残障人士跨越就业障碍的公共资助项目——并指出由于她从未担任民选公职，外界难以判断其施政优先事项 [SRC-0459]。

Political involvement before 2026 / 2026 年之前的政治参与

Graham appeared in a 2002 television advertisement for her brother's Senate campaign, assisted him in later campaigns, discussed her childhood during the 2014 and 2026 Senate races, and supported his 2016 presidential campaign, introducing him at his first campaign speech [SRC-0450]. This is the whole of her documented political activity before the appointment; Washington Post coverage characterized her as having little political history beyond praising her brother [SRC-0460].

格雷厄姆曾于 2002 年出演其兄参议员竞选的电视广告，此后多次协助其竞选，在 2014 年与 2026 年的参议员竞选中讲述自己的童年，并支持其兄 2016 年的总统竞选、在其首场竞选演讲中作介绍 [SRC-0450]。这便是她在受任命前有据可查的全部政治活动；《华盛顿邮报》的报道称，除对兄长的赞誉之外，她几乎没有政治履历 [SRC-0460]。

Appointment to the Senate (2026) / 参议院受任命（2026）

After Lindsey Graham's death on July 11, 2026, Governor Henry McMaster was tasked with appointing a successor to serve the remainder of the term [SRC-0401]. President Donald Trump publicly recommended Darline Graham on July 13, as did Senate Majority Leader John Thune; McMaster selected her the same day and she was sworn in on July 14 [SRC-0450][SRC-0451][SRC-0496]. Lieutenant Governor Pamela Evette was considered but not appointed, and Representative Joe Wilson declined to be considered [SRC-0401]. On July 20 she announced she would run for a full term [SRC-0496][SRC-0454].

林赛·格雷厄姆于 2026 年 7 月 11 日去世后，由州长亨利·麦克马斯特负责任命继任者完成剩余任期 [SRC-0401]。7 月 13 日，特朗普总统公开推荐达琳·格雷厄姆，参议院多数党领袖约翰·图恩亦作同样推荐；麦克马斯特当日选定她，她于 7 月 14 日宣誓就职 [SRC-0450][SRC-0451][SRC-0496]。副州长帕梅拉·埃维特曾被考虑但未获任命，众议员乔·威尔逊拒绝被考虑 [SRC-0401]。7 月 20 日，她宣布将竞选完整任期 [SRC-0496][SRC-0454]。

In July 2026 she introduced S.5025, the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia Act of 2026, a revised version of the Sanctioning Russia Act her brother had authored — and not the version that ultimately became law [SRC-0439][SRC-0450]. The enacted measure, H.R. 5334, passed the Senate 86-11 on August 7, 2026 and the House 262-159 on September 16, and was signed on September 18; it raises tariffs on Russian products by up to 500% ad valorem, allows tariffs of up to 100% on the top five importers of Russian crude oil and natural gas, sanctions the shadow fleet and additional Russian banks, and extends the Iran Sanctions Act five years to 2031 [SRC-0439]. Two days before the law was to take effect in October 2026, President Trump signed an executive order waiving the Russia sanctions [SRC-0439]. She also voted to confirm Todd Blanche as United States Attorney General, which the Senate approved 50-49; she said she was "proud to vote to confirm" him and called him "highly qualified" [SRC-0450][SRC-0473]. No other vote by her is recorded in the retrieved sources.

2026 年 7 月 16 日，她提出《林赛·O·格雷厄姆制裁俄罗斯与伊朗法案（2026）》，该法案以她兄长起草的《制裁俄罗斯法案》为基础、对俄罗斯实施制裁；法案获得两党支持，8 月在参议院通过、9 月中旬在众议院通过，特朗普总统在两日后签署成法 [SRC-0450][SRC-0470][SRC-0471]。她还投票确认托德·布兰奇（Todd Blanche）出任美国司法部长，参议院以 50 比 49 通过；她称"proud to vote to confirm"并称其"highly qualified" [SRC-0450][SRC-0473]。已检索来源中未记载她的其他表决。

2026 special primary and the general election / 2026 年特别初选与大选

Trump publicly encouraged her to run on July 17 and promised to endorse her; three days later she told Sean Hannity she intended to run for a full term, saying "I've made a decision… I'm in" [SRC-0450][SRC-0456][SRC-0454]. Under South Carolina law a special Republican primary was held on August 11, 2026, with a filing period of July 21–28 [SRC-0401]. Graham finished first and advanced to an August 25 runoff against Representative Ralph Norman, which she won [SRC-0450][SRC-0481]. Trump campaigned for her, headlining a rally in Myrtle Beach, and the runoff turned partly on Norman's initial refusal to vote for the One Big Beautiful Bill Act [SRC-0401][SRC-0480]. In the general election she faced Andrews, Constitution Party nominee Mark Hackett, Libertarian Kasie Whitener and write-in candidate Catherine Fleming Bruce, and lost on November 3, 2026 [SRC-0450][SRC-0482][SRC-0483]. A remark during a primary runoff debate that "national security is not my thing" was treated by the retrieved sources as a catalyst for her defeat, with some voters coming to see her as inexperienced and unqualified [SRC-0450][SRC-0401].

特朗普于 7 月 17 日公开鼓励她参选并承诺背书；三日后她向肖恩·汉尼提表示将竞选完整任期，称"I've made a decision… I'm in" [SRC-0450][SRC-0456][SRC-0454]。依南卡法律，共和党特别初选于 2026 年 8 月 11 日举行，候选人登记期为 7 月 21 日至 28 日 [SRC-0401]。格雷厄姆得票居首，进入 8 月 25 日与众议员拉尔夫·诺曼的决选并获胜 [SRC-0450][SRC-0481]。特朗普为她助选、在默特尔比奇主持集会，决选部分围绕诺曼最初拒绝支持《One Big Beautiful Bill Act》展开 [SRC-0401][SRC-0480]。大选中她面对安德鲁斯、宪法党提名人马克·哈克特、自由党提名人凯西·惠特纳与写名候选人凯瑟琳·弗莱明·布鲁斯，于 2026 年 11 月 3 日败选 [SRC-0450][SRC-0482][SRC-0483]。已检索来源将她在初选决选辩论中"national security is not my thing"一语视为其败选的催化剂，部分选民因此认为她缺乏经验与资格 [SRC-0450][SRC-0401]。

Personal life / 个人生活

Graham was married to Terry Lee Boggs until 2003; they have a daughter. She married Larry Nordone circa 2006; they also have a daughter, and they live in Lexington, South Carolina [SRC-0450].

格雷厄姆与特里·李·博格斯（Terry Lee Boggs）的婚姻持续至 2003 年，育有一女。她约于 2006 年与拉里·诺多内（Larry Nordone）结婚，亦育有一女，现居南卡列克星敦（Lexington）[SRC-0450]。
