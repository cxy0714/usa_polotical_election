Communication analysis — Annie Andrews / 传播分析——安妮·安德鲁斯

1. The evidence base / 1. 证据基础

Nine Andrews videos were downloaded (1.47 GB corpus, see ../10_video_frames/video_sources.csv); eight yielded captions and therefore text. The set spans the launch (2025-05-29), the primary-night speech (2026-06-10), two interviews (Face the Nation 2026-05-07; WBTV 2026-08-26), the "Gas, Grits" tour kickoff (2026-09-04), two network uploads of the victory speech (2026-11-04), an MS NOW profile (2026-10-15) and a projection clip.

本次下载了 9 个安德鲁斯视频（语料 1.47 GB，见 video_sources.csv），其中 8 个有字幕因而有文本。时间跨度覆盖启动（2025-05-29）、初选之夜讲话（2026-06-10）、两次采访（《面向全国》2026-05-07；WBTV 2026-08-26）、「Gas, Grits」巡回启动（2026-09-04）、胜选讲话的两个电视网版本（2026-11-04）、MS NOW 人物报道（2026-10-15）与一则判定剪辑。

The corpus does not include a single rally speech with a venue and a crowd. That absence is itself the most important fact about her communication record: what survives is studio appearance, produced video and podium speech, not the travelling campaign.

语料中没有一场有场地、有人数的集会演讲。这一缺失本身即是其传播记录中最重要的事实：留存下来的是演播室露面、成品视频与讲台讲话，而不是巡回竞选。

2. The launch: biography as argument / 2. 启动：以传记作为论证

The 2025-05-29 launch video opens with credentials, not with a policy list [caption]: "My name is Dr. Annie Andrews, and for almost two decades, I've been a pediatrician in South Carolina. Parents trust me to treat their kids for just about everything you can imagine."

2025-05-29 的启动影片以资历开场，而非政策清单 [caption]：「我叫安妮·安德鲁斯医生，近二十年来一直是南卡罗来纳的儿科医生。家长们信任我治疗他们孩子几乎能想象到的一切疾病。」

It then does something unusual for a launch: it shows clinical evidence. The transcript records an x-ray of a child with measles pneumonia ("a condition easily prevented by vaccines") and a child with cancer. The argument structure is evidence → conclusion, with the candidate as the witness who has seen the consequence of policy failure, rather than as the author of a programme.

随后它做了一件在启动影片中不常见的事：展示临床证据。转写记录了患麻疹肺炎儿童的 X 光片（「一种用疫苗很容易预防的疾病」）与一名患癌儿童。论证结构是证据 → 结论，候选人充当目睹政策失败后果的证人，而不是纲领的作者。

INTERPRETATION: this is a "witness" register. It makes the candidate's authority clinical rather than political, which suits a first-time candidate with no legislative record — and it constrains her later: a witness must keep pointing at consequences, and the record shows her doing exactly that with cost-of-living examples in the fall.

INTERPRETATION：这是一种「证人」语域。它把候选人的权威建立在临床而非政治之上，适合一位没有立法记录的首次参选者——同时也构成约束：证人必须不断指向后果，而记录显示她在秋季正是以生活成本的具体例子这样做。

3. The primary-night speech: coalition language / 3. 初选之夜讲话：联盟语言

The 2026-06-10 speech (245 s) is the first text in which she addresses a general electorate rather than a primary one. Its structure is acknowledgment-first: supporters, then the party's other primary candidates, then the argument that the Republican side's chaos is the opening.

2026-06-10 的讲话（245 秒）是她第一篇面向大选选民而非初选选民的文本。其结构以承认开场：先支持者，再党内其他初选对手，然后论证共和党一方的混乱正是机会。

INTERPRETATION: delivered eleven days before the Republican primary and thirty-two days before the incumbent's death, this speech argues against an opponent who would not be on the November ballot. The archive keeps it as evidence of what the campaign was saying when it still expected Lindsey Graham.

INTERPRETATION：这篇讲话发表于共和党初选前十一天、在任者去世前二十二天，其论辩对象是一位最终不会出现在 11 月 ballot 上的对手。档案保留它，作为「竞选方仍预期对手是林赛·格雷厄姆时说了什么」的证据。

4. The fall: from witness to cost / 4. 秋季：从证人到成本

By September the message vocabulary shifts to household economics. The 2026-09-04 SCETV coverage of the "Gas, Grits and …" tour kickoff (719 s) is the clearest instance: the tour is named for consumption, not for rights or institutions.

到九月，讯息词汇转向家庭经济。2026-09-04 SCETV 对「Gas, Grits and …」巡回启动的报道（719 秒）是最清晰的例子：巡回以消费命名，而不是以权利或制度命名。

INTERPRETATION: the shift is consistent with the exit poll's coalition — she won 18–44 year-olds 66–29 and lost 65+ 45–54, and cost-of-living framing is aimed at the former. It is also the message an opponent with a 12.6:1 money disadvantage cannot outspend, which is why the tour mattered more than its coverage volume suggests.

INTERPRETATION：这一转向与出口民调的联盟结构一致——她以 66–29 赢下 18–44 岁、以 45–54 输掉 65 岁以上，而生活成本框架正指向前者。这也是一个资金处于 12.6:1 劣势的对手无法用投放压过的讯息，因此巡回的重要性高于其报道量所显示的程度。

5. The victory speech: history-claim and enemy-list / 5. 胜选讲话：历史宣称与敌人清单

The 2026-11-04 victory speech (two network uploads, 743 s and 735 s) opens with a history claim [caption]: "I think we just made some history… You have shown the nation that in this era of partisan divides, of vitriol and name-calling, in a politics dominated by billionaires and special interest groups and insiders in Washington DC, that we will not bow down."

2026-11-04 的胜选讲话（两个电视网上传，743 秒与 735 秒）以历史宣称开场 [caption]：「我想我们刚刚创造了一些历史……你们向全国证明，在这个党派对立、恶语与辱骂的时代，在这个由亿万富翁、特殊利益集团与华盛顿圈内人主导的政治中，我们不会低头。」

The sentence does three things at once: it nationalises a state result, it names three enemies (billionaires, interest groups, insiders), and it avoids naming the opponent. The concession speech by Graham the same day is the mirror image — family, gratitude, no enemy list.

这句话同时做三件事：把一个州的结果全国化；点名三类敌人（亿万富翁、利益集团、圈内人）；避免点名对手。同日格雷厄姆的承认败选讲话则是镜像——家庭、感谢，没有敌人清单。

6. Debate register: one encounter, three candidates / 6. 辩论语域：一场交锋、三位候选人

The only general-election debate (2026-10-11, SCETV/SCPR, moderator Gavin Jackson) put three candidates on stage, which forced a format of short answers rather than exchange. Her sharpest documented line is aimed at the appointment rather than at policy [caption]:

唯一一场大选辩论（2026-10-11，SCETV/SCPR，主持人 Gavin Jackson）让三位候选人同台，形式上被迫以短答为主而非交锋。她有记录的最锋利一句指向任命本身而非政策 [caption]：

"I thought it was a nice gesture when the governor and the president appointed Senator Graham to fulfill her brother's term. But now we're talking about a full term in the US Senate, and it is nothing personal, but she has shown time and time again that she is not up for the job."

「当初州长与总统任命格雷厄姆参议员完成她兄长的任期，我认为这是个善意的举动。但现在我们谈的是联邦参议院的完整任期，这不是针对个人，但她一次又一次显示出她不胜任这份工作。」

INTERPRETATION: the move is precise — concede the appointment's legitimacy, then separate it from the full term. It also creates a trap the opponent walked into: Graham's reply reused her own runoff-debate phrase ("not my thing") as a counter-attack, which kept the competence frame alive rather than closing it.

INTERPRETATION：这一手法精确——先承认任命的正当性，再把它与完整任期切割。它也设下对手踏入的陷阱：格雷厄姆的回应把自己在决选辩论中的说法（「不是我的专长」）改作反击，结果让「胜任与否」的框架继续存活而非终结。

7. What the record shows about delivery, not content / 7. 记录显示的呈现特征（非内容）

audio_metrics.csv records for the launch video a mean level of −20.1 dBFS and a silence ratio of 0.005 — a tightly cut produced video with almost no dead air, as expected for a launch film. The victory-speech uploads are podium recordings and read differently in loudness and pause structure. These are measurements of the file, not judgements about the speaker.

audio_metrics.csv 记录启动影片的平均电平为 −20.1 dBFS、静音比 0.005——这是一支剪辑紧密、几乎没有空白的成品视频，符合启动影片的预期。胜选讲话的上传是讲台录音，在响度与停顿结构上读来不同。这些是对文件的测量，不是对讲话者的评价。

No claim is made here about appearance, age, health, accent or likability: the archive does not record such judgements, and the frame data exists only to describe staging (see ../04_visual_archive/visual_analysis_andrews.md).

本文件不对相貌、年龄、健康、口音或亲和力作任何断言：档案不记录此类判断，帧数据的存在只为描述舞台（见 visual_analysis_andrews.md）。

8. Where the analysis stops / 8. 分析止于何处

The archive cannot say whether the "witness" register persuaded anyone, whether the September pivot to cost of living was a response to polling, or whether the debate line moved votes. There is no ad script, no internal polling and no panel data in the corpus. Those questions are open, and are listed in ../research_gaps.md.

档案无法判定「证人」语域是否说服了任何人、九月的转向是否回应了民调、辩论那句话是否改变了选票。语料中没有广告脚本、没有内部民调、没有面板数据。这些问题开放，列于 research_gaps.md。
