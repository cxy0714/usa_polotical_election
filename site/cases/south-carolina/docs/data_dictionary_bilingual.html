Data dictionary / 数据字典

1. How to read any row / 1. 如何读任何一行

Three rules govern every table in this archive.

三条规则支配本档案中的每一张表。

Rule 1 — unknown is a value, not a gap. A cell reading unknown means the retrieved sources do not state it. It never means "not applicable" and it never means zero. Filtering unknown out silently changes the denominator of any count you compute.

规则一——unknown 是取值，不是缺失。 单元格为 unknown 表示已检索来源未作说明；它从不表示「不适用」，也从不表示零。把 unknown 静默过滤掉，会悄悄改变任何计数的分母。

Rule 2 — every row carries SRC-####|https://…. The token identifies the source, the URL after the pipe is the retrievable address. Multi-source cells separate entries with ;. The id resolves in sources.csv; the token's numeric block tells you which collection stream produced it (§5).

规则二——每一行都带 SRC-####|https://…。 token 标识来源，竖线后的 URL 是可检索地址；多来源单元格用 ; 分隔。id 在 sources.csv 中解析；token 的号段说明它由哪个采集流产生（见 §5）。

Rule 3 — inference is labelled. Anything the analyst concluded rather than read begins with INTERPRETATION:. A sentence without that prefix is a statement about a source.

规则三——推断有标记。 分析师得出的、而非读到的内容，都以 INTERPRETATION: 开头。没有该前缀的句子是对来源的陈述。

3. Column reference / 3. 列说明

3.1 campaign_events.csv

event_id (GRA-/AND-/JNT-EVT-####) · candidate_id · event_date (ISO or unknown) · event_time · event_title · event_category · city · venue · state_region · event_status · campaign_phase · stated_purpose (what the campaign said it was for) · observable_function (what the record shows happened) · analyst_interpretation · target_audience · main_messages · policy_issues · speech_summary · transcript_url · video_url · photo_urls · media_coverage · opponent_reference · visual_notes · source_urls · verification_status · actor_note · vacancy_context · analyst_notes

stated_purpose 与 observable_function 必须分开：前者是竞选方的说法，后者是记录显示的实际功能。两者相同的情况本身就是一个发现。

3.2 policy_positions.csv

pos_id · candidate_id · issue · position_summary · verbatim_quote · statement_date · context · source_urls · verification_status · legislative_record · position_changed · change_notes · policy_claims · source · notes

legislative_record is unknown for all 34 Andrews rows because she has held no office; it is a real record for Graham's Senate votes and bill sponsorships. position_changed = yes requires two dated statements that conflict — not a change of emphasis inferred by the analyst.

legislative_record 在安德鲁斯全部 34 行为 unknown，因为她未担任过公职；对格雷厄姆的参议院表决与法案联署则是真实记录。position_changed = yes 要求两条有日期的陈述相互冲突，而非分析师推断的语气变化。

3.3 county_results.csv

One row per county: county, andrews_votes, andrews_pct, graham_votes, graham_pct, whitener_votes, whitener_pct, hackett_votes, hackett_pct, writein_votes, total_votes, winner, region, source_urls, verification_status, notes.

The 46 county totals sum to 2,041,487, which equals the statewide total — the reconciliation is documented in ../07_electoral_context/results_notes.md. One source cell for Edgefield literally reads 10.821 (a full stop where a comma belongs); the component sum is 10,821 and that is what the table records.

46 个县的总和等于全州总数 2,041,487，对账过程见 results_notes.md。Edgefield 的来源单元格字面写作 10.821（应为逗号处写成句点）；各分项之和为 10,821，表中即记录该值。

3.4 sources.csv

source_id · url · title · publisher · publication_date · event_date · access_date · source_tier · source_type · relevant_quote_or_info · reliability_notes · used_by

used_by is computed, not asserted: the merge scans every CSV and Markdown file in the archive for each SRC-#### and lists the files that cite it. unknown there means the source is registered background that no delivered row cites.

used_by 是计算出来的，不是断言的：合并脚本扫描档案内每个 CSV 与 Markdown 文件中的 SRC-####，列出引用它的文件。该列为 unknown 表示该来源是已登记的背景资料，没有交付行引用它。

3.5 keyframes_identity.csv

frame_id · identity · identity_sim · second_identity · second_sim · faces · candidate_faces · identity_note

identity is one of ANDREWS, GRAHAM, unknown, other, noface, nofile. It comes from an SFace cosine comparison against 23 photographs already in 04_visual_archive/ (12 Andrews, 11 Graham), filtered so that only photographs where the candidate's face fills at least 3% of the frame are used as references. A face is labelled with a candidate only if its best cosine reaches 0.363 (OpenCV's published SFace threshold) and beats the other candidate by 0.05. Everything else is unknown, which is why 156 of 300 frames carry no candidate label: a debate frame contains a moderator, an audience and two candidates, and only the faces that clear both tests are attributed.

Sanity figures from the run: same-class reference pairs average 0.740 cosine, cross-class 0.104. As an independent check, 136 frames come from videos in which only one candidate appears; 3 of them (2.2%) carry the other candidate's label, which is the archive's working estimate of the false-positive rate.

identity 取值 ANDREWS、GRAHAM、unknown、other、noface、nofile。来源是对 04_visual_archive/ 中 23 张照片（安德鲁斯 12、格雷厄姆 11）做 SFace 余弦比对，且只采用候选人脸部占画幅 至少 3% 的照片作为参照。只有最佳余弦达到 0.363（OpenCV 公布的 SFace 阈值）且领先另一候选人 0.05 时，才把该脸标注为某位候选人；其余一律 unknown——所以 300 帧里有 156 帧没有候选人标签：一场辩论帧里同时有 主持人、观众与两位候选人，只有通过双重检验的脸才被归属。

本次运行的合理性数据：同类参照对平均余弦 0.740，跨类 0.104。独立检验：来自「只出现一位候选人」视频的 136 帧中， 有 3 帧（2.2%）带上了另一候选人的标签，这就是本档案对误报率的经验估计。

6. Tier rules / 6. 等级规则

T1 — official records: .gov domains (FEC, South Carolina Election Commission, Congress, Senate), and the state's public broadcaster (SCETV) for debate video. · T2 — established news organisations with editorial standards, including the state's major papers and the networks. · T3 — everything else: party and advocacy sites, aggregators, polls published by the sponsoring firm, social posts, Wikipedia, and unclassified domains.

T1——官方记录：.gov 域（FEC、南卡选举委员会、国会、参议院），以及辩论视频的州公共广播机构 SCETV。T2——有编辑标准的既有新闻机构，包括州内主要报纸与全国电视网。T3——其余：政党与倡议组织网站、聚合站、由委托机构自行发布的民调、社交帖子、维基百科，以及未分类域名。

Tier is assigned from the domain, mechanically, in the merge script. It describes the venue, not the accuracy of any particular claim. Wikipedia is T3 by rule even where it is the most complete account of the race, and every dataset built from it says so.

等级在合并脚本中由域名机械判定。它描述的是发表场所，而非任何具体主张的准确性。按规则维基百科为 T3，即使它是这场选举最完整的记述；所有据其构建的数据集都注明了这一点。

7. 口径 rules that prevent wrong arithmetic / 7. 防止算错的口径规则

  1. Candidate-committee totals and outside independent expenditures are never summed. They live in campaign_finance.csv and advertisements.csv respectively.
  2. Primary-round vote totals are per-round and cannot be added across rounds: the same voters appear in the 08-11 special primary and the 08-25 runoff.
  3. County rows sum to the statewide total exactly; any future edit must preserve that identity.

  4. Percentages are as published. Where a source publishes only votes, the percentage is derived and flagged in notes.

  5. 候选人委员会总额与外部独立支出绝不相加，分别存放于 campaign_finance.csv 与 advertisements.csv。

  6. 各轮初选票数是分轮的，不能跨轮相加：同一批选民同时出现在 08-11 特别初选与 08-25 决选中。
  7. 县级行精确加总为全州总数；今后任何修改都必须保持该恒等式。
  8. 百分比按来源发表值记录。来源只给票数时，百分比为推算值并在 notes 中标注。

8. Known deviations from the conventions / 8. 与研究规范的已知偏离

Deviation / 偏离 Why / 原因
campaign_events.csv has 28 rows against a 70-row target The record contains almost no dated, located appearances; padding would have broken traceability. Documented in event_coverage_gaps.md.
campaign_finance.csv has 5 rows against a 30-row target Only four FEC snapshots are quoted in the retrievable sources; quarterly line items are not. Non-principal candidates live in other_candidates_finance.csv.
source_type vocabulary extended See §4.
Video corpus registered at SRC-2200+, outside the allocated 1700 block The video stream delivered no source table; the Lead registered the corpus instead of leaving 27 videos uncitable.
No frame captions No vision-language model was available in this environment; frames are described by measurable properties only.
media_coverage.csv titles and framings come from the media stream The Lead's parallel build produced a thinner index and was discarded in favour of the fuller file.
偏离 原因
campaign_events.csv 为 28 行，目标 70 行 记录中几乎没有带日期、带地点的露面；填充会破坏可追溯性。详见 event_coverage_gaps.md。
campaign_finance.csv 为 5 行，目标 30 行 可检索来源只引用了四个 FEC 时点；季度明细未获取。非主要候选人存放于 other_candidates_finance.csv。
source_type 词表扩展 见 §4。
视频语料登记在 SRC-2200+，超出分配的 1700 号段 视频流未交付来源表；主研究选择登记语料，而不是让 27 个视频无法被引用。
无帧解说词 本环境无视觉语言模型；帧只以可测量属性描述。
media_coverage.csv 的标题与框架来自媒体流 主研究并行构建的索引更单薄，已弃用，改用更完整的文件。
