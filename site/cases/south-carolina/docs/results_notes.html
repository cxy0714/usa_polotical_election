Results notes — provenance, integrity checks and reading rules / 结果说明——来源、完整性校验与阅读规则

1. Provenance: projected, not certified / 1. 来源：预测值，非认证值

Every figure in election_results.csv and county_results.csv is transcribed from the Wikipedia results tables, which in turn cite the New York Times results page and the South Carolina Election Commission. CBS News "projects" Andrews the winner. No state certification (scvotes.gov canvass) was retrieved in this pass, so the whole file is partially verified — including the general-election totals.

election_results.csv 与 county_results.csv 中的每个数字都转录自维基百科结果表，而这些表又引用《纽约时报》结果页与南卡罗来纳州选举委员会。CBS 新闻「预测」安德鲁斯胜选。本次未取得州级认证（scvotes.gov 的官方计票），因此整份文件——包括大选总票数——均标记为 partially verified。

The article's own last_update field reads November 11, 2026, 12:27 AM, which is the moment the transcribed tables were last consistent with each other.

条目自身的 last_update 字段为 2026 年 11 月 11 日 12:27（美东），即所转录表格彼此一致的最后时点。

2. Integrity checks actually run / 2. 实际执行的完整性校验

Rather than trusting the county table, four checks were run and their results are recorded here:

不是直接信任县表，而是执行了四项校验，结果记录如下：

Check / 校验 Result / 结果
Sum of 46 county Andrews votes vs statewide 1,044,001 = 1,044,001 ✅
Sum of 46 county Graham votes vs statewide 952,191 = 952,191 ✅
Sum of 46 county total_votes vs statewide total 2,041,487 = 2,041,487 ✅ (after the fix in §3)
Sum of other_votes (Whitener + Hackett + write-in) 45,295 = 23,585 + 16,811 + 4,899 ✅

The county table is therefore internally consistent with the statewide result, and the two candidate columns are the strongest part of the dataset: they reconcile to the vote, not to a percentage.

因此县表与全州结果内部自洽，两位候选人的票数列是整份数据中最可靠的部分：它们与总票数对得上，而非只与百分比对得上。

3. One source typo, corrected by arithmetic / 3. 一处源数据笔误，以算术修正

The row for Edgefield writes its total as 10.821 — a decimal point where the table's own convention uses a thousands separator. Its five component cells are 4,593 + 5,972 + 127 + 101 + 28 = 10,821, which is the value carried in county_results.csv. The correction is this one cell; it was not applied silently, and it is the reason the third check above passes.

Edgefield 一行的总数写作 10.821——该表的惯例是用千位分隔符，此处却写成了小数点。其五个分项单元格为 4,593 + 5,972 + 127 + 101 + 28 = 10,821，这正是 county_results.csv 中所载的值。修正仅涉及这一个单元格；它并非静默套用，也正是上表第三项校验得以通过的原因。

4. One discrepancy left unresolved / 4. 一处未解决的差异

The article's analysis paragraph states that Andrews "won a total of 25 counties". The county table yields 24: Allendale, Bamberg, Barnwell, Berkeley, Calhoun, Charleston, Clarendon, Colleton, Darlington, Dillon, Dorchester, Fairfield, Florence, Greenville, Hampton, Jasper, Lee, Marion, Marlboro, Orangeburg, Richland, Sumter, Williamsburg and York. Graham carried the other 22.

条目的分析段落称安德鲁斯「共赢下 25 个县」。县表给出 24 个：Allendale、Bamberg、Barnwell、Berkeley、Calhoun、Charleston、Clarendon、Colleton、Darlington、Dillon、Dorchester、Fairfield、Florence、Greenville、Hampton、Jasper、Lee、Marion、Marlboro、Orangeburg、Richland、Sumter、Williamsburg、York。格雷厄姆赢下其余 22 个。

The archive does not pick a winner between the two statements. The likely explanations — a county counted at a rounding boundary, an error in the prose, or a county the table folds in elsewhere — cannot be distinguished without the precinct or county canvass. ⚠ unknown

档案不在两种说法之间择一。可能的解释——某个县处在四舍五入边界、段落有误、或某个县被表格并入他处——在没有选区级或县级官方计票的情况下无法区分。⚠ unknown

5. How to read the numbers / 5. 如何阅读这些数字

  • andrews_pct / graham_pct are each candidate's share of that county's total votes, as printed. They do not sum to 100 with other_votes, because the write-in share is small and rounding is per cell.
  • other_votes is a derived sum of three documented columns (Whitener, Hackett, write-in); the county schema has no separate column for each minor candidate, and the parsed source table that carries them is kept at _staging/mirrors/county_results_parsed.csv.
  • winner in election_results.csv is a per-row flag, not a certification.

  • Party labels are as printed by the source: Democratic, Republican, Libertarian, Constitution, Write-in.

  • andrews_pct／graham_pct 是各候选人在该县总票数中的占比，按原文照录。它们与 other_votes 不会恰好合计 100，因为写名票占比很小且逐格四舍五入。

  • other_votes 是三个有据可查的列（惠特纳、哈克特、写名）的求和派生值；县表模式中没有为每位小党候选人单列一列，承载这些原始数据的解析表保存在 _staging/mirrors/county_results_parsed.csv。
  • election_results.csv 中的 winner 是逐行标记，不是认证结果。
  • 党籍标签按来源原文：Democratic、Republican、Libertarian、Constitution、Write-in。

6. What the exit poll can and cannot support / 6. 出口民调能支撑什么、不能支撑什么

../09_sources/exit_poll_2026.csv holds 45 subgroups in 15 groups, transcribed from the CNN table. It supports statements of the form "among self-identified independents, Andrews led 63–32". It does not support causal statements, subgroup turnout estimates, or claims about individual voters, and its subgroup sizes are not published in the source, so no subgroup margin of error is computable here. ⚠ unknown

../09_sources/exit_poll_2026.csv 收录 15 组共 45 个子群，转录自 CNN 表格。它可以支撑「在自认独立人士中，安德鲁斯以 63–32 领先」这类陈述。它不能支撑因果陈述、子群投票率估计或关于个体选民的断言；来源未公布各子群样本量，因此此处无法计算任何子群的误差范围。⚠ unknown
