这套档案是怎么做的How this archive was built

采集 / Collection

The Wikipedia race article supplied the skeleton: 63 sections and 534 cited URLs were mirrored to disk, parsed into county, polling and finance tables, then cross-checked against CBS's results reporting and the FEC tables quoted in the sources. Video came from yt-dlp searches plus a curated list of 27 uploads, downloaded at 720p (480p for the two hour-long debates).

维基百科的选举条目提供了骨架：63 个章节与 534 条引用 URL 被镜像到本地，解析出县级、民调与财务表，再与 CBS 的 结果报道以及来源中引用的 FEC 表格交叉核对。视频来自 yt-dlp 搜索与手工筛选的 27 条上传，720p 下载（两场一小时的辩论为 480p）。

校验 / Verification

_staging/_lead_validate.py enforces header conformance, field counts, no blank cells, controlled vocabularies, ISO dates, and an SRC-#### token plus URL in every row. The archive passes with zero hard failures.

_staging/_lead_validate.py 强制表头一致、字段数、无空单元格、受控词表、ISO 日期，以及每行都带 SRC-#### 与 URL。档案目前以零硬失败通过。

等级规则 / Tier rules

T1 official records (.gov domains, SCETV debate video); T2 established news organisations; T3 everything else, including Wikipedia, advocacy sites and social posts. Tier is assigned from the domain mechanically: it describes the venue, not the accuracy of a claim.

T1 官方记录（.gov 域、SCETV 辩论视频）；T2 既有新闻机构；T3 其余，包括 维基百科、倡议组织网站与社交帖子。等级按域名机械判定：它描述发表场所，而非某条主张的准确性。

与德州案例的关系 / Relation to the Texas case

Both cases use the same components and the same stylesheet, so a reader moving between them does not have to learn a second interface. The data models differ where the races differ: South Carolina adds actor_note and vacancy_context because a death and an appointment sit inside its event sequence.

两个案例使用同一套组件与同一份样式表，读者在两个案例之间切换时不必重新适应界面。数据模型随选举不同而不同：南卡增加了 actor_note 与 vacancy_context，因为其事件序列中包含一次死亡与一次任命。

Compiled 2026-11-12. Every CSV row carries SRC-####|https://...; unknown is a value, not a gap.

2026-11-12 编制。每个 CSV 行均带 SRC-####|https://...；unknown 是取值，不是缺失。
