Campaign media notes

Advertising

Eight rows in advertisements.csv. The best-documented paid messages are outside-group ads: the NRSC's 30-second spot using footage of the April 2026 Interstate 71 crash that killed three members of the Soposki family, and the Senate Leadership Fund's testimonial ads in which a woman says "Sherrod Brown supports sex changes for minors, irreversible surgery on kids like me" — an ad the Ohio Capital Journal fact-checked, finding the speaker was not an Ohioan, was 19 at the time of surgery, and was treated in Texas.

Brown's side is documented through a single quoted spot and two companions: the Ohio Capital Journal reports that three Brown ads dealt with data centers and quotes one — "(Jon) Husted is a chief reason Ohio has so many of those centers. He force-fed the massive energy-sucking facilities." The other two exist as rows with unknown creative, not as reconstructed scripts.

Two AFP Action spots, "Common Sense" and "Taking Root", are documented from the sponsor's own release, inside a $1.4 million digital and streaming buy in a $9 million Ohio programme.

The money behind the airwaves

AdImpact, as reported by the Ohio Capital Journal, placed Ohio as the most expensive Senate race in the country with outside groups spending more than $325 million. That figure is an ad-tracking estimate, not an FEC total, and is labelled as such.

Social media

Six rows, and this is the thinnest media file. No campaign social post was retrievable through a blocked platform, so four of the six rows are documented indirectly — quoted in a Commons file description, quoted in reporting, or (in one case) a post that was deleted. Each row says how it was documented. Only the 2026-10-29 vertical video has a stable public URL.

Coverage

Twenty-six items, weighted toward the Ohio Capital Journal (nine), Cleveland.com (three) and wire copy carried by the Boston Herald and Yahoo. Two items are syndicated copies because the original returned 403 (AP) or 402 (the Columbus Dispatch via Yahoo); both are labelled in reliability_notes. Paywalled and blocked outlets — Politico, Axios, the Cincinnati Enquirer, Reuters — appear only as the subjects of other outlets' reporting, never as cited content.
