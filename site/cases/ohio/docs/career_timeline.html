2. The appointment sequence / 任命序列

Four dates matter and they are frequently confused:

  • 2025-01-10 — Vance's resignation takes effect (SRC-1096).
  • 2025-01-17 — DeWine announces Husted at the Statehouse

    • (SRC-1016).

  • 2025-01-21 — Husted takes the oath, administered by Vance; the Commons photograph is dated

    • the same day (SRC-1178, SRC-1014).

  • 2026-11-03 — the special election decides who serves the remainder.

The brief's "appointed 2025-01-10 / sworn in 2025-01-14" conflates the resignation date with the appointment and uses the 2019 inauguration date for the oath. The archive corrects both and says so in results_notes.md.

四个日期常被混淆：1月10日范斯辞职生效、1月17日德万在州议会大厦宣布任命、1月21日宣誓（范斯主持，Commons 照片同日）、 2026年11月3日特别选举决定剩余任期归属。简报的“1月10日任命、1月14日宣誓”把辞职日当任命日、把2019年就职日当宣誓日， 本档案两处均已更正并在 results_notes.md 说明。

3. Continuous officeholding / 连续任职

From 2001-01-08 to 2026-11-03 Husted held elected or appointed office without interruption — 25 years and 10 months. Brown's "50 years" attack runs the other way, and Husted's counter is generational rather than numerical: "we took a state that was dying and made it a state that's now growing again" (SRC-1080).

自2001年1月8日至2026年11月3日连续任职25年10个月。布朗以“50年”攻击，赫斯特德的反驳是代际而非数字的： “我们把一个垂死的州变成了如今在增长的州”。
