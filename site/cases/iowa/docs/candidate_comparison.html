7. What the comparison does not settle / 比较未能解决的部分

English: The archive compares records, not virtues. It does not rank the candidates, score their performances, or decide whether biography outperformed incumbency. The one asymmetry it establishes firmly is documentary: Hinson has a public-domain visual record and Turek, in this archive, does not.

中文：本档案比较的是记录，而非优劣。它不为候选人排序、不评分，也不判定"传记"是否胜过"现任资历"。它确凿证明的唯一不对称是文献层面的：Hinson 拥有公有领域的影像记录，而 Turek 在本档案中没有。
