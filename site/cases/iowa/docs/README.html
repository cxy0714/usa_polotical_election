2026 U.S. Senate election in Iowa — case archive / 2026 年艾奥瓦州联邦参议员选举案例档案

1. What this archive covers / 本档案覆盖什么

English: The open-seat 2026 U.S. Senate race in Iowa between Josh Turek (D), a state representative and four-time Paralympian, and Ashley Hinson (R), a three-term U.S. representative. It covers both primaries, the general election, 34 campaign events, 45 documented policy positions, 16 speech units, 12 images, 5 advertisements, 7 social posts, 28 coverage items, 11 finance rows, 7 result rows, all 99 counties and 11 general-election polls. Nothing outside this race is described.

中文：本档案覆盖 2026 年艾奥瓦州联邦参议员开放席位选举：民主党人 Josh Turek（州众议员、四届残奥选手）对阵共和党人 Ashley Hinson（三届联邦众议员）。涵盖两场初选、大选、34 场竞选活动、45 条政策立场、16 条演说记录、12 张图片、5 条广告、7 条社媒记录、28 条媒体报道、11 条资金记录、7 条结果记录、全部 99 个县以及 11 份大选民调。不描述本场选举之外的任何内容。

3. Five structural facts this archive establishes / 本档案确立的五项结构性事实

a. The county table reconciles exactly — and it shows a 15-of-99 win. All 99 counties sum to 688,746 / 631,482 / 42,109 and to 1,362,337, with no residual and no rounding slack. The six-vote gap against the article's election box is a snapshot difference between two published versions, documented rather than smoothed. Turek carried only 15 counties — Polk, Linn, Johnson, Scott, Dallas, Black Hawk, Story, Dubuque, Pottawattamie, Cerro Gordo, Marshall, Muscatine, Des Moines, Jefferson and Winneshiek — and those 15 gave him 466,998 votes to Hinson's 299,323, while the other 84 counties gave Hinson 332,159 to his 221,748. Those six suburban and university counties alone netted him 151,915 votes — nearly three times his statewide margin of 57,264 — while the other 93 counties netted him -94,651.

b. The documentary record is asymmetric, not the campaign record. Turek has more dated events (18 to 14) but no downloadable official portrait: his four Legislature portraits carry the licence "Copyrighted free use", which this project does not permit, so they are link-only. Hinson's congressional portraits are public domain and three were downloaded. The archive can therefore show one candidate's face and not the other's.

c. Hinson's spoken record is thinner than her schedule. Five of seven Hinson speech rows are paraphrase-only, and four of her largest events were carried by surrogates (Cruz twice, Barrasso, Kennedy). Turek's nine speech rows carry verbatim quotations in eight cases.

d. Money ran in opposite directions at two 口径. By 14 October 2026 Turek had out-raised Hinson $24,290,521 to $14,147,973, while outside spending in the race was reported at $137M, including a $20M Senate Leadership Fund buy for Hinson. The archive keeps campaign receipts and outside spending as separate rows and never sums them.

e. The ad record is one-sided. Nine advertisement rows exist: seven Turek items (six spots plus a July endorsement video) and two headline-level outside buys — and no Hinson campaign ad creative at all. The substantive exchange — Turek's "I-35" immigration ad and Hinson's attack on it — is visible only because the attack was delivered at a fundraiser that a reporter covered. No Hinson campaign ad creative was retrievable at all.

中文摘要：(a) 99 县合计与全州总数完全吻合，且 Turek 只赢 15 个县却拿下过半选票；(b) 文献记录不对称：Turek 活动更多但无可下载官方肖像，Hinson 有公有领域国会肖像；(c) Hinson 的言语记录比日程更薄，七条演说中五条为转述，四场大型活动由代理人主导；(d) 资金在两个口径上方向相反，本档案从不将竞选收支与外部支出相加；(e) 只有一组广告攻防有详细记录。

    3. headline-level sources and marked unverified or partially verified.
