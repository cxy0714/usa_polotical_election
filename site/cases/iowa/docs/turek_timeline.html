Structural reading / 结构性解读

English: The schedule is built around places where a wheelchair is a normal presence — a senior-living facility, a university hall, a county party fundraiser, a state-fair-style steak fry — rather than around workplace tours or factory gates, which is the opposite of the classic Iowa Democratic circuit. Two of the seventeen events are explicitly framed by reporters as disability-access stories (a supporter describing him getting out of his chair to knock doors; coverage of his first legislative session), and neither is a campaign event in the strict sense.

中文：日程围绕"轮椅本就常见"的场所构建——养老社区、大学礼堂、县党部筹款会、州集市式牛排煎饼会——而不是工厂车间与工地门口，这与艾奥瓦民主党的传统路线相反。17 场中有两场被记者明确写成无障碍议题的故事（支持者描述他下轮椅敲门、首個会期的报道），但两者严格来说都不是竞选活动。
