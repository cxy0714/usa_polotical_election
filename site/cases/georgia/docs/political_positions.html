How the positions were compiled / 立场整理方法

Rows in 01_candidates/policy_positions.csv (17 for Ossoff) record a stated position only when a retrieved source states it; where no statement exists the row says unknown and the verification status is unverified. Nothing here is inferred from party platform. SRC-0096

01_candidates/policy_positions.csv 中奥索夫有 17 行；仅在已获取来源明确表述时才记录立场，无表述则填 unknown 并标注 unverified，不从党纲推断。SRC-0096

Abortion and health care / 堕胎与医疗

Ossoff supports abortion rights and said the Supreme Court "stripped American women of autonomy over their most personal health care decisions" after Dobbs; Reproductive Freedom for All endorsed him. On health care he supports the ACA, opposes single-payer, backs a public option, and states three principles: no preventable suffering, no medical bankruptcy, no employer premium shock. SRC-0096 SRC-0073

奥索夫支持堕胎权，在 Dobbs 判决后称最高法院"剥夺了美国女性对最私密医疗决策的自主权"；Reproductive Freedom for All 为其背书。医疗方面支持《平价医疗法案》、反对单一支付者、支持公共选项，并提出三项原则：不得因可预防疾病受苦、不得因病破产、不得让雇主保费剧震。SRC-0096 SRC-0073

No direct 2026 statement on Georgia Medicaid expansion was found; the campaign instead attacked Republican "healthcare cuts". SRC-0016

未发现其就佐治亚州扩大医疗补助（Medicaid）的直接表态；竞选转而攻击共和党的"医疗削减"。SRC-0016

Democracy and voting rights / 民主与投票权

He supports the John Lewis Voting Rights Act, criticised Georgia's 2021 Election Integrity Act while opposing corporate boycotts of the state, and voted to convict Trump in the second impeachment. In 2026 he framed the race as a defence of the republic. SRC-0096 SRC-0019

支持《约翰·刘易斯投票权法案》；批评佐治亚 2021 年《选举诚信法》但反对企业抵制该州；在第二次弹劾中投票认定特朗普有罪。2026 年将选战框定为捍卫共和国。SRC-0096 SRC-0019

Immigration / 移民

Supports comprehensive reform with border enforcement plus a path to citizenship; was one of 12 Democratic senators to vote for the Laken Riley Act in 2025 ("Mike, you know I voted for the bill"); said in January 2026 he would not support DHS funding after the Minneapolis shooting. SRC-0096 SRC-0017 SRC-0035

支持"边境执法＋入籍路径"的全面改革；2025 年是 12 名投票支持《拉肯·莱利法案》的民主党参议员之一（"迈克，你知道我投了赞成票"）；2026 年 1 月表示在明尼阿波利斯枪击案后不支持国土安全部拨款。SRC-0096 SRC-0017 SRC-0035

Foreign policy / 外交

Backs a two-state solution; voted for the 2024 Sanders resolutions on military aid and against the 2025 versions; calls the U.S.-Israel relationship "ironclad" but not unconditional; voted to block a 20,000-rifle sale; opposed the 2026 Iran war and voted for a War Powers Resolution. SRC-0096

支持两国方案；2024 年支持桑德斯军援决议、2025 年反对同类决议；称美以关系"坚如磐石"但非无条件；投票阻止 2 万支步枪军售；反对 2026 年伊朗战争并支持《战争权力决议》。SRC-0096

Economy, environment, guns, labour, agriculture / 经济、环境、枪支、劳工、农业

Calls for repealing Trump's tariffs ("These tariffs are crushing our state"); accepts climate science but not the Green New Deal and blocked the Okefenokee mine; is endorsed by GIFFORDS PAC; supports a $15 minimum wage; and is credited by Republican endorsers with advocacy for farmers after Hurricane Helene. SRC-0016 SRC-0096 SRC-0071 SRC-0004

主张废除特朗普关税（"这些关税正在压垮我们的州"）；承认气候科学但反对"绿色新政"，阻止奥克弗诺基采矿；获 GIFFORDS PAC 背书；支持 15 美元最低工资；共和党背书人称赞其在飓风海伦后为农民发声。SRC-0016 SRC-0096 SRC-0071 SRC-0004

Contradictions and attacks / 矛盾与攻防

Collins calls him a "trust fund kid" and says he voted to keep borders open; Republicans say he delayed Helene relief during a shutdown fight. The archive records these as opponent claims, not findings. SRC-0016 SRC-0035

柯林斯称其为"信托基金子弟"、指其投票让边境敞开；共和党人称其在政府停摆之争中拖延海伦救灾。档案将这些记为对手主张，而非事实认定。SRC-0016 SRC-0035
