The race / 选举概况

Class II Senate seat held by Democrat Jon Ossoff, first elected in the January 2021 runoff. Republican Mike Collins, the U.S. representative for GA-10, announced on 27 July 2025. Primaries were held on 19 May 2026; the Republican runoff on 16 June 2026; the general election on 3 November 2026. SRC-0097 SRC-0006 SRC-0007

该席位为民主党人乔恩·奥索夫持有的第二类参议院席位，他于 2021 年 1 月第二轮补选中首次当选。共和党人、第十选区联邦众议员迈克·柯林斯于 2025 年 7 月 27 日宣布参选。初选 2026 年 5 月 19 日，共和党第二轮 6 月 16 日，大选 11 月 3 日。SRC-0097 SRC-0006 SRC-0007

Primary and runoff / 初选与第二轮

Republican first round: Collins 369,642 (40.5%), Dooley 275,534 (30.2%), Carter 229,223 (25.1%), McColumn 28,447 (3.1%), Coyne 9,850 (1.1%); total 912,696. Runoff: Collins 390,174 (55.5%), Dooley 312,339 (44.5%); total 702,513. Ossoff was unopposed with 1,042,331 votes (100.0%). SRC-0006 SRC-0007 SRC-0008

共和党首轮：柯林斯 369,642（40.5%）、杜利 275,534（30.2%）、卡特 229,223（25.1%）、麦克科勒姆 28,447（3.1%）、科因 9,850（1.1%），合计 912,696。第二轮：柯林斯 390,174（55.5%）、杜利 312,339（44.5%），合计 702,513。奥索夫无人挑战，得 1,042,331 票（100.0%）。SRC-0006 SRC-0007 SRC-0008

Geography / 地理分布

The published district table shows Ossoff winning 7 of 14 congressional districts. Nine counties flipped from Republican in 2024 to Democratic in 2026: Burke, Chattahoochee, Dooly, Fayette, Houston, Lowndes, Peach, Spalding and Twiggs — a mix of metro-exurban (Fayette, Spalding, Houston), Black Belt (Burke, Dooly, Peach, Twiggs) and south Georgia (Lowndes). SRC-0001

公布的选区表显示奥索夫在 14 个国会选区中赢得 7 个。9 个县从 2024 年的共和党转向 2026 年的民主党：伯克、查塔胡奇、杜利、费耶特、休斯敦、朗兹、皮奇、斯帕尔丁、特威格斯——包含都市外围（费耶特、斯帕尔丁、休斯敦）、黑人带（伯克、杜利、皮奇、特威格斯）与南佐治亚（朗兹）。SRC-0001

Turnout / 投票率

Early voting opened on 13 October 2026 with roughly 189,000 ballots cast on the first day; Ossoff voted that morning at the Bessie Branham Recreation Center. NBC's live results page showed 4,182,642 votes counted at 99% of expected. SRC-0025 SRC-0033 SRC-0003

提前投票于 2026 年 10 月 13 日开始，首日约 189,000 张选票；奥索夫当日上午在贝西·布拉纳姆娱乐中心投票。NBC 实时结果页显示在预期 99% 时已计 4,182,642 票。SRC-0025 SRC-0033 SRC-0003

Polling environment / 民调环境

Final public polls clustered between Ossoff +7 and +15 (InsiderAdvantage 53-46, Research Co. 55-45, Echelon 55-40), and the aggregator average on the eve of the vote was Ossoff +9.4 (52.9 to 43.5). The result was 18.06 points. SRC-0058 SRC-0057 SRC-0059 SRC-0001

选前公开民调集中在奥索夫 +7 至 +15（InsiderAdvantage 53-46、Research Co. 55-45、Echelon 55-40），投票前夜的汇总平均为奥索夫 +9.4（52.9 对 43.5）。实际结果为 18.06 个百分点。SRC-0058 SRC-0057 SRC-0059 SRC-0001

Why the district and county tables matter / 选区与县表的意义

They are the only sub-state evidence available: the Secretary of State's results site is a client-rendered application with no retrievable county table, and NBC's county widget renders on the client. The district table and the county-flip list are therefore the archive's only geographic evidence. SRC-0001 SRC-0003

它们是唯一可得的州级以下证据：州务卿结果网站为客户端渲染应用，无法取得县级表格；NBC 的县级组件亦在客户端渲染。因此选区表与"翻转县"清单是本档案唯一的地理证据。SRC-0001 SRC-0003
