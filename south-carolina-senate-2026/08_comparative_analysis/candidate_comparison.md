# Candidate comparison / 候选人对比

## 1. The one asymmetry that shapes everything else / 1. 决定其余一切的不对称

Andrews spent 523 days as a candidate before election day. Graham spent 113. Andrews declared on
2025-05-29 against a sitting senator who was then expected to seek a fourth term [SRC-2108].
Graham became a candidate only because that senator — her brother — died on 2026-07-11, and she
was appointed to the seat on 2026-07-13 [SRC-2108][SRC-2111].

安德鲁斯在投票日前当了 523 天候选人；格雷厄姆只有 113 天。安德鲁斯于 2025-05-29 宣布挑战一位当时预计寻求第四个任期的在任参议员 [SRC-2108]。格雷厄姆之所以成为候选人，只是因为她兄长于 2026-07-11 去世，而她于 2026-07-13 被任命填补席位 [SRC-2108][SRC-2111]。

Every other difference in this file is downstream of that: the money curve, the debate count, the
incumbency advantage, the "appointed, not elected" line of attack, and the fact that one campaign
had a year to build a coalition while the other had sixteen weeks.

本文件中的其他差异都由这一点派生：资金曲线、辩论场次、在任优势、「被任命而非当选」的攻击线，以及一方有一年时间构建联盟、另一方只有十六周这一事实。

## 2. Path to candidacy / 2. 参选路径

| | Annie Andrews / 安妮·安德鲁斯 | Darline Graham / 达琳·格雷厄姆 |
|---|---|---|
| Declared / 宣布 | 2025-05-29 [SRC-2108] | full-term bid announced 2026-07-20 [SRC-2108] |
| Prior office / 曾任公职 | none documented; 2022 nominee for SC-01, lost 42.5–56.5 [SRC-2108] | SC Commission for the Blind commissioner [SRC-2108] |
| Route to the ballot / 上 ballot 路径 | won a contested Democratic primary 2026-06-09 with 61.5% [SRC-2108] | appointed 2026-07-13, then won a ten-candidate special primary and a runoff [SRC-2108][SRC-2111] |
| Incumbency at election / 选举时的在任状态 | challenger | appointed incumbent since 2026-07-14 |

安德鲁斯的从政经历在可检索来源中没有记录，她 2022 年作为 SC-01 提名人以 42.5–56.5 落败；格雷厄姆则是南卡盲人委员会委员。安德鲁斯通过有竞争的初选（61.5%）上 ballot；格雷厄姆先受任命，再赢下十人特别初选与决选。

INTERPRETATION: the two candidacies are not comparable in kind. One is a two-year build against a
named incumbent; the other is a succession that became an election. The archive therefore
compares *observable behaviour* — money, appearances, messages, coalition — not candidate quality.

INTERPRETATION：两者在类型上不可比。一个是针对具名在任者的两年期构建；另一个是由继承转化而来的选举。因此本档案比较的是**可观察行为**——资金、露面、讯息、联盟——而非候选人素质。

## 4. Coalition / 4. 联盟结构

The exit poll is the only comparable instrument, and it covers the general election only
[SRC-2108]. Among its documented findings: Andrews won independents 63–32, won 18–44 year-olds
66–29, lost voters 65 and older 45–54, lost rural voters 48–51, and won Black voters 85–12 in an
electorate that was 18% Black. The electorate was 27% Democratic, 41% Republican and 32%
independent, with Trump's approval at 44% approve / 55% disapprove.

出口民调是唯一可比的测量工具，且只覆盖大选 [SRC-2108]。其记录显示：安德鲁斯以 63–32 赢得独立选民，以 66–29 赢得 18–44 岁选民，以 45–54 输掉 65 岁以上选民，以 48–51 输掉乡村选民，并在占 electorate 18% 的黑人选民中以 85–12 获胜。选民构成为 27% 民主党、41% 共和党、32% 独立人士；特朗普支持率为 44% 支持／55% 不支持。

INTERPRETATION: a Democratic win in this state required winning independent voters by roughly
two to one while losing seniors and rural voters narrowly — which is what the numbers show. No
equivalent subgroup data exists for the primaries, so no claim is made about either candidate's
primary coalition beyond the vote totals.

INTERPRETATION：在该州赢得民主党胜利，需要以约二比一赢下独立选民，同时仅以微弱差距输掉老年与乡村选民——数据正是如此。初选没有对应的分组数据，因此除总票数外，本档案不对任何一方的初选联盟作出断言。

## 5. Message / 5. 讯息

Andrews's documented message vocabulary is healthcare, corruption in Washington, and later the
cost of living: the launch video is titled "Unafraid" and was framed around RFK Jr.'s HHS
confirmation; the September tour is titled "Gas, Grits and …" [SRC-2108]. Graham's documented
message vocabulary is continuity and endorsement: she was appointed to her late brother's seat,
endorsed by Trump the same day, and campaigned with Trump in Clemson on 2026-10-21 [SRC-2108].

安德鲁斯可考的讯息词汇是医疗、华盛顿的腐败，后期转向生活成本：启动影片名为《Unafraid》，围绕小罗伯特·肯尼迪的 HHS 任命展开；九月的巡回名为「Gas, Grits and …」[SRC-2108]。格雷厄姆可考的讯息词汇是延续与背书：她被任命到兄长的席位、同日获得特朗普背书，并于 2026-10-21 与特朗普在克莱姆森同台 [SRC-2108]。

Neither campaign's full message architecture is reconstructable from the retrieved sources: no ad
scripts, no mail, no stump transcripts were retrievable. That gap is recorded in
[`../research_gaps.md`](../research_gaps.md) rather than papered over with plausible slogans.

双方完整的讯息架构无法从已检索来源重建：没有可检索到的广告脚本、邮件或站台演讲逐字稿。该缺口记录在 `../research_gaps.md` 中，而不用看似合理的口号去糊上。

## 6. Debate exposure / 6. 辩论曝光

Graham appeared in four televised debates: 2026-08-03 (Gray Media), 2026-08-04 (Nexstar),
2026-08-18 (SCETV/Newsmax runoff) and 2026-10-11 (SCETV/SCPR general) [SRC-2108][SRC-2113].
Andrews appeared in one, the 2026-10-11 general debate [SRC-2108][SRC-2113]. The asymmetry is a
product of the calendar, not of choice: Andrews had no contested general-election primary
opponent after 06-09, while Graham had to survive two Republican rounds.

格雷厄姆参加了四场电视辩论：2026-08-03（Gray Media）、2026-08-04（Nexstar）、2026-08-18（SCETV/Newsmax 决选）和 2026-10-11（SCETV/SCPR 大选）[SRC-2108][SRC-2113]。安德鲁斯只参加了一场，即 2026-10-11 的大选辩论 [SRC-2108][SRC-2113]。这一不对称是日程的产物，而非选择：安德鲁斯在 06-09 之后没有有竞争的初选对手，而格雷厄姆必须挺过两轮共和党投票。

## 7. What the record cannot settle / 7. 记录无法判定的事项

- Which campaign was better organized: no staff lists, no field-programme data, no volunteer counts.
- Whether the "debate blunder" framing moved votes: coverage exists, vote-movement evidence does not.
- Whether Andrews's 12.6:1 money advantage was spent on persuasion or turnout: disbursement line items were not retrieved.
- How much of Graham's 46.64% was personal vote versus Trump endorsement: no ballot-level or panel data.

- 哪一方组织得更好：没有人员名单、没有地面项目数据、没有志愿者数量。
- 「辩论失误」的框架是否改变了选票：有报道，没有选票移动的证据。
- 安德鲁斯 12.6:1 的资金优势用于说服还是催票：支出明细未检索到。
- 格雷厄姆的 46.64% 中多少是个人票、多少是特朗普背书：没有选票级别或面板数据。
