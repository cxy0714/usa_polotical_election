# South Carolina Senate 2026 / 2026 南卡罗来纳州联邦参议员选举

## 1. What this archive covers / 1. 本档案覆盖什么

This is a documentary reconstruction of one race: the 2026 U.S. Senate election in South
Carolina, from Lindsey Graham's January 2025 announcement to Annie Andrews's 2026-11-04 victory
speech. It follows an unusual sequence — an incumbent's death on 2026-07-11, the appointment of
his sister Darline Graham on 2026-07-13, a statutory three-week special primary, a runoff, and a
general election in which the appointed incumbent lost by 4.5 points.

本档案是对单一选举的文献重建：2026 年南卡罗来纳州联邦参议员选举，从 2025 年 1 月林赛·格雷厄姆宣布连任，到 2026-11-04 安妮·安德鲁斯的胜选讲话。它记录了一条不寻常的序列——在任者于 2026-07-11 去世、其妹达琳·格雷厄姆于 2026-07-13 受任命、法定的三周特别初选、决选，以及被任命的在任者以 4.5 个百分点落败的大选。

The archive does not argue that either campaign was better run. It records what can be sourced
and marks the rest `unknown`.

本档案不主张任何一方的竞选运作更优。它记录有来源可依的内容，其余标记为 `unknown`。

**The Republican path to that ballot.** Lindsey Graham won the 2026-06-09 primary with 56.8%
(264,091 of 465,076). He died 32 days later. After the appointment, the 2026-08-11 special
primary produced Graham 32.8% (109,881) and Ralph Norman 24.6% (82,627) from a ten-candidate
field, and the 2026-08-25 runoff produced Graham 52.4% (193,486) to Norman 47.6% (175,412).

**共和党方面通往该 ballot 的路径。** 林赛·格雷厄姆以 56.8%（465,076 票中的 264,091）赢下 2026-06-09 初选，32 天后去世。任命之后，2026-08-11 的特别初选在十人竞争中产生格雷厄姆 32.8%（109,881）与拉尔夫·诺曼 24.6%（82,627），2026-08-25 决选为格雷厄姆 52.4%（193,486）对诺曼 47.6%（175,412）。

**The Democratic path.** Andrews won the 2026-06-09 primary with 61.5% (226,075) over Brandon
Brown (110,962) and Kyle Freeman (30,374), then had no opponent until November.

**民主党方面。** 安德鲁斯以 61.5%（226,075）赢下 2026-06-09 初选，击败布兰登·布朗（110,962）与凯尔·弗里曼（30,374），此后直到 11 月无对手。

## 3. Five structural facts the archive establishes / 3. 档案确立的五项结构性事实

1. **The race changed nominee, not party.** The Republican nominee on the November ballot had
   never won an election before; she had been a candidate for 113 days.
2. **Money was asymmetric by an order of magnitude.** As of 2026-10-14 Andrews had raised
   $24,216,841 to Graham's $1,917,646 — 12.6 : 1. Outside money ran the other way: Senate
   Leadership Fund reported $5,000,000 in October and MAGA Inc. $827,000 in August.
3. **Debate exposure was asymmetric in the opposite direction.** Graham stood in four televised
   debates (08-03, 08-04, 08-18, 10-11); Andrews in one.
4. **The coalition that won was built on independents.** Andrews won independents 63–32, 18–44
   year-olds 66–29, and Black voters 85–12 in an 18%-Black electorate, while losing 65+ voters
   45–54 and rural voters 48–51.
5. **The vacancy compressed the calendar by statute.** Filing opened 2026-07-21 and closed
   2026-07-28; the special primary followed fourteen days later.

1. **易位的是提名人，不是政党。** 11 月 ballot 上的共和党提名人此前从未赢过选举；她只当了 113 天候选人。
2. **资金量级不对称。** 截至 2026-10-14，安德鲁斯筹得 24,216,841 美元，格雷厄姆 1,917,646 美元——12.6 : 1。外部资金方向相反：参议院领导力基金 10 月报告 500 万美元，MAGA Inc. 8 月 827,000 美元。
3. **辩论曝光的非对称方向相反。** 格雷厄姆参加四场电视辩论（08-03、08-04、08-18、10-11）；安德鲁斯只参加一场。
4. **获胜联盟建立在独立选民之上。** 安德鲁斯以 63–32 赢得独立选民、以 66–29 赢得 18–44 岁选民、在占 18% 的黑人选民中以 85–12 获胜，同时以 45–54 输掉 65 岁以上选民、以 48–51 输掉乡村选民。
5. **空缺以法定方式压缩了日程。** 登记期 2026-07-21 开始、07-28 结束；特别初选在十四天后举行。

## 5. What is verifiably strong, and what is thin / 5. 哪些部分扎实，哪些单薄

**Strong.** Election arithmetic (46 counties reconcile to the statewide total), the primary and
runoff sequences, the formal appointment and swearing-in dates, the four debates with hosts and
moderators, the four FEC snapshots, and a 370-source table with a computed citation graph.

**扎实。** 选举算术（46 县与全州总数对账）、初选与决选序列、任命与宣誓的正式日期、四场辩论的主办方与主持人、四个 FEC 时点，以及 370 条来源表与计算得出的引用图。

**Thin, and labelled as such.** Campaign appearances with venues and crowds (28 events, many
venue `unknown`), advertising creative (3 rows, no scripts), social posts (3 rows, two
unretrievable), and any Graham position not documented in one bill, one confirmation vote, four
committee assignments and one debate remark.

**单薄，且已如此标注。** 带场地与人数的竞选露面（28 项活动，多数场地 `unknown`）、广告创意（3 行，无脚本）、社交帖子（3 行，其中两条无法检索），以及任何未见于一项法案、一次确认投票、四项委员会任命与一句辩论发言的格雷厄姆立场。

The reasons are environmental, not editorial: `drannieandrews.com` and `darlinegraham.com` both
return HTTP 403, several profiles are paywalled, and the shell's TLS stack could not reach most
publishers (only Python `urllib` with an explicit User-Agent worked). See
[`research_gaps.md`](research_gaps.md).

原因属于环境而非编辑取舍：`drannieandrews.com` 与 `darlinegraham.com` 均返回 HTTP 403，若干人物特写需付费，而 shell 的 TLS 栈无法访问多数媒体站点（只有带显式 User-Agent 的 Python `urllib` 可用）。详见 `research_gaps.md`。
