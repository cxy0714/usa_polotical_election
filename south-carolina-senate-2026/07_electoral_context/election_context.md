# Electoral context — 2026 South Carolina U.S. Senate election / 选举背景——2026 年南卡罗来纳州联邦参议员选举

## 1. The structural baseline / 1. 结构性基线

South Carolina entered 2026 as one of the most reliably Republican states in the country. No
Democrat had been elected to the U.S. Senate from the state since 1998, and the last Democratic
presidential nominee to carry it was Jimmy Carter in 1976. In 2024 Donald Trump carried South
Carolina by roughly 18 points. Republicans held both chambers of the state legislature with
supermajorities and six of the state's seven U.S. House seats; the seventh, the 6th District, was
held by Democrat James Clyburn. Every statewide executive office was held by a Republican.

南卡罗来纳在 2026 年伊始是全美最稳定的共和党州之一。自 1998 年以来，该州再未选出民主党联邦参议员；最后一位赢得该州的民主党总统候选人还是 1976 年的吉米·卡特。2024 年，唐纳德·特朗普以约 18 个百分点赢得南卡。共和党以绝对多数掌控州议会两院，并持有该州 7 个联邦众议院席位中的 6 个——第 7 席（第 6 选区）由民主党人詹姆斯·克莱伯恩持有。所有州级行政职位均由共和党人担任。

This baseline is the reason the race was rated as a Republican hold for most of its life, and the
reason the result is described by outlets as an upset rather than a swing. The archive records the
baseline because it is the condition against which every campaign decision was taken; it does not
treat the baseline as a prediction.

这一基线正是该场选举在大部分时间里被评为「共和党稳守」的原因，也是媒体把结果称为「冷门」而非「钟摆」的原因。档案记录基线，是因为它是双方每一次竞选决策所处的条件；档案不把基线当作预测。

## 4. Turnout and the result / 4. 投票率与结果

Total votes cast in the Senate race: **2,041,487** — Andrews 1,044,001 (51.14%), Graham 952,191
(46.64%), Kasie Whitener (Libertarian) 23,585 (1.16%), Mark Hackett (Constitution) 16,811 (0.82%),
write-in 4,899 (0.24%, derived from the county table's write-in column).

参议员选举总票数：**2,041,487** —— 安德鲁斯 1,044,001（51.14%）、格雷厄姆 952,191（46.64%）、自由党卡西·惠特纳 23,585（1.16%）、宪法党马克·哈克特 16,811（0.82%）、写名票 4,899（0.24%，由县表写名列求和得出）。

Andrews carried **24 of 46 counties** on the county table, including Greenville, York, Charleston,
Richland, Dorchester, Berkeley and Florence. The article's analysis paragraph says 25 counties; the
table yields 24. The discrepancy is recorded, not resolved — see
[`results_notes.md`](results_notes.md).

按县表，安德鲁斯赢下 **46 个县中的 24 个**，包括格林维尔、约克、查尔斯顿、里奇兰、多切斯特、伯克利与弗洛伦斯。条目的分析段落称「25 个县」，而表格给出 24 个。此差异被记录而非被解释——见 [`results_notes.md`](results_notes.md)。

## 5. The exit poll / 5. 出口民调

The CNN exit poll (section 60 of the article) is reproduced in full in
[`../09_sources/exit_poll_2026.csv`](../09_sources/exit_poll_2026.csv). The headline composition:
Democrats 27% of the electorate, Republicans 41%, independents 32%; Trump job approval 44%
approve / 55% disapprove; 2024 Trump voters 52% of the electorate, 2024 Harris voters 43%.

CNN 出口民调（条目第 60 节）已完整重录于 [`../09_sources/exit_poll_2026.csv`](../09_sources/exit_poll_2026.csv)。总体构成：民主党人占选民 27%、共和党人 41%、独立人士 32%；特朗普工作支持率 44% 支持／55% 不支持；2024 年特朗普选民占 52%、哈里斯选民占 43%。

Two composition facts matter for reading the result and neither is a causal claim: Andrews won
independents 63–32 and won 18–44 year olds 66–29, while losing voters 65+ 45–54 and rural areas
48–51. The archive reports the crosstabs as measured; it does not model why.

有两个构成性事实对解读结果很重要，且都不构成因果断言：安德鲁斯以 63–32 赢得独立人士、以 66–29 赢得 18–44 岁选民，同时在 65 岁以上（45–54）与乡村地区（48–51）落后。档案按测得的交叉表如实报告，不对「为什么」建模。
