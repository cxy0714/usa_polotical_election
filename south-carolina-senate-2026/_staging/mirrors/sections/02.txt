# [2] Interim appointment

Interim appointment
After Graham died, Governor Henry McMaster was tasked with appointing a successor to serve the remainder of his term. Lieutenant Governor Pamela Evette was considered a possible appointee, while U.S. Representative Joe Wilson declined consideration. On July 13, President Donald Trump publicly recommended that McMaster appoint Darline Graham, Lindsey's sister, to serve the remainder of the term, as did Senate Majority Leader John Thune. McMaster selected Darline on July 13 and she was sworn in on July 14. She is the first woman to represent South Carolina in the U.S. Senate. On July 20, Darline announced that she would run for her first full term in office.
