核心事实 / Key facts

Result / 结果

Andrews 1,044,001 (51.14%) to Graham 952,191 (46.64%); 2,041,487 votes cast.

安德鲁斯 1,044,001 票（51.14%），格雷厄姆 952,191 票（46.64%）；总投票 2,041,487。

First since 1998 / 1998 年以来首次

The first Democrat elected to this Senate seat since 1998.

该参议院席位自 1998 年以来选出的第一位民主党人。

A vacancy, not a normal cycle / 不是常规周期，而是空缺

Lindsey Graham died 2026-07-11; Darline Graham was appointed 07-13, sworn in 07-14, won a ten-candidate special primary and a runoff, then lost the general.

林赛·格雷厄姆于 2026-07-11 去世；达琳·格雷厄姆 07-13 受任命、07-14 宣誓，赢下十人特别初选与决选，随后输掉大选。

Money / 资金

As of 2026-10-14: Andrews $24,216,841 raised, Graham $1,917,646 — 12.6:1. Outside money favoured Graham: SLF $5M, MAGA Inc. $827,000.

截至 2026-10-14：安德鲁斯筹得 24,216,841 美元，格雷厄姆 1,917,646 美元——12.6:1。外部资金偏向格雷厄姆：SLF 500 万美元、MAGA Inc. 82.7 万美元。

Debates / 辩论

Graham took part in four televised debates; Andrews in one.

格雷厄姆参加四场电视辩论；安德鲁斯一场。
