Darline Graham — Political Positions / 达琳·格雷厄姆——政治立场

How the record is separated here / 本文件如何区分证据层次

Graham took office on 2026-07-14 and had never held elected office before that, so almost nothing in the usual candidate-position record exists: no long campaign platform, no roll-call history beyond a single confirmation vote, and no legislative authoring beyond one bill [SRC-0450][SRC-0459]. The blocks below are therefore separated by evidence type. Block (a) holds things she said herself; block (b) holds what she actually did in the Senate; block (c) holds positions attributed to her because of her brother, and is labelled as association, not as her own position; block (d) holds positions attributed to her by endorsing organizations. Two candidate websites were attempted and returned HTTP 403, so no platform text is quoted anywhere in this file.

格雷厄姆于 2026-07-14 就职，此前从未担任民选公职，因此通常的候选人立场档案几乎不存在：没有长篇竞选纲领，除一次确认表决外没有表决记录，除一项法案外没有立法起草记录 [SRC-0450][SRC-0459]。故下列各区块按证据类型划分。区块 (a) 为她本人所述；区块 (b) 为她在参议院的实际行为；区块 (c) 为因她兄长而归于她的立场，明确标注为"关联"，而非她本人的立场；区块 (d) 为背书组织归于她的立场。曾尝试访问两个候选人网站，均返回 HTTP 403，故本文件任何处均未引用纲领原文。

(a) Her own statements / （a）她本人的表述

National security. During a Republican primary runoff debate she said that "national security is not my thing" [SRC-0450]. The retrieved sources treat the remark as a gaffe that became a catalyst for her defeat, with some voters coming to see her as inexperienced and unqualified, and the race moving from safe Republican to contested tossup [SRC-0450][SRC-0401]. It is recorded in policy_positions.csv as GRA-POS-0002, with legislative_record marked as not applicable. This is the only self-assessment of her own competence on a policy domain located in the retrieved sources.

国家安全。 在共和党初选决选辩论中，她说"national security is not my thing" [SRC-0450]。已检索来源将该语视为失言、并成为其败选的催化剂：部分选民因此认为她缺乏经验与资格，选情由共和党稳操胜券转为势均力敌 [SRC-0450][SRC-0401]。此条记为 policy_positions.csv 的 GRA-POS-0002，legislative_record 标注为不适用。这是已检索来源中唯一一处她对本人在某一政策领域能力的自我评价。

Second Amendment. At the point the NRA Political Victory Fund endorsed her, Graham declared firearm ownership a constitutional right and argued that Annie Andrews has a record of supporting gun control measures [SRC-0477]. The retrieved report paraphrases rather than quotes her, and the endorsement release itself was not retrievable; the row is therefore marked verified for the endorsement event and paraphrase-only for the wording (GRA-POS-0011).

第二修正案。 在全国步枪协会政治胜利基金为其背书之际，格雷厄姆宣称持枪是宪法权利，并称安妮·安德鲁斯有支持控枪措施的记录 [SRC-0477]。已检索报道为转述而非直接引语，且背书新闻稿本身无法检索；因此该行对"背书事件"记为 verified，对措辞则仅为转述（GRA-POS-0011）。

Trump alignment. She was appointed on President Trump's recommendation, was endorsed by him the same day, and campaigned as committed to advancing his agenda — using opponent Ralph Norman's initial refusal to vote for the One Big Beautiful Bill Act as evidence that Norman was insufficiently committed to it [SRC-0450][SRC-0456][SRC-0478]. Her own verbatim words on that argument were not retrieved; the row (GRA-POS-0004) is a description of campaign conduct, not a quotation.

与特朗普的立场一致。 她因特朗普总统的推荐而受任命，同日获得其背书，并以推进其议程为竞选定位——把对手拉尔夫·诺曼最初拒绝支持《One Big Beautiful Bill Act》作为诺曼对该法案投入不足的证据 [SRC-0450][SRC-0456][SRC-0478]。该论述中她的原话未被检索到；相关行（GRA-POS-0004）描述的是竞选行为，而非引语。

The sanctions law, precisely. The act that became law is H.R. 5334, the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026: it raises tariffs on Russian products by up to 500% ad valorem, allows tariffs of up to 100% on each of the top five importers of Russian crude oil and of natural gas, sanctions the Russian shadow fleet and additional Russian banks, and extends the Iran Sanctions Act by five years to 2031 [SRC-0439]. Its legislative lineage began with Lindsey Graham's Sanctioning Russia Act, introduced 2025-04-01 with 84 cosponsors; Darline Graham's S.5025 was a revised version introduced in July 2026 that was not the version passed [SRC-0439][SRC-0450]. Two days before the act was to take effect in October 2026, President Trump signed an executive order waiving the Russia sanctions — a fact that bears directly on what her authorship achieved [SRC-0439].

制裁法的确切情形。 最终成法的是 H.R. 5334《林赛·O·格雷厄姆制裁俄罗斯与伊朗法案（2026）》：对俄罗斯产品加征最高 500% 的从价关税，允许对俄罗斯原油与天然气的各自前五大进口国加征最高 100% 关税，制裁俄罗斯"影子船队"及更多俄罗斯银行，并将《伊朗制裁法》延长五年至 2031 年 [SRC-0439]。其立法谱系始于林赛·格雷厄姆 2025-04-01 提出、有 84 名共同提案人的《制裁俄罗斯法案》；达琳·格雷厄姆的 S.5025 是 2026 年 7 月提出的修订版，并非最终通过的版本 [SRC-0439][SRC-0450]。在该法于 2026 年 10 月生效前两天，特朗普总统签署行政令豁免了对俄制裁——这一事实直接关系到她的起草究竟实现了什么 [SRC-0439]。

On the Blanche vote she said: "I was proud to vote to confirm Todd Blanche as Attorney General of the United States. President Trump couldn't have chosen a better nominee. Mr. Blanche is highly qualified. I know he will work hard to fight fraud and protect the safety of South Carolinians" [SRC-0473]. No other vote, bill, amendment or floor statement by her appears in the retrieved sources; in particular, no floor remarks on the Russia sanctions bill were located, and no statement by her on Iran was found even though the act's title names Iran (GRA-POS-0017).

关于布兰奇的表决，她表示："I was proud to vote to confirm Todd Blanche as Attorney General of the United States. President Trump couldn't have chosen a better nominee. Mr. Blanche is highly qualified. I know he will work hard to fight fraud and protect the safety of South Carolinians" [SRC-0473]。已检索来源中未见她的其他表决、法案、修正案或院会发言；尤其是未检索到她就对俄制裁法案所作的院会发言，也未发现她就伊朗的任何表述——尽管该法案标题包含伊朗（GRA-POS-0017）。

Her committee seats are jurisdictions, not positions: nothing in the retrieved material states her views on federal spending levels, environmental regulation or judicial philosophy, and policy_positions.csv marks those rows accordingly (GRA-POS-0005, GRA-POS-0006, GRA-POS-0007, GRA-POS-0016).

她的委员会席位属于管辖范围，而非立场：已检索材料中没有任何内容说明她对联邦支出水平、环境监管或司法哲学的看法，policy_positions.csv 相应标注了这些行（GRA-POS-0005、GRA-POS-0006、GRA-POS-0007、GRA-POS-0016）。

Administrative career as record, not platform. Her documented specialization is vocational rehabilitation and blindness services: staff of the South Carolina Vocational Rehabilitation Department from 2007, commissioner of the South Carolina Commission for the Blind from September 2019 to July 2026, member of the state workforce development board, and president-elect of the National Council of State Agencies for the Blind [SRC-0450][SRC-0467]. Coverage of that career states plainly that it was difficult to know what her priorities would be because she had no political experience [SRC-0459]. Rows GRA-POS-0008, GRA-POS-0009 and GRA-POS-0010 record the record, and say in terms that no verbatim policy statement was retrieved.

行政履历作为记录，而非纲领。 她有据可查的专业领域是职业康复与盲人服务：2007 年起任职于南卡罗来纳州职业康复部，2019 年 9 月至 2026 年 7 月任南卡盲人委员会委员兼机构负责人，任州劳动力发展委员会委员，并当选全国州级盲人机构理事会候任主席 [SRC-0450][SRC-0467]。关于这段履历的报道直言：由于她没有政治经验，外界难以判断其优先事项 [SRC-0459]。GRA-POS-0008、GRA-POS-0009、GRA-POS-0010 记录该履历，并明确说明未检索到任何直接的政策表述。

(c) Positions attributed to her by association with her brother / （c）因与兄长的关联而归于她的立场

This block is association, not evidence of her own views. Lindsey Graham was for two decades the Senate's most visible Republican advocate of an interventionist foreign policy and of sanctions on Russia; the bill Darline Graham introduced in July 2026 was modelled on legislation he authored and carries his name [SRC-0450][SRC-0472]. Analysis that reads her Senate foreign-policy posture as a continuation of his therefore rests on the bill's lineage and on the political logic of her appointment, not on any statement by her about Ukraine, NATO, Russia policy or military intervention — none of which was located. Recorded as GRA-POS-0013, with the association note carried in change_notes.

本区块属于关联推断，不构成她本人观点的证据。 林赛·格雷厄姆二十年来是参议院最显眼的共和党干预主义外交与对俄制裁倡导者；达琳·格雷厄姆 2026 年 7 月提出的法案以其起草的立法为蓝本、并以他命名 [SRC-0450][SRC-0472]。因此，把她的参议院外交姿态解读为其兄路线的延续，依据是法案的谱系与她受任命的政治逻辑，而非她关于乌克兰、北约、对俄政策或军事干预的任何表述——这些表述均未检索到。此条记为 GRA-POS-0013，关联说明置于 change_notes。

Succession framing. The same association operates at the level of the seat itself: she is described as the first sister to succeed her brother in Senate history, and her appointment was recommended by the president and the majority leader as a way of continuing his term [SRC-0450][SRC-0496]. Any claim that she "inherited" his positions is an inference from that framing, and the archive records it as such rather than as a position.

继任框架。 同样的关联也作用于席位本身：她被描述为参议院史上首位接替兄长席位的妹妹，其任命由总统与多数党领袖推荐，意在延续他的任期 [SRC-0450][SRC-0496]。任何称她"继承"其立场的说法，都是从这一框架推出的推断，档案据此记录为推断而非立场。

(d) Positions attributed to her by endorsing organizations / （d）背书组织归于她的立场

Anti-abortion groups endorsed her in October 2026 and attacked her opponent as pro-abortion: National Right to Life and South Carolina Citizens for Life Federal PAC endorsed Graham, and Students for Life Action congratulated her as the official Republican pick [SRC-0474][SRC-0475]. These materials attribute a pro-life position to her, but no verbatim statement of her own on abortion was located, and the National Right to Life release returned HTTP 403 when fetched, so only its cited existence is recorded. The row (GRA-POS-0012) is marked partially verified and its change_notes states the attribution basis.

反堕胎组织于 2026 年 10 月为她背书，并将对手指为"支持堕胎"：全国生命权委员会（National Right to Life）与南卡公民生命联邦政治行动委员会为格雷厄姆背书，Students for Life Action 祝贺她成为共和党官方提名人 [SRC-0474][SRC-0475]。这些材料将"支持生命"（pro-life）立场归于她，但未检索到她本人关于堕胎的直接表述；且全国生命权委员会的新闻稿在抓取时返回 HTTP 403，故仅记录其被引用的事实。该行（GRA-POS-0012）标注为 partially verified，并在 change_notes 中说明归属依据。

What is not documented / 未记录的内容

No position of hers was located on healthcare financing, Medicaid, Medicare, education, immigration, taxes, tariffs, energy production, agriculture, labor law, or judicial nominations. No campaign website text could be retrieved (HTTP 403), and the Senate office site also returned HTTP 403. Her stated positions on abortion and on any foreign country other than Russia remain unattributed in her own words. These absences are the substance of the finding, not an omission of research effort: they are consistent with coverage describing her as having little political history beyond praising her brother [SRC-0460][SRC-0459].

未检索到她在医疗融资、医疗补助、联邦医疗保险、教育、移民、税收、关税、能源生产、农业、劳动法或司法任命方面的任何立场。竞选网站文本无法检索（HTTP 403），参议员办公网站亦返回 HTTP 403。她关于堕胎以及除俄罗斯以外任何外国的立场，仍无她本人的原话可据。这些缺失本身即为发现的内容，而非检索不足：这与称其除赞誉兄长之外几乎没有政治履历的报道相符 [SRC-0460][SRC-0459]。
