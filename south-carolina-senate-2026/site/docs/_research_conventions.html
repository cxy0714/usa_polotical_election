1. Non-negotiable rules / 1. 不可谈判的规则

  1. Never invent a date, venue, quotation, dollar figure, endorsement, photo URL or poll number. If it is not in a source you actually retrieved, it does not go in the archive. 绝不虚构任何日期、场地、引语、金额、背书、照片 URL 或民调数字。若某项内容不在你实际检索到的来源中，它就不能进入本档案。
  2. Unknown is a value. Use unknown (lowercase) in CSV cells and ⚠ unknown in Markdown. Never leave a cell blank when a value is expected; never substitute a guess. unknown 是一个取值。 在 CSV 单元格中使用 unknown（小写），在 Markdown 中使用 ⚠ unknown。在应有取值时绝不将单元格留空；绝不以猜测替代。
  3. Every row carries at least one source. The source_urls cell holds one or more SRC-#### ids from 09_sources/sources.csv, plus the raw URL for the most important one. Format: SRC-0007|https://.... 每一行至少携带一个来源。 source_urls 单元格存放一个或多个来自 09_sources/sources.csv 的 SRC-#### 编号，并附上其中最重要一条的原始 URL。格式：SRC-0007|https://...。
  4. Separate what was said from what an analyst infers. stated_purpose / observable_function / analyst_interpretation are three distinct columns. Interpretation is prefixed INTERPRETATION:. 把「所述」与「分析者所推断」区分开来。 stated_purpose／observable_function／analyst_interpretation 是三个彼此独立的列。解读须以 INTERPRETATION: 为前缀。
  5. Announcement ≠ event. A press release announcing a rally is an announcement row (event_status = announced). Only mark held when there is evidence it took place. Verified cancellations go in with event_status = cancelled and the cancellation source. 宣布不等于活动。 宣布一场集会的新闻稿是一行宣布记录（event_status = announced）。只有在有证据表明活动确实发生时，才标记为 held。经核实的取消以 event_status = cancelled 记录，并附上取消的来源。
  6. Distinguish campaign-produced material from independent media. Campaign, outside PAC and newsroom material get different sponsor_type values. 区分竞选团队制作的材料与独立媒体。 竞选团队、外部政治行动委员会与新闻机构的材料使用不同的 sponsor_type 取值。
  7. Copyright: do not download copyrighted news photographs into the repo. Record metadata + source URL only. Download only assets that are public domain (official U.S. government / Senate portraits, federal works) or explicitly licensed. When in doubt: link, don't copy. 版权： 不要将受版权保护的新闻照片下载到仓库中。只记录元数据＋来源 URL。仅下载属于公有领域（美国政府／参议院官方肖像、联邦作品）或已获明确许可的素材。存疑时：只链接，不复制。
  8. No premature conclusions. Do not rank the candidates, score their branding, or declare a winner of the "communication contest". Descriptive observation only; strategic interpretation must be labelled as interpretation. 不作过早结论。 不得为候选人排名、为其品牌打分，也不得宣布「传播竞赛」的胜者。只作描述性观察；策略性解读必须标注为解读。
  9. Do not write the blog post or video script. This is an evidence archive. 不要撰写博客文章或视频脚本。这是一份证据档案。
  10. Graham's incumbency is dated, not assumed. Any row that treats her as "the incumbent" must carry the appointment date (2026-07-13) in analyst_notes or notes; she was a sitting senator for 112 days before the election. 格雷厄姆的「现任」身份必须标注日期，不得默认。 任何把她当作「现任参议员」处理的行，都必须在 analyst_notes／notes 中写明受任命日期（2026-07-13）；她在选举前仅担任参议员 112 天。

6. Event purpose: the three-layer rule / 6. 活动目的：三层规则

Fill all three, never blur them: - stated_purpose — what the campaign/candidate explicitly said it was for (quote the release, or unknown). - observable_function — what the activity actually was ("35-minute stump speech to ~80 people in a church hall, then 6 minutes of press questions"). - analyst_interpretation — labelled inference, prefixed INTERPRETATION:.

For each of the 29 event rows in 001 the same discipline applied; keep it here. 001 中 29 行活动均适用同一纪律；此处照旧。
