Annie Andrews — Political Positions / 安妮·安德鲁斯——政治立场

How positions are documented here / 本文件的记录方式

Two retrieved sources carry most of the load: the candidate biography, which quotes her at length on health care and abortion, and the WIS News 10 full-interview transcript of October 28, 2026, which is the only source in this archive where she speaks in her own words across a wide range of issues [SRC-0400][SRC-0402]. Where a position is reported without a direct quotation the entry says so rather than manufacturing one. legislative_record is unknown for every Andrews row in policy_positions.csv, because she has never held public office: the 2022 House race and the 2026 Senate race were her only campaigns [SRC-0400].

本文件主要依据两项已检索来源：候选人传记（对医疗与堕胎议题有长篇引语）以及 2026 年 10 月 28 日 WIS News 10 的完整访谈实录——后者是本档案中唯一一份她在广泛议题上以原话表述的来源 [SRC-0400][SRC-0402]。凡仅有立场记载而无直接引语者，条目均予以说明，不臆造引语。在 policy_positions.csv 中，安德鲁斯各行的 legislative_record 一律为 unknown，因为她从未担任公职：2022 年众议员选举与 2026 年参议员选举是她仅有的两次竞选 [SRC-0400]。

Self-description and party fit / 自我定位与党派关系

Andrews describes herself as a pragmatic, "radical centrist" [SRC-0400]. In the WIS interview she put the same idea as independence from party: "nobody in Washington, D.C., or nobody in my party recruited me to be here… I will be a brick wall between Washington, D.C., and South Carolina if anyone in D.C. tries to take advantage of South Carolina—even if it means standing up to my own party" [SRC-0402]. She also frames her qualification as professional rather than political, citing "about 15 years" as a pediatrician caring for sick and injured children [SRC-0402].

安德鲁斯自称务实的"激进中间派"（radical centrist）[SRC-0400]。在 WIS 访谈中，她把同一想法表述为对党派的独立性："nobody in Washington, D.C., or nobody in my party recruited me to be here… I will be a brick wall between Washington, D.C., and South Carolina if anyone in D.C. tries to take advantage of South Carolina—even if it means standing up to my own party" [SRC-0402]。她亦将自身资格表述为专业性的而非政治性的，称已作为儿科医生照护患病与受伤儿童"about 15 years" [SRC-0402]。

Healthcare affordability and Medicare / 医疗可负担性与联邦医疗保险

Her stated first priority is stopping the Medicaid cuts: "We have to stop the impending Medicaid budget cuts. Medicaid is really the backbone of the healthcare system here in South Carolina, and if these budget cuts go through, more of our rural hospitals will be forced to close… that's my number one priority: to stop these Medicaid budget cuts and protect our rural hospitals" [SRC-0402]. She links the cuts to lost coverage, overcrowded emergency departments and higher premiums for everyone [SRC-0402].

她所述的第一优先事项是阻止医疗补助（Medicaid）削减："We have to stop the impending Medicaid budget cuts. Medicaid is really the backbone of the healthcare system here in South Carolina, and if these budget cuts go through, more of our rural hospitals will be forced to close… that's my number one priority: to stop these Medicaid budget cuts and protect our rural hospitals" [SRC-0402]。她将这些削减与保险丧失、急诊科拥挤以及所有人保费上升相联系 [SRC-0402]。

On rural access she argues place of residence should not determine care: "I believe every South Carolinian deserves access to high-quality, affordable healthcare, regardless of the ZIP code in which they live" [SRC-0402]. She cites a visit to Allendale Hospital and warns that losing its emergency department would cost stroke, heart-attack and preterm-labor patients the minutes that decide outcomes [SRC-0402].

在乡村医疗可及性上，她主张居住地不应决定照护水平："I believe every South Carolinian deserves access to high-quality, affordable healthcare, regardless of the ZIP code in which they live" [SRC-0402]。她提及走访阿伦代尔（Allendale）医院，并警告若失去其急诊科，中风、心脏病与早产患者将失去决定结果的关键时间 [SRC-0402]。

Her structural proposal is a public option, framed as choice rather than government-financed care: "My plan includes a public option, and a public option doesn't mean the government is paying for someone's healthcare. A public option means that you or I have the option to purchase an affordable public plan if we choose. No one gets kicked off their private insurance" [SRC-0402]. She argues it would force private insurers to offer more comprehensive plans at lower premiums by directing more premium money to care rather than to "padding the pockets of big pharmaceutical companies, big insurance companies, and their shareholders" [SRC-0402].

她的结构性主张是"公共选项"（public option），将其界定为一种选择而非政府付费的医疗："My plan includes a public option, and a public option doesn't mean the government is paying for someone's healthcare… No one gets kicked off their private insurance" [SRC-0402]。她认为这会迫使私营保险公司以更低保费提供更全面的方案，因为更多保费将用于医疗服务本身，而非"padding the pockets of big pharmaceutical companies, big insurance companies, and their shareholders" [SRC-0402]。

She presents prevention as cost containment — "the more folks who have access to primary care doctors for early diagnosis and treatment of diseases, the less our entire healthcare system is going to cost" — and rejects single payer as dated, calling Medicare for All "a policy from a few decades ago" while describing her method as incremental: "you don't let perfect be the enemy of good" [SRC-0400][SRC-0403]. Her other stated health priorities are restoring the enhanced Affordable Care Act tax provisions and expanding Medicare prescription drug price negotiation [SRC-0400].

她以预防作为控费手段——"the more folks who have access to primary care doctors for early diagnosis and treatment of diseases, the less our entire healthcare system is going to cost"——并以"过时"为由拒绝单一支付制度，称"全民医保"是"a policy from a few decades ago"，同时把自身方法描述为渐进式："you don't let perfect be the enemy of good" [SRC-0400][SRC-0403]。她所述的其他医疗优先事项包括恢复《平价医疗法案》的强化税收补贴，以及扩大联邦医疗保险的处方药价格谈判 [SRC-0400]。

Reproductive health care and access / 生殖健康与可及性

Andrews campaigned on a woman's right to choose and on increasing access to health services; her campaign website stated: "These decisions belong to women and families, not to politicians" [SRC-0400][SRC-0404]. She vowed to restore the protections of Roe v. Wade, guarantee access to contraception and infertility care, and expand maternal health services, especially in rural communities, and her campaign stated: "No woman should have to leave her state or risk her life to get medical care" [SRC-0400][SRC-0404]. The vows are reported without direct quotation; only the two website sentences are verbatim. The WIS transcript of October 28, 2026 does not raise abortion.

安德鲁斯以女性选择权与扩大医疗服务可及性为竞选主张，其竞选网站写道："These decisions belong to women and families, not to politicians" [SRC-0400][SRC-0404]。她承诺恢复 Roe v. Wade 的保护、保障避孕与不孕治疗可及性、扩大孕产健康服务（尤在农村社区），其竞选团队并表示："No woman should have to leave her state or risk her life to get medical care" [SRC-0400][SRC-0404]。上述承诺为转述而非直接引语；仅两句网站表述为原文。2026 年 10 月 28 日的 WIS 访谈实录未涉及堕胎议题。

LGBTQ+ rights and gender-affirming care / LGBTQ+ 权益与性别肯定医疗

Two positions are documented and they do not point the same way. In 2022, responding to ads accusing her of "child abuse", Andrews said: "I do not support gender-affirming surgery for anyone under 18—nor does my hospital perform those procedures. What I support is evidence-based medical care, with parental consent, for teens struggling with gender identity issues", and condemned the attacks as putting "LGBTQ kids" at increased risk of targeted attacks and self-harm [SRC-0400][SRC-0433]. In October 2026, days after a social-media post by President Trump used an anti-LGBT slur against her, she released an ad saying she opposed "biological men playing women's sports" and would not allow "gender surgery for minors" — contradicting her 2022 post denouncing the Save Women's Sports Act as "shameful" and saying she would "always support trans youth" [SRC-0400][SRC-0409]. Both are recorded in policy_positions.csv (AND-POS-0015, AND-POS-0016), with position_changed flagged on the later one.

有据可查的两条立场方向并不一致。2022 年，针对指其"虐待儿童"的广告，安德鲁斯表示："I do not support gender-affirming surgery for anyone under 18—nor does my hospital perform those procedures. What I support is evidence-based medical care, with parental consent, for teens struggling with gender identity issues"，并谴责该攻击使"LGBTQ kids"面临更高的针对性攻击与自残风险 [SRC-0400][SRC-0433]。2026 年 10 月，在特朗普总统以反 LGBT 侮辱性称呼指称她数日之后，她发布广告称反对"biological men playing women's sports"、不会允许"gender surgery for minors"——这与她 2022 年称《Save Women's Sports Act》"shameful"并将"always support trans youth"的帖文相矛盾 [SRC-0400][SRC-0409]。两条分别记为 AND-POS-0015 与 AND-POS-0016，后一条标注 position_changed。

Gun policy / 枪支政策

Andrews supports universal background checks [SRC-0400]. Her advocacy began after the 2018 Parkland shooting, when she joined Moms Demand Action and started clinical research into gun-violence prevention aimed at reducing pediatric firearm injuries; she described gun-violence prevention as directly related to her work as a pediatrician [SRC-0400]. Opponents contested this ground: the NRA endorsed her Republican opponent in October 2026 and argued that Andrews has a record of supporting gun control measures [SRC-0477]. The WIS transcript does not address firearms.

安德鲁斯支持普遍背景审查 [SRC-0400]。其倡议始于 2018 年帕克兰枪击案：她加入 Moms Demand Action，并开始以减少儿童枪伤为目标的枪支暴力预防临床研究，称该议题与自己的儿科医生工作直接相关 [SRC-0400]。对手就此攻击：全国步枪协会于 2026 年 10 月为其共和党对手背书，并称安德鲁斯有支持控枪措施的记录 [SRC-0477]。WIS 访谈实录未涉及枪支议题。

Affordability and cost of living / 可负担性与生活成本

Her immediate affordability proposal is a fuel-tax suspension: "Suspending the federal gas and diesel tax… We need immediate relief right now, and that's why many weeks ago, I called for suspension of the federal gas and diesel tax," citing farmers in Barnwell paying about $900 to fill a tractor and Lowcountry shrimpers and fishermen paying over $2,000 to fuel their boats [SRC-0402]. On tariffs she says a governing majority in January would let Congress "stop these unconstitutional, on-again, off-again tariffs" [SRC-0402]. She also supports President Trump's "no tax on tips" and "no tax on Social Security" proposals, reported without quotation [SRC-0400].

她提出的即时可负担性方案是暂停燃油税："Suspending the federal gas and diesel tax… We need immediate relief right now, and that's why many weeks ago, I called for suspension of the federal gas and diesel tax"，并举例巴恩韦尔（Barnwell）农民加满拖拉机约需 900 美元、低地地区捕虾与渔民加满船只超过 2,000 美元 [SRC-0402]。关于关税，她表示 1 月取得执政多数后国会可"stop these unconstitutional, on-again, off-again tariffs" [SRC-0402]。她还支持特朗普总统的"小费免税"与"社会保障免税"主张（该项为转述，无引语）[SRC-0400]。

Growth, housing and data centers / 增长、住房与数据中心

She argues growth must be matched by housing and that residents' interests outrank developers': "we need to be sure we put community needs above developers' needs… oftentimes that leaves behind South Carolinians who have been in these communities for generations", citing a workforce-housing shortage near the Michelin plant in the Upstate [SRC-0402]. She calls for a two-year moratorium on new data centers, federal transparency on economic and environmental impact, ratepayer protections, and decisions returned to the local level: "if the answer from the community members is no, the answer needs to be no" [SRC-0402]. On energy she supports an "all-of-the-above approach to diversifying our energy sources, including nuclear energy", and argues for reducing reliance on foreign oil and building grid resilience [SRC-0402].

她主张增长必须与住房供给相匹配、居民利益优先于开发商利益："we need to be sure we put community needs above developers' needs… oftentimes that leaves behind South Carolinians who have been in these communities for generations"，并举例上州（Upstate）米其林工厂周边的劳动力住房短缺 [SRC-0402]。她呼吁对新建数据中心实施两年禁令、要求联邦层面就经济与环保障响作出透明披露、保护电费缴纳者，并把决策权交回地方："if the answer from the community members is no, the answer needs to be no" [SRC-0402]。能源方面她支持"all-of-the-above approach to diversifying our energy sources, including nuclear energy"，主张减少对外国石油的依赖并提升电网韧性 [SRC-0402]。

Democracy, corruption and political reform / 民主、腐败与政治改革

She supports term limits and a ban on congressional stock trading [SRC-0400], and in the WIS interview expanded this into an anti-corruption programme: "We need to stop the congressional gravy train… by passing term limits, by banning members of Congress from trading stock or from becoming lobbyists, by getting dark money out of politics, by ending Citizens United" [SRC-0402]. She ties dark money directly to her opponent's advertising: "you don't actually know who's funding these ads, who's behind these attack ads" [SRC-0402]. She also says she is embarrassed that her party does not uniformly support the stock-trading ban or term limits [SRC-0402].

她支持任期限制与禁止国会议员炒股 [SRC-0400]；在 WIS 访谈中将其扩展为一套反腐主张："We need to stop the congressional gravy train… by passing term limits, by banning members of Congress from trading stock or from becoming lobbyists, by getting dark money out of politics, by ending Citizens United" [SRC-0402]。她把"暗钱"与对手的广告直接联系："you don't actually know who's funding these ads, who's behind these attack ads" [SRC-0402]。她还表示，本党并非一致支持禁止炒股或任期限制，令她感到难堪 [SRC-0402]。

Immigration / 移民

She criticizes her own party on the border and takes a enforcement position: "My party has been very weak on the border, and I'm one of the first people to point that out… We need a secure border to be a secure nation. We need to deport illegal immigrants who break the law" [SRC-0402]. The biography separately records that she supports strengthened border security as one of the areas where she differs from some in her party [SRC-0400].

她在此议题上批评本党并采取执法立场："My party has been very weak on the border, and I'm one of the first people to point that out… We need a secure border to be a secure nation. We need to deport illegal immigrants who break the law" [SRC-0402]。传记另记载她支持加强边境安全，并将此列为其与本党部分立场不同的领域之一 [SRC-0400]。

Bipartisanship and first-year goals / 跨党合作与首年目标

She names stopping or delaying the Medicaid cuts as her measure of success and says her first act would be to invite Republican Senator Tim Scott to lunch, citing his work with Cory Booker on Medicaid coverage for South Carolina children with sickle cell disease: "I want to have productive bipartisan legislative successes… I think we both agree that South Carolinians deserve two fighters in the U.S. Senate" [SRC-0402]. She also frames children as her decision standard: "Children always have been and always will be my North Star" [SRC-0402].

她以阻止或延缓 Medicaid 削减作为成功标准，并表示上任第一件事是邀请共和党参议员蒂姆·斯科特共进午餐，援引其与科里·布克合作推动南卡镰状细胞病患儿 Medicaid 覆盖的先例："I want to have productive bipartisan legislative successes… I think we both agree that South Carolinians deserve two fighters in the U.S. Senate" [SRC-0402]。她亦以儿童作为决策标准："Children always have been and always will be my North Star" [SRC-0402]。

Coverage gaps / 覆盖缺口

No statement by Andrews on education, religion, judicial nominations, Social Security's finances beyond the tax proposal, or on any foreign country was located. Her tax positions ("no tax on tips", "no tax on Social Security") are reported without quotation or cost detail, and the biography gives no date for them. The claim that she is a "radical centrist" is her self-description as reported, not a measured ideological score, and this archive makes no such score.

已检索来源中未发现安德鲁斯关于教育、宗教、司法任命、除税收主张之外的社会保障财政，或任何外国的表述。其税收立场（"小费免税""社会保障免税"）为转述，无引语亦无成本测算，传记未给出日期。"激进中间派"之说系其自称的转述，并非可量化的意识形态评分，本档案不作此类评分。
