# Dan S. Sullivan / 丹·沙利文

## Who he is

Dan S. Sullivan (born 13 November 1964, Fairview Park, Ohio) is a Republican who has represented
Alaska in the U.S. Senate since 3 January 2015. Before that he was Alaska's attorney general
(June 2009 – November 2010) and commissioner of the Department of Natural Resources
(December 2010 – September 2013). He is a retired colonel in the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve: active
service 1993–1997 and 2004–2006, reserve service thereafter, retiring in early 2024 after more than
thirty years (SRC-0008, SRC-0092).

He moved to Alaska in the 1990s, and his political identity is built on the combination of military
service, resource development and opposition to federal regulation — a mix he repeats in nearly every
speech (SRC-0008, SRC-0045). He and his wife, Julie Fate Sullivan, appear together at campaign events;
she is visible in press photographs of the 27 August 2026 AOGA forum (SRC-0045).

丹·沙利文（1964 年 11 月 13 日生于俄亥俄州费尔维尤帕克）是共和党人，自 2015 年 1 月 3 日起代表
阿拉斯加出任联邦参议员。此前曾任阿拉斯加州总检察长（2009 年 6 月—2010 年 11 月）与自然资源厅
厅长（2010 年 12 月—2013 年 9 月）。他是美国海军陆战队预备役退役上校：1993—1997 年与
2004—2006 年现役，此后转入预备役，2024 年初退役，服役逾三十年（SRC-0008、SRC-0092）。他于
1990 年代移居阿拉斯加，政治形象建立在军旅、资源开发与反对联邦监管三者之上——这套组合几乎出现在
他每一次演讲中（SRC-0008、SRC-0045）。他与妻子朱莉·费特·沙利文共同出席竞选活动；2026 年 8 月
27 日 AOGA 论坛的新闻照片中可见其妻（SRC-0045）。

## The 2026 campaign

Sullivan announced a third term at the Fairbanks Republican Women's Club Lincoln Day Dinner on
21 March 2025 — a setting he framed as historically apt for a Republican ("It's very appropriate, in
Fairbanks, at a Fairbanks Republican Women's Club Lincoln Day Dinner..."). He was endorsed by Donald
Trump in July 2025 and by Sen. Lisa Murkowski hours after Peltola entered the race in January 2026
(SRC-0057, SRC-0018, SRC-0049).

His coalition was institutional rather than populist: Teamsters Local 959, the U.S. Chamber of
Commerce, the ANCSA Regional Association, the Alaska public-safety unions, NRA-PVF (A+), Safari Club
International, Americans for Prosperity Action (with a six-figure ad buy), Citizens for Responsible
Energy Solutions, the Alaska Republican Party, and — notably — Sealaska's board, which co-endorsed
him and Peltola (SRC-0042, SRC-0103 to SRC-0107, SRC-0102). He finished second in the August primary
with 41.4%, then lost the general with 131,844 votes (45.80%) to Peltola's 144,302 (50.12%)
(SRC-0006, SRC-0001).

沙利文于 2025 年 3 月 21 日在费尔班克斯共和党妇女俱乐部林肯日晚宴上宣布竞选第三任期，并称这一
场合对共和党人具有历史意味（"在费尔班克斯、在共和党妇女俱乐部的林肯日晚宴上宣布，非常合适……"）。
他于 2025 年 7 月获特朗普背书，2026 年 1 月佩尔托拉参选数小时后获参议员莉萨·穆尔科斯基背书
（SRC-0057、SRC-0018、SRC-0049）。其支持联盟偏建制而非民粹：卡车司机工会 959 分会、美国商会、
ANCSA 区域协会、阿拉斯加公共安全工会、NRA-PVF（A+）、国际狩猎俱乐部、Americans for Prosperity
Action（配合六位数广告投放）、Citizens for Responsible Energy Solutions、阿拉斯加共和党，以及
值得注意的是与佩尔托拉共同背书的 Sealaska 董事会（SRC-0042、SRC-0103 至 SRC-0107、SRC-0102）。
他在 8 月初选以 41.4% 列第二，大选以 131,844 票（45.80%）败给佩尔托拉的 144,302 票（50.12%）
（SRC-0006、SRC-0001）。

## The namesake fight

From June 2026 Sullivan's campaign argued that a Petersburg man named Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. had been
recruited to split his vote, and that the state should remove him from the ballot. A superior court
judge ordered the challenger restored; the Alaska Supreme Court affirmed that he could appear; the
Division of Elections then said he could not run *as a Republican*, and he switched to "undeclared" in
late October (SRC-0009 to SRC-0016, SRC-0032 to SRC-0034, SRC-0044). Sullivan also accused Peltola of
rigging the election, while his own outlet coverage noted that evidence of her involvement was unclear
(SRC-0047). He lost by 12,458 votes; the namesake candidate took 4,760 (SRC-0001).

自 2026 年 6 月起，沙利文团队主张彼得斯堡居民小丹尼尔·沙利文是被人招募来分走其选票，州政府应将其
除名。高等法院法官裁定恢复其参选资格；阿拉斯加最高法院确认其可出现在选票上；选举委员会随后表示他
不能以共和党人身份参选，他于 10 月下旬改为"无党派声明"（SRC-0009 至 SRC-0016、SRC-0032 至
SRC-0034、SRC-0044）。沙利文还指责佩尔托拉操纵选举，但相关报道同时指出佩尔托拉是否参与证据不明
（SRC-0047）。他最终以 12,458 票之差落败；同名候选人获 4,760 票（SRC-0001）。

## How he campaigned

Sullivan ran as a legislator with receipts — bycatch bills, the Seafood Policy Office in the Farm Bill,
ANCSA contracting, military pay — and as an attack campaign against a "Lower 48 liberal", a phrase
Alaska Beacon counted sixteen times in a single forum (SRC-0036, SRC-0045). He leaned on surrogates
late: Vice President JD Vance in a Fairbanks hangar on 5 October and President Trump in Wasilla on
13 October (SRC-0025, SRC-0062). Two of his three debates turned personal; in the last one he accused
Peltola of receiving questions in advance, a claim the moderator publicly rejected (SRC-0024).

As of 2026-11-18 he had neither conceded nor given a concession speech; his spokesman said only that
"Alaskans deserve a complete and accurate count" (SRC-0056). That absence is recorded as a gap, not
filled (see `research_gaps.md`).

沙利文以"有账可查的立法者"姿态竞选——兼捕法案、农业法案中的海产政策办公室、ANCSA 承包、军人薪酬——
同时以攻击"下四十八州自由派"为主线；Alaska Beacon 统计他在一场论坛中说了十六次这一说法
（SRC-0036、SRC-0045）。他在选战末段倚重代理人：10 月 5 日副总统万斯在费尔班克斯机库、10 月 13 日
特朗普在瓦西拉（SRC-0025、SRC-0062）。三场辩论中有两场转向人身攻击；最后一场他指责佩尔托拉提前
拿到问题，主持人公开否认（SRC-0024）。截至 2026-11-18，他既未认输也未发表认输演讲，其发言人仅称
"阿拉斯加人理应得到完整、准确的计票"（SRC-0056）。这一缺席被记为缺口，而非填补
（见 `research_gaps.md`）。
