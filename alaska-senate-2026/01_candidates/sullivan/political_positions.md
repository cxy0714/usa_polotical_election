# Political positions / 政治立场

Sixteen positions are coded for Sullivan in `01_candidates/policy_positions.csv` (SUL-POS-01 to
SUL-POS-16). Each row states whether the evidence is a verbatim statement, a reported position, a
legislative record, or an endorsement inference.

沙利文在 `01_candidates/policy_positions.csv` 中编码 16 条立场（SUL-POS-01 至 SUL-POS-16）。
每一行均标明证据类型：逐字表态、报道所述、立法记录，或由背书推断。

## Energy and federal lands

| Position | What is established | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ANWR and NPR-A (SUL-POS-01) | Supports oil development in the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge and the National Petroleum Reserve–Alaska; warns Peltola would let environmentalists block ANWR | Verbatim (reported) | SRC-0045 |
| 2025 tax-and-spending vote (SUL-POS-14) | Voted for the 2025 budget act; attacked by national groups over it | Legislative record | SRC-0018 |

Sullivan's energy position is the spine of his case against Peltola: he argues she "consistently voted
with Lower 48 liberal allies of hers against our interests" and that her election would hand
environmental litigants the ability to shut ANWR down (SRC-0045). The phrase "Lower 48 liberal", or a
variant, appeared sixteen times in one forum — Alaska Beacon counted (SRC-0045).

能源立场是他攻击佩尔托拉的骨干：他称她"一贯与下四十八州的自由派盟友一起投票、反对我们的利益"，
并称她当选将让环保诉讼者有能力关停 ANWR（SRC-0045）。"下四十八州自由派"或其变体在一场论坛中出现
十六次——由 Alaska Beacon 统计（SRC-0045）。

## Fisheries and seafood policy

| Position | What is established | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bycatch and seafood policy (SUL-POS-02) | Author of bycatch-reduction legislation; says the Seafood Policy Office is in the Farm Bill; Bycatch Reduction Act scheduled for a Commerce Committee vote | Verbatim | SRC-0036 |
| Commercial-fishing endorsements (SUL-POS-03) | Endorsed by United Fishermen of Alaska | Endorsement | SRC-0043 |

At Kodiak he made the most concrete policy claim of either candidate in the retrieved corpus: "My
bill for establishment of the Seafood Policy Office is in the Farm Bill right now. That helps with
regard to enabling fishermen to have access to all the programs that farmers have" (SRC-0036). He
also promised to get "my bycatch bill over the goal line next week in the Commerce Committee"
(SRC-0036). Both claims are his own statements, not independently verified legislative status.

在科迪亚克，他给出了语料中两位候选人最具体的政策主张："设立海产政策办公室的法案现在就在农业法案
里。这有助于让渔民获得农民所享有的一切项目"（SRC-0036）。他还承诺"下周让我的兼捕法案在商务委员会
过关"（SRC-0036）。两项均为其本人陈述，未独立核实立法进度。

Health care is where the coalition arithmetic is clearest: the same 2025 vote that Sullivan's
business and energy endorsers praised is the vote SEIU 775 used to endorse his opponent (SRC-0042,
SRC-0103). The archive records the vote as legislative record and the electoral use of it as an
endorsement claim, without adjudicating the policy merits.

医疗议题最能体现联盟算术：沙利文的商业与能源背书者称赞的那次 2025 年投票，正是 SEIU 775 用来背书
其对手的投票（SRC-0042、SRC-0103）。档案把投票记为立法记录、把选举层面的使用记为背书方主张，
不评判政策本身。
