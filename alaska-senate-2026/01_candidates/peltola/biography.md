# Mary Peltola / 玛丽·佩尔托拉

## Who she is

Mary Peltola (born 31 August 1973, Anchorage) is a Yup'ik Democrat from Alaska who served in the
Alaska House of Representatives from 1999 to 2009, in the U.S. House from September 2022 to January
2025, and was elected to the U.S. Senate on 3 November 2026 — becoming the first Alaska Native
person to hold a Senate seat. Before Congress she was a fisheries biologist and manager for the
Kuskokwim River Inter-Tribal Fish Commission, which is why fisheries is her signature issue rather
than a campaign theme (SRC-0007, SRC-0035).

When she won the 2022 special election she became the first Alaska Native woman elected to Congress.
She was sworn in on 13 September 2022, won a full term that November, and lost the 2024 general
election to Republican Nick Begich III (SRC-0007, SRC-0008).

玛丽·佩尔托拉（1973 年 8 月 31 日生于安克雷奇）是阿拉斯加尤皮克族民主党人，1999—2009 年任阿拉斯加
州众议员，2022 年 9 月至 2025 年 1 月任联邦众议员，2026 年 11 月 3 日当选联邦参议员，成为首位担任
参议员的阿拉斯加原住民。进入国会前，她曾任库斯科奎姆河部落间渔业委员会渔业生物学家与管理者，
因此渔业是她的本行议题，而非竞选包装（SRC-0007、SRC-0035）。2022 年特别选举胜出时，她成为首位
当选国会议员的阿拉斯加原住民女性。她于 2022 年 9 月 13 日就职，同年 11 月赢得完整任期，
2024 年大选败给共和党人尼克·贝吉奇（SRC-0007、SRC-0008）。

## The 2026 campaign

She announced on 12 January 2026 by video; the AP dateline was Juneau (SRC-0060, SRC-0035). Her
message was affordability and "Alaska First", with rural Alaska framed as "not a boutique issue"
(SRC-0060). She was endorsed on launch day by SEIU 775 (SRC-0042) and later by the Alaska AFL-CIO,
AFGE, Elect Democratic Women, End Citizens United, J Street PAC, NOW PAC, Reproductive Freedom for
All, AFA-CWA, Sealaska's board (a co-endorsement with Sullivan) and former Gov. Tony Knowles
(SRC-0094 to SRC-0102, SRC-0027).

She finished first in the 18 August top-four primary with 82,244 votes (49.5%) against Sullivan's
68,726 (41.4%) (SRC-0006, SRC-0064). In the general she raised far more than the incumbent —
$37 million to $14 million through mid-October (SRC-0056) — and won 144,302 votes (50.12%), an
outright first-round majority that made the scheduled ranked-choice tabulation unnecessary
(SRC-0001, SRC-0053).

她于 2026 年 1 月 12 日以视频宣布参选，AP 稿的发稿地为朱诺（SRC-0060、SRC-0035）。其信息主轴是
"可负担性"与"阿拉斯加优先"，并把乡村阿拉斯加定位为"不是小众议题"（SRC-0060）。宣布当天即获
SEIU 775 背书（SRC-0042），此后又获阿拉斯加 AFL-CIO、AFGE、Elect Democratic Women、
End Citizens United、J Street PAC、NOW PAC、Reproductive Freedom for All、AFA-CWA、
Sealaska 董事会（与沙利文共同背书）以及前州长托尼·诺尔斯的支持（SRC-0094 至 SRC-0102、SRC-0027）。
她在 8 月 18 日前四名初选中以 82,244 票（49.5%）居首，沙利文 68,726 票（41.4%）（SRC-0006、
SRC-0064）。大选中她筹款远超现任议员——截至 10 月中旬 3,700 万美元对 1,400 万美元（SRC-0056）——
最终获 144,302 票（50.12%），首轮即过半，使原定排序复选计票失去必要（SRC-0001、SRC-0053）。

## Personal history that became campaign material

Her husband Eugene "Buzzy" Peltola Jr. was killed in a small-plane crash in September 2023, while she
was serving in Congress; her mother died in the same period, and the campaign later attributed some
of the conduct alleged in a September 2026 New York Times story to that stress (SRC-0007, SRC-0022).
Two of her sons have served in the military — a fact she used on stage during the 15 October debate
when Sullivan accused her of voting against troop pay (SRC-0024). After leaving Congress in January
2025 she joined the law and lobbying firm Holland & Hart, and was barred from lobbying Congress for a
year (SRC-0040).

她的丈夫尤金·"巴齐"·佩尔托拉二世于 2023 年 9 月在小型飞机失事中遇难，当时她仍在国会任职；其母亲
也在同一时期去世，竞选团队后来把 2026 年 9 月《纽约时报》报道中的部分行为归因于这段压力
（SRC-0007、SRC-0022）。她的两个儿子曾在军中服役——10 月 15 日辩论中沙利文指责她反对军人加薪时，
她当场援引了这一事实（SRC-0024）。2025 年 1 月离开国会后，她加入 Holland & Hart 律师事务所，
一年内被禁止对国会游说（SRC-0040）。

## Style and reputation, as reported

Reporters describe a plain-spoken, fisheries-literate campaigner who leans on personal history and
local detail rather than national Democratic framing. Her announcement and victory statements used
the same anti-Washington register — "if you're not at the table, you're on the menu", "take on the
rigged system in DC" (SRC-0060, SRC-0056). The September 2026 allegations of hostile treatment of
staff were denied in part by her campaign and defended against by the Alaska Democratic Party and
Sen. Lisa Murkowski; ADN's reporting found operatives did not expect them to move the race
(SRC-0022).

记者笔下的她是直言、熟悉渔业细节的竞选者，倚重个人经历与地方细节，而非全国民主党的框架。她的
参选声明与胜选声明使用同一套反华盛顿语气——"不在桌边，就在菜单上"、"对抗华盛顿的操纵体系"
（SRC-0060、SRC-0056）。2026 年 9 月关于她苛待员工的指控，竞选团队部分否认，阿拉斯加民主党与
参议员莉萨·穆尔科斯基为其辩护；ADN 报道称业内人士认为此事不会改变选情（SRC-0022）。
