# Political positions / 政治立场

Fifteen positions are coded for Peltola in `01_candidates/policy_positions.csv` (PEL-POS-01 to
PEL-POS-15). This page groups them and states, for each, whether the evidence is a verbatim
statement, a reported position, an endorsement inference, or nothing at all.

佩尔托拉在 `01_candidates/policy_positions.csv` 中编码 15 条立场（PEL-POS-01 至 PEL-POS-15）。
本页按议题分组，并逐条说明证据类型：逐字表态、报道所述、由背书推断，或无证据。

## Economy and affordability

| Position | What is established | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Affordability (PEL-POS-01) | Campaign's central theme: grocery and fuel prices, "price gouging", corporate greed; rural prices quoted as proof | Verbatim | SRC-0043 |
| Campaign finance (PEL-POS-10) | Supports a constitutional amendment to overturn *Citizens United* | Reported | SRC-0043 |
| Labor and grocery merger (PEL-POS-13) | Organized town halls against the Kroger–Albertson's merger; AFGE cites oil-sector union jobs | Endorsement release | SRC-0094 |

Her affordability message is not a slogan layered on later: it was the announcement frame. In the
12 January video she argued rural Alaska is "not a boutique issue", and by May she had a five-part
platform whose first item was prices (SRC-0060, SRC-0043). She attributes inflation partly to
corporate concentration — "I think we're all going to be looking at where the price gouging is and
where we can halt corporate greed and inflation" (SRC-0043).

经济议题并非后期包装：2026 年 1 月 12 日的参选视频即以乡村阿拉斯加"不是小众议题"立论，到 5 月
已形成以物价为首项的五点纲领（SRC-0060、SRC-0043）。她把通胀部分归因于企业集中——"我们都会去查
价格欺诈在哪里，以及在哪里能遏制企业贪婪与通胀"（SRC-0043）。

## Energy, federal lands and mining

| Position | What is established | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oil development (PEL-POS-02) | Reported as backing large projects including the 2023 Willow approval while criticizing "Lower 48" regulators | Reported | SRC-0040 |
| Federal lands and mining (PEL-POS-15) | Reported as backing large-scale mining as well as oil, and opposing new federal land restrictions | Reported | SRC-0040 |
| Fisheries (PEL-POS-03) | Anti-trawl-bycatch; pledges to take on "Lower 48 out-of-state factory trawlers" | Verbatim | SRC-0036 |

This is the position set that most surprises readers expecting a national Democrat: on resource
development she runs to Sullivan's right in tone, attacking "Lower 48" regulators and environmental
litigation rather than defending them (SRC-0040, SRC-0045). At the AOGA forum she used her energy
answers to talk about fisheries, including work against an Endangered Species Act listing for
Chinook salmon (SRC-0045). The archive codes this as reported position, not verbatim policy detail;
no Peltola energy plan document was retrieved.

这一组立场最易让期待"全国民主党人"的读者意外：在资源开发上，她的语气比沙利文更靠右，攻击而非辩护
"下四十八州"监管者与环保诉讼（SRC-0040、SRC-0045）。在 AOGA 论坛上，她用能源问题的回答谈渔业，
包括阻止帝王鲑被列入《濒危物种法》（SRC-0045）。档案将其编码为"报道所述"，而非逐字政策细节；
未取得佩尔托拉的能源计划文件。

## Health care

| Position | What is established | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rural health care (PEL-POS-04) | Frames health and energy costs as a rural burden; no specific rural-health program retrieved | Reported | SRC-0040 |
| Medicaid / Medicare (PEL-POS-05) | Opposes the 2025 budget act's Medicaid cuts; SEIU 775 cited those cuts when endorsing her | Endorsement + reported | SRC-0042 |

Health care entered the race through the 2025 budget vote. Peltola's first social-media post after
months of silence, on 1 July 2025, attacked the bill for raising health-care and energy costs "so
millionaires ... can get even richer" (SRC-0040). SEIU 775's endorsement release made Medicaid the
reason (SRC-0042). No standalone Medicare proposal from her was retrieved — a gap recorded in the
position row rather than filled.

医疗议题经由 2025 年预算案进入选战。佩尔托拉沉寂数月后的第一条社交帖（2025 年 7 月 1 日）即抨击该案
推高医疗与能源成本，"好让百万富翁……更富"（SRC-0040）。SEIU 775 的背书稿明确以 Medicaid 为由
（SRC-0042）。未取得她单独的 Medicare 方案——该缺口在立场行中如实记录，未作填补。

The archive does not convert identity into a policy program. Peltola's Alaska Native background is
documented and electorally relevant, but no retrieved source has her proposing ANCSA reform or a

AFN's "Calls for Full Participation" statement (SRC-0069). Likewise, Measure 2 — the 2026 initiative

campaign does not appear in the retrieved coverage of it (SRC-0077).

档案不把身份转换为政策纲领。佩尔托拉的阿拉斯加原住民背景有据可查且具选举意义，但没有任何已取得
来源显示她提出 ANCSA 改革或部落主权法案；存在的是 Sealaska 董事会与沙利文的共同背书（SRC-0102）

"未取得立场"，因为已取得的公投报道中没有她的竞选团队（SRC-0077）。

## Foreign policy: the Iran war

| Position | What is established | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iran / war powers (PEL-POS-14) | Ties the war to Alaska fuel prices; wants Congress to act through the War Powers Act but stops short of opposing the war outright | Verbatim | SRC-0043 |

This is her most-quoted attack line and her most careful position. She says Sullivan "voted for the
war in Iran over 10 times" and that "this war in Iran is driving up our gas and diesel prices"
(SRC-0036), and she calls for a War Powers Act role — "I do not believe that any President should be
making these kinds of substantive decisions unilaterally" (SRC-0043) — without saying she would have
voted against the war. The archive codes that distinction explicitly.

这是她被引用最多的攻击线，也是最谨慎的立场。她说沙利文"十多次投票支持对伊朗的战争"，且"这场对伊
战争正在推高我们的汽油与柴油价格"（SRC-0036），并主张国会依《战争权力法》介入——"我不认为任何
总统应单方面作出这类实质性决定"（SRC-0043）——但并未表示她会投票反对这场战争。档案明确编码了
这一区分。
