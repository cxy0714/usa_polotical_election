Event coverage gaps / 活动报道缺口

The shape of the evidence

campaign_events.csv holds 30 rows: 14 for Peltola, 16 for Sullivan. Of these, 16 are documented appearances, 11 are endorsement announcements, and 3 are unverified placeholders (two AFN rows and Peltola's written victory statement). Five occasions are recorded once per candidate — the primary day, the AOGA forum, the Kodiak debate, Debate for the State and the AFN convention — so the file describes 25 distinct dated occasions, not 30.

campaign_events.csv 共 30 行：佩尔托拉 14 行、沙利文 16 行。其中 16 条为有据可查的露面、11 条为 背书公告、3 条为未核实占位（两行 AFN 与佩尔托拉的书面胜选声明）。有五个场合按候选人各记一行—— 初选日、AOGA 论坛、科迪亚克辩论、Debate for the State 与 AFN 大会——因此文件描述的是 25 个不同日期场合，而非 30 个。

The AFN hole

The Alaska Federation of Natives' 60th convention began 2026-10-22 (SRC-0077). AFN is the central Alaska Native political gathering of the cycle, and both candidates had obvious reasons to appear: Peltola as the first Alaska Native woman in Congress, Sullivan as the candidate endorsed by the ANCSA Regional Association (SRC-0104). No retrieved report documents either one speaking there. Two rows (PEL-EVT-09, SUL-EVT-16) exist as unverified placeholders with explicitly hedged titles ("candidate appearance not documented"), and this file records why they are placeholders rather than events. Filling this gap is the single highest-value addition to the archive.

阿拉斯加原住民联合会（AFN）第 60 届大会于 2026-10-22 开幕（SRC-0077）。AFN 是本周期阿拉斯加原住民 政治的核心场合，两位候选人都有明显理由出席：佩尔托拉是国会首位阿拉斯加原住民女性，沙利文则获 ANCSA 区域协会背书（SRC-0104）。但没有任何已取得报道记录其中一人在此发言。两行 （PEL-EVT-09、SUL-EVT-16）作为 unverified 占位存在，标题明确保留（"候选人露面未记载"）， 本文件说明其为何只是占位而非事件。补齐这一缺口是档案中最有价值的增补。

The imbalance in the primary phase is a source artifact: Sullivan's team pushed endorsement releases steadily through the spring and summer, while Peltola's endorsements were announced by the groups themselves on fewer dates.
