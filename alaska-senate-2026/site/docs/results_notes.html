Why no ranked-choice tabulation was required

Alaska elects its senators by ranked-choice voting: a candidate who receives more than 50% of first-choice votes in the first round wins outright; otherwise the last-place candidate is eliminated and ballots are retabulated until two remain. The threshold here is half of 287,896, i.e. 143,948 votes. Peltola's 144,302 clears it by 354 votes, so the division's scheduled 2026-11-18 elimination round was unnecessary. The Division of Elections reported the race as an outright first-round win, and CNN and NBC called it on 2026-11-17 (SRC-0053, SRC-0056).

Consequences for the record: Heikes's 6,105 and Daniel J. Sullivan Jr.'s 4,760 votes were never transferred; there are no elimination-round tallies; and any statement that Peltola won "after ranked-choice tabulation" is wrong. NBC's 2026-11-14 explainer describes the arithmetic that would have applied had she fallen short (SRC-0055).

阿拉斯加参议员选举采用排序复选制：首轮获得超过 50% 第一选择的候选人直接当选，否则淘汰末位并重新 分配选票。门槛为 287,896 的一半，即 143,948 票；佩尔托拉 144,302 票，超出 354 票，因此 选举委员会原定 2026-11-18 的淘汰轮无需进行。海克斯的 6,105 票与小丹尼尔·沙利文的 4,760 票从未被 转移，档案中不存在淘汰轮计票数据。任何称佩尔托拉"经排序复选计票后获胜"的说法都不正确。

Margins, and a counterfactual that does not change the outcome

  • Margin over Sullivan: 144,302 − 131,844 = 12,458 votes, i.e. 4.32 percentage points.
  • Turnout: 290,003 ballots cast of 605,433 registered voters = 47.90%.
  • The 2,107-ballot discrepancy: 290,003 ballots were cast but only 287,896 contained a valid vote for

    • U.S. Senator — 2,107 ballots (0.73% of those cast) were exhausted, blank or overvoted on that contest. The summary report does not break this out, so the composition of the 2,107 is unknown.

  • Same-name counterfactual: if all 4,760 votes for Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. had gone to the incumbent,

    • Sullivan would have had 136,604 (47.45%) to Peltola's 144,302 (50.12%) — a 7,698-vote loss.

  • Maximal counterfactual: even if every minor-candidate vote (4,760 + 6,105 + 885 = 11,750) had

    • gone to Sullivan, he would have had 143,594 (49.88%) and still lost by 708 votes. The race cannot be explained by minor-candidate diversion.

  • 对沙利文的优势：144,302 − 131,844 = 12,458 票，即 4.32 个百分点。
  • 投票率：投票 290,003 张，登记选民 605,433 人，47.90%。
  • 2,107 票差额：共投出 290,003 张选票，但仅 287,896 张含有效参议员选票，**2,107 张（占投票数

    • 0.73%）** 在该项上为耗尽票、空白票或重复圈选。汇总报告未细分，构成不明。

  • 同名反事实：若小丹尼尔·沙利文的 4,760 票全归现任议员，沙利文得 136,604 票（47.45%），

    • 佩尔托拉 144,302 票（50.12%），仍少 7,698 票。

  • 极限反事实：即使全部小党与写入票（4,760 + 6,105 + 885 = 11,750）都归沙利文，他也只有

    • 143,594 票（49.88%），仍少 708 票。本场选举无法用"小候选人分票"解释。

The primary, and the Leslie-to-Heikes substitution

Primary held 2026-08-18 under the top-four nonpartisan system. The results table lists sixteen candidates and 166,004 votes; Peltola led with 82,244 (49.5%), Sullivan followed with 68,726 (41.4%), Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. had 4,107 (2.5%) and Democrat David Leslie 1,850 (1.1%). Leslie placed fourth and would have advanced, but withdrew on 2026-09-01; under Alaska's procedure the fifth-place finisher, Republican Gerald Heikes (1,741, 1.1%), took the fourth general-election slot (SRC-0020, SRC-0006, SRC-0064).

Note the arithmetic asymmetry: the primary had 166,004 votes cast; the general had 290,003 ballots cast. Primary percentages above are as published to one decimal and do not sum to 100.0 because of rounding and the long tail of sub-1% candidates.

初选于 2026-08-18 举行，采用前四名无党派初选制。结果表列出 16 名候选人、共 166,004 票；佩尔托拉 82,244 票（49.5%）领先，沙利文 68,726 票（41.4%），小丹尼尔·沙利文 4,107 票（2.5%），民主党人 大卫·莱斯利 1,850 票（1.1%）。莱斯利列第四、本应晋级，但于 2026-09-01 退选；按阿拉斯加程序， 第五名共和党人杰拉尔德·海克斯（1,741 票，1.1%）取得大选第四个席位（SRC-0020、SRC-0006、 SRC-0064）。注意初选投票 166,004 张，大选投票 290,003 张，两者不可直接比较。

county_results.csv is header-only, deliberately

Alaska has no county-level election administration. The state is divided into boroughs and into 40 state house districts, and the Division of Elections reports legislative results by house district, not by county. The retrieved official summary (SRC-0001) is a statewide aggregate only; no machine-readable district-level table for this race was retrieved. Rather than map house districts onto invented "counties", county_results.csv carries the required header with zero rows, and this note records why. Closing it would require the division's district-level results file (typically published as a separate spreadsheet or through its results API).

阿拉斯加没有县级选举行政。全州划分为自治市镇与 40 个州众议院选区，选举委员会按众议院选区而非 "县"公布立法选举结果。已取得的官方汇总（SRC-0001）仅为全州合计；本次选举的可机读选区级表格未取得。 为避免把众议院选区臆造为"县"，county_results.csv 仅保留合同要求的表头、零数据行，原因记录于此。 补齐需取得选举委员会的选区级结果文件（通常为单独表格或经其结果 API 发布）。

One adjacent result worth flagging

The same summary report lists Ballot Measure 2 (SRC-0001): YES 126,919, NO 147,831 — 53.81% to 46.19%, a 7.62-point margin. The Wikipedia article on the race describes the measure as failing "by a six-point margin" (SRC-0006). The archive follows the official report; the discrepancy is recorded rather than reconciled, because the measure is outside this case's scope and only one official source was retrieved.

同一份汇总报告载有第 2 号公投（SRC-0001）：赞成 126,919、反对 147,831，即 53.81% 对 46.19%， 差距 7.62 个百分点。维基百科条目称该公投"以约六个百分点失败"（SRC-0006）。档案以官方报告为准， 并记录该差异而不强行调和——该公投不属本案例范围，且仅取得一个官方来源。

Status of the count on 2026-11-18

The summary is labelled UNOFFICIAL, and the race had been called by networks, not certified. The Trump administration's suit to block certification if Sullivan lost was rejected by a federal judge on 2026-11-01 and was on appeal (SRC-0038, SRC-0039). As of the archive's compile date, no certification document and no concession speech by Sullivan had been retrieved.

汇总报告标注为"非官方"，胜负由媒体判定而非认证。特朗普政府试图在沙利文落败时阻止认证的诉讼， 已于 2026-11-01 被联邦法官驳回，仍在 appeals 阶段（SRC-0038、SRC-0039）。截至档案编译日， 未取得认证文件，也未取得沙利文的认输演讲。
