Political positions / 政治立场

Sixteen positions are coded for Sullivan in 01_candidates/policy_positions.csv (SUL-POS-01 to SUL-POS-16). Each row states whether the evidence is a verbatim statement, a reported position, a legislative record, or an endorsement inference.

沙利文在 01_candidates/policy_positions.csv 中编码 16 条立场（SUL-POS-01 至 SUL-POS-16）。 每一行均标明证据类型：逐字表态、报道所述、立法记录，或由背书推断。

Energy and federal lands

PositionWhat is establishedEvidenceSource
ANWR and NPR-A (SUL-POS-01)Supports oil development in the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge and the National Petroleum Reserve–Alaska; warns Peltola would let environmentalists block ANWRVerbatim (reported)SRC-0045
2025 tax-and-spending vote (SUL-POS-14)Voted for the 2025 budget act; attacked by national groups over itLegislative recordSRC-0018

Sullivan's energy position is the spine of his case against Peltola: he argues she "consistently voted with Lower 48 liberal allies of hers against our interests" and that her election would hand environmental litigants the ability to shut ANWR down (SRC-0045). The phrase "Lower 48 liberal", or a variant, appeared sixteen times in one forum — Alaska Beacon counted (SRC-0045).

能源立场是他攻击佩尔托拉的骨干：他称她"一贯与下四十八州的自由派盟友一起投票、反对我们的利益"， 并称她当选将让环保诉讼者有能力关停 ANWR（SRC-0045）。"下四十八州自由派"或其变体在一场论坛中出现 十六次——由 Alaska Beacon 统计（SRC-0045）。

Fisheries and seafood policy

At Kodiak he made the most concrete policy claim of either candidate in the retrieved corpus: "My bill for establishment of the Seafood Policy Office is in the Farm Bill right now. That helps with regard to enabling fishermen to have access to all the programs that farmers have" (SRC-0036). He also promised to get "my bycatch bill over the goal line next week in the Commerce Committee" (SRC-0036). Both claims are his own statements, not independently verified legislative status.

在科迪亚克，他给出了语料中两位候选人最具体的政策主张："设立海产政策办公室的法案现在就在农业法案 里。这有助于让渔民获得农民所享有的一切项目"（SRC-0036）。他还承诺"下周让我的兼捕法案在商务委员会 过关"（SRC-0036）。两项均为其本人陈述，未独立核实立法进度。

Health care is where the coalition arithmetic is clearest: the same 2025 vote that Sullivan's business and energy endorsers praised is the vote SEIU 775 used to endorse his opponent (SRC-0042, SRC-0103). The archive records the vote as legislative record and the electoral use of it as an endorsement claim, without adjudicating the policy merits.

医疗议题最能体现联盟算术：沙利文的商业与能源背书者称赞的那次 2025 年投票，正是 SEIU 775 用来背书 其对手的投票（SRC-0042、SRC-0103）。档案把投票记为立法记录、把选举层面的使用记为背书方主张， 不评判政策本身。

Abortion

PositionWhat is establishedEvidenceSource
Abortion law (SUL-POS-07)Opposes abortion except to save the mother's life or in cases of rape or incest (2014 position); welcomed Dobbs; co-signed a 2025 letter urging tighter mifepristone rulesReported + recordSRC-0008

This is the best-documented social position in the file. The 2014 formulation is still the only detailed statement retrieved; the 2025 mifepristone letter shows continuity rather than a shift. No 2026 campaign statement on abortion was retrieved, and the archive does not claim one.

这是全档证据最完整的社会议题立场。2014 年的表述仍是唯一取得细节的声明；2025 年关于米非司酮的联名 信显示延续而非转向。未取得 2026 年竞选期间的堕胎表态，档案不作此声称。

Alaska Native affairs and ANCSA

PositionWhat is establishedEvidenceSource
ANCSA and Native corporations (SUL-POS-08)Endorsed by the ANCSA Regional Association and by a listed group of Alaska Native leaders; cites CARES Act relief and contracting accessEndorsementSRC-0104

Sullivan's Alaska Native support is corporate and institutional — the twelve land-based regional corporations' association, plus a leaders' list carried by Delta Discovery (SRC-0104, SRC-0069) — and it coexists with Sealaska's board endorsing both senators (SRC-0102). The archive does not present this as monolithic Alaska Native support for either candidate.

沙利文的阿拉斯加原住民支持是公司性与建制性的——十二家土地型区域公司协会，以及 Delta Discovery 刊载的领袖名单（SRC-0104、SRC-0069）——并与 Sealaska 董事会同时背书两位参议员并存（SRC-0102）。 档案不将此呈现为阿拉斯加原住民对任一候选人的一致支持。
