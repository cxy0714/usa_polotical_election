# 2026 U.S. Senate election in Alaska / 2026 年阿拉斯加州联邦参议员选举

## What this archive covers

This case documents the 2026 Alaska U.S. Senate race from the incumbent's March 2025 launch to the
17 November 2026 call. Republican Sen. Dan S. Sullivan sought a third term; Democrat Mary Peltola, a
former at-large U.S. representative, challenged him and won with an outright first-round majority.
The archive holds 124 sources, 30 campaign events, 31 policy positions, 12 archived speeches, 10
video-index entries, 12 images (6 downloaded portraits), 6 advertisements, 7 social/email items,
24 media-coverage records, 8 finance rows, 12 election-result rows and 8 polls.

## Five structural facts this archive establishes

1. **The ranked-choice machinery was never used.** Peltola's 50.12% first-round share made the
   scheduled 2026-11-18 elimination round moot; her margin over Sullivan was 4.32 points and 12,458
   votes (144,302 − 131,844). Every "second choice" claim in the campaign is therefore
   counterfactual, not tabulated.
2. **The same-name challenger did not decide the race.** Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. took 4,760 votes
   (1.65%). Even if every one of those ballots had gone to the incumbent, Sullivan would have had
   136,604 (47.45%) against Peltola's 144,302 (50.12%) — still a 7,698-vote loss, still short of 50%.
3. **The challenger out-raised the incumbent roughly two and a half to one.** NBC reported
   $37 million for Peltola against $14 million for Sullivan through mid-October (SRC-0056);
   Sullivan's own campaign reported $13.9 million in Q3 receipts and about $5 million cash on hand
   on 2026-10-16 (SRC-0024).
4. **Sullivan ran behind his party's House candidate; Peltola ran far ahead of hers.** In the same
   election, Republican House candidate Nick Begich III took 136,215 votes (47.54%) — 4,371 more
   than Sullivan — while independent House candidate Bill Hill took 119,689 (41.77%), 24,613 fewer
   than Peltola (SRC-0001). Peltola's vote was within 966 of the 145,268 drawn by the governor's-race
   leader, Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins (SRC-0001).
5. **The primary was crowded and deliberately distorted.** The primary results table lists sixteen
   Senate candidates; 166,004 votes were cast, Peltola took 49.5% and Sullivan 41.4% (SRC-0006,
   SRC-0064). Outside groups spent more than $2 million promoting fringe Republican Gerald Heikes,
   and about $180,000 each promoting Green Richard Grayson and Democrat Carol Hafner (SRC-0017,
   SRC-0041, SRC-0045).

1. **排序复选机制从未启用。** 佩尔托拉首轮得票 50.12%，使原定 2026-11-18 的淘汰轮失去意义；
   她对沙利文的优势为 4.32 个百分点、12,458 票（144,302 − 131,844）。因此竞选中的一切"第二选择"
   说法都只是反事实推演，而非实际计票结果。
2. **同名挑战者并未决定选举。** 小丹尼尔·沙利文获 4,760 票（1.65%）。即使这些票全部归现任议员，
   沙利文也只有 136,604 票（47.45%），对手 144,302 票（50.12%）——仍少 7,698 票，仍未过半。
3. **挑战者筹款约为现任议员的两倍半。** NBC 报道截至 10 月中旬佩尔托拉筹得 3,700 万美元，
   沙利文 1,400 万美元（SRC-0056）；沙利文竞选团队 2026-10-16 自报第三季度收入 1,390 万美元、
   手头现金约 500 万美元（SRC-0024）。
4. **沙利文得票低于本党众议员候选人，佩尔托拉远高于众议员候选人。** 同一场选举中，共和党众议员
   候选人尼克·贝吉奇获 136,215 票（47.54%），比沙利文多 4,371 票；独立众议员候选人比尔·希尔获
   119,689 票（41.77%），比佩尔托拉少 24,613 票（SRC-0001）。佩尔托拉与州长选战领先者
   乔纳森·克莱斯-汤姆金斯所得 145,268 票仅差 966 票（SRC-0001）。
5. **初选候选人拥挤且被人为扭曲。** 初选结果表列出 16 名参议员候选人，共 166,004 票，佩尔托拉 49.5%、
   沙利文 41.4%（SRC-0006、SRC-0064）。外部团体花费逾 200 万美元推举边缘共和党人海克斯，
   并各花约 18 万美元推举绿党人格雷森与民主党人哈夫纳（SRC-0017、SRC-0041、SRC-0045）。
