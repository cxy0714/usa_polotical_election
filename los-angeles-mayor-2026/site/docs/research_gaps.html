Research gaps / 研究缺口

A. Official records / 官方记录

G1. No official canvass for either round. The county registrar (results.lavote.gov, www.lavote.gov, content.lavote.gov) returned URLError on every attempt, and the city clerk's election pages publish candidate lists rather than vote totals. Consequence: the primary rests on the NBC News results page SRC-0021 and the runoff on the Wikipedia table SRC-0001, which is why the runoff rows are partially verified. Closing it: the Los Angeles County Registrar-Recorder's certified statement of votes, or the city clerk's official canvass PDF for the Nov. 3, 2026 election.

G1. 两轮选举均无官方计票文件。 县选务处（results.lavote.gov、www.lavote.gov、 content.lavote.gov）全部返回 URLError；市书记官选举页面只公布候选人名单而非票数。后果： 初选依赖 NBC News 结果页 SRC-0021，决选依赖维基百科表 SRC-0001，因此决选行标为 partially verified。弥补方式：洛县选务处认证计票声明，或市书记官 2026-11-03 官方计票 PDF。

G2. Two one-vote discrepancies are unresolved. NBC News gives Juanita Lopez 13,033 and Bryant Acosta 3,471; the Wikipedia table gives 13,032 and 3,470 (and a total of 853,892 instead of 853,894). Both tables are internally self-consistent. The archive records the NBC figures, flags both rows conflicting sources, and does not adjust anything. Closing it: the official canvass (see G1).

G2. 两处一票差异未解决。 NBC News 记 Juanita Lopez 13,033、Bryant Acosta 3,471；维基表记 13,032 与 3,470（总数为 853,892 而非 853,894）。两表内部各自自洽。档案采用 NBC 数字，两行标 conflicting sources，不作任何调整。弥补方式：官方计票（见 G1）。

G3. Write-in candidates are certified but uncounted here. The city clerk lists two certified write-ins (griselda diaz, misael ortega) whose votes do not appear in any retrieved results table, so the published total excludes them and the archive cannot state their share. Closing it: the canvass's write-in appendix.

G3. 补写候选人已获认证但票数缺失。 市书记官列出两名获认证的补写候选人（griselda diaz、 misael ortega），其票数不见于任何已获取结果表，故公布总数不含其票，档案也无法给出份额。 弥补方式：计票文件的补写附录。

G4. No turnout or registration denominator. No retrieved source published ballots cast, registered voters, or turnout rate for either round; the Wikipedia infobox carries volume = 0. The archive records turnout as unknown in election_context.md rather than computing a share from an assumed denominator.

G4. 缺少投票率与登记选民基数。 任何已获取来源都未公布两轮的投票数、登记选民数或投票率； 维基信息框为 volume = 0。档案在 election_context.md 中将投票率记为 unknown，不以假设基数 反推比例。

B. Geography / 地理分布

G5. No precinct, council-district or neighbourhood results. The L.A. Times precinct map is both metered and client-rendered SRC-0053; nothing else publishes sub-city totals. Consequence: county_results.csv is a header-only file — the race is citywide, so a county table would be a category error — and no district-level claim is made anywhere in the archive. What would close it: the registrar's precinct-level returns, which would allow the "Raman won renters' districts, Bass won homeowner districts" hypothesis to be tested instead of asserted.

G5. 缺少选区、市议会分区或社区级结果。 洛杉矶时报的选区地图既有付费墙又由前端渲染 SRC-0053， 其他来源不发布市域以下合计。后果：county_results.csv 仅为表头文件（该选举为全市范围，县级表 属范畴错误），档案中不作任何分区级论断。弥补方式：县选务处的选区级计票，可用以检验而非断言 "Raman 赢租客区、Bass 赢自住业主区"的假设。

C. Broadcast and transcript material / 广播与文字稿

G6. No debate transcripts. Five debates/forums have full video (three via YouTube uploads by the hosts, two via NBCLA), but none has a published transcript, and this build did not transcribe audio. Consequence: speech_archive.csv quotes only what written reporting quoted verbatim; several rows carry major_quotes = unknown. Closing it: manual transcription of VID-0001 … VID-0005, or the hosts' caption tracks.

G6. 辩论无文字稿。 五场辩论/论坛均有完整视频（三场由主办方上传 YouTube，两场由 NBCLA）， 但均无公开文字稿，本次构建也未转写音频。后果：speech_archive.csv 只引用书面报道的逐字引语， 数行 major_quotes 为 unknown。弥补方式：人工转写 VID-0001…VID-0005，或获取主办方字幕轨。

G7. The Jewish-community forum's substantive exchange is not transcribed. LAist hosts the full 1:39:47 audio SRC-0033 and describes a difference over Gaza, but the archive could not retrieve verbatim quotes, so RAM-POL-0016 is partially verified. Closing it: transcribing the audio.

G7. 犹太社区论坛的实质交锋未转写。 LAist 提供 1:39:47 完整音频 SRC-0033 并描述双方对加沙 问题的差异，但档案未能取得逐字引语，故 RAM-POL-0016 为 partially verified。弥补方式：转写音频。

G8. Television and digital ads have no transcripts and mostly no video files. Only Raman's Emmy-themed spot is available in full (its YouTube upload) SRC-0044; the Bass ICE spot, the police union's "Doesn't Get It" and the Streets For All spots are known from the L.A. Times newsletter's description, so main_visual_elements and narration_soundtrack are the newsletter's reporting rather than the archive's viewing. Closing it: the Ethics Commission's independent-expenditure filings, which attach ad scripts and buy schedules, or the stations' public files.

G8. 电视与数字广告无文字稿，多数也无视频文件。 只有 Raman 的艾美奖主题广告有完整视频 （其 YouTube 上传版）SRC-0044；Bass 的移民执法广告、警察工会的 "Doesn't Get It"、Streets For All 的广告均只见于洛杉矶时报通讯的描述，因此 main_visual_elements 与 narration_soundtrack 是 该通讯的报道而非档案的观看结果。弥补方式：伦理委员会的独立支出申报（附广告脚本与投放表）， 或电视台公共档案。

G9. Debate-stage visuals are unrecorded. Because this build could not view video or image content, visual_notes is unknown for every event and framing/camera_angle/clothing_notes are unknown for every image except where reporting states a detail (the mayor's aquamarine pantsuit, SRC-0044). Closing it: a build with image input, or the hosts' press photos with captions.

G9. 辩论现场视觉未被记录。 本次构建无法查看视频或图像内容，故所有活动的 visual_notes 为 unknown，所有图像的 framing/camera_angle/clothing_notes 为 unknown，仅在报道明确提及 细节时例外（市长的水蓝色套装，SRC-0044）。弥补方式：具备图像输入的构建，或主办方带说明的新闻照。

D. The styling question / 造型问题

G10. No reporting documents campaign styling, stylists, or a "styling team". The original brief asked about a "campaign styling team" and image-making. No retrieved source — L.A. Times, LAist, CalMatters, THR, Deadline, TheWrap, Our Weekly, FOX 11 — mentions a stylist, a glam team, a wardrobe consultant, or Raman's personal styling. The archive therefore records the documented substitutes (official portraits, ad wardrobe, production grammar) and states plainly that the styling claim is unsupported. Closing it: fashion/industry trade coverage (WWD, The Cut, THR's style desk), campaign press-kit credits, or Instagram credit tags — none of which was retrievable here (Instagram and X returned 403/login walls).

G10. 没有任何报道记录竞选造型、造型师或"造型团队"。 原始任务问及"竞选造型团队"与形象塑造。 所有已获取来源——洛杉矶时报、LAist、CalMatters、THR、Deadline、TheWrap、Our Weekly、FOX 11—— 均未提及造型师、妆造团队、服装顾问或 Raman 的个人造型。因此档案记录有据可查的替代项 （官方肖像、广告服装、影像语法），并明言造型说法缺乏支撑。弥补方式：时尚/行业媒体（WWD、The Cut、 THR 时尚版）、竞选新闻资料包署名，或 Instagram 署名标签——本环境均无法获取（Instagram 与 X 返回 403/登录墙）。

E. Candidate substance and the wider field / 候选人实质与更广泛的候选人阵容

G11. Bass's second-term agenda has no retrievable body text. karenbass.com/term-2-vision renders its eight issue sections as JavaScript tabs; only the headings and the page title were extracted SRC-0006. Consequence: BAS-POL-0011 and BAS-POL-0015 are heading-level records, not policy positions. Closing it: the same page with JS execution, or a PDF version of the agenda.

G11. Bass 的第二任期议程无正文可取。 karenbass.com/term-2-vision 的八个议题板块由 JavaScript 标签页渲染，仅提取到标题与页面题目 SRC-0006。后果：BAS-POL-0011 与 BAS-POL-0015 只是标题级记录， 不是政策立场。弥补方式：执行 JS 后抓取同一页面，或议程的 PDF 版本。

G12. The three non-front-running primary candidates are thin. Pratt, Miller and Huang appear in results, finance and two Commons images, but the archive holds no policy positions, no speeches and no events for them; the only Pratt-specific reporting retrieved is his post-primary video statement SRC-0026 and a Guardian piece SRC-0079. This is a deliberate scope choice (the case is a two-candidate comparison) but it limits any claim about why Pratt's 217,977 voters split as they did. Closing it: the Daily News candidate questionnaires (only Logsdon's was retrieved, SRC-0076) for all fourteen.

G12. 三位非领先初选候选人资料单薄。 Pratt、Miller、Huang 出现在结果、财务与两张 Commons 图片中， 但档案没有他们的政策立场、演讲或活动；关于 Pratt 的报道仅得其初选后视频声明 SRC-0026 与卫报一篇 SRC-0079。这是有意的范围选择（本案为两人对比），但限制了对 Pratt 的 217,977 票如何分流做出论断。 弥补方式：Daily News 对全部十四位候选人的问卷（本次仅取得 Logsdon 的，SRC-0076）。

G13. Several candidate-site sections were never captured. Raman's issue menu lists DWP, Nightlife, Street Vendors, Family & Childcare, Climate & Environment, Animal Welfare, Cannabis, Small Business, Emergency Preparedness and "Save Hollywood Jobs" SRC-0007 — none of those pages was fetched, so no position row covers them. Closing it: fetching the remaining issue pages.

G13. 若干候选人网站板块未被采集。 Raman 的议题菜单列出 DWP、夜生活、街头摊贩、家庭与托育、 气候与环境、动物福利、大麻、小企业、应急准备与"Save Hollywood Jobs" SRC-0007，这些页面均未抓取， 故无对应立场行。弥补方式：抓取其余议题页面。

F. Polling / 民调

G14. Four of eight runoff polls rest on the Wikipedia table alone. Impact Research (Oct 2–7), Tavern Research (Aug 20–25) and Tulchin (Aug) have no retrieved primary document; the Manhattan Institute's leaned figures were cross-checked against its report but the report's own table was not extracted. Those rows are partially verified. Closing it: the pollsters' own releases (Impact, Tavern and Tulchin are Democratic firms that rarely publish publicly) or the sponsors' memos.

G14. 八项决选民调中有四项仅依赖维基表。 Impact Research（10/2–7）、Tavern Research（8/20–25） 与 Tulchin（8 月）无已获取原始文件；Manhattan Institute 的含倾向值虽与其报告交叉核对，但报告表格 未被提取。这些行标 partially verified。弥补方式：民调机构自身发布（Impact、Tavern、Tulchin 为民主党机构，通常不公开发布）或委托方备忘录。

G15. One poll was fabricated and is excluded from the dataset. A widely shared runoff poll showing Bass far ahead was retracted by its publisher SRC-0047 SRC-0080 SRC-0081. polling.csv contains only polls that reconcile arithmetically with their own published components; the fabricated one is recorded here and in election_context.md, never as a row.

G15. 一项民调系伪造，未纳入数据集。 一份广为流传、显示 Bass 大幅领先的决选民调被其发布方撤回 SRC-0047 SRC-0080 SRC-0081。polling.csv 只收录与自身公布分项在算术上自洽的民调；该伪造 民调仅记录于本文与 election_context.md，不作为数据行。

G. Money / 资金

G16. No individual-versus-PAC breakdown, and no IE spending totals. The Ethics Commission's "totals by seat" table publishes receipts, matching funds, spending and cash on hand per candidate SRC-0003, not the contributor-type split; and it does not aggregate independent expenditures. Consequence: individual_contributions and pac_contributions are unknown in all twelve finance rows, and the five IE rows carry the contribution to the outside committee rather than its spending. Closing it: the commission's per-filer Form 460/465 schedules and its independent-expenditure disclosure list.

G16. 无个人/委员会捐款拆分，也无独立支出合计。 伦理委员会"按席位合计"表只公布每位候选人的 收入、匹配资金、支出与库存现金 SRC-0003，不含按捐款人类型的拆分，也不汇总独立支出。后果： 十二行财务数据的 individual_contributions 与 pac_contributions 均为 unknown，五行独立支出 记录的是对外部委员会的捐款而非其支出。弥补方式：委员会按申报人的 460/465 表及其独立支出披露清单。

G17. The Airbnb-linked $4 million is Wikipedia-only. FIN-0011 rests on the Wikipedia summary's "part of nearly $4 million" phrasing; LAist's donor story SRC-0034 covers the Raman-side committees in detail but not this transfer. Closing it: the committee's filings or LAist's follow-up reporting.

G17. 与 Airbnb 相关的 400 万美元仅见于维基百科。 FIN-0011 依据维基摘要"接近 400 万美元的一部分" 的表述；LAist 的捐款报道 SRC-0034 详述 Raman 一侧的委员会，但未涉及这笔转账。弥补方式： 该委员会申报文件或 LAist 后续报道。

H. Blocked and paywalled outlets / 被封锁与付费墙来源

G18. Ten outlets returned non-200 responses and are excluded as sources. NYT (URLError), Politico (403), Reuters (401), USA Today (402), AP article pages (403), L.A. Sentinel (403), The Hill (403), Beverly Press (403), KTLA (403), UCLA Luskin (403), YIMBY LA (404), Envirovoters (URLError), Working Families Party (403), and the Sierra Club Angeles Chapter endorsements page (200 but 212 bytes of content). Each is logged in _staging/fetch_log.csv with its status. Their facts are therefore not in this archive, which is why the NYT's reporting on Raman's entry is cited only as "not retrievable" in RAM-EVT-0002. Closing it: a proxy or library access, or an institutional subscription.

G18. 十家来源返回非 200，未作来源使用。 NYT（URLError）、Politico（403）、Reuters（401）、 USA Today（402）、AP 文章页（403）、L.A. Sentinel（403）、The Hill（403）、Beverly Press（403）、 KTLA（403）、UCLA Luskin（403）、YIMBY LA（404）、Envirovoters（URLError）、Working Families Party（403），以及 Sierra Club 洛杉矶分会背书页（200 但仅 212 字节内容）。逐条记录于 _staging/fetch_log.csv 及其状态。其事实因此不在本档案内——RAM-EVT-0002 中 NYT 关于 Raman 参选 的报道只能注为"不可获取"。弥补方式：代理/图书馆访问或机构订阅。

G19. L.A. Times is metered, so most of its 19 cited pages are headline-and-lede only. Each such row says so in reliability_notes; no fact is taken from a part of the page that did not arrive. Where a lede did arrive (the two poll stories, the ad-blitz newsletter, the Caruso story), the retrievable paragraph is quoted. Closing it: a subscription, or the paper's syndicated wire versions.

G19. 洛杉矶时报有付费墙，故其 19 个被引页面多为标题与导语。 每行均在 reliability_notes 中 注明；不从未能到达的页面部分取任何事实。若导语可用（两篇民调报道、广告通讯、Caruso 报道）， 则引用可获取段落。弥补方式：订阅或该报的通讯社转载版本。

I. Cross-cutting / 跨领域

G20. No concession speech or concession statement from Bass was located. The archive has Raman's victory remarks SRC-0064 and the outcome headline SRC-0036, but no Bass concession text, so no concession_speech row exists in campaign_events.csv. Closing it: the Nov. 4–5, 2026 coverage or the campaign's own video.

G20. 未找到 Bass 的承认败选演讲或声明。 档案有 Raman 的胜选讲话 SRC-0064 与结果标题 SRC-0036，但没有 Bass 的败选文本，故 campaign_events.csv 中无 concession_speech 行。 弥补方式：2026-11-04–05 的报道或竞选团队自有视频。

G21. No crowd sizes, fundraising-event totals, or volunteer counts beyond campaign claims. The only numbers of this kind are the campaign's own (2,000 volunteers, 80,000 doors, SRC-0014) and a columnist's "dozens of working men and women" at Sylmar SRC-0059. Closing it: pool reports, fire department crowd estimates, or the campaign's own event recaps.

G21. 除竞选方自述外，无人数、募款活动总额或志愿者数量。 此类数字仅有竞选方自述（2,000 名志愿者、 80,000 扇门，SRC-0014）与专栏作者对 Sylmar 现场"数十名工人男女"的描述 SRC-0059。弥补方式： 联合采访报道、消防部门人数估计或竞选方活动纪要。

Every CSV row carries SRC-####|https://...; unknown is a value, not a gap.

每个 CSV 行均带 SRC-####|https://...；unknown 是取值，不是缺失。
