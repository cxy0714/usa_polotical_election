What the sequence shows / 序列说明了什么

The arc is a front-runner's arc that narrows rather than widens. The first four months are institutional consolidation with no competitive events; the primary's competitive phase is two debates, one of which she skipped by withdrawing from a third. Between the primary and the runoff, her calendar is dominated by forums in which the two candidates appear separately — a format that protects an incumbent from direct contrast — and by endorsements from officials rather than from movements.

这条弧线是"领先者"的弧线，且越走越窄。前四个月是机构整合，没有竞争性活动；初选的竞争阶段只有两场 辩论，而第三场她以退出方式避开。初选到决选之间，她的日程以"两位候选人分开出场"的论坛为主——这种 形式保护现任者免于直接对照——以及来自官员而非运动的背书。

The absence / 缺席之处

There is no documented primary-night event, no election-eve rally, and no concession speech or statement in any retrieved source. campaign_events.csv therefore contains no watch_party or concession_speech row for Bass, and research_gaps.md records this as G20. The absence is itself informative — the incumbent's campaign published a record page and an agenda page but no closing narrative — but it is recorded as a gap, not interpreted as a choice.

任何已获取来源中都没有初选夜活动、选前集会或败选演讲/声明。因此 campaign_events.csv 中 Bass 无 watch_party 或 concession_speech 行，research_gaps.md 将其记为 G20。这一缺席本身有信息量—— 现任竞选发布了政绩页与议程页，却没有收尾叙事——但档案按缺口记录，不解释为策略选择。
