Bass vs. Raman / Bass 与 Raman

The asymmetry is the campaign's central fact: one candidate had held citywide office for three years and the other had never run outside a council district. Raman's own framing turned that into an argument — "a complete outsider — no political machine, no insider backing" SRC-0014 — while Bass's framing turned it into a qualification test: "Now is not the time to turn the city over to Nithya Raman" SRC-0062.

这种不对称是竞选的核心事实：一位候选人已担任全市性职务三年，另一位从未在单一市议会选区之外参选。 Raman 把这一点转成论点——"彻底的外来者——没有政治机器，没有内部背书" SRC-0014——而 Bass 把它 转成资格测试："现在不是把这座城市交给 Nithya Raman 的时候" SRC-0062。

2. Where they genuinely differ / 真正的分歧

Housing: target versus delivery. Raman's plan is quantitative — "triple annual housing construction" across all income levels (RAM-POL-0001) SRC-0007. Bass's is incremental — "nearly 40,000 units" already being accelerated, with permitting streamlined by executive order (BAS-POL-0001) SRC-0005. The disagreement is not about whether to build but about whether the current rate is a success or a failure, which is why the same 40,000 figure appears as achievement in one document and as insufficiency in the other.

住房：目标 vs 交付。 Raman 的方案是量化的——各收入层级"年度建设提高两倍"（RAM-POL-0001） SRC-0007。Bass 的是增量式的——"近 40,000 套"已在加速，许可经行政令简化（BAS-POL-0001） SRC-0005。分歧不在于是否建房，而在于当前速度算成功还是失败——这正是同一个 40,000 数字在一份 文件里是政绩、在另一份里是不足的原因。

Homelessness: programme economics versus a cap. Bass defends Inside Safe as the city's first comprehensive encampment strategy (BAS-POL-0004). Raman attacks its unit economics — "$85,000 a year for a motel room and services", 40% returning to the streets — and sets a measurable cap: tent and encampment sleeping down "at least 50% by the 2028 Olympics" (RAM-POL-0005, RAM-POL-0006) SRC-0008. This is the race's sharpest substantive difference: one candidate offers a programme's continuation, the other offers a target and a cost critique.

无家可归：项目经济学 vs 上限。 Bass 把 Inside Safe 辩护为全市首个综合营地战略（BAS-POL-0004）。 Raman 攻击其单位经济——"每间汽车旅馆房加服务年均 8.5 万美元"、40% 重返街头——并设定可测上限： 到 2028 年奥运会把睡在帐篷与营地的人数"至少减少 50%"（RAM-POL-0005、RAM-POL-0006）SRC-0008。 这是本场选举最锐的实质分歧：一位提供项目的延续，另一位提供目标与成本批评。

Public safety: growth versus redesign. Bass expanded hiring and created a civilian crisis-response office (BAS-POL-0005, BAS-POL-0006). Raman rejects "the binary choice of defunding the police or defending business as usual", points to $152 million in LAPD liability payouts and says the chief has "lost the confidence" of the city (RAM-POL-0007, RAM-POL-0008, RAM-POL-0009) SRC-0009 SRC-0060. The difference is real but narrower than the debate suggested: neither proposed cutting the department.

公共安全：扩张 vs 重构。 Bass 扩充招聘并设立民事危机响应办公室（BAS-POL-0005、BAS-POL-0006）。 Raman 拒绝"削警或维护现状的二元选择"，指出洛市警局 1.52 亿美元赔偿责任，并称警长已"失去这座城市的 信任"（RAM-POL-0007、RAM-POL-0008、RAM-POL-0009）SRC-0009 SRC-0060。分歧真实，但比辩论所 呈现的更窄：两人都未提议削减警局预算。

Budget and the fires. Raman names a fiscal cause — "City Hall made fiscal decisions that weakened our budget" (RAM-POL-0013) — and a concrete case, the Convention Center financing (RAM-POL-0014) SRC-0071. Bass lists "Budgeting & Fiscal Responsibility" as an agenda heading without retrievable content (BAS-POL-0011) SRC-0006. On the fires, Raman proposes a single accountable Recovery District (RAM-POL-0010); Bass names recovery as unfinished work (BAS-POL-0010). Here the difference is partly an artefact of disclosure: one campaign published detail, the other published headings.

预算与火灾。 Raman 指出财政成因——"市府做出的财政决定削弱了我们的预算"（RAM-POL-0013）——与 具体案例：会议中心融资（RAM-POL-0014）SRC-0071。Bass 把"预算与财政责任"列为议程标题，无可取正文 （BAS-POL-0011）SRC-0006。火灾方面，Raman 主张单一问责的"重建区"（RAM-POL-0010）；Bass 把重建 列为未完成工作（BAS-POL-0010）。此处差异部分是披露差异：一方发布了细节，另一方发布了标题。

3. Where they do not differ — and the collision / 并无分歧之处与归属冲突

Both want more housing; both oppose cooperation with federal immigration enforcement, with different vocabularies ("in ICE's face" versus "Immigrants In, ICE Out"); both support the county's transit measures; both claim the same Rent Stabilization Ordinance achievement. That last item is the archive's most instructive finding: BAS-POL-0002 and RAM-POL-0004 assert the identical "first time in 40 years" RSO improvement as each campaign's own SRC-0005 SRC-0014. Two campaigns cannot both have authored the same ordinance, but both can believe the credit is worth claiming — which tells us the runoff was, in part, a fight over ownership of a shared record rather than a choice between rival programmes.

两人都要多建住房；都反对配合联邦移民执法，但语汇不同（"直面 ICE" 对 "Immigrants In, ICE Out"）； 都支持县交通措施；且都声称同一项租金稳定条例政绩。最后一项是档案最具启发性的发现： BAS-POL-0002 与 RAM-POL-0004 都把同一"四十年来首次"的租金条例改进称为本方功劳 SRC-0005 SRC-0014。两个阵营不可能都起草了同一部条例，但都可能认为这项功劳值得认领——这说明决选在某种 程度上是对同一份共享政绩归属的争夺，而非两套纲领之间的选择。

4. Money and coalition / 资金与联盟

Bass out-raised Raman inside the committees ($3.25m to $1.02m in the primary period; $1.99m to $1.51m in the runoff period) and out-spent her by a wide margin in the primary ($4.51m to $2.36m) SRC-0003. Raman ended the runoff with more cash on hand ($1.38m to $0.63m) SRC-0003. Outside the committees, the pro-Raman side had about $7 million — two $3 million donors plus $1 million more from the same hedge-fund founder — while the police union put about $3 million into defeating her SRC-0034 SRC-0001.

Bass 在委员会内部募款多于 Raman（初选期 325 万对 102 万美元；决选期 199 万对 151 万美元），初选 支出也远超（451 万对 236 万美元）SRC-0003。Raman 决选结束时库存现金更多（138 万对 63 万美元） SRC-0003。委员会之外，支持 Raman 一方约有 700 万美元——两笔 300 万加同一对冲基金创始人的另外 100 万——而警察工会投入约 300 万美元反对她 SRC-0034 SRC-0001。

INTERPRETATION: the coalitional shape matters more than the totals. Bass's money and endorsements came from institutions (county government, statewide officials, most unions); Raman's came from movements, two rentier-adjacent industries (hotel workers, carpenters) and individual megadonors. That is a classic insurgent-versus-establishment configuration, and it is the configuration that won.

INTERPRETATION（解释）：联盟形状比总额更重要。Bass 的资金与背书来自机构（县政府、州级官员、多数 工会）；Raman 的来自运动、两个与租赁相关的行业（酒店工人、木匠）以及个人巨额捐款者。这是经典的 "造反者对建制"格局，而获胜的是后者。

5. Communication contrast / 传播对比

Bass communicated as the office: an official portrait, a record page, a coalition-listing launch speech, and a closing argument about risk SRC-0005 SRC-0023 SRC-0062. Raman communicated as an expediter: ten issue videos, constituent stories, a diagnostic register ("four years of decline"), and one high-production Emmy-parody ad narrated by Bob Odenkirk SRC-0044. The documented contrast is production grammar, not personal styling — no retrieved source describes a stylist or a wardrobe strategy for either candidate (03_speeches/communication_analysis_*.md; research_gaps.md G10).

Bass 以"职位"传播：官方肖像、政绩页、列举联盟的启动演讲、以及关于风险的收尾论证 SRC-0005 SRC-0023 SRC-0062。Raman 以"催办者"传播：十个议题视频、选民故事、诊断式语域（"四年的衰退"）， 以及一支由 Bob Odenkirk 配音、高制作的艾美奖戏仿广告 SRC-0044。有据可查的对比是影像语法， 而非个人造型——没有任何已获取来源描述两位候选人中任何一位的造型师或服装策略 （03_speeches/communication_analysis_*.md；research_gaps.md G10）。

6. What the outcome arithmetic permits us to say / 结果算术允许我们说什么

Raman's runoff vote was 2.48× her primary vote; Bass's was 1.73×. The runoff margin (110,305) is about half of Pratt's primary vote (217,977). Therefore the arithmetic is consistent with — but does not prove — a mechanism in which Pratt's elimination released a bloc larger than the margin, and in which the challenger's coalition grew faster than the incumbent's. Any stronger claim (that Pratt's voters went to Raman in a specific proportion) requires the precinct data the archive does not have (research_gaps.md G5).

Raman 的决选票数是初选的 2.48 倍；Bass 是 1.73 倍。决选差额（110,305）约为 Pratt 初选票数 （217,977）的一半。因此算术与以下机制相容——但不能证明——Pratt 的淘汰释放了一个大于差额的票块， 且挑战者的联盟增长快于现任者。任何更强的论断（例如 Pratt 的选民以某一具体比例流向 Raman）都需要 本档案没有的选区数据（research_gaps.md G5）。

INTERPRETATION: three things the documents support as plausible drivers, in the archive's own assessment — the fires' unfinished rebuild, the cost-of-living argument that Raman tied to eviction filings and rent burden, and the incumbent's defensive closing. Three things they do not support — that ideology shifted, that turnout collapsed, or that a single ad decided the race.

INTERPRETATION（解释）：按档案自身的评估，文件支持三项可能动因——火灾后未完成的重建、Raman 与 驱逐通知及租金负担相连的生活成本论证、以及现任者的防守性收尾。文件不支持三项——意识形态发生了 转移、投票率崩落、或某支广告决定了选举。

7. Unresolved / 未解决

Whether the RSO credit belongs to either campaign; whether Raman's housing targets are achievable at the permit volumes she cites; whether the RSO/eviction statistics are accurate; what Pratt's voters did; and what either candidate's campaign imagery actually looked like. The archive records these as open questions with named closing documents in research_gaps.md, not as gaps to be filled by inference.

租金条例功劳归属何方；Raman 的住房目标在她所引许可量下是否可达；租金/驱逐统计是否准确； Pratt 的选民流向何处；以及两位候选人的竞选影像实际是什么样子。档案把这些列为开放问题，并在 research_gaps.md 中指明可闭合它们的文件，而不是用推测填补。
