Event coverage gaps / 活动报道缺口

campaign_events.csv has 33 rows. They are not equally evidenced, and the difference matters: an event known from a full report, an event known from a headline, and an event known from a photo caption are three different epistemic objects. This file states which is which.

campaign_events.csv 共 33 行，证据强度并不相同，而这一差别很重要：由完整报道得知的活动、仅由标题 得知的活动、仅由图片说明得知的活动，是三种不同的认知对象。本文件说明每行属于哪一种。

1. Full reporting, quotes and venue / 完整报道、引语与场地（11 rows）

BAS-EVT-0002 (launch rally, SRC-0023); BAS-EVT-0011 / RAM-EVT-0008 (Aug. 19 debate, SRC-0032); BAS-EVT-0012 / RAM-EVT-0013 (South L.A. forum, SRC-0035); BAS-EVT-0013 / RAM-EVT-0014 (Jewish Federation forum, SRC-0033); BAS-EVT-0014 / RAM-EVT-0016 (Oct. 13 debate, SRC-0062); RAM-EVT-0007 (Sylmar carpenters, SRC-0059); RAM-EVT-0018 (victory night, SRC-0064). These carry verbatim quotes, stated venues and a date confirmed by the outlet.

BAS-EVT-0002、BAS-EVT-0011 / RAM-EVT-0008、BAS-EVT-0012 / RAM-EVT-0013、BAS-EVT-0013 / RAM-EVT-0014、 BAS-EVT-0014 / RAM-EVT-0016、RAM-EVT-0007、RAM-EVT-0018 属此类，均有逐字引语、明确场地与经媒体确认 的日期。

2. Headline-only, metered L.A. Times / 仅标题（洛杉矶时报付费墙）（4 rows）

BAS-EVT-0001 (filing, SRC-0037), BAS-EVT-0006 / RAM-EVT-0003 (May 5 debate, SRC-0040), BAS-EVT-0008 (canceled forum, SRC-0041), RAM-EVT-0009 (UNITE HERE, SRC-0048), RAM-EVT-0010 (DSA, SRC-0049), RAM-EVT-0017 (Horvath, SRC-0050). The headline states the fact; nothing beyond it is asserted. For the May 5 debate the venue and moderator come from the Wikipedia debate table, which is why the row itself is verified while its venue rests on a T3 source.

此类行的标题即事实本身，不越过标题作任何断言。5 月 5 日辩论的场地与主持来自维基百科辩论表， 故该行整体为 verified，但其 venue 依据的是 T3 来源。

3. Caption-only / 仅图片说明（1 row）

RAM-EVT-0005 (tenant-protections event, 2026-06-01, SRC-0057). The only evidence is an Associated Press photo caption: "Raman speaks during a campaign event about tenant protections with renters in Los Angeles". No venue, no crowd, no speech content; the row says so and is partially verified.

唯一证据是美联社图片说明："Raman 在洛杉矶一场关于租客保护的竞选活动上发言"。无场地、无人数、 无演讲内容；该行如此注明，为 partially verified。

4. Endorsement releases with no event / 无活动的背书发布（10 rows）

BAS-EVT-0003, 0004, 0005, 0009, 0010; RAM-EVT-0009, 0010, 0011, 0012, 0017. These are dated public endorsements, not rallies: event_status = announced, venue = unknown. They are in the events table because endorsements are the race's real coalition data, not because they looked like events.

这些是有日期的公开背书，不是集会：event_status = announced、venue = unknown。列入活动表是因为 背书才是这场选举真正的联盟数据，而不是因为它们像活动。

5. Format and access problems / 形式与获取问题

Date conflict. BAS-EVT-0012 / RAM-EVT-0013: the LAist body says "Saturday", the photo caption says Sept. 29 (a Tuesday). 2026-09-26 recorded; both rows conflicting sources SRC-0035.

日期冲突。 BAS-EVT-0012 / RAM-EVT-0013：LAist 正文称"星期六"，图片说明称 9 月 29 日（星期二）。 记录 2026-09-26；两行标 conflicting sources SRC-0035。

Attribution slippage. The line "You want to hear frustrated? I'm frustrated too" is quoted by LAist as said "at a recent campaign event", not necessarily at the Aug. 19 debate where the archive files it. The row's analyst_notes says so; SPE-BAS-0003 is partially verified SRC-0032.

归属滑动。 "你想听沮丧的话吗？我也沮丧"被 LAist 引为"在最近一场竞选活动"所说，未必是在档案 归入的 8 月 19 日辩论上。该行 analyst_notes 已注明；SPE-BAS-0003 为 partially verified SRC-0032。

Cancellation as an event. BAS-EVT-0008 is a debate that did not happen, kept because the cancellation is itself a campaign decision — Bass withdrew after committing, Pratt declined on scheduling, Raman withdrew on May 11 and the forum collapsed the same day SRC-0041 SRC-0078.

取消也是事件。 BAS-EVT-0008 是一场未发生的辩论，保留它是因为取消本身即竞选决策——Bass 在承诺后 退出、Pratt 因日程冲突婉拒、Raman 于 5 月 11 日退出，论坛当日取消 SRC-0041 SRC-0078。

Venue resting on Wikipedia. BAS-EVT-0007 / RAM-EVT-0004 (Skirball) and the Oct. 13 NBC4 venue (Universal City) come from the Wikipedia debate table SRC-0001; the NBCLA uploads confirm date and participants but not the address.

场地依据维基百科。 BAS-EVT-0007 / RAM-EVT-0004（Skirball）与 10 月 13 日 NBC4 场地（Universal City）来自维基辩论表 SRC-0001；NBCLA 上传视频确认日期与参与者，但不含地址。

6. What no source reports / 无来源报道的部分

No crowd size for any event. No fundraising totals per event. No press pool reports. No event-level security or protest activity. No primary-night watch party for either candidate. Those absences are listed as G20 and G21 in research_gaps.md; the events table does not fill them with estimates.

任何活动都无人数；无单场募款总额；无联合采访报道；无活动层面的安保或抗议信息；两位候选人都无初选夜 观票会。这些缺失在 research_gaps.md 中列为 G20 与 G21；活动表不以估算填补。

Every CSV row carries SRC-####|https://...; unknown is a value, not a gap.

每个 CSV 行均带 SRC-####|https://...；unknown 是取值，不是缺失。
