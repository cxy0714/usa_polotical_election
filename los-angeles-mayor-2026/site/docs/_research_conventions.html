Research conventions / 研究方法约定

1. Source citation / 来源引用

Every source_urls cell holds exactly one SRC-####|https://… pair. The validator's pattern admits a single pair per cell, so corroboration is never stacked inside the cell: it is described in the row's notes, reliability_notes or verification_status. 09_sources/sources.csv is the registry; the used_by column lists which datasets cite the source, computed after the tables were built.

每个 source_urls 单元格只含一个 SRC-####|https://… 对：校验器的正则只接受单对，因此交叉印证 不堆在单元格里，而写入该行的 notes、reliability_notes 或 verification_status。 09_sources/sources.csv 是登记表，used_by 列在表格建成后计算得出。

2. Joint debates are two rows / 联合辩论拆为两行

Five events were joint (both candidates on the same stage). Each gets one row per candidate (BAS-EVT-#### and RAM-EVT-####) with identical event_date, event_time and venue, and each row names its counterpart in analyst_notes ("Joint event; the Raman counterpart row is …"). This was chosen over a shared JNT-EVT-#### id because every downstream query filters by candidate_id, and a neutral id would have dropped out of both filters.

五场联合活动按候选人各建一行（BAS-EVT-#### 与 RAM-EVT-####），日期、时间、场地完全一致， 并在 analyst_notes 中互指对方行。之所以不用共享的 JNT-EVT-####，是因为所有下游查询都按 candidate_id 过滤，中立 id 会在两个过滤条件中同时消失。

3. unknown is a value, not a zero / unknown 是取值，不是零

unknown means "no retrieved source establishes this". It is never a stand-in for zero, for "no", or for a number that could be computed. Numeric columns carry unknown where the number was not published (e.g. debts_owed in every finance row, other_pct in candidate-only polls). Row-level absence of a measurable (crowd size, ad duration) is unknown even when the qualitative fact is known.

unknown 意为"没有已获取来源可以确立此项"，绝不是零、"否"或可计算数值的替代。数字列在未公布时 写 unknown（如全部财务行的 debts_owed、两选一民调中的 other_pct）；可测量值缺失（人数、广告 时长）即使定性事实明确，也记 unknown。

4. Date conventions / 日期约定

YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; unknown when only a month or a range is reported. Where a source gives only a month, the first of the month is recorded and the row says so in notes or description (e.g. Tulchin's "August 2026" poll → field_start 2026-08-01, field_end 2026-08-31). Two events are dated from conflicting text on a single page: the South L.A. forum body says "Saturday" while its photo caption says Sept. 29; 2026-09-26 is the Saturday consistent with the page's Oct. 3 publication date, so that date is used and both rows are conflicting sources.

统一使用 YYYY-MM-DD；只有月份或区间时写 unknown。若来源只给月份，则记该月 1 日，并在 notes/description 中说明（如 Tulchin "2026 年 8 月"民调 → field_start 2026-08-01、 field_end 2026-08-31）。有两处活动日期依据同一页面的矛盾文字：南洛杉矶论坛正文称"星期六"， 图片说明却称 9 月 29 日；与页面 10 月 3 日发布时间相符的星期六是 2026-09-26，故采用该日， 两行均标 conflicting sources。

5. period in campaign_finance / 财务表的 period

period is the reported-through date stamped on the filing aggregate, not a fiscal quarter or an election cycle. report_type carries the cycle and the committee type. Rows whose report_type contains independent_expenditure are not candidate-committee money: receipts there means money received by the outside committee, disbursements/cash_on_hand/debts_owed are unknown because the aggregate does not publish them, and the candidate named in candidate_id is the beneficiary (or, for independent_expenditure_against_candidate, the target).

period 是该申报汇总的截止日，不是财季或选举周期；周期与委员会类型写在 report_type 中。 report_type 含 independent_expenditure 的行不是候选人委员会资金：此时 receipts 指外部 委员会收到的款项，disbursements/cash_on_hand/debts_owed 因汇总未公布而记 unknown； candidate_id 所指为受益候选人（若为 …_against_candidate 则为被针对者）。

6. candidate_id outside the two principals / 两位主角之外的 candidate_id

case_meta.json defines BASS and RAMAN. Three further values appear deliberately: JOINT for shared video and shared coverage rows (used in speech_video_index.csv and media_coverage.csv), and PRATT, MILLER, HUANG for field-context rows in campaign_finance.csv and image_archive.csv. No filter in the archive assumes exactly two values.

case_meta.json 只定义 BASS 与 RAMAN。另外三个取值是有意引入的：JOINT 用于共享视频与共享 报道行（见 speech_video_index.csv、media_coverage.csv），PRATT、MILLER、HUANG 用于 campaign_finance.csv 与 image_archive.csv 中的"战场背景"行。档案中没有任何按"仅两个取值" 假设的过滤逻辑。

7. Poll fields / 民调字段

leader is the candidate ahead, or tie when the two published shares are equal; margin is the leader minus the second candidate and is therefore never negative. Published shares sometimes sum to 87–101 because of rounding, omitted undecideds, or two-candidate subsamples; the archive does not rescale. Each such row states its sum in notes, and where a pollster published both an initial and a "with leaners" line, notes records both and the table carries the leaned figure.

leader 为领先者，两位公布份额相等时为 tie；margin 为第一减第二，永不为负。公布份额有时合计 87–101，原因包括四舍五入、未列未决者、或两选一子样本；档案不做归一化。每行在 notes 中说明合计， 若民调机构同时公布初始值与"含倾向者"值，notes 两者皆记，表内取含倾向值。

8. Verification status rules / 核验状态规则

verified — an official record, or two independent retrieved sources agree, or the fact is a direct quotation present in the retrieved page. partially verified — one retrieved source only, or a paywalled page whose lede/headline was all that arrived, or Wikipedia-only. conflicting sources — two retrieved sources disagree on a specific figure or date (the disagreement is recorded in notes, never averaged). unverified — published by one campaign and not corroborated at all (kept, labelled).

verified——官方记录，或两个独立已获取来源一致，或为已获取页面中的直接引语。partially verified—— 仅一个已获取来源，或付费墙页面仅得标题/导语，或仅见维基百科。conflicting sources——两个已获取 来源在具体数字或日期上不一致（差异写入 notes，绝不取平均）。unverified——仅一方竞选团队发布、 完全无旁证（保留并标注）。

9. Interpretation labels / 解释标签

Any sentence that evaluates rather than reports — staging choices, what an ad "means", why an event was scheduled — begins with INTERPRETATION:. This applies in analyst_interpretation, observable_function stay descriptive, and in the Markdown analyses where the label appears inline. Descriptive columns (stated_purpose, observable_function, visual_notes) never carry inference.

任何评价性而非报道性的句子——活动编排、广告"意味着什么"、为何安排某场活动——一律以 INTERPRETATION: 开头。analyst_interpretation 如此，Markdown 分析中亦内联标注。 描述性列（stated_purpose、observable_function、visual_notes）不承载推断。

10. What is never done / 绝不做的事

No party label other than Nonpartisan. No invented venue, crowd size, quote, or number. No HTML in Markdown. No ranking or scoring of candidates. No image-content claim that was not in the reporting or the file description — this build could not view image pixels, so visual fields are unknown and say so explicitly. No use of a URL that returned non-200: blocked and paywalled pages appear in _staging/fetch_log.csv and in research_gaps.md, never as a source row.

除 Nonpartisan 外不使用任何党派标签；不虚构场地、人数、引语或数字；Markdown 中不使用 HTML； 不对候选人排名或打分；不作未见于报道或文件描述的图像内容判断——本次构建无法查看图像像素， 故视觉字段写 unknown 并明示原因；非 200 响应的 URL 一律不作来源行，只记入 _staging/fetch_log.csv 与 research_gaps.md。

11. Bilingual rule / 双语规则

Each ## section is an English block followed by its Chinese counterpart. English governs; the Chinese is a faithful rendering, not a separate argument. Proper nouns, dataset names and column names stay in their original form inside Chinese text.

每个 ## 小节先英文后中文。以英文为准，中文为忠实对译而非另一套论证。专有名词、数据集名与列名 在中文中保持原形。

Every CSV row carries SRC-####|https://...; unknown is a value, not a gap.

每个 CSV 行均带 SRC-####|https://...；unknown 是取值，不是缺失。
