What the sequence shows / 序列说明了什么

Raman's calendar is the mirror image of Bass's: it starts late, is dense with movement and labour endorsements rather than officials, and after the primary it adds two formats the incumbent avoided — a direct two-person debate and a sequence of separate-but-consecutive community forums. The endorsement ladder is ordered from insurgency to establishment: DSA and the hotel workers first, then a former controller, then a sitting controller, then a county supervisor two weeks before election day.

Raman 的日程是 Bass 的镜像：起步晚，密集的是运动与劳工背书而非官员背书；初选之后增加了现任者回避的 两种形式——直接两人辩论，以及一连串"分开但前后紧接"的社区论坛。背书阶梯由"造反"走向"建制"： 先是 DSA 与酒店工人，然后一位前主计长、在任主计长，最后是选举日前两周的一位县政委员。

The gap at the end / 结尾的空缺

The victory night is documented by a venue, a quotation and a photograph caption, but not by video, a transcript or a crowd count; SPE-RAM-0007 is therefore short and its policy_claims are unknown. The same absence affects the transition: no appointment announcements, no transition-team reporting.

胜选之夜有场地、引语与图片说明的记录，但没有视频、文字稿或人数；因此 SPE-RAM-0007 很短， 其 policy_claims 为 unknown。同样的缺失也影响过渡期：没有人事任命公告，也没有过渡团队的报道。
