# 2026 Los Angeles Mayoral Election / 2026 年洛杉矶市长选举

## 1. What this archive covers / 本档案覆盖什么

This is a single-race archive for the nonpartisan Los Angeles mayoral election of 2026: a top-two
primary on 2026-06-02 and a runoff on 2026-11-03 between incumbent Mayor Karen Bass and
City Councilmember Nithya Raman (District 4). Spencer Pratt finished third in the primary and was
eliminated. The archive holds 90 registered sources, 291 rows across 15 datasets, and the documents
listed in the folder map below. It was compiled on 2026-11-18; every source is accessed on that date.

本档案针对 2026 年洛杉矶市长选举（无党派市政选举）：2026-06-02 前两名初选、2026-11-03 决选，
由现任市长 Karen Bass 与第四区市议员 Nithya Raman 对决。Spencer Pratt 初选第三名被淘汰。
档案含 90 条登记来源、15 个数据集共 291 行，以及下列文件夹中的说明文档；编制日期 2026-11-18，
所有来源的访问日期均为该日。

## 3. Five structural facts this archive establishes / 本档案确立的五项结构性事实

**1. The runoff reversed a 5.3-point primary lead into a 9.8-point loss — a 15.1-point swing.**
Bass finished the primary 44,812 votes ahead of Raman; she finished the runoff 110,305 votes behind.
Raman's runoff vote was 2.48 times her primary vote (247,781 → 615,264) while Bass's was 1.73 times
hers (292,593 → 504,959). The runoff margin is roughly half of Pratt's primary vote (217,977), which
is the arithmetic reason his elimination, not a persuasion campaign, is the central mechanism.

**1. 决选把初选 5.3 个百分点的领先翻转为 9.8 个百分点的落败——摆动 15.1 个百分点。**
Bass 初选领先 44,812 票，决选落后 110,305 票。Raman 决选票数为初选的 2.48 倍（247,781 → 615,264），
Bass 为 1.73 倍（292,593 → 504,959）。决选差额约为 Pratt 初选票数（217,977）的一半，这是"淘汰而非说服"
成为核心机制的算术原因。

**2. This was an intra-party contest recorded as a nonpartisan one.** Every institutional cue that a
partisan archive would use — party registration, primary type, ballot label — is absent or
`Nonpartisan`, yet the decisive endorsements were factional: DSA-LA and UNITE HERE Local 11 for Raman
[SRC-0049] [SRC-0048], the county federation of labour and most unions for Bass, three progressive
council colleagues endorsing Bass against a council colleague [SRC-0042]. The archive therefore
records ideology through endorsements and issue positions, never through a party label.

**2. 这是一场被记录为无党派的党内竞争。** 党派登记、初选类型、选票标签等党派档案惯用的线索全部缺失
或为 `Nonpartisan`，但决定性背书是派系性的：DSA-LA 与 UNITE HERE Local 11 支持 Raman [SRC-0049]
[SRC-0048]，县劳工联合会及多数工会支持 Bass，三位进步派市议员背书 Bass 而非同僚 [SRC-0042]。
因此档案只通过背书与议题立场记录意识形态，绝不使用党派标签。

**3. Both campaigns claim the same 40-year-first rent ordinance.** Bass's record page claims
"the first improvements to the City's Rent Stabilization Ordinance in 40 years" [SRC-0005]; Raman's
biography claims credit for "revising the Rent Stabilization Ordinance to reduce rental increases for
the first time in 40 years" [SRC-0014]. This is not a policy disagreement but a fight over who owns
the same achievement — and it is recorded as `conflicting sources` in `policy_positions.csv`
(BAS-POL-0002 / RAM-POL-0004) rather than resolved.

**3. 两个阵营都把同一部"四十年来首次"租金条例算作自己的功劳。** Bass 政绩页称"四十年来首次改进
租金稳定条例" [SRC-0005]；Raman 传记页称其"四十年来首次修订租金稳定条例以降低涨幅" [SRC-0014]。
这不是政策分歧，而是对同一政绩归属的争夺；档案在 `policy_positions.csv` 中记为
`conflicting sources`（BAS-POL-0002 / RAM-POL-0004），不作裁决。

**4. Direct money favoured the incumbent; outside money did not.** Bass's committee raised
$3,252,743 in the primary period against Raman's $1,015,075, and $1,991,461 against $1,505,815 in the
runoff period [SRC-0003]. But with a $1,800 per-donor cap for mayor [SRC-0034], the decisive sums sat
outside the committees: about $7 million behind Raman (two $3 million donors plus $1 million more) and
about $3 million from the police union against her [SRC-0034] [SRC-0001]. The archive keeps candidate
committees and independent-expenditure committees in separate rows and states the 口径 in `notes`.

**4. 直接捐款有利于现任者，外部资金则不然。** Bass 委员会初选期募得 $3,252,743，Raman 为 $1,015,075；
决选期分别为 $1,991,461 与 $1,505,815 [SRC-0003]。但市长选举单人捐款上限为 $1,800 [SRC-0034]，
决定性金额在委员会之外：支持 Raman 的约 700 万美元（两笔 300 万加 100 万），以及警察工会针对她的
约 300 万美元 [SRC-0034] [SRC-0001]。档案把候选人委员会与独立支出委员会分行记录，并在 `notes`
中写明口径。

**5. The visual record documents incumbency, not glamour.** The only freely licensed images of the two
candidates are official portraits — Bass has a City of Los Angeles mayoral portrait dated to her
2022-12-12 swearing-in [SRC-0001]; Raman's is a council-office press-kit photo from 2022. The only
documented wardrobe detail anywhere in the runoff coverage is the mayor's "aquamarine pantsuit" in her
ICE spot [SRC-0044]. No retrieved source describes a stylist, a "styling team", or Raman's personal
styling. The archive therefore records the question as a gap rather than answering it with inference.

**5. 视觉记录展现的是"在位身份"而非"明星气质"。** 两位候选人唯一可自由使用的图像都是官方肖像——
Bass 有标注 2022-12-12 宣誓就职日的洛杉矶市官方市长肖像 [SRC-0001]，Raman 的是 2022 年市议会
新闻资料照。决选报道中唯一被记录的服装细节，是市长在移民执法广告中的"水蓝色套装" [SRC-0044]。
没有任何已获取来源提到造型师、"造型团队"或 Raman 的个人造型。因此档案把该问题记为研究缺口，
而不是用推测填补。

## 5. What is strong and what is thin / 强项与薄弱环节

Strong: the documented vote arithmetic (every candidate row sums to the published total; the two
one-vote discrepancies are recorded, not smoothed); the debate series (five dated, full-video-linked
joint events, each with one row per candidate); campaign self-description (four Bass pages, eight
Raman issue pages, quoted verbatim); the money structure with the committee/IE split; and the LAist
candidate guide as a neutral baseline [SRC-0030].

Weak: no precinct or council-district results (the L.A. Times map is metered and its tables are
client-side [SRC-0053]); no official canvass for the runoff (the county registrar returned a URLError);
no television-ad transcripts beyond what the L.A. Times newsletter describes [SRC-0044]; no reporting
on campaign styling; no crowd sizes, rally attendance counts, or fundraising-event totals; and almost
no verbatim quotes from the three non-front-running primary candidates.

强项：投票算术（每位候选人一行均可加总到公布总数，两处一票差异如实记录而非抹平）；辩论序列
（五场有日期、有完整视频链接的联合活动，每场按候选人各一行）；竞选自我描述（Bass 四个页面、
Raman 八个议题页面，逐字引用）；资金结构（委员会与独立支出分开）；以及作为中立基准的 LAist
候选人指南 [SRC-0030]。

薄弱：缺少选区/市议会分区结果（洛杉矶时报地图有付费墙且表格由前端渲染 [SRC-0053]）；决选无官方
计票文件（县选务处返回 URLError）；除洛杉矶时报通讯的描述外没有电视广告文字稿 [SRC-0044]；
没有关于竞选造型的报道；没有集会人数、造势规模或募款活动总额；三位非领先初选候选人几乎没有
逐字引语。
