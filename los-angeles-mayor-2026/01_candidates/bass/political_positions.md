# Karen Bass — positions / Karen Bass — 立场

All sixteen rows live in `../policy_positions.csv` as `BAS-POL-0001` … `BAS-POL-0016`. `issue` is
`Other` for municipal domains the contract's federal vocabulary does not cover; the substantive label is
in `subissue`. Below, positions are grouped and every claim is attributed.

十六行均见 `../policy_positions.csv`，编号 `BAS-POL-0001`…`BAS-POL-0016`。合同中的联邦议题词表不覆盖
市政领域，故 `issue` 多为 `Other`，实质标签在 `subissue`。以下按领域归组，每项主张均注明来源。

## 1. Housing and rent / 住房与租金

Her housing argument is production-plus-permitting: "nearly 40,000 units" being accelerated by new
policies, with Executive Order No. 1 — codified by the Council in December 2025 — permanently
streamlining affordable-housing approvals (BAS-POL-0001) [SRC-0005] [SRC-0023]. On renters she claims
"the first improvements to the City's Rent Stabilization Ordinance in 40 years to limit rent increases
and protect tenants" (BAS-POL-0002) [SRC-0005]. That claim is `conflicting sources` because Raman's
campaign claims the same 40-year-first revision for her own council work (RAM-POL-0004) [SRC-0014];
the archive records both and adjudicates neither.

她的住房论证是"生产＋许可"：新政策正在加速"近 40,000 套住房"，第 1 号行政令（2025 年 12 月由市议会
法典化）永久简化可负担住房审批（BAS-POL-0001）[SRC-0005] [SRC-0023]。租金方面，她声称"四十年来
首次改进租金稳定条例以限制涨幅、保护租客"（BAS-POL-0002）[SRC-0005]。该主张标为
`conflicting sources`，因为 Raman 阵营把同一"四十年首次"修订算作自己的市议会工作（RAM-POL-0004）
[SRC-0014]；档案两者皆记，不作裁决。

## 2. Homelessness / 无家可归问题

Three rows: the 17.5% street-homelessness decline and "first-ever back-to-back decreases"
(BAS-POL-0003); Inside Safe as the city's "first comprehensive strategy to move people inside from
encampments" (BAS-POL-0004); and the affordability frame that ties the issue to schools — "a city where
our kids no longer dodge tents on their walk to school" (BAS-POL-0014) [SRC-0005] [SRC-0004]. The 17.5%
is a campaign figure; the independent 2026 count (45,194 in the city, up from 43,695) measures a
different quantity and does not contradict it, but the archive does not reconcile the two [SRC-0059].
Raman's counter — an $85,000 average cost per Inside Safe motel room and a 40% return rate — is recorded
as the opponent's criticism on BAS-POL-0004 [SRC-0008].

三行：街头无家可归者下降 17.5% 与"史上首次连续两年下降"（BAS-POL-0003）；Inside Safe 作为全市
"首个把人们从营地安置到室内的综合战略"（BAS-POL-0004）；以及把该议题与学校相连的可负担性框架——
"一个我们的孩子上学路上不再躲避帐篷的城市"（BAS-POL-0014）[SRC-0005] [SRC-0004]。17.5% 是竞选
数字；独立的 2026 年统计（全市 45,194 人，上年 43,695 人）度量的是不同口径，并不与之矛盾，
但档案未将两者调和 [SRC-0059]。Raman 的反驳——Inside Safe 每间汽车旅馆房平均 8.5 万美元、
40% 的人重返街头——作为对手批评记录在 BAS-POL-0004 [SRC-0008]。

## 3. Public safety / 公共安全

Police hiring by executive action after "years of a shrinking LAPD" (BAS-POL-0005), the first-ever
Office of Community Safety for civilian crisis response (BAS-POL-0006), and homicide "to the lowest
levels since the 1960s" (BAS-POL-0007) [SRC-0005]. None of the three outcome figures was verified
against an LAPD or city dataset in this environment; all three rows are `partially verified`. Her debate
attack on Raman's public-safety credibility — that Raman would leave LAPD in "disarray" before the 2028
Olympics — is recorded under BAS-EVT-0014 [SRC-0062].

以行政手段扩充警力，结束"警队多年萎缩"（BAS-POL-0005）；设立首个社区安全办公室负责民事危机响应
（BAS-POL-0006）；凶杀案"降至 1960 年代以来最低"（BAS-POL-0007）[SRC-0005]。三项结果数字在本环境
均未对照洛市警局或市府数据集核实，三行皆 `partially verified`。她在辩论中对 Raman 公共安全资质的
攻击——称 Raman 会让警局在 2028 奥运会前陷入"混乱"——记录于 BAS-EVT-0014 [SRC-0062]。

## 4. Fire recovery / 火灾重建

Recovery from the January 2025 Palisades fire is named as one of the continuing challenges of her term
(BAS-POL-0010) [SRC-0023], and the city's permit-relief effort is reported separately [SRC-0052].
Raman's counter-claims — "nearly 17 months … still no plan", a chief recovery officer who left after 90
days with no replacement (RAM-POL-0010, RAM-POL-0011) — are recorded as opponent criticism; the
allegation that the after-action report was altered is kept as `unverified` (RAM-POL-0011) [SRC-0011].

她把 2025 年 1 月帕利塞兹大火的恢复列为任内持续挑战之一（BAS-POL-0010）[SRC-0023]，市府的许可纾困
工作另有报道 [SRC-0052]。Raman 的反主张——"近 17 个月……仍无重建计划"、首席恢复官 90 天后离职且
无人接替（RAM-POL-0010、RAM-POL-0011）——作为对手批评记录；关于事后报告被篡改的指控保留为
`unverified`（RAM-POL-0011）[SRC-0011]。

## 5. Budget, economy, immigration / 预算、经济、移民

"Budgeting & Fiscal Responsibility" is a heading on the second-term agenda with no retrievable body
text (BAS-POL-0011) [SRC-0006] — a real limit, not a rhetorical one. Economic development is concrete:
a Film Czar, fee cuts, an expanded film and TV tax credit (BAS-POL-0012), and a permanent al fresco
programme plus a new Convention Center (BAS-POL-0013) [SRC-0005] [SRC-0023]. On immigration she claims
to have been "in ICE's face on the streets" and to have organised "dozens of local mayors"
(BAS-POL-0009) [SRC-0005]. She also rules out another office: "I'm not running for another office after
this" (BAS-POL-0016) [SRC-0004].

"预算与财政责任"是第二任期议程上的标题，无正文可取（BAS-POL-0011）[SRC-0006]——这是实际限制而非
修辞问题。经济发展则具体：电影事务专员、费用削减、扩大影视税收抵免（BAS-POL-0012），以及永久
户外餐饮计划与新会议中心（BAS-POL-0013）[SRC-0005] [SRC-0023]。移民方面她称自己"在街头直面 ICE"、
组织"数十位地方市长"（BAS-POL-0009）[SRC-0005]。她并排除再选其他职位："此后我不再竞选其他职位"
（BAS-POL-0016）[SRC-0004]。
