# Karen Bass / Karen Bass

## 1. Who she is / 人物概述

Karen Bass is the 43rd Mayor of Los Angeles, sworn in on 2022-12-12, and the incumbent defeated in the
2026 runoff. Before City Hall she spent eighteen years in elected office in Sacramento and Washington:
the California State Assembly (2004–2010), including two years as Speaker — the first Black woman to
hold that post — and the U.S. House of Representatives (2011–2022), where she chaired the Congressional
Black Caucus in the 116th Congress [SRC-0001]. She filed paperwork for a 2026 reelection campaign on
2024-07-01, twenty-three months before the runoff [SRC-0037], and launched formally on 2025-12-13 at
Los Angeles Trade-Technical College [SRC-0023].

Karen Bass 是洛杉矶第 43 任市长，2022-12-12 宣誓就职，在 2026 年决选中落败。进入市府之前，她在
萨克拉门托与华盛顿从政十八年：加州众议院（2004–2010），其中两年任议长——首位担任该职的黑人女性；
美国众议院（2011–2022），在第 116 届国会任黑人党团主席 [SRC-0001]。她于 2024-07-01 递交 2026 年
竞选连任文件，距决选 23 个月 [SRC-0037]，并于 2025-12-13 在洛杉矶贸易技术学院举行正式启动集会
[SRC-0023]。

## 2. Record as mayor / 市长任期记录

The campaign's own summary is the densest available account and is treated as a campaign claim, not as
an audit: street homelessness down 17.5% with "the first-ever back-to-back decreases"; "nearly 40,000
units of housing" being accelerated; the "first improvements to the City's Rent Stabilization Ordinance
in 40 years"; a Film Czar plus fee cuts and an expanded film tax credit; a new Convention Center broken
ground; the first-ever Office of Community Safety; homicide "to the lowest levels since the 1960s"; and
the city's first Comprehensive Infrastructure Plan [SRC-0005]. Two of these are corroborated from
outside the campaign: the RSO claim collides with Raman's identical claim (see
`political_positions.md`), and the homelessness direction is compatible with — not proven by — the 2026
count of 45,194 unhoused people in the city, up from 43,695 [SRC-0059].

竞选团队的自我总结是目前最密集的叙述，按"竞选主张"而非审计对待：街头无家可归者下降 17.5%，为
"史上首次连续两年下降"；"近 40,000 套住房"正在加速；"四十年来首次改进租金稳定条例"；设电影事务
专员并削减费用、扩大影视税收抵免；新会议中心破土；首个社区安全办公室；凶杀案"降至 1960 年代以来
最低"；以及全市首个综合基础设施计划 [SRC-0005]。其中两项有竞选之外的材料：租金条例主张与 Raman
的相同主张冲突（见 `political_positions.md`）；无家可归者走向与 2026 年全市 45,194 人（上年
43,695 人）的统计相容，但后者并不能证明前者 [SRC-0059]。

## 3. The 2026 campaign / 2026 年竞选

She entered as the clear front-runner and finished the primary first with 292,593 votes (34.3%)
[SRC-0021]. Her coalition was institutional: four of five county supervisors [SRC-0075], the governor
[SRC-0074], Senator Padilla [SRC-0024], Vice-President Harris [SRC-0083], IATSE [SRC-0068], the musicians'
union [SRC-0016], La Opinión's editorial board [SRC-0082], and — most tellingly — three progressive
council colleagues who endorsed her over their own colleague Raman [SRC-0042]. She skipped the final
pre-primary forum, which collapsed when the leading candidates withdrew [SRC-0041] [SRC-0078].

她以明显领先者身份入场，初选第一，得 292,593 票（34.3%）[SRC-0021]。其联盟是机构性的：五名县政委员
中的四名 [SRC-0075]、州长 [SRC-0074]、Padilla 参议员 [SRC-0024]、Harris 副总统 [SRC-0083]、
IATSE [SRC-0068]、音乐家工会 [SRC-0016]、La Opinión 社论委员会 [SRC-0082]，以及最能说明问题的
三位进步派市议员——他们背书她而非同僚 Raman [SRC-0042]。她缺席初选前最后一场论坛，该论坛因领先
候选人退出而取消 [SRC-0041] [SRC-0078]。

Her launch speech set the frame she never abandoned: coalition management ("They say we can't come
together. Labor can't come together with business… But we all come together always") and resistance to
the federal government ("Who would have imagined that we would have to defend ourselves from our very
government?") [SRC-0023]. Her closing line in the final debate was defensive rather than forward:
"Now is not the time to turn the city over to Nithya Raman" [SRC-0062]. She lost the runoff with
504,959 votes (45.1%) [SRC-0001].

她的启动演讲确立了她始终未改的框架：联盟管理（"他们说我们无法团结。劳工无法与企业团结……但我们
始终团结在一起"）与对抗联邦政府（"谁能想到我们竟要防御自己的政府？"）[SRC-0023]。最后一场辩论的
收尾语是防守性的："现在不是把这座城市交给 Nithya Raman 的时候" [SRC-0062]。她以 504,959 票
（45.1%）输掉决选 [SRC-0001]。

## 4. What the archive does not establish / 档案未能确立的部分

Birth date, education, family detail and pre-2004 career are not verified in any retrieved source and are
omitted rather than inferred. The launch-rally wire copy calls 2025-12-13 "two days after the third
anniversary of her swearing-in", which would imply a Dec. 11 swearing-in; the official date 2022-12-12 is
recorded in `career_timeline.csv` and the discrepancy is flagged there. The "$1 billion budget deficit"
that frames her fiscal record is stated by a local column [SRC-0073] and echoed in the city controller's
endorsement coverage [SRC-0072], but no city budget document was retrieved.

出生日期、教育背景、家庭细节与 2004 年前经历在任何已获取来源中均未核实，故省略而非推测。启动集会
通讯称 2025-12-13 是"宣誓就职三周年后两天"，据此推算宣誓日为 12 月 11 日；`career_timeline.csv`
记录官方日期 2022-12-12 并在该行标注此差异。界定其财政记录的"10 亿美元预算缺口"由本地专栏提出
[SRC-0073]，并在市主计长背书报道中呼应 [SRC-0072]，但未取得任何市预算文件。
