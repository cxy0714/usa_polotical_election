# Nithya Raman / Nithya Raman

## 1. Who she is / 人物概述

Nithya Raman is the Los Angeles City Councilmember for District 4, first elected in November 2020 and
re-elected in 2022 [SRC-0014] [SRC-0030], and the mayor-elect of Los Angeles after the 2026 runoff. Her
public career began in homelessness mutual aid: she co-founded the SELAH Neighborhood Homeless Coalition
and organised neighbours to provide direct help, then concluded that volunteer effort could not match
the scale of the crisis [SRC-0014]. She describes her 2020 run as that of "a complete outsider — no
political machine, no insider backing", in which 2,000 volunteers knocked on more than 80,000 doors and
she defeated a sitting councilmember "for the first time in 17 years" — a result the L.A. Times called
"a political earthquake" [SRC-0014].

Nithya Raman 是洛杉矶市议会第四区议员，2020 年 11 月首次当选、2022 年连任 [SRC-0014] [SRC-0030]，
并在 2026 年决选后成为候任市长。她的公共生涯始于无家可归者互助：共同创办 SELAH 社区无家可归者
联盟，组织邻里提供直接帮助，随后认定志愿努力无法匹配危机规模 [SRC-0014]。她把自己 2020 年的
参选描述为"彻底的外来者——没有政治机器，没有内部背书"，2,000 名志愿者敲了 80,000 多扇门，
"17 年来首次"击败在任市议员——洛杉矶时报称之为"一场政治地震" [SRC-0014]。

## 2. Council record as she tells it / 她自述的市议会记录

Her campaign biography claims legislation to accelerate housing production through zoning and planning
reform, a permitting overhaul, and "the strongest set of renter protections in the entire country",
including a revision of the Rent Stabilization Ordinance that reduced rental increases "for the first
time in 40 years", a right-to-counsel programme and anti-displacement measures [SRC-0014]. The renters
page adds the numbers she uses in argument: 245,599 eviction notices received by the Housing Department
from 2023 through 2025, 93% for non-payment, average arrears under $4,000 [SRC-0010].

她的竞选传记称其推动了通过区划与规划改革加速住房生产的立法、许可流程改革，以及"全国最强的一整套
租客保护"，包括四十年来首次修订租金稳定条例以降低涨幅、租客咨询权计划与反逼迁措施 [SRC-0014]。
租客页面补充了她论证时使用的数字：2023 至 2025 年住房局收到 245,599 份驱逐通知，93% 为欠租，
平均欠款不足 4,000 美元 [SRC-0010]。

## 3. The 2026 campaign / 2026 年竞选

She entered on the last day before the filing deadline — "even for me, the decision felt unexpected —
but it also felt necessary" — after the January 2025 fires, the city's slow response and what she calls
a lack of accountability at City Hall [SRC-0013]. She finished the primary second with 247,781 votes
(29.0%) [SRC-0021], then won the runoff with 615,264 (54.9%) [SRC-0001]. Her coalition was movement plus
labour plus renters: DSA-LA [SRC-0049], UNITE HERE Local 11 [SRC-0048], the Carpenters [SRC-0059], the
Writers Guild of America West [SRC-0067], the city controller [SRC-0072], a former city controller
[SRC-0071] and, in the final fortnight, a county supervisor [SRC-0050].

她在提交期限最后一天入场——"即使对我而言，这个决定也出乎意料——但它同样显得必要"——时点在 2025 年
1 月大火、市府应对迟缓以及她所称的市府问责缺失之后 [SRC-0013]。初选第二，得 247,781 票（29.0%）
[SRC-0021]，随后以 615,264 票（54.9%）赢得决选 [SRC-0001]。她的联盟由运动、劳工与租客组成：
DSA-LA [SRC-0049]、UNITE HERE Local 11 [SRC-0048]、木匠工会 [SRC-0059]、美国西部编剧工会
[SRC-0067]、市主计长 [SRC-0072]、一位前主计长 [SRC-0071]，以及最后两周的一位县政委员 [SRC-0050]。

Her rhetoric is consistently administrative-moral rather than personal: "Mayors don't build housing" at a
carpenters' training facility [SRC-0059]; "four years of decline, of mismanagement, of excuses" in the
final debate [SRC-0062]; and, on election night at the Greek Theatre, "Are you ready for an L.A. that
loves you back?" [SRC-0064].

她的修辞一贯是"行政—道德"式而非个人化的：在木匠培训设施说"市长不建房子" [SRC-0059]；在最后一场
辩论说"四年的衰退、管理不善与借口" [SRC-0062]；在希腊剧场的选夜晚会说"你们准备好迎接一个会回爱
你们的洛杉矶了吗？" [SRC-0064]。
