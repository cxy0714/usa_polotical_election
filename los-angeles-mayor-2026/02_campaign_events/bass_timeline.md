## What the sequence shows / 序列说明了什么

The arc is a front-runner's arc that narrows rather than widens. The first four months are institutional
consolidation with no competitive events; the primary's competitive phase is two debates, one of which
she skipped by withdrawing from a third. Between the primary and the runoff, her calendar is dominated by
forums in which the two candidates appear separately — a format that protects an incumbent from direct
contrast — and by endorsements from officials rather than from movements.

这条弧线是"领先者"的弧线，且越走越窄。前四个月是机构整合，没有竞争性活动；初选的竞争阶段只有两场
辩论，而第三场她以退出方式避开。初选到决选之间，她的日程以"两位候选人分开出场"的论坛为主——这种
形式保护现任者免于直接对照——以及来自官员而非运动的背书。
