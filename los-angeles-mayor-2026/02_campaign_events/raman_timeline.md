## What the sequence shows / 序列说明了什么

Raman's calendar is the mirror image of Bass's: it starts late, is dense with movement and labour
endorsements rather than officials, and after the primary it adds two formats the incumbent avoided — a
direct two-person debate and a sequence of separate-but-consecutive community forums. The endorsement
ladder is ordered from insurgency to establishment: DSA and the hotel workers first, then a former
controller, then a sitting controller, then a county supervisor two weeks before election day.

Raman 的日程是 Bass 的镜像：起步晚，密集的是运动与劳工背书而非官员背书；初选之后增加了现任者回避的
两种形式——直接两人辩论，以及一连串"分开但前后紧接"的社区论坛。背书阶梯由"造反"走向"建制"：
先是 DSA 与酒店工人，然后一位前主计长、在任主计长，最后是选举日前两周的一位县政委员。
