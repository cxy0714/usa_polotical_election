Biography and the credibility each one sold

El-Sayed sold outsider expertise: a Rhodes Scholar, Oxford-trained epidemiologist and physician who had run two public health departments but never held office SRC-0002. Rogers sold insider competence: an FBI agent and Army officer who chaired the House Intelligence Committee and had spent fourteen years writing and voting on the laws he was now promising to change SRC-0003. The two biographies imply opposite theories of legislative effectiveness — one argues that the institution needs someone who has never been compromised by it, the other that it needs someone who knows how it works.

中文：赛义德推销的是局外人的专业能力：罗德学者、牛津培养的流行病学家与医生，管理过两个公共卫生部门，却从未担任公职 SRC-0002。罗杰斯推销的是局内人的胜任力：联邦调查局特工与陆军军官，曾任众议院情报委员会主席，用十四年时间参与撰写并表决他现在承诺要改变的法律 SRC-0003。两种生平隐含相反的立法效能理论——一方主张机构需要从未被其同化的人，另一方主张需要懂得其运作的人。

Coalition and geography

The coalition data is the most consequential comparison in the archive. El-Sayed won 30 of 83 counties but lost Wayne, Oakland and Macomb, the three counties that hold most of the Democratic base, and Stevens took Detroit with more than 60% SRC-0008. He nonetheless won the general election, becoming the first Democrat to win a Michigan statewide race without those three counties since 2016 SRC-0001. Rogers, by contrast, carried 53 counties and still lost by 7.08 points, which means his county advantage was concentrated in low-population areas SRC-0001. Five counties flipped Republican-to-Democratic between 2024 and 2026: Grand Traverse, Isabella, Kent, Leelanau and Muskegon SRC-0001.

中文：联盟与地理是本档案中最具意义的比较。赛义德赢得 83 个县中的 30 个，却输掉韦恩、奥克兰与马科姆三县——民主党票仓的主体，史蒂文斯在底特律得票逾 60% SRC-0008。他仍赢得大选，成为 2016 年以来首位在没有这三县的情况下赢得密歇根全州选举的民主党人 SRC-0001。相比之下，罗杰斯赢得 53 个县却仍以 7.08 个百分点落败，说明其县级优势集中在低人口地区 SRC-0001。2024 至 2026 年间有五个县由共和党转向民主党：大特拉弗斯、伊莎贝拉、肯特、利拉诺与马斯基根 SRC-0001。

Money

El-Sayed raised 2.3 times what Rogers raised through October 14, 2026 — $53,830,872 against $23,016,439 — but entered the final weeks with less cash on hand ($2,734,029 against $3,902,506), meaning he spent at a higher burn rate down the stretch SRC-0001. Outside money dwarfed both: about $62 million backed Stevens in the primary, of which AIPAC and its United Democracy Project supplied roughly $33 million SRC-0002 SRC-0034, and $135.7 million of outside spending was reported in the general election as of October 12 SRC-0073. The Detroit News' $243 million total makes this the most expensive Senate race in Michigan history, a figure that must never be summed with the FEC committee totals in 06_campaign_finance/campaign_finance.csv SRC-0073.

Two further money facts belong to the same accounting family and are not in the finance file. AIPAC's national spending target — Senator Bernie Sanders said in October that AIPAC planned $233 million against critics of the Israeli government and had spent $33 million in the Michigan primary — explains why the primary figure was treated as a national test rather than a local one SRC-0061. And a survey of the race's donor landscape found the outside money came from several directions, not AIPAC alone SRC-0052.

中文：截至 2026 年 10 月 14 日，赛义德筹得罗杰斯的 2.3 倍——53,830,872 美元对 23,016,439 美元——但进入最后几周时手头现金更少（2,734,029 美元对 3,902,506 美元），说明他在冲刺阶段的支出率更高 SRC-0001。外部资金使两者都相形见绌：初选中约 6200 万美元支持史蒂文斯，其中 AIPAC 及其“联合民主项目”提供约 3300 万美元 SRC-0002 SRC-0034；截至 10 月 12 日，大选的外部支出报告为 1.357 亿美元 SRC-0073。《底特律新闻》给出的 2.43 亿美元总额使本次选举成为密歇根州历史上最昂贵的参议院选举——该数字绝不可与 06_campaign_finance/campaign_finance.csv 中的 FEC 委员会总额相加 SRC-0073。

中文：另有两项财务事实属同一核算体系，但未列入财务文件。AIPAC 的全国性支出目标——参议员伯尼·桑德斯在 10 月称 AIPAC 计划投入 2.33 亿美元反对批评以色列政府的候选人，并已在密歇根初选投入 3300 万美元——解释了为何初选数字被当作全国性检验而非地方性事件 SRC-0061。此外，一项梳理本次选举捐款格局的报道发现，外部资金来自多个方向，并非仅来自 AIPAC SRC-0052。

联盟比较在双方各有一个有文献记载的失效模式。罗杰斯争取民主党人的努力被报道为试图“利用”围绕赛义德的分歧，而这正是其自身启动仪式所邀请的框架 SRC-0021；一年前他还被描述为共和党最有希望翻转的席位之一 SRC-0024；而史蒂文斯竞选团队在 2025 年 9 月不得不撤回一项虚假背书声明，这是初选事实之争将何等激烈的早期信号 SRC-0047。赛义德一侧则被报道为一场关于政党“相互竞争的未来的”之争，哈桑·皮克的参与被视为实质问题而非趣闻 SRC-0071；Roll Call 把密歇根与其他五大湖参议院选战并列为民主党的头痛 SRC-0069；史蒂文斯于 2 月推出国会黑人核心小组的背书 SRC-0074；结果出炉后，Newsweek 报道海莉·史蒂文斯加倍坚持其与赛义德的分裂，而 MAGA 阵营欢呼 SRC-0053。

Schedule and public activity

The events file records 18 El-Sayed rows and 14 Rogers rows, but the asymmetry is in kind rather than count. El-Sayed appeared in seven debates and forums, including one broadcast he had to himself after Rogers declined SRC-0049, and closed with a rally alongside Barack Obama SRC-0002. Rogers appeared in two joint debates, one policy forum, one surrogate rally and one barn party with a visiting senator SRC-0019 SRC-0020 SRC-0062 SRC-0048. Because neither campaign published a retrievable calendar, the comparison of schedules is a comparison of what was reported, not of what happened SRC-0082 SRC-0083.

What this comparison supports and what it does not

It supports three claims with numbers: that El-Sayed won with a coalition that excluded the Democratic base counties; that he out-raised Rogers while spending faster; and that outside money, not candidate money, was the largest financial fact of the race. It does not support any claim about persuasion — no exit poll, no vote-movement data and no ad-recipient data were retrieved, so why the 2024 Rogers voters split as they did remains unexplained in this archive.

中文：本比较以数字支撑三项判断：赛义德的胜选联盟排除了民主党票仓县；他筹款多于罗杰斯但支出更快；外部资金而非候选人资金是本次选举最大的财务事实。它不支持任何关于说服效果的判断——未取得出口民调、选民流动数据或广告触达数据，因此 2024 年支持罗杰斯的选民为何如此分流，在本档案中仍未得到解释。
