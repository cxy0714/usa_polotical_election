Overview

Michael J. Rogers (born June 2, 1963, in Livonia, Michigan) is a former FBI special agent and seven-term Republican congressman who lost the 2026 Michigan Senate election to Abdul El-Sayed, 45.46% to 52.54% SRC-0001 SRC-0003. He chaired the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence from 2011 to 2015, ran for the Senate once before in 2024 and lost by fewer than 20,000 votes, and was the Republican nominee again in 2026 after an uncontested primary SRC-0003.

中文：迈克尔·J·罗杰斯（1963 年 6 月 2 日生于密歇根州利沃尼亚）曾任联邦调查局特工与连任七届的共和党联邦众议员，2026 年以 45.46% 对 52.54% 败给阿卜杜勒·赛义德 SRC-0001 SRC-0003。他于 2011 至 2015 年出任众议院常设情报委员会主席，2024 年首次竞选参议员以不足 2 万票之差落败，2026 年在无对手的初选中再次成为共和党提名人 SRC-0003。

Education and pre-political career

He graduated from Adrian College in 1985 with a B.S. in criminal justice and sociology, served in the U.S. Army from 1985 to 1989 with the 7th Infantry Division, reaching the rank of first lieutenant, and was an FBI special agent in the Chicago office from 1989 to 1994, investigating organized crime SRC-0003.

中文：他 1985 年毕业于阿德里安学院，获刑事司法与社会学学士学位；1985 至 1989 年在美国陆军第 7 步兵师服役，军衔至中尉；1989 至 1994 年在联邦调查局芝加哥分局任特工，负责有组织犯罪调查 SRC-0003。

Michigan Senate and U.S. House (1995–2015)

He was elected to the Michigan Senate in 1994 and re-elected in 1998 with 68% of the vote, sponsoring the Michigan Education Savings Program. In 2000 he won an open U.S. House seat in Michigan's 8th district by 111 votes and took office on January 3, 2001. He served seven terms and did not seek an eighth. As Intelligence Committee chairman he introduced CISPA in 2011 and the Intelligence Authorization Acts for FY2014 and FY2015 SRC-0003.

His House voting record is the raw material for most of the policy contrasts in this archive: he voted to end key Affordable Care Act provisions, opposed extended ACA subsidies, voted to bar federal funding for Planned Parenthood, voted for the 2004 Federal Marriage Amendment, voted against repealing "don't ask, don't tell", voted to authorize the 2002 Iraq invasion, and held a 100% National Right to Life Committee rating alongside a 0% NARAL rating SRC-0003.

中文：他 1994 年当选密歇根州参议员，1998 年以 68% 得票连任，并推动设立密歇根教育储蓄计划。2000 年他以 111 票的优势赢得密歇根州第 8 选区的联邦众议员空缺席位，2001 年 1 月 3 日就职，连任七届后不再寻求第八届。任情报委员会主席期间，他于 2011 年提出 CISPA，并提出 2014 与 2015 财年情报授权法案 SRC-0003。

他的众议院投票记录构成本档案中大部分政策对比的素材：他投票取消《平价医疗法案》的关键条款、反对延长 ACA 补贴、投票禁止联邦资金资助计划生育协会、投票支持 2004 年联邦婚姻修正案、投票反对废除“不问不说”政策、投票授权 2002 年入侵伊拉克，并获得全国生命权委员会 100% 评分与 NARAL 0% 评分 SRC-0003。

Interlude: media, business and the 2024 race (2015–2024)

After leaving Congress he became a CNN national security commentator, launched the daily radio segment "Something to Think About", and hosted CNN's "Declassified" from 2016 to 2019. He also worked as a sales consultant for AT&T and held board and advisory roles, including at the cybersecurity firm IronNet SRC-0003. In August 2023 he announced a Senate campaign for 2024, won the Republican primary with more than 60%, and lost the general election to Elissa Slotkin by fewer than 20,000 votes SRC-0003.

中文：离开国会后，他成为 CNN 国家安全评论员，创办每日广播栏目《Something to Think About》，并于 2016 至 2019 年主持 CNN 的《Declassified》。他还曾担任 AT&T 的销售顾问，并在包括网络安全公司 IronNet 在内的机构担任董事与顾问 SRC-0003。2023 年 8 月他宣布参加 2024 年参议员选举，以逾 60% 得票赢得共和党初选，最终以不足 2 万票之差败给埃莉萨·斯洛特金 SRC-0003。

The 2026 campaign

Rogers announced his second Senate bid in April 2025 SRC-0003 SRC-0070, filed petitions on April 17, 2026 SRC-0005, and was unopposed on the primary ballot after the Board of State Canvassers disqualified Bernadette Smith for insufficient signatures; he received 883,328 votes (100.0%) on August 4 SRC-0001 SRC-0027. Donald Trump endorsed him in July 2025 SRC-0003.

His general-election strategy was built on three moves: an explicit pitch to Democrats who had voted against El-Sayed in the primary, launched on August 20 under the banner "Democrats for Mike Rogers" SRC-0063; a business-community endorsement slate in September SRC-0022; and a policy offer on affordability, including a proposal to pause Michigan's gas tax that a Republican legislative leader immediately rejected SRC-0013. On October 8 he said he would accept help from "anybody" who wanted to help his campaign, including AIPAC, reversing his August request that AIPAC stay out SRC-0019 SRC-0073. Haley Stevens, El-Sayed's primary opponent, endorsed Rogers on November 1, two days before the election SRC-0057 SRC-0044.

中文：罗杰斯于 2025 年 4 月宣布第二次竞选参议员 SRC-0003 SRC-0070，2026 年 4 月 17 日提交联署 SRC-0005；在州选举审查委员会因签名不足取消伯纳黛特·史密斯资格后，他在初选选票上无对手，8 月 4 日获得 883,328 票（100.0%）SRC-0001 SRC-0027。唐纳德·特朗普于 2025 年 7 月为其背书 SRC-0003。

他的大选策略建立在三步之上：8 月 20 日以“支持迈克·罗杰斯的民主党人”为旗号，直接向初选中未投赛义德的民主党选民招手 SRC-0063；9 月发布商界背书名单 SRC-0022；在生活成本议题上提出暂停密歇根州汽油税，但立即遭共和党议会领袖否决 SRC-0013。10 月 8 日他表示会接受“任何愿意帮忙的人”的协助，包括 AIPAC，这逆转了他 8 月要求 AIPAC 不要介入的立场 SRC-0019 SRC-0073。赛义德的初选对手海莉·史蒂文斯于 11 月 1 日、即选举前两天背书罗杰斯 SRC-0057 SRC-0044。

The result and after

The AP called the race for El-Sayed at 2:27 a.m. on November 4 with 85% counted SRC-0001. Rogers released a written statement thanking supporters; it did not explicitly concede, and sources differ on whether it amounted to a concession SRC-0003. It was his second statewide loss in two cycles.

中文：美联社于 11 月 4 日凌晨 2:27 在计票 85% 时判定赛义德胜选 SRC-0001。罗杰斯发表书面声明感谢支持者，但未明确承认败选，各方消息对其是否构成认输说法不一 SRC-0003。这是他两个选举周期内第二次全州性败选。
