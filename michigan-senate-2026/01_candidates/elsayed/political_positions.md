## Method and confidence

This document summarises the eighteen position rows held in `01_candidates/policy_positions.csv` (ELS-POS-0001 to ELS-POS-0018). Fourteen rows quote El-Sayed directly from a transcribed or verbatim-reported debate answer; four rest on encyclopedic or endorsement summaries and are marked `partially verified` in the CSV. El-Sayed has never held elective office, so there is no legislative voting record to check any of these positions against — the `legislative_record` column therefore records executive actions from the Detroit and Wayne County health departments where they exist [SRC-0002].

## Health care

El-Sayed supports Medicare for All understood as a government-run insurer that replaces private coverage, described in his own words as "guaranteeing everybody health insurance without a deductible, a premium, or a co-pay through Medicare for All" (WOOD-TV8 primary debate, July 7, 2026) [SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0001]. He co-authored *Medicare for All: A Citizen's Guide* (Oxford University Press, 2021) [SRC-0002]. Rogers attacks the position as ending private insurance and raising taxes; at the October 8 debate he said "We can't afford Abdul" [SRC-0019]. No independent cost estimate of El-Sayed's financing plan was retrieved, so the archive records the commitment without validating its arithmetic.

His healthcare credibility rests on the Wayne County medical-debt programme rather than on legislation, and that programme is the clearest case in this archive of a campaign claim outpacing the record: the campaign described the programme as erasing $700 million in debt, while Bridge Michigan found about $95 million erased for more than 100,000 residents as of September 2026 [SRC-0011] [ELS-POS-0002].

中文：赛义德支持“全民医保”，即由政府运营、取代私营保险的保险体系；他本人的说法是“通过全民医保让每个人获得没有免赔额、没有保费、没有共付额的医疗保险”（WOOD-TV8 初选辩论，2026 年 7 月 7 日）[SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0001]。他与他人合著《Medicare for All: A Citizen's Guide》（牛津大学出版社，2021 年）[SRC-0002]。罗杰斯攻击该主张将终结私营保险并加税，在 10 月 8 日的辩论中说“我们养不起阿卜杜勒”[SRC-0019]。本档案未取得对该融资方案的独立成本估算，因此只记录承诺，不为其算术背书。

他在医疗议题上的可信度更多来自韦恩县医疗债务项目而非立法，而该项目也是本档案中竞选说法超出实际记录的最清晰案例：竞选方称该项目注销了 7 亿美元债务，而 Bridge Michigan 查证截至 2026 年 9 月实际注销约 9500 万美元、惠及逾 10 万人 [SRC-0011] [ELS-POS-0002]。

## Immigration and policing

He calls for abolishing ICE rather than reforming it: "You can't reform ICE, you can't retrain ICE, you have to abolish ICE", adding that "ICE is about normalizing paramilitary force on our streets" (July 7, 2026) [SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0003]. In the same answer he insisted that abolishing the agency does not mean abandoning border enforcement: "that's not to say that we can't secure our border, that's not even to say that we can't actually enforce our immigration policy" [SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0004]. Stevens argued for reforming ICE and holding agents accountable instead.

The policing issue is the one position in the archive with documented change over time. In 2020 he said on radio that "we do need to defund the police insofar as defunding the police is disinvesting in the means of incarcerating someone or killing them on the streets and investing more in the means of educating and empowering, engaging communities"; CNN's KFILE recovered the recording and published it on July 7, 2026, alongside his later claim that he "never, never called for defunding" [SRC-0031] [ELS-POS-0005]. His 2026 platform funds police recruitment, retention and retirement. FactCheck.org found that some Republican advertisements exaggerated his record, including a claim that he wanted to abolish prisons [SRC-0002].

中文：他主张废除而非改革移民与海关执法局（ICE）：“你无法改革 ICE，无法重新培训 ICE，必须废除 ICE”，并称“ICE 的实质是把准军事暴力正常化”（2026 年 7 月 7 日）[SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0003]。在同一回答中他强调，废除该机构并不意味着放弃边境执法：“这并不是说我们不能守住边境，甚至不是说我们不能真正执行移民政策”[SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0004]。史蒂文斯则主张改革 ICE 并追究执法人员责任。

警务议题是本档案中唯一有文献记录立场变化的议题。2020 年他在广播中说“我们确实需要削减警察经费，前提是削减警察经费意味着不再把钱花在监禁或街头杀人的手段上，而是更多投入教育、赋权和社区参与”；CNN 的 KFILE 找到该录音并于 2026 年 7 月 7 日刊出，同时刊出他后来“从未、从未呼吁削减警察经费”的说法 [SRC-0031] [ELS-POS-0005]。他 2026 年的政纲则为警察招募、留任与退休提供资金。FactCheck.org 认为部分共和党广告夸大了他的记录，包括称其想废除监狱 [SRC-0002]。

## Foreign policy: Israel, Gaza and Iran

El-Sayed calls for ending unconditional U.S. military aid to Israel and describes Israel's conduct in Gaza as genocide, while recognising Israel's right to exist and supporting self-determination for both Palestinians and Jewish Israelis [ELS-POS-0006] [ELS-POS-0007]. On AIPAC he said: "AIPAC has spent tens of millions of dollars in attack ads against me... They clearly want one individual, and it's not me", and "I don't take that money" (July 7, 2026) [SRC-0015]. He argued that AIPAC "is perfectly fine with either of my two opponents", and that the 2026 Iran war was fought because of "the impact of AIPAC in our politics" [SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0008]. On Iran he said the threat "had been contained" by the Obama-era nuclear deal and that the war should not have been fought [SRC-0036].

The Gaza position carries the archive's main rhetorical risk for him. A video statement after the August 2026 Temple Israel attack included the phrase "Hurt people hurt people", for which he apologised, saying "I should have just issued a statement, of course condemning it, and it should have been the full stop" [SRC-0002]. AIPAC-linked United Democracy Project nonetheless built a direct-mail piece around the remark in October, spending $94,620 to reach 130,000 Jewish voters [SRC-0073] [EXT-AD-0002].

中文：赛义德主张终止对以色列的无条件军事援助，并将以色列在加沙的行动称为种族灭绝，同时承认以色列的生存权，并支持巴勒斯坦人与犹太以色列人双方的自决权 [ELS-POS-0006] [ELS-POS-0007]。关于 AIPAC，他说：“AIPAC 花了数千万美元投放攻击我的广告……他们显然只想要某一个人，而那个人不是我”，并表示“我不拿那笔钱”（2026 年 7 月 7 日）[SRC-0015]。他认为 AIPAC“对我的两位对手中的任何一位都完全满意”，并称 2026 年伊朗战争之所以发生，是因为“AIPAC 在我国政治中的影响力”[SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0008]。关于伊朗，他表示其威胁“已经被”奥巴马时期的核协议“遏制”，这场战争本不该打 [SRC-0036]。

加沙立场给他带来本档案中最大的修辞风险。2026 年 8 月圣殿以色列会堂袭击事件后，他的一段视频声明中出现“受伤的人会伤害别人”一语，他随后道歉，称“我本应只发表一份声明，当然要谴责它，而且那应该是句号”[SRC-0002]。即便如此，与 AIPAC 关联的“联合民主项目”仍在 10 月围绕这句话制作直邮，花费 94,620 美元触达 13 万名犹太选民 [SRC-0073] [EXT-AD-0002]。

## Economy, trade and industrial policy

On tariffs he rejects the Trump administration's execution rather than the instrument: "Like all the things that Donald Trump does, they are chaotic, they are ham-fisted, and they are self-serving", and "this version has been terrible for the American auto industry. It has been terrible for workers" (Detroit Economic Club, October 20, 2026) [SRC-0020] [ELS-POS-0009]. His industrial offer is a subsidised $25,000, 300-mile-range EV built in the United States over seven years, with tariffs used to protect the domestic EV market while it scales — the same debate in which he used the chemotherapy metaphor, "you don't give all the chemo all at once, all at the same time" [SRC-0020] [SRC-0036].

On domestic economic policy he proposes antitrust enforcement ("we need to stand up to the monopolies and oligopolies that are picking our pockets"), elimination of corporate tax breaks, an 8% tax on billionaire wealth that the campaign estimates would raise $4.6 trillion, and fully free childcare — "I think we need to make childcare free, 100% free" [SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0010] [ELS-POS-0011] [ELS-POS-0012]. None of these figures has independent scoring in the retrieved record, and the $4.6 trillion is the campaign's own estimate.

中文：关于关税，他反对的是特朗普政府的执行方式而非工具本身：“就像唐纳德·特朗普做的所有事情一样，它们混乱、笨拙且自私自利”，且“这一版本对美国汽车工业极为不利，对工人极为不利”（底特律经济俱乐部，2026 年 10 月 20 日）[SRC-0020] [ELS-POS-0009]。他的产业方案是在七年内于美国本土造出售价 2.5 万美元、续航 300 英里的电动车，并在其规模化期间用关税保护国内电动车市场——同一场辩论中他使用了化疗的比喻：“你不能一次性把所有的化疗都用上”[SRC-0020] [SRC-0036]。

在国内经济政策上，他主张反垄断执法（“我们必须对抗那些掏空我们钱包的垄断与寡头”）、取消企业税收优惠、对十亿富翁财富征收 8% 的税（竞选方估计可筹 4.6 万亿美元），以及全面免费托育——“我认为我们需要让托育免费，100% 免费”[SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0010] [ELS-POS-0011] [ELS-POS-0012]。这些数字在已取得的材料中均无独立评分，4.6 万亿美元系竞选方自估。

## Democracy, money and the primary argument

His closing argument was procedural as much as programmatic: "all of this has to happen by way of getting the corruption out of our politics" [SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0015]. He refused PAC and corporate money, and the refusal became the emotional core of the primary against a candidate backed by $62 million in outside spending [SRC-0002] [SRC-0010]. He also released his financial disclosure before the July 27 debate, reporting a net worth of up to $2.1 million, after weeks of pressure [SRC-0016].

中文：他的收官论述既是政策性的，也是程序性的：“这一切都必须通过清除政治中的腐败来实现”[SRC-0015] [ELS-POS-0015]。他拒收 PAC 与企业资金，而这一拒收在初选中成为情感核心——对手背后有 6200 万美元外部支出 [SRC-0002] [SRC-0010]。在数周压力之后，他还在 7 月 27 日辩论前公布了财务披露，净资产最高约 210 万美元 [SRC-0016]。

## Energy, abortion, religion and technology

On energy he supports 100% renewable energy in Michigan by 2030, tougher environmental enforcement, and merging the Department of Natural Resources with the Department of Environmental Quality into a Department of the Environment — a platform carried over from 2018 rather than restated in 2026, and therefore `partially verified` [ELS-POS-0014] [SRC-0002]. On abortion he is pro-choice and was endorsed in the general election by Planned Parenthood Action Fund and Reproductive Freedom for All, but no statement in his own words during this race was retrieved [ELS-POS-0016] [SRC-0001]. He defends First Amendment religious freedom for all Americans [ELS-POS-0017] [SRC-0002]. On technology he released a June 2026 AI plan calling for more regulation, opposed approving new data centres until federal safeguards exist, and demanded guarantees that data-centre costs are not passed to ratepayers [ELS-POS-0018] [SRC-0018].

中文：能源方面，他支持密歇根州在 2030 年前实现 100% 可再生能源、加强环境执法，并将自然资源部与环境质量部合并为环境部——这一政纲延续自 2018 年而非 2026 年重申，故标为 `partially verified` [ELS-POS-0014] [SRC-0002]。堕胎方面他支持选择权，大选中获计划生育行动基金与“生育自由为全体”背书，但本档案未取得他在本次竞选中就此的原话 [ELS-POS-0016] [SRC-0001]。他捍卫所有美国人的第一修正案宗教自由 [ELS-POS-0017] [SRC-0002]。技术方面，他于 2026 年 6 月发布人工智能计划，主张加强监管，反对在联邦保障措施到位前批准新建数据中心，并要求保证数据中心成本不转嫁给电费用户 [ELS-POS-0018] [SRC-0018]。

## Where the record is thin

Three gaps matter for future comparison. First, there is no legislative record at all, so positions cannot be checked against votes. Second, abortion and gun policy rest entirely on endorsement lists, with no first-person statement retrieved. Third, the financing arithmetic behind Medicare for All, free childcare and the wealth tax is campaign-supplied throughout, and no independent scoring appears in any retrieved source.

中文：三处缺口对后续比较研究很重要。第一，他完全没有立法记录，因此立场无法与投票核对。第二，堕胎与枪支政策完全依赖背书名单，未取得本人表述。第三，全民医保、免费托育与财富税背后的融资算术全部来自竞选方，已取得的材料中没有任何独立评分。
