## Overview

Abdulrahman Mohamed El-Sayed (born October 31, 1984, in Rochester Hills, Michigan) is a physician and epidemiologist who was elected to the U.S. Senate in Michigan on November 3, 2026, defeating Republican Mike Rogers 52.54% to 45.46% [SRC-0001]. He had never held elective office before this election. His career before politics ran through public health: a doctorate at Oxford, a medical degree at Columbia, a professorship in epidemiology, and then the Detroit and Wayne County health departments [SRC-0002].

中文：阿卜杜勒·拉赫曼·穆罕默德·赛义德（1984 年 10 月 31 日生于密歇根州罗切斯特山）是一名医生与流行病学家，2026 年 11 月 3 日以 52.54% 对 45.46% 击败共和党人迈克·罗杰斯，当选密歇根州联邦参议员 [SRC-0001]。此次当选之前他从未担任过民选公职。从政之前的职业生涯集中在公共卫生领域：牛津大学博士、哥伦比亚大学医学博士、流行病学教授，以及底特律市与韦恩县卫生部门 [SRC-0002]。

## Family, education and early formation

He is the son of Egyptian immigrants; his father was a civil engineer and his mother a nurse who worked in a Missouri hospital, a detail El-Sayed used in the October 21, 2026 debate when rejecting a claim about an organisation he had supposedly addressed [SRC-0036]. He graduated from Bloomfield Hills Andover High School in 2003, where he captained the wrestling, football and lacrosse teams, then took a B.S. in biology and political science at the University of Michigan in 2007, graduating Phi Beta Kappa with highest distinction and giving the student commencement address. He went to Oxford as a Rhodes Scholar, completing a DPhil in public health at Oriel College in 2011 with a thesis on inequalities in obesity in England, and then took an M.D. at Columbia University College of Physicians and Surgeons in 2014, supported by a Paul & Daisy Soros Fellowship and the Medical Scientist Training Program [SRC-0002].

中文：他是埃及移民之子，父亲是土木工程师，母亲是曾在密苏里州一家医院工作的护士——赛义德在 2026 年 10 月 21 日的辩论中正是用这一细节回击了关于他曾为某组织站台的指控 [SRC-0036]。他 2003 年毕业于布卢姆菲尔德山安多弗高中，曾是摔跤、橄榄球和长曲棍球队队长；2007 年在密歇根大学获得生物学与政治学学士学位，以最优等成绩毕业并获 Phi Beta Kappa，并在毕业典礼上代表学生致辞。随后他以罗德学者身份赴牛津大学奥里尔学院攻读公共卫生博士，2011 年完成关于英格兰肥胖不平等的论文；2014 年在哥伦比亚大学内外科医学院获得医学博士学位，期间获得 Paul & Daisy Soros 奖学金与医学科学家培养计划资助 [SRC-0002]。

## Public health career (2014–2025)

He was an assistant professor of epidemiology at Columbia's Mailman School of Public Health from 2014 to 2015, directing its Systems Science Program. In August 2015 he became health officer and executive director of the Detroit Health Department at age 30 — described as the youngest health officer of a major U.S. city — and rebuilt the department after the city's bankruptcy. As Detroit health officer he led opposition to increases in sulfur dioxide emissions by Marathon Petroleum's Southwest Refinery. He resigned in February 2017 to run for governor [SRC-0002].

From March 2023 to April 2025 he was director of Wayne County's Department of Health, Human and Veterans Services, where he installed air quality monitors, launched a programme to buy and erase residents' medical debt, and restructured Juvenile Youth Services [SRC-0002]. The medical-debt programme became a campaign asset and then a campaign liability: Bridge Michigan reported that the programme was designed to erase up to $700 million in debt for about 300,000 residents, but that about $95 million had been erased for more than 100,000 residents as of September 2026, and that the campaign's claim of having erased $700 million overstated the results [SRC-0011].

中文：2014 至 2015 年，他在哥伦比亚大学梅尔曼公共卫生学院任流行病学助理教授，主持系统科学项目。2015 年 8 月，30 岁的他出任底特律市卫生局局长兼执行主任，被称为美国大城市中最年轻的卫生官员，并在城市破产后重建了该部门。任内他带头反对马拉松石油公司西南炼油厂提高二氧化硫排放。2017 年 2 月他辞职竞选州长 [SRC-0002]。

2023 年 3 月至 2025 年 4 月，他担任韦恩县卫生、人类与退伍军人服务部主任，期间安装空气质量监测设备、启动收购并注销居民医疗债务的项目，并重组青少年司法服务 [SRC-0002]。医疗债务项目先成为竞选资产，后成为竞选负担：Bridge Michigan 报道，该项目原计划为约 30 万居民注销最多 7 亿美元债务，但截至 2026 年 9 月实际注销约 9500 万美元、惠及逾 10 万人，因此竞选方“已注销 7 亿美元”的说法夸大了结果 [SRC-0011]。

## Political career before the Senate

His first campaign was the 2018 Democratic gubernatorial primary, launched in February 2017. He pledged to accept no corporate donations, raised more than $5 million, and finished second with 340,560 votes (about 30%) behind Gretchen Whitmer [SRC-0002]. In September 2018 he founded Southpaw Michigan PAC to elect progressive candidates and support ballot initiatives, and from 2019 he hosted the Crooked Media podcast "America Dissected". In 2020 he served on the healthcare group of the Biden–Sanders Unity Task Force [SRC-0002].

中文：他的首次竞选是 2017 年 2 月启动的 2018 年民主党州长初选。他承诺不接受企业捐款，筹得逾 500 万美元，最终以 340,560 票（约 30%）位居第二，落后于格雷琴·惠特默 [SRC-0002]。2018 年 9 月他创立 Southpaw Michigan PAC，用于支持进步派候选人与公投倡议；2019 年起主持 Crooked Media 播客《America Dissected》；2020 年参加拜登—桑德斯团结工作组的医疗保健小组 [SRC-0002]。

## The 2026 Senate campaign

El-Sayed announced his candidacy in April 2025, after Gary Peters declined to seek re-election, and was endorsed at the launch by Bernie Sanders [SRC-0070] [SRC-0078]. He filed nominating petitions on April 14, 2026 [SRC-0005]. The Democratic primary was a three-way contest until Mallory McMorrow suspended her campaign on July 5, 2026; she remained on the ballot and took 4.00% [SRC-0001]. El-Sayed won with 744,952 votes (48.45%) to Haley Stevens' 731,160 (47.55%), a margin of 13,792 votes, and the Associated Press called the race at 9:56 a.m. on August 5 [SRC-0009] [SRC-0001].

The primary was dominated by outside money and by Israel policy. AIPAC and its United Democracy Project spent roughly $33 million in the primary, and total outside spending in support of Stevens reached about $62 million against roughly 9-to-1 odds for El-Sayed [SRC-0002] [SRC-0010]. El-Sayed refused PAC money throughout [SRC-0015]. After the primary, party leaders who had stayed neutral — including Chuck Schumer and Kirsten Gillibrand — endorsed him [SRC-0060].

中文：2025 年 4 月，在加里·彼得斯宣布不再竞选连任之后，赛义德宣布参选，并在启动时获得伯尼·桑德斯背书 [SRC-0070] [SRC-0078]。他于 2026 年 4 月 14 日提交提名联署 [SRC-0005]。民主党初选原本是三人之争，直到马洛里·麦克莫罗于 2026 年 7 月 5 日暂停竞选；她仍留在选票上并获得 4.00% [SRC-0001]。赛义德以 744,952 票（48.45%）对海莉·史蒂文斯 731,160 票（47.55%）获胜，差距 13,792 票，美联社于 8 月 5 日上午 9:56 判定其胜选 [SRC-0009] [SRC-0001]。

初选被外部资金与以色列政策主导。AIPAC 及其“联合民主项目”在初选投入约 3300 万美元，支持史蒂文斯的外部支出总额约 6200 万美元，对赛义德形成约 9 比 1 的压倒性优势 [SRC-0002] [SRC-0010]。赛义德全程拒收 PAC 资金 [SRC-0015]。初选之后，此前保持中立的党内领袖——包括查克·舒默与柯尔斯滕·吉利布兰德——表态支持他 [SRC-0060]。

## How he won: the geography of the coalition

El-Sayed carried 30 of Michigan's 83 counties, including nine in the Upper Peninsula and nine north of Grand Rapids, but lost Wayne, Oakland and Macomb counties — the three counties that make up most of the Democratic base [SRC-0008]. Stevens won Detroit with more than 60% [SRC-0008]. Turnout was unusually high for a midterm primary and El-Sayed's margins came from liberal university towns and from "walkup" votes cast on election day [SRC-0008]. The resulting general-election coalition was the first Democratic statewide win without Wayne, Oakland and Macomb since 2016, which is the single most important structural fact in this archive [SRC-0001].

中文：赛义德赢得密歇根州 83 个县中的 30 个，包括上半岛 9 个县与大急流城以北 9 个县，却输掉了韦恩、奥克兰与马科姆三县——民主党票仓的主体 [SRC-0008]。史蒂文斯在底特律得票逾 60% [SRC-0008]。该初选投票率对中期选举初选而言异常之高，赛义德的优势来自大学城的自由派选民与选举日现场登记投票 [SRC-0008]。由此形成的大选联盟，是 2016 年以来民主党首次在没有韦恩、奥克兰与马科姆三县的情况下赢得全州选举——这是本档案中最重要的结构性事实 [SRC-0001]。

## After the election

The Associated Press called the general election at 2:27 a.m. on November 4, 2026, with 85% counted and El-Sayed leading 51.9% to 46.1% [SRC-0029] [SRC-0001]. He spoke to supporters in Detroit shortly afterwards, and takes office on January 3, 2027 as the first Muslim American elected to the U.S. Senate [SRC-0002]. His victory speech avoided a transition announcement and restated both his unifying message and his position on U.S. aid to Israel [SRC-0029].

中文：美联社于 2026 年 11 月 4 日凌晨 2:27 在计票 85%、赛义德以 51.9% 对 46.1% 领先时判定其胜选 [SRC-0029] [SRC-0001]。他随后在底特律向支持者发表讲话，并将于 2027 年 1 月 3 日就职，成为美国参议院首位穆斯林议员 [SRC-0002]。胜选演说未宣布过渡安排，同时重申了团结信息与他对美国援助以色列的立场 [SRC-0029]。
