## Register: the former agent

Rogers speaks in credentials and consequences. The credentials are institutional and repeated: House Intelligence Committee chairman, FBI special agent, Army officer. He used them to answer an immigration question — "his time in the FBI taught him that raids of that nature should be quick, decisive, and without public fanfare" — and to answer a national-security question: "you can smile and you can talk real pretty, but I'm going to tell you what, that is a risk to our national security" [ROG-SPC-0003] [SRC-0019] [SRC-0036]. The consequences are material and colloquial: "Money in your pocket? You'll be lucky to have a damn pair of pants when you're done" [ROG-SPC-0003] [SRC-0036].

中文：罗杰斯以资历与后果说话。资历是制度性的、反复出现的：众议院情报委员会主席、联邦调查局特工、陆军军官。他用这些资历回答移民问题——“他在联邦调查局的经历让他认为此类突袭应当迅速、果断且不事张扬”——也用来回答国家安全问题：“你可以微笑，可以说得漂亮，但我告诉你，这是对我们国家安全的威胁”[ROG-SPC-0003] [SRC-0019] [SRC-0036]。后果则是物质性的、口语化的：“你口袋里的钱？等你搞完，能剩条裤子就不错了”[ROG-SPC-0003] [SRC-0036]。

## The two slogans and what they do

Two condensations carry most of his message. "We can't afford Abdul" reduces a policy argument to a household budget and appeared in the first general-election debate [ROG-SPC-0001] [SRC-0019]. "The house is on fire. And we can't sit back as it rages on" frames urgency without naming a policy, and was used to introduce an endorsement slate about schools and the economy [ROG-SPC-0005] [SRC-0022]. Both are designed to work without a policy predicate — the first needs no cost estimate, the second needs no diagnosis.

中文：两个凝练表述承载了他大部分信息。“我们养不起阿卜杜勒”把政策争论压缩为家庭预算，出现在首场大选辩论中 [ROG-SPC-0001] [SRC-0019]。“房子着火了，我们不能坐视它烧下去”制造紧迫感而不指明任何政策，被用于引出关于学校与经济的背书名单 [ROG-SPC-0005] [SRC-0022]。两者都设计为无需政策谓词即可生效：前者不需成本估算，后者不需诊断。

## The coalition pitch

His most distinctive speech act is the direct pitch to the other party's voters, delivered at the launch of "Democrats for Mike Rogers": "The Democrat voters who feel their nominee doesn't represent them in their values, all hope is not lost", and "I'm not asking you to be a Republican. I'm asking you for the opportunity to earn your trust" [ROG-SPC-0004] [SRC-0063]. The construction is unusually concessive for a general-election candidate — it names the audience's party identity and explicitly does not ask them to abandon it.

中文：他最具特色的言语行为是直接向另一党选民招手，在“支持迈克·罗杰斯的民主党人”启动仪式上说：“那些觉得本党提名人不代表自己价值观的民主党选民，希望并未丧失”，以及“我不是要求你成为共和党人，我是请求你给我一个赢得你信任的机会”[ROG-SPC-0004] [SRC-0063]。对一位大选候选人而言，这种让步式构造相当罕见——它点明听众的党派身份，并明确不要求其放弃。

## Trade as autobiography

The tariff answer at the Detroit Economic Club is the only place in the file where Rogers argues from experience rather than credentials: "the vehicles, including Michigan, from Mexico and China, are because of the tariffs that were applied to help us, I think, correct about 40 years of bad trade policy" [ROG-SPC-0002] [SRC-0020]. The hedging "I think" is notable in a campaign otherwise built on declaratives, and it sits beside the one issue where he breaks with the administration — Canada, where "they didn't account for how integrated we are in the automotive business" [SRC-0020].

中文：底特律经济俱乐部的关税回答是本档案中罗杰斯唯一以经验而非资历立论之处：“包括密歇根生产的车型在内，来自墨西哥和中国的车辆之所以如此，是因为所施加的关税帮助我们纠正了约 40 年的糟糕贸易政策”[ROG-SPC-0002] [SRC-0020]。在一场基本由陈述句构成的竞选中，“我认为”这一限定语值得注意，而它恰好出现在他与政府立场分歧的议题旁——加拿大：“他们没有考虑到我们在汽车产业上的高度一体化”[SRC-0020]。

## Attack structure

His attacks move from character to capacity. In the final debate he questioned whether El-Sayed could obtain a security clearance, called him "too extreme" for Democratic defectors, and then closed on taxes: three frames in one answer, none supported by a document [ROG-SPC-0003] [SRC-0036]. The AIPAC answer earlier in the month was the campaign's clearest unguarded moment — "Anybody that wants to help my campaign ... I'll take all of that help" — and within 24 hours AIPAC-linked money began arriving [ROG-SPC-0001] [SRC-0019] [SRC-0073].

中文：他的攻击从人品转向能力。在最后一场辩论中，他质疑赛义德能否取得安全许可，称其对民主党脱党者而言“过于极端”，最后以税收收尾：一个回答里三种框架，均无文件支撑 [ROG-SPC-0003] [SRC-0036]。同月早些时候关于 AIPAC 的回答是竞选中最坦率的一刻——“任何愿意帮我的竞选的人……我都会接受这一切帮助”——不到 24 小时，与 AIPAC 关联的资金便开始流入 [ROG-SPC-0001] [SRC-0019] [SRC-0073]。
