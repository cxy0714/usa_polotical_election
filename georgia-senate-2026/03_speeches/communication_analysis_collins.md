## Delivery format / 表达形式

Collins communicates through retail politics: a coffee shop in Toccoa, a Savannah golf-club breakfast, a Chamber audience in Athens. He answers questions off the cuff — the Savannah Q&A produced a viral answer on Jan. 6 compensation that the campaign then had to clarify. His rallies are short and depend on a surrogate (Trump in Dalton). SRC-0026 SRC-0029 SRC-0023

柯林斯以"零售政治"沟通：托科阿咖啡店、萨凡纳高尔夫俱乐部早餐会、雅典商会听众。他即兴回答问题——萨凡纳问答产生了一段关于 1 月 6 日赔偿的病毒式回答，竞选团队随后不得不澄清。其集会简短且依赖代理人（道尔顿的特朗普）。SRC-0026 SRC-0029 SRC-0023

## Recurring frames / 反复出现的框架

Three frames: "Georgia vs Georgetown" (class and place), "I created jobs on this red clay" (business biography), and "they weaponised the government against you" (grievance). The first is the only one aimed at persuasion; the other two are base-consolidation. SRC-0017 SRC-0016 SRC-0029

三个框架："佐治亚对乔治城"（阶层与地方）、"我在这片红土上创造了就业"（商业传记）、"他们把政府武器化来对付你"（怨愤）。第一个是唯一面向说服的框架，后两个用于巩固基本盘。SRC-0017 SRC-0016 SRC-0029

## Debate behaviour / 辩论表现

In the first debate he attacked biography ("a trust fund kid who's never had a job in his life") and the border; in the second he leaned on the geographic antithesis. He interrupts less than Ossoff but escalates faster; his answers are shorter and more declarative. SRC-0016 SRC-0017

首场辩论攻击对方出身（"一个从未工作过的信托基金子弟"）与边境；第二场倚重地理对立。他打断次数少于奥索夫，但升级更快；回答更短、更多陈述句。SRC-0016 SRC-0017

## Vernacular and religious register / 口语与宗教语域

His concession is the clearest sample: "y'all", "whatever the good Lord has in store for us", and a closing tribute to Trump as "the best president in my lifetime". The register is Southern-vernacular and church-adjacent, and it is consistent across the kickoff (a pastor opened) and the concession. SRC-0004 SRC-0014

败选讲话是最清晰的样本："y'all"、"无论上帝为我们安排了什么"，并以称特朗普为"我一生中最好的总统"收尾。语域为南方口语并带有教会色彩，从启动集会（牧师开场）到败选讲话保持一致。SRC-0004 SRC-0014

## Digital and synthetic media / 数字与合成媒体

The campaign released an AI-generated likeness of Ossoff in November 2025 and depended on meme-style posting; his personal account produced the "Matt Lauer's sloppy seconds" post that forced a chief-of-staff departure. Digital output is high-volume and low-production. SRC-0092 SRC-0097

竞选团队于 2025 年 11 月发布 AI 生成的奥索夫形象，并依赖模因式发帖；其个人账号发出"Matt Lauer's sloppy seconds"帖文，导致幕僚长离职。数字内容产量高、制作度低。SRC-0092 SRC-0097
