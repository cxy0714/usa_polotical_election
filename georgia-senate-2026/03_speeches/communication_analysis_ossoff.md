## Delivery format / 表达形式

Ossoff's events are produced events: a teleprompter at the Savannah rally, campaign staff coaching the crowd behind the podium, prepared signage, and a 30-minute scripted speech at the February rally. The register is formal-legal ("necessary and undeniable blow on behalf of justice and progress"), and sentences are built as triads. SRC-0018 SRC-0019 SRC-0004

奥索夫的活动是"制作型"活动：萨凡纳集会有提词器、工作人员指导讲台后的观众、预制标语；2 月集会为 30 分钟脚本化演讲。语域偏正式—法律式（"为正义与进步带来了必要且不可否认的一击"），句子常以三段式排比构成。SRC-0018 SRC-0019 SRC-0004

## Recurring frames / 反复出现的框架

Three frames repeat: "the republic in its 250th year" (democracy threat), "costs are crushing Georgia families" (tariffs, insurance, groceries), and "I will never stop fighting for you" (constituency service). The first is aimed at national donors and cable, the second at swing voters, the third at the base. SRC-0019 SRC-0016 SRC-0004

三个框架反复出现："共和国第 250 年"（民主受威胁）、"成本正在压垮佐治亚家庭"（关税、保险、食品杂货）、"我永远不会停止为你们而战"（选区服务）。第一个面向全国捐助者与有线电视，第二个面向摇摆选民，第三个面向基本盘。SRC-0019 SRC-0016 SRC-0004

## Debate behaviour / 辩论表现

In both debates he opened by prosecuting Collins's associations rather than defending his record: "You brought dishonor" in the second debate, and in the first the accusation that Collins was "lying about a young woman's death". He used Collins's first name repeatedly, a familiarity device that reads as condescension in transcript. SRC-0017 SRC-0016

两场辩论中，他都先攻击柯林斯的人际关联而非为自身记录辩护：第二场"你带来了耻辱"，第一场指其"拿一名年轻女性的死亡撒谎"。他反复直呼柯林斯的名字，文本上呈现为一种居高临下的熟稔。SRC-0017 SRC-0016

## Language switching / 语码转换

He shifts between Senate-floor register and a Southern-adjacent plain style ("y'all" appears in his victory speech's reported quotes), but the shift is thinner than Collins's. No Spanish-language campaigning was retrieved. SRC-0004

他在参议院式语域与略带南方口语的平实风格间切换（胜选演讲引语中出现"y'all"），但切换幅度小于柯林斯。未获取到西语竞选活动。
