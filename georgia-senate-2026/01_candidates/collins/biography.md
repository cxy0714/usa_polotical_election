## Origins and education / 出身与教育

Mike Collins was born in Jackson, Georgia, the seat of Butts County, and attended Piedmont Academy in Monticello, graduating in 1985. He earned a business degree from Georgia State University in 1990. SRC-0097

迈克·柯林斯生于佐治亚州巴茨县县城杰克逊，就读于蒙蒂塞洛的皮德蒙特学院并于 1985 年毕业，1990 年获佐治亚州立大学商学学位。SRC-0097

## Business career / 商业生涯

Before politics he founded and ran a trucking company and other small businesses in Georgia, a biography he used constantly on the 2026 trail: "Somebody that's created jobs and opportunities right here in Georgia on this red clay." SRC-0097 SRC-0017

从政前在佐治亚创办并经营货运公司等小企业；他在 2026 年竞选反复强调这一经历："在佐治亚这片红土上创造就业与机会的人。" SRC-0097 SRC-0017

## House career / 众议院生涯

His first congressional run, for Georgia's 10th district in 2014, ended in a runoff loss to Jody Hice. He ran again in 2022 for the seat Hice vacated and won, taking office on 3 January 2023; he was re-elected in 2024. In the House he co-sponsored the Life at Conception Act, introduced the RAZOR Act, authored the Laken Riley Act, voted to impeach Homeland Security Secretary Alejandro Mayorkas and voted against Ukraine aid while supporting Taiwan arms sales. SRC-0097

2014 年首次竞选第十选区众议员，第二轮败于乔迪·海斯；2022 年再战该空缺席位并当选，2023 年 1 月 3 日就职，2024 年连任。在众议院共同提出《生命始于受孕法案》、提出 RAZOR 法案、主笔《拉肯·莱利法案》，投票弹劾国土安全部长马约卡斯，反对援助乌克兰但支持对台军售。SRC-0097

## The 2026 Senate campaign / 2026 年参议院竞选

Collins announced on 27 July 2025. He won the May 19 primary with 40.5% to Dooley's 30.2% and Carter's 25.1%, then won the June 16 runoff 55.5% to 44.5% after Trump endorsed him two days before voting ended. He lost the general election 40.97% to 59.03% and conceded at his campaign headquarters on election night. SRC-0097 SRC-0006 SRC-0007 SRC-0004

柯林斯于 2025 年 7 月 27 日宣布参选；5 月 19 日初选以 40.5% 居首（杜利 30.2%、卡特 25.1%），在特朗普于投票结束前两天背书后，6 月 16 日第二轮以 55.5% 对 44.5% 胜出；大选以 40.97% 对 59.03% 落败，当晚在竞选总部承认败选。SRC-0097 SRC-0006 SRC-0007 SRC-0004
