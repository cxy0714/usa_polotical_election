## Abortion / 堕胎

"100 percent pro-life, period. No exceptions." He co-sponsored the Life at Conception Act and treats Georgia's six-week ban as a model; Politico reported Trump privately questioned whether the stance could win a general election. SRC-0097 SRC-0089

"百分之百反堕胎，句号，没有例外。" 共同提出《生命始于受孕法案》，视佐治亚六周禁令为范本；Politico 报道特朗普私下质疑该立场能否赢得大选。SRC-0097 SRC-0089

## Immigration / 移民

Primary author of the Laken Riley Act, which expanded detention requirements and was the first bill signed in Trump's second term. He introduced the RAZOR Act to stop federal removal of Texas border barriers, and in 2024 posted that a migrant should get "a ticket on Pinochet Air for a free helicopter ride back" — a man later reported to have been misidentified. SRC-0097 SRC-0016

《拉肯·莱利法案》主笔，扩大拘留要求，是特朗普第二任期签署的首部法律；提出 RAZOR 法案阻止联邦拆除得州边境屏障；2024 年发帖称某移民应得"皮诺切特航空免费直升机之旅"，该男子后被报道为误认。SRC-0097 SRC-0016

## Democracy and January 6 / 民主与 1 月 6 日

He said at a 2026 Savannah breakfast that "when the federal government harms you, then yes, they have a liability to you. That's easy, yes sir", then clarified he does not back a "blank-check fund". He repeats that the 2020 election was stolen and told a 2026 rally that Georgia is "red". SRC-0029 SRC-0097 SRC-0023

2026 年在萨凡纳早餐会上称"当联邦政府伤害了你，是的，它对你负有责任。这很简单，先生"，随后澄清不支持"空白支票基金"；反复称 2020 年选举被窃取，并在 2026 年集会上称佐治亚是"红州"。SRC-0029 SRC-0097 SRC-0023

## Economy, energy and labour / 经济、能源与劳工

He denies the scientific consensus on climate change, presents himself as a job creator ("This is a race between Georgia and Georgetown"), and used a 2022 ad in which he shot a voting machine while saying Trump won Georgia. SRC-0097 SRC-0017

否认气候变化科学共识；以"就业创造者"自居（"这是佐治亚与乔治城的对决"）；2022 年广告中一边称特朗普赢得佐治亚一边开枪射击投票机。SRC-0097 SRC-0017
