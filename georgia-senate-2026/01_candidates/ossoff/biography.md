## Origins and education / 出身与教育

Jon Ossoff was born on 16 February 1987 in Atlanta and raised in Northlake, an unincorporated community in DeKalb County northeast of the city. He attended Paideia School, graduated from Georgetown University's School of Foreign Service in 2009 with a degree in culture and politics, and took a master's in international political economy at the London School of Economics in 2013. SRC-0096

乔恩·奥索夫 1987 年 2 月 16 日生于亚特兰大，在德卡尔布县非建制社区北湖长大；先后就读于派迪亚学校、乔治城大学外交学院（2009 年）与伦敦政治经济学院（2013 年硕士）。SRC-0096

## Professional career before the Senate / 进入参议院前的职业经历

He worked as a national-security staffer and legislative assistant for U.S. Representative Hank Johnson from 2007 to 2012, then became managing director and chief executive of Insight: The World Investigates (TWI), a London-based investigative television production company, from 2013 until entering the Senate in 2021. SRC-0096

2007–2012 年任联邦众议员汉克·约翰逊的国安与立法助理；2013–2021 年任伦敦调查性电视制作公司 Insight: TWI 的董事总经理兼首席执行官。SRC-0096

## Electoral record / 选举记录

His first campaign, the 2017 special election in Georgia's 6th district, ended in a runoff loss to Karen Handel. In the January 5, 2021 runoff he defeated incumbent Republican David Perdue; together with Raphael Warnock's win the same night, Democrats took both Georgia Senate seats. He was sworn in on 20 January 2021. SRC-0096 SRC-0004

2017 年第六选区特别选举第二轮败于卡伦·汉德尔；2021 年 1 月 5 日第二轮击败共和党现任参议员大卫·珀杜，与沃诺克同时获胜，使民主党拿下佐治亚两个参议院席位。SRC-0096 SRC-0004

## The 2026 re-election / 2026 年连任

Ossoff was unopposed in the Democratic primary (1,042,331 votes, 100.0%) and faced Republican Mike Collins, who won a June 16 runoff against Derek Dooley. Ossoff won 2,469,037 votes (59.03%) to Collins's 1,713,664 (40.97%), an 18.06-point margin. Networks called the race shortly after polls closed and Collins conceded the same evening. ## Governance profile / 治理形象

The Lugar Center ranked him 33rd among senators for bipartisanship in 2023, and the Associated Press described a senator who both attacks Trump and "works with Republicans, advancing the interests of Georgia's farmers and military bases". SRC-0096

2023 年 Lugar 中心将其两党合作程度列为参议院第 33 位；美联社形容他既攻击特朗普，又与共和党合作推进佐治亚农民与军事基地的利益。SRC-0096
