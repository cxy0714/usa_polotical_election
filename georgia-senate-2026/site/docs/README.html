What this archive covers / 本档案覆盖范围

The 2026 Georgia U.S. Senate election: the 19 May primary, the 16 June Republican runoff, the 3 November general election, both candidates' careers, positions, events, speeches, images, advertising, money and the electoral geography published by the sources. Everything is dated and cited to a URL that appears in _staging/fetch_log.csv. SRC-0001 SRC-0004

2026 年佐治亚州联邦参议员选举：5 月 19 日初选、6 月 16 日共和党第二轮、11 月 3 日大选，以及两位候选人的履历、立场、活动、演讲、图像、广告、资金与来源公布的地理数据。所有内容均标注日期，并引用出现在 _staging/fetch_log.csv 中的链接。SRC-0001 SRC-0004

Structural facts the archive establishes / 档案确立的结构性事实

  1. Money was not close: $136.6m to $10.7m raised by 14 October, a ratio above 12:1, with outside groups spending $25.9m to partly offset it. SRC-0001 SRC-0022
  2. Two debates happened (8 and 13 October), although the Wikipedia debate table lists only the first. SRC-0016 SRC-0017 SRC-0001
  3. The campaign had two distinct grammars: produced rallies with teleprompters for Ossoff, retail stops and unscripted Q&A for Collins. SRC-0018 SRC-0029
  4. Collins's coalition consolidated late — Trump on 14 June, Kemp on 14 September — after a runoff in which he took 55.5%. SRC-0043 SRC-0021 SRC-0007
  5. Nine counties flipped Democratic, including four metro-exurban counties, the only sub-state geographic evidence retrievable. SRC-0001
  1. 资金差距悬殊：至 10 月 14 日筹款 1.366 亿对 1,070 万美元，比例超过 12:1，外部团体支出 2,590 万美元部分抵消。SRC-0001 SRC-0022
  2. 实际举行了两场辩论（10 月 8 日与 13 日），而维基百科辩论表仅列第一场。SRC-0016 SRC-0017 SRC-0001
  3. 两种竞选语法：奥索夫为提词器制作型集会，柯林斯为零售式停留与即兴问答。SRC-0018 SRC-0029
  4. 柯林斯联盟整合较晚——特朗普 6 月 14 日、肯普 9 月 14 日——此前他在第二轮得 55.5%。SRC-0043 SRC-0021 SRC-0007
  5. 九个县转向民主党，其中四个为都市外围县，是唯一可获取的州级以下地理证据。SRC-0001
