The money asymmetry / 资金不对称

Ossoff out-raised Collins by more than twelve to one and the FEC listed him as the top-raising Senate candidate in the country. Collins's campaign averaged $19.05 per donation across about 16,000 donations, a small-dollar base far narrower than the incumbent's. Outside money partly compensated: Senate Leadership Fund and One Nation spent $25.9m with $17.3m more reserved. SRC-0032 SRC-0022

奥索夫筹款超过柯林斯十二倍以上，FEC 将其列为全国筹款最多的参议院候选人。柯林斯竞选团队约 16,000 笔捐款、平均 19.05 美元，小额基本盘远窄于现任者。外部资金部分补偿：参议院领导基金与 One Nation 支出 2,590 万美元，另有 1,730 万美元预留。SRC-0032 SRC-0022

Two campaign grammars / 两种竞选语法

INTERPRETATION: Ossoff ran a national-media campaign with local staging; Collins ran a local-media campaign with national surrogates. The observable markers are the teleprompter and coached crowd on one side, the golf-club breakfast and unscripted answer on the other. SRC-0018 SRC-0029

解读：奥索夫以"全国媒体＋地方舞台"运作；柯林斯以"地方媒体＋全国代理人"运作。可观察标志一侧是提词器与受指导的观众，另一侧是高尔夫俱乐部早餐会与即兴回答。SRC-0018 SRC-0029

Coalition evidence / 联盟证据

Ossoff's visible coalition included Republican endorsers (Flake, three Georgia Republicans, Republican mayors), Obama and Harris; Collins's included Trump, Kemp, Thune, Rick Scott and House Republicans. The county-flip list — Fayette, Houston, Spalding, Lowndes among nine — is consistent with suburban and small-metro movement toward Ossoff. SRC-0048 SRC-0049 SRC-0004 SRC-0021 SRC-0001

奥索夫可见联盟包括共和党背书人（弗莱克、三位佐治亚共和党人、共和党市长）、奥巴马与哈里斯；柯林斯包括特朗普、肯普、图恩、里克·斯科特与众议院共和党人。九个翻转县中的费耶特、休斯敦、斯帕尔丁、朗兹，与郊区及小都市区向奥索夫移动一致。SRC-0048 SRC-0049 SRC-0004 SRC-0021 SRC-0001

Where the comparison is weak / 比较的薄弱处

No vote-file or precinct data was retrieved, so the coalition claims rest on endorsements and county flips, not on individual-level behaviour. Ad creatives were not watched, so message effects cannot be assessed.

未取得选民档案或 precinct 数据，联盟判断依赖背书与翻转县，而非个体行为。未观看广告素材，故无法评估信息效果。
