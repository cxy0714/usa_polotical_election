# Image, staging and personal presentation / 形象、舞台与个人呈现

## 1. Why this note exists / 1. 这份说明为什么存在

The eight case folders were commissioned around a menu of researchable phenomena: religious language,
"minimalist" versus professional presentation, regional and outdoor identity, two women's professional
image, a Paralympic athlete's public identity, a working-class brand, formal speech and large rallies,
and campaign styling in a mayoral race. This note asks a narrower question than the menu does: **which
of those phenomena are actually documented in the retrieved record, in what form, and where does the
evidence stop?**

八个案例围绕一组可研究现象立项：宗教语言、「极简」与职业化呈现、地域与户外身份、两位女性的职业形象、
残奥运动员的公共身份、工人阶层品牌、正式演讲与大型集会，以及市长选战中的竞选造型。本说明问的问题比菜单更窄：
**这些现象中，哪些真正见于已检索的记录、以何种形式出现、证据止于何处？**

It is an evidence audit. It does not score either candidate's appearance, infer wardrobe from party or
persona, or declare a "style winner".

这是一份证据审计。它不为任何候选人的外貌打分，不根据党派或人设推断衣橱，也不宣布「风格胜者」。

## 2. Three kinds of visual evidence, kept separate / 2. 三类视觉证据，彼此分离

| Class | What it is | Where it lives | How it is labelled |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Text-described staging** | A retrieved article or caption says what the setting, backdrop, signage, crowd or clothing looked like | `02_campaign_events.visual_notes`, `05_campaign_media.media_coverage.key_framing`, `04_visual_archive.notes` | cited to the outlet; `unknown` where silent |
| **B. Direct observation of a licensed image** | The compiler opened a downloaded PD/CC image and described what is visible (framing, clothing, setting) | `04_visual_archive.clothing_notes`, `image_quality`, `face_visibility` | "observed in [IMG-id]" — an observation of one image, not a general claim |
| **C. Branding and iconography** | Tour names, slogans, borrowed cultural references, ad creative described in reporting | `08_comparative_analysis`, `05_campaign_media` | cited; interpretation prefixed `INTERPRETATION:` |

| 类别 | 是什么 | 存放在哪里 | 如何标注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. 文本描述的舞台** | 已检索的文章或图注描述场景、背景、标语、人群或着装 | 同上 | 注明媒体；未提及则 `unknown` |
| **B. 对授权图像的直接观察** | 编纂者打开已下载的公有领域／CC 图像，描述可见内容 | 同上 | 「见于 [IMG-id]」——对单张图像的观察，不是普遍论断 |
| **C. 品牌与图像符号** | 巡游名称、口号、借用的文化符号、报道描述的广告创意 | 同上 | 有引证；解读以 `INTERPRETATION:` 开头 |

**The rule that follows from this table:** a claim such as "candidate X dresses minimally" is only
supportable if it is a Class A statement from a retrieved source, or a Class B observation tied to a
named image and date. Otherwise the field stays `unknown`. This is the same rule Case Study 001
applied when it recorded clothing as `unknown` for all 116 Texas image rows.

**由上表得出的规则：** 「某候选人穿着极简」这类主张，只有在它是已检索来源的 A 类陈述，或与具名图像和日期绑定的
B 类观察时，才可支持。否则字段保持 `unknown`。案例研究 001 对德州全部 116 条影像记录均记着装为 `unknown`，遵循的正是这条规则。

## 4. Cross-cutting findings / 4. 跨案发现

**4.1 Staging is documented; clothing is not.** Across the eight archives there are 637 image rows and
171 stored files, but only **two** rows carry a clothing statement that a retrieved sentence supports:
Ossoff's rolled-up sleeves (Georgia Recorder) and Bass's "aquamarine pantsuit" (L.A. Times). Peltola's
rainbow scarf at Pride is a third documented clothing detail, but it appears in an event row, not an
image row. Texas, South Carolina, Iowa, Michigan and Ohio record **zero** clothing statements.

**4.2 What can be stored is mostly portraits.** Of the 171 files, the overwhelming majority are official
portraits or congressional headshots. Campaign-trail photography is largely link-only (`all rights
reserved`), so the visual archive cannot reconstruct a candidate's appearance across a campaign.

**4.3 Tenure predicts photographic depth, not competitiveness.** Paxton's decade as attorney general
supplies 116 Texas image rows; Graham's long Senate tenure supplies 36 of South Carolina's 55. The
first-time candidates (Andrews 19, El-Sayed/Rogers 13, Turek/Hinson 12) have thin records — a property
of archival supply, not of the races' importance.

**4.4 Branding is where the image evidence actually is.** Class C material is abundant and dated: tour
names ("Clear Eyes, Full Hearts", "Come and Take It"), launch-video titles ("Underdog"), institutes
("Dignity of Work"), rally brands ("Fighting For Ohio"), and borrowed cultural symbols. These are
documented in event and coverage rows; they are claims about message, not about bodies.

**4.5 Identity is recorded as fact, not inferred from appearance.** Turek's spina bifida and wheelchair
use, Peltola's Yup'ik identity and first-Alaska-Native status, and the "first sister to succeed a
brother" frame for Darline Graham are all sourced statements. No case infers capability, health or
character from a photograph.

**4.6 No styling operation appears anywhere.** Across 1,345 registered sources in the eight
cases, not one names a stylist, image consultant or campaign wardrobe team. The Los Angeles case states
this absence explicitly (gap G10). The "campaign styling team" premise is therefore unsupported by the
retrieved record — a finding about the record, not proof that no such work happens.

**4.7 The method's hard limit.** The compiler had no image input, so Class B (direct observation) is
empty in all eight cases: `framing`, `camera_angle` and `clothing_notes` are `unknown` unless a text
source states them. Every clothing claim in this repository is therefore Class A and should be read as
"a reporter wrote this", not "the archive saw this".

**4.1 舞台有记录，着装没有。** 八案合计 637 条影像记录、171 个已存文件，但只有 **2** 条记录的着装陈述有
已检索句子支持：奥索夫卷起的袖子（Georgia Recorder）与巴斯的「海蓝绿色长裤套装」（《洛杉矶时报》）。
佩尔托拉在骄傲游行的彩虹围巾是第三项有记录的服饰细节，但它出现在活动行而非影像行。德州、南卡、艾奥瓦、
密歇根与俄亥俄的着装陈述均为 **零**。

**4.2 能存下来的大多是肖像。** 171 个文件中绝大多数是官方肖像或国会头像。竞选现场摄影多为「仅链接」
（版权保留），因此影像档案无法重建候选人在一场选战中的外观变化。

**4.3 任期的长短决定影像深度，而非选情激烈程度。** 帕克斯顿十年总检察长任内提供德州 116 条影像记录；
格雷厄姆的长期参议员任期提供南卡 55 条中的 36 条。首次参选者（安德鲁斯 19 条，赛义德/罗杰斯 13 条，
图雷克/欣森 12 条）记录单薄——这是档案供给的属性，不是选举重要性的反映。

**4.4 品牌才是影像证据真正所在。** C 类材料丰富且有日期：巡游名称（「Clear Eyes, Full Hearts」、
「Come and Take It」）、启动视频标题（「Underdog」）、研究所（「Dignity of Work」）、集会品牌
（「Fighting For Ohio」）以及借用的文化符号。这些记录在活动与报道行中；它们关于讯息，不关于身体。

**4.5 身份被记为事实，而非从外观推断。** 图雷克的脊柱裂与轮椅使用、佩尔托拉的尤皮克身份与首位阿拉斯加
原住民参议员地位、达琳·格雷厄姆「首位接替兄长的妹妹」框架，都是有来源的陈述。没有任何案例从照片推断能力、
健康或品格。

**4.6 任何地方都没有出现造型运作。** 八案共 1,345 个登记来源中，没有一个点名造型师、形象顾问或竞选衣橱团队。
洛杉矶案例明确记录了这一缺失（缺口 G10）。因此「竞选造型团队」这一前提不为已检索记录所支持——这是关于记录的
发现，不是证明此类工作不存在。

**4.7 方法的硬限制。** 编纂者没有图像输入，因此八案的 B 类（直接观察）为空：除非文本来源说明，`framing`、
`camera_angle` 与 `clothing_notes` 均为 `unknown`。本仓库中的每一项着装陈述因此都是 A 类，应读作「记者这样写」，
而不是「档案看见了」。

## 5. What would close the gaps / 5. 如何弥补缺口

1. **Campaign-trail photography with captions.** The single largest gap is dated, captioned images of
   the same candidate across many events. Official portraits cannot establish a campaign wardrobe.
2. **Pool and wire captions.** AP/Reuters/Getty caption text frequently describes clothing and setting;
   those captions are often retrievable even when the image is not licensed.
3. **Candidate-produced video.** Launch and tour videos show wardrobe and staging directly; a frame
   sample per video (10–20 stills) would make Class B observation systematic rather than anecdotal.
4. **Stylist/consultant credits.** Trade press (Hollywood Reporter, Women's Wear Daily, Puck) sometimes
   names campaign stylists; none of the eight cases' retrieved sources did.

1. **带图注的竞选现场摄影。** 最大缺口是同一候选人在多场活动中的、带日期与图注的影像。官方肖像无法建立竞选衣橱。
2. **通讯社图注。** 美联社／路透／盖蒂的图注常描述着装与场景；即使图像未获授权，图注往往可检索。
3. **竞选团队自产视频。** 启动与巡游视频直接展示着装与舞台；每段抽 10–20 帧，可使 B 类观察系统化而非轶事化。
4. **造型师／顾问署名。** 行业媒体有时会点名竞选造型师；八案已检索来源中无一如此。

## 6. What the video layer adds (2026-11-18) / 6. 视频层补充了什么

The six new cases now carry a video corpus built like the earlier two: 19 uploaded videos, 228 scene
keyframes, and a YuNet+SFace pass that compares every detected face with the licensed portraits in
`04_visual_archive/`. Attribution is deliberately conservative (cosine ≥ 0.363 and a 0.05 margin over
the runner-up); 137 of 228 frames (60%) are attributed, the rest stay `unknown`.

六场新案现在与先前两案一样带有视频语料：19 段上传视频、228 张场景关键帧，以及一次 YuNet+SFace 比对，
把每张检测到的脸与该案 `04_visual_archive/` 中的授权肖像对照。判定刻意保守（余弦 ≥ 0.363 且领先第二名
0.05）；228 帧中 137 帧（60%）得到归属，其余保持 `unknown`。

| Case | Videos | Frames | Attributed | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Michigan | 3 | 30 | 14 | Rogers launch + interview + debate clip |
| Alaska | 3 | 32 | 17 | Sullivan floor speech, Peltola rally, CNN win package |
| Iowa | 3 | 37 | 12 | **Turek never clears the threshold** — his only licensed reference is a group photo |
| Ohio | 4 | 54 | 42 | strongest coverage: victory, Toledo rally, concession, launch |

**6.1 The video layer does not produce clothing evidence.** No frame was read by a vision-language
model and this compiler has no image input, so `clothing_notes` remains `unknown` for every keyframe.
The value of the layer is narrower: it establishes *who appears, when, in what setting* with a
measurable confidence, not what they wore.

**6.2 The recogniser is a two-class instrument.** It can only choose between the two nominees. A
moderator, surrogate or crowd member is assigned to whichever nominee is closer; the margin rule
reduces but does not remove this. The Los Angeles concession video is the clearest example: its
frames attribute to Raman more often than to Bass, which may reflect the broadcast's cutaways rather
than a reference error — the archive records the score and leaves the question open.

**6.3 Reference quality drives coverage.** Ohio (7 portraits per candidate) attributes 78% of frames;
Iowa (1 usable Turek reference, a group photograph) attributes 32% and never identifies Turek. This is
a property of the licensed portrait supply, and is stated in each `10_video_frames/README.md`.

**6.4 News photography is now a caption layer, not an image layer.** 384 credited news-photo rows were
added across the six cases from archived article HTML. They are link-and-credit only: no file is
stored, because the photographs are all rights reserved. 637 image rows now exist across the eight
cases; 171 files are stored. Captions can name clothing, but none of the harvested captions does.

**6.1 视频层不产生着装证据。** 没有一帧被视觉语言模型读过，编纂者也没有图像输入，因此每张关键帧的
`clothing_notes` 仍是 `unknown`。这一层的价值更窄：它以可度量的置信度确认*谁在何时、何种场合出现*，
而不是他们穿了什么。

**6.2 识别器是二分类工具。** 它只能在两位提名人之间选择。主持人、代理人或观众会被归给较接近的一位；
边际规则降低但不能消除这一风险。洛杉矶的承认败选视频是最清楚的例子：其帧更多被判为 Raman 而非 Bass，
这可能反映转播的切换镜头，而非参考错误——档案记录分数，把问题留开。

**6.3 参考质量决定覆盖率。** 俄亥俄（每位候选人 7 张肖像）覆盖 78% 的帧；艾奥瓦（Turek 唯一可用参考是
一张合影）覆盖 32%，且从未识别出 Turek。这是授权肖像供给的属性，已写入各案 `10_video_frames/README.md`。

**6.4 新闻摄影现在是图注层，不是图像层。** 六案从存档文章 HTML 中新增 384 条带署名的新闻图片记录。
它们仅提供链接与署名：不存文件，因为照片均为版权保留。八案现有 637 条影像记录、171 个已存文件。
图注可能描述着装，但已采集的图注中没有一条这样做。
