# Parallel campaign chronology / 平行竞选时间线

A phase-by-phase reading of the two campaigns on the same calendar, built from
[`campaign_events.csv`](../02_campaign_events/campaign_events.csv) (93 rows:
Talarico 49, Paxton 44) with the per-candidate narratives in
[`talarico_timeline.md`](../02_campaign_events/talarico_timeline.md) and
[`paxton_timeline.md`](../02_campaign_events/paxton_timeline.md).

这是一份在同一日历上按阶段解读两场竞选的文本，以 [`campaign_events.csv`](../02_campaign_events/campaign_events.csv)（93 行：塔拉里科 49 行，帕克斯顿 44 行）为基础，并结合 [`talarico_timeline.md`](../02_campaign_events/talarico_timeline.md) 与 [`paxton_timeline.md`](../02_campaign_events/paxton_timeline.md) 中按候选人分述的叙事。

## 0. How to read this, and the two cautions that matter / 0. 阅读方式与两项关键告诫

1. **Evidence asymmetry runs through the whole chronology.** Paxton has more
   *announced-but-undated* activity (14 rows without a usable date, 15 of 19 named
   tour cities schedule-only), while Talarico has more *dated and held* activity.
   A count of rows is not a count of rallies.
   **证据不对称贯穿整条时间线。** 帕克斯顿有更多*已宣布但无日期*的活动（14 行没有可用日期，19 个具名巡回城市中有 15 个仅有日程），而塔拉里科有更多*有日期且已举行*的活动。行数并不等同于集会数。
2. **Phase buckets are not perfectly symmetrical for undated rows.** Undated tour
   stops default to `gotv` for both candidates, so Paxton's `gotv` count (21) is
   inflated relative to Talarico's (5). For any phase comparison, use the dated
   subset: Talarico 12 dated `early_voting` rows vs Paxton 4.
   **对无日期行而言，阶段归类并不完全对称。** 两位候选人无日期的巡回停留点均默认归入 `gotv`，因此帕克斯顿的 `gotv` 计数（21）相对塔拉里科的（5）被抬高。任何阶段比较都应使用有日期的子集：塔拉里科有 12 行带日期的 `early_voting`，帕克斯顿为 4 行。

## 5. Documented absences that shape the chronology / 5. 塑造时间线的有记录空缺

- **No debate, no joint appearance.** No row in either timeline puts the two
  candidates on the same stage. The only "joint" event type in the archive is
  surrogate appearances by Trump and Vance for Paxton.
  **没有辩论，也没有联合露面。** 两份时间线中没有任何一行将两位候选人放在同一舞台上。本档案中唯一的 "joint"（译：联合）活动类型，是特朗普与万斯为帕克斯顿出面的代理人活动。
- **No HQ openings, no closed-door fundraisers, no labour events, no county-party
  meetings, no podcasts, no press conferences** for either campaign, despite
  targeted searches (`event_coverage_gaps.md`).
  **没有总部启用、没有闭门筹款会、没有劳工活动、没有县党会议、没有播客、没有新闻发布会**——两场竞选均如此，尽管进行了有针对性的检索（`event_coverage_gaps.md`）。
- **Talarico's two-week flu absence** in early October (reported 2026-10-09) is a
  documented gap in his activity, and no events are recorded inside it.
