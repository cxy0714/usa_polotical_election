spokespeople, headquarters, major endorsers, party-organization involvement, and finance.

names what is **missing**. It does not rank the two operations.

## 2. Talarico campaign / 塔拉里科竞选

### 2.1 Leadership and staff / 领导层与团队

- **Campaign manager: Seth Krasne.** Documented in a campaign press release quoted by the El Paso
  Times (2026-10-16): "Winning Texas will take the support of every Texan who is tired of a
  politics of hatred, division, and corruption ... Billionaire-backed dark money groups and super
  PACs are spending over $200 million to lie about James because we're on the verge of defeating
  the most corrupt politician in Texas."
  [Source](https://www.elpasotimes.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/10/16/james-talarico-widens-cash-edge-in-texas-us-senate-race-showdown/92262790007/)
  **竞选经理：塞思·克拉斯内。** 有据可查于《埃尔帕索时报》（2026-10-16）引用的一份竞选新闻稿："赢下德州需要每一位厌倦了仇恨、分裂与腐败政治（译）的德州人的支持……亿万富翁资助的黑金团体与超级政治行动委员会正在花费超过 2 亿美元来抹黑詹姆斯，因为我们即将击败德州最腐败的政治人物。"（译）
  [来源](https://www.elpasotimes.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/10/16/james-talarico-widens-cash-edge-in-texas-us-senate-race-showdown/92262790007/)
- **Campaign spokesperson: JT Ennis.** Documented when Ennis publicly accused Paxton of "lying"
  about debate negotiations (X post, 2026-10-12, reported by
  [Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/media/tv/ken-paxton-pressed-on-fox-news-about-whether-hell-debate-talarico/)).
  **竞选发言人：JT·恩尼斯。** 有据可查于恩尼斯公开指责帕克斯顿在辩论协商问题上"撒谎"（译）（X 平台帖文，2026-10-12，由 [Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/media/tv/ken-paxton-pressed-on-fox-news-about-whether-hell-debate-talarico/) 报道）。
- **Deputy political director / deputy policy director: Jacob Smith**, announced 2026-08-18 — a
  career Republican staffer who had most recently been Sen. John Cornyn's deputy legislative
  director. His hiring was itself a message: a Republican staffer for the senator Talarico sought
  to replace joined the Democratic campaign.
  [FOX 7 Austin, 2026-08-18](https://www.fox7austin.com/news/talarico-pulls-former-cornyn-staff-member-campaign-team)
  **副政治主任／副政策主任：雅各布·史密斯**，2026-08-18 公布——一位职业共和党幕僚，最近职务为参议员约翰·科宁的副立法主任。这项延揽本身即是一则讯息：为塔拉里科试图取代的那位参议员工作过的共和党幕僚，加入了民主党竞选。
  [FOX 7 Austin, 2026-08-18](https://www.fox7austin.com/news/talarico-pulls-former-cornyn-staff-member-campaign-team)
  - Smith: "I've worked for Republicans like Senator Cornyn my entire career, but this year I'm
    proud to be joining James Talarico's campaign. Instead of playing partisan politics, Talarico
    speaks to the needs of everyday people who, day in and day out, work too hard for too little."
    史密斯："我整个职业生涯都在为科宁参议员这样的共和党人工作，但今年我很自豪加入詹姆斯·塔拉里科的竞选。塔拉里科不玩党派政治，而是回应那些日复一日辛勤劳作却所得甚少的普通人的需求。"（译）
  - Smith on Paxton: "Ken Paxton is the most corrupt, self-dealing politician in Texas who would

    史密斯谈帕克斯顿："肯·帕克斯顿是德州最腐败、最自肥的政治人物，为了抓住权力，他会把劳动民众的未来出卖给出价最高的金主。"（译）

    ⚠ 注意同一篇文章内部的职务表述不一致：文末"The Brief"摘要称史密斯将任**副政策主任**，而正文称**副政治主任**。两种说法均予记录；真实职务未定。

### 2.2 Structure and scale signals / 结构与规模信号

- Campaign launched **September 2025**; by mid-October 2026 it reported **$134.3 million** raised
  from **more than 3 million donations by over 1.3 million individual donors**, with **97% of
  donations $100 or less** and teachers the most common donor occupation
  ([El Paso Times](https://www.elpasotimes.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/10/16/james-talarico-widens-cash-edge-in-texas-us-senate-race-showdown/92262790007/)).
  竞选于 **2025 年 9 月**启动；至 2026 年 10 月中旬，据其公布共筹得 **1.343 亿美元**，来自**超过 130 万名个人捐款者的逾 300 万笔捐款**，其中 **97% 的捐款在 100 美元及以下**，捐款者职业最多的是教师（[《埃尔帕索时报》](https://www.elpasotimes.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/10/16/james-talarico-widens-cash-edge-in-texas-us-senate-race-showdown/92262790007/)）。
- TIME reported the campaign gained **4,000 new volunteers and $2.5 million in 24 hours** after a
  Colbert interview went viral on YouTube (~9 million views)
  ([TIME, 2026-02-27, via mirror](https://www.yerepouni-news.com/she-cant-win-he-can-inside-james-talaricos-faith-based-bid-to-flip-texas/)).
  《时代》周刊报道，在一段科尔伯特访谈在 YouTube 走红（约 900 万次观看）后，竞选在 **24 小时内新增 4,000 名志愿者与 250 万美元**（[TIME, 2026-02-27，经镜像](https://www.yerepouni-news.com/she-cant-win-he-can-inside-james-talaricos-faith-based-bid-to-flip-texas/)）。
- TIME also documents that in February 2026 the campaign disputed public polling: "Talarico's
  campaign says it was seeing polls showing him several points ahead even before the Colbert
  flare-up" — a campaign claim about its own internals, not an established fact.
  《时代》还记载，2026 年 2 月该竞选团队质疑公开民调："塔拉里科的竞选团队称，甚至在科尔伯特事件发酵之前，其看到的民调就显示他领先数个点"（译）——这是阵营关于自身内部数据的宣称，不是已确立的事实。

## 4. Asymmetry of documentation (an honest finding) / 记录的不对称（一项诚实的发现）

The public record is **materially asymmetric**: Talarico's operation is documented at the level of
named manager, spokesperson and a specific hire with quotes, while Paxton's is documented at the
level of one senior advisor and the surrogate/consultant layer.

公开记录**存在实质性的不对称**：塔拉里科的运作被记录到具名竞选经理、发言人以及一次附带引语的具体人事延揽的层级；而帕克斯顿的运作只记录到一位高级顾问与代理人／顾问层。

Two plausible explanations, neither of which the archive can adjudicate:
1. **Reporting bias** — national outlets invested more in the novel Democratic campaign; a
   campaign-manager profile is a standard feature of such coverage.
2. **Operating style** — a campaign run through outside validators and consultants may simply
   surface fewer internal names.

两种可能的解释，档案无法裁定其中任何一种：
1. **报道偏差**——全国性媒体对这场新奇的民主党竞选投入更多；竞选经理的人物侧写本就是这类报道的标配。
2. **运作风格**——一场通过外部加持者与顾问来运作的竞选，可能本来就较少暴露内部人名。

The correct handling is to **state the asymmetry and leave the cause open**. Do not treat
Talarico's richer org-chart record as evidence that his operation was larger or more professional.

正确的处理方式是**陈述不对称、把原因留空**。不要把塔拉里科更丰富的组织架构记录当作其运作规模更大或更专业的证据。
