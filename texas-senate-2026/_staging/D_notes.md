### Final Houston-area rallies (Houston Public Media, Andrew Schneider, 2026-11-02)
- https://www.houstonpublicmedia.org/articles/news/politics/election-2026/2026/11/02/565811/paxton-and-talarico-make-final-election-pitches-of-u-s-senate-campaign-to-houston-voters/

  - "I'm asking you to look at my record and then compare it to a guy named James Talarico." "Everything that I just laid out puts money in your pocket. His entire record takes it out. James Talarico does have a plan for your money. It just doesn't include you."

  - "Do you believe that faith matters? I do too." "Do you believe in secure elections and want the SAVE America Act passed? I do too. Do you believe in protecting Texas and stopping Sharia law? I do too. Do you believe that parents, not the government, should raise your children? I do too. And do you believe that you and your family should keep more of your hard-earned money? Yes.
