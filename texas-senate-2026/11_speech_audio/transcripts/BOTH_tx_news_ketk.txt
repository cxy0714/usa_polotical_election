A newly released, Texas Senate race poll has James Talarico maintaining a lead with less than 40 days until the midterm election. Texas Public Opinion Research has surveyed 1,007 likely Texas voters. They found that Talarico holding a five-point advantage over Attorney General Ken Paxton, 49% to 44%. The survey also shows that Talarico's edge over Paxton has dropped one point since this poll was last conducted in August. Meanwhile, 3% of people surveyed said they are either undecided or voting for someone else.
