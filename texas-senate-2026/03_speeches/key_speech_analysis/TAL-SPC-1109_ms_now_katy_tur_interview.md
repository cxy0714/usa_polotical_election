## 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That corruption, not ideology, is the root cause of Texas' cost-of-living problem, and that the remedy is structural. The chain runs: big money buys politicians (Paxton as exhibit) → politicians rig the economy → working families cannot afford housing, health care, groceries → therefore the first bill must be anti-corruption. He pairs this with an explicit availability doctrine: he will work with Trump where they agree (housing supply, criminal-justice reform, marijuana legalisation) and "stand up to him" where they do not — whereas "Ken Paxton will be a lapdog" ([00:10:27]).

德州生活成本问题的根本原因是腐败而非意识形态，且处方必须是结构性的。链条如下：大钱收买政客（Paxton 为例证）→ 政客操纵经济 → 工薪家庭负担不起住房、医疗、食品 → 因此首项法案必须是反腐。与此配套的是一套明确的「可合作」原则：在意见一致的领域（住房供给、刑事司法改革、大麻合法化）与 Trump 合作，在意见不一致处 "stand up to him"（译：「与他抗衡」）——而 "Ken Paxton will be a lapdog"（译：「Ken Paxton 会当一条哈巴狗」）（[00:10:27]）。

Two argumentative moves stand out. First, the reframing of "minority" politics: "the only minority destroying America is the billionaire class" ([00:07:54]), after noting that trans people, Muslims and undocumented immigrants are each about one percent of the population. Second, the moralisation of economics: "trickle down economics is not a theory. It's theft" ([00:08:51]).

两个论证动作尤为突出。第一，对「少数群体」政治的重新框定："the only minority destroying America is the billionaire class"（译：「唯一正在摧毁美国的少数群体，就是亿万富翁阶层」）（[00:07:54]）——在此之前他指出跨性别人士、穆斯林与无证移民各约占人口的百分之一。第二，把经济学道德化："trickle down economics is not a theory. It's theft"（译：「涓滴经济学不是一种理论，而是偷窃。」）（[00:08:51]）。
