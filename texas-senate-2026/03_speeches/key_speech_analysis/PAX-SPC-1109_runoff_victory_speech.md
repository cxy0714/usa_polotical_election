## 1. Speech context / 演讲背景

Paxton defeated incumbent Sen. John Cornyn in the Republican runoff on 2026-05-26, winning roughly 63% to 37% after trailing Cornyn by 2.5 points in the March 3 first round ([SRC-0129](https://s.chron.com/politics/article/paxton-cornyn-texan-senate-runoff-22272654.php)). The decisive intervention was Donald Trump's endorsement on 2026-05-19, delivered at a rally at Matt's Rancho Martinez in Allen — the same venue as Paxton's final general-election rally. Paxton spoke to supporters in Plano, a North Texas suburb, and left the stage to AC/DC's "Thunderstruck."

Paxton 于 2026-05-26 在共和党决选中击败现任参议员 John Cornyn，以约 63% 对 37% 胜出——此前在 3 月 3 日首轮中他曾落后 Cornyn 2.5 个百分点（[SRC-0129](https://s.chron.com/politics/article/paxton-cornyn-texan-senate-runoff-22272654.php)）。决定性介入是 Donald Trump 于 2026-05-19 的背书，发布会在 Allen 的 Matt's Rancho Martinez 举行——与 Paxton 大选最后一场集会同一场地。Paxton 在北德州郊区 Plano 向支持者讲话，并以 AC/DC 的《Thunderstruck》离场。

## 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That the win proves Trump's judgement against Washington's: "When everyone in Washington told him to abandon me and abandon the people of Texas, he didn't listen." The argument has two audiences — the base (loyalty rewarded) and the general electorate (an outsider who survived the establishment). No policy programme is quoted from the speech.

这场胜利证明 Trump 的判断胜过华盛顿的判断："When everyone in Washington told him to abandon me and abandon the people of Texas, he didn't listen."（译：「当华盛顿所有人都让他抛弃我、抛弃德州人民时，他没有听。」）该论证有两个受众——基本盘（忠诚得到回报）与大选选民（一个挺过建制派的局外人）。演讲未引述任何政策纲领。
