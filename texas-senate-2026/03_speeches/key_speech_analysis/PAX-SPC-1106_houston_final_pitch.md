## 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That the election is a straight comparison of records, and that the opponent's record is a net cost to households: "Everything that I just laid out puts money in your pocket. His entire record takes it out. James Talarico does have a plan for your money. It just doesn't include you." The second argument is nationalisation: a vote for Talarico is a vote for Chuck Schumer to run the Senate, and "if we lose Texas, we lose America." The third is religious-cultural alignment, delivered as a call-and-response litany of beliefs.

这场选举是对两份记录的直截了当的比较，而对手的记录对家庭而言是净成本："Everything that I just laid out puts money in your pocket. His entire record takes it out. James Talarico does have a plan for your money. It just doesn't include you."（译：「我刚才列出的每一项，都是往你口袋里装钱。他的全部记录则是在把钱拿走。James Talarico 对你的钱确实有个方案，只是方案里不包括你。」）第二个论证是全国化：投 Talarico 就是投 Chuck Schumer 去执掌参议院，而 "if we lose Texas, we lose America"（译：「如果我们失去德州，我们就失去美国」）。第三是宗教—文化上的契合，以一问一答的信念连祷方式呈现。
