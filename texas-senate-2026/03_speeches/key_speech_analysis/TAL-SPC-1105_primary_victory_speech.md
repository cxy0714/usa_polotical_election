## 1. Speech context / 演讲背景

Talarico won the Democratic primary for U.S. Senate on 2026-03-03, defeating a field that included U.S. Rep. Jasmine Crockett (who had led in some February polling) and former U.S. Rep. Colin Allred ([SRC-0052](https://www.yerepouni-news.com/she-cant-win-he-can-inside-james-talaricos-faith-based-bid-to-flip-texas/), [SRC-0119](https://apnews.com/article/james-talarico-texas-senate-cornyn-crockett-08c8716aed7e66c29d7e29f2c035ac5d)). Talarico 于 2026-03-03 赢得美国参议院民主党初选，击败的对手包括美国众议员 Jasmine Crockett（曾在 2 月部分民调中领先）与前美国众议员 Colin Allred（[SRC-0052](https://www.yerepouni-news.com/she-cant-win-he-can-inside-james-talaricos-faith-based-bid-to-flip-texas/)、[SRC-0119](https://apnews.com/article/james-talarico-texas-senate-cornyn-crockett-08c8716aed7e66c29d7e29f2c035ac5d)）。同夜，共和党一侧被迫进入 Cornyn–Paxton（科宁–帕克斯顿）决选——Talarico 此前曾预言，这会让共和党提名人变弱（《TIME》引他在投票前说："They're going to be even weaker after a bloody Republican primary that goes to a runoff"（译：「经过一场打到决选的腥风血雨式共和党初选，他们会更弱。」））（[SRC-0052](https://www.yerepouni-news.com/she-cant-win-he-can-inside-james-talaricos-faith-based-bid-to-flip-texas/)）。
