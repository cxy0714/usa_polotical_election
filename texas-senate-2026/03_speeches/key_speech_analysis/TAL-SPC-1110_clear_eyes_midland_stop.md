## 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That the election is in its final play and that the opponent's money advantage is the last obstacle to a coalition of "Texans from all backgrounds and beliefs." The speech converts the FNL motto into an electoral-clock argument: "There is a moment in every game, the last play of the last drive where you have to leave it all on the field. Midland, we are in that moment." The remedy offered is an anti-corruption programme: overturning Citizens United, banning corporate and super PACs, banning congressional stock trading, ending gerrymandering, and term limits.

这场选举已进入最后一次进攻，而对手的资金优势是 "Texans from all backgrounds and beliefs"（译：「来自各种背景与信仰的德州人」）联盟的最后障碍。演讲把《周五夜灯光》的箴言转化为选举时钟式论证："There is a moment in every game, the last play of the last drive where you have to leave it all on the field. Midland, we are in that moment."（译：「每场比赛都有这样一个时刻——最后一次进攻的最后一档——你必须把一切都留在场上。Midland，我们就处在那个时刻。」）所提处方是反腐方案：推翻 Citizens United、禁止企业 PAC 与超级 PAC、禁止国会议员炒股、终结不公正划分选区、实行任期限制。
