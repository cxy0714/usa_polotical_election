## 1. Speech context / 演讲背景

Announced five days before early voting, the tour was the Paxton campaign's closing-argument vehicle: an 18-day, statewide bus circuit originally slated to end with a rally at the Alamo on 2026-11-02 ([SRC-0004](https://docklinemagazine.com/politics-government/paxton-and-talarico-hit-the-road-on-dueling-bus-tours-as-early-voting-begins), [SRC-0002](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/ken-paxton-appears-cancel-final-rally-his-come-and-take-it-tour-alamo)). The slogan borrows the 1835 Gonzales "Come and Take It" flag — a Texas Revolution artifact — and converts a defiance story into a modern anti-federal, anti-regulation posture. Dock Line's illustration note explicitly identifies "a replica of the 1835 Gonzales 'Come and Take It' flag" as the tour's visual signature.

该巡游于提前投票前五天宣布，是 Paxton 竞选的收官论证载体：一场为期 18 天的全州巴士巡回，原定以 2026-11-02 在 Alamo（阿拉莫）的集会收尾（[SRC-0004](https://docklinemagazine.com/politics-government/paxton-and-talarico-hit-the-road-on-dueling-bus-tours-as-early-voting-begins)、[SRC-0002](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/ken-paxton-appears-cancel-final-rally-his-come-and-take-it-tour-alamo)）。口号借用 1835 年 Gonzales（冈萨雷斯）的 "Come and Take It"（译：「有种来拿」）旗帜——一件德州革命文物——把一段反抗故事转化为现代的反联邦、反监管姿态。Dock Line 的插图说明明确指出 "a replica of the 1835 Gonzales 'Come and Take It' flag"（译：「一面 1835 年冈萨雷斯『有种来拿』旗的复制品」）是该巡游的视觉标志。

## 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That Texas history authorises his posture: "In Texas, 'come and take it' has never been a slogan. It's a promise." The closing message paired this with a record-based claim — that as attorney general he sued opioid companies, Big Tech and the Biden administration, so "when they come after Texans, Ken Paxton goes after them" ([SRC-0004](https://docklinemagazine.com/politics-government/paxton-and-talarico-hit-the-road-on-dueling-bus-tours-as-early-voting-begins), [SRC-0002](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/ken-paxton-appears-cancel-final-rally-his-come-and-take-it-tour-alamo)). The argument's structure is: defiance is a Texas inheritance; I have practised it in office; therefore send me to Washington.

德州历史为其姿态背书："In Texas, 'come and take it' has never been a slogan. It's a promise."（译：「在德州，『有种来拿』从来不是一句口号，而是一个承诺。」）收官讯息把这一点与基于记录的声明配对——作为检察总长，他起诉过阿片类药物企业、大型科技公司与拜登政府，因此 "when they come after Texans, Ken Paxton goes after them"（译：「当他们冲着德州人来的时候，Ken Paxton 就会冲着他们去」）（[SRC-0004](https://docklinemagazine.com/politics-government/paxton-and-talarico-hit-the-road-on-dueling-bus-tours-as-early-voting-begins)、[SRC-0002](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/ken-paxton-appears-cancel-final-rally-his-come-and-take-it-tour-alamo)）。论证结构是：反抗是德州的传承；我在任内实践过它；因此把我送进华盛顿。
