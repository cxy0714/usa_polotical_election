## 1. Speech context / 演讲背景

The campaign's final scheduled event — 220 miles north of Talarico's Austin finale the same night — held at a Tex-Mex restaurant where Trump had endorsed Paxton in May ([SRC-0129](https://s.chron.com/politics/article/paxton-cornyn-texan-senate-runoff-22272654.php), [SRC-0049](https://cmf.chron.com/texas/article/paxton-talarico-campaign-last-words-22508736.php)). The Washington Examiner's framing is that Paxton was addressing "an anxious GOP" — the result by then expected to be close and the state's 32-year Republican streak described by Paxton himself as hanging by a thread.

这是竞选活动的最后一场预定活动——位于同一晚 Talarico 在 Austin（奥斯汀）收官场以北 220 英里——地点是一家 Tex-Mex 餐厅，Trump 曾于 5 月在此为 Paxton 背书（[SRC-0129](https://s.chron.com/politics/article/paxton-cornyn-texan-senate-runoff-22272654.php)、[SRC-0049](https://cmf.chron.com/texas/article/paxton-talarico-campaign-last-words-22508736.php)）。《Washington Examiner》的框架是：Paxton 面对的是 "an anxious GOP"（译：「一个焦虑的共和党」）——当时预期结果接近，而该州 32 年的共和党连胜被 Paxton 本人形容为系于一线。

## 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That Texas itself is at risk: "Texas has never been this close to slipping away. We have never been this close to losing everything that we hold here: our jobs, our livelihoods, our way of life in Texas." The conditional closes it: "If Texas falls, we don't have another Texas to move to." The practical ask (documented by Chron) was to canvass contacts "until the last minute."

德州本身处于风险之中："Texas has never been this close to slipping away. We have never been this close to losing everything that we hold here: our jobs, our livelihoods, our way of life in Texas."（译：「德州从未如此接近滑落。我们从未如此接近失去我们在此拥有的一切：我们的工作、我们的生计、我们在德州的生活方式。」）以条件句收束："If Texas falls, we don't have another Texas to move to."（译：「如果德州失守，我们没有另一个德州可搬去。」）实际请求（据 Chron 记录）是扫街联系熟人 "until the last minute"（译：「直到最后一分钟」）。
