## 1. Speech context / 演讲背景

The first stop of the 18-day tour, launched in a North Texas exurban airport — the same corridor where Talarico would launch his own tour the next day in Plano. Both campaigns chose North Texas suburbs, and Dock Line (citing the Dallas Morning News) reads this as a region both campaigns were counting on. The venue type — an airport, not a church or a barbecue joint — is unusual for Texas retail politics and tends to produce a rally-of-the-base atmosphere rather than a swing-voter event.

## 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That the election is a national-security and civilisational contest, not a normal Senate race: "This race is about defending our country and this great state of Texas." The second reported line — "We will be out working, but we need your prayers" — shifts agency onto the audience: the campaign will work, but the outcome depends on supporters' spiritual and electoral labour.

这场选举事关国家安全与文明存续，而非一场普通的参议院选举："This race is about defending our country and this great state of Texas."（译：「这场选举关乎保卫我们的国家和伟大的德州。」）被报道的第二句——"We will be out working, but we need your prayers"（译：「我们会一直在外奔走，但我们需要你们的祈祷」）——把行动主体转移到听众身上：竞选团队会努力工作，但结果取决于支持者的精神与选举劳动。
