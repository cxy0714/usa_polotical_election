## 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That American (and Texan) politics is divided along a vertical, not horizontal, axis. The announcement's central claim, as quoted by Spectrum News, is that "the biggest divide in our country is not left vs. right — it's top vs. bottom," and that money is powerful but beatable: "Big Money is powerful, but it's nothing compared to people power." The candidacy is therefore framed as a transfer of power from donors to wage-earners.

美国（以及德州）政治的分界线是纵向的，而非横向的。据 Spectrum News 引述，宣布的核心主张是 "the biggest divide in our country is not left vs. right — it's top vs. bottom"（译：「我们国家最大的分歧不是左与右，而是上与下」），并且金钱虽强却可被击败："Big Money is powerful, but it's nothing compared to people power."（译：「大钱很有力量，但与人民的力量相比不值一提。」）因此这场参选被框定为把权力从金主转移给工薪者。
