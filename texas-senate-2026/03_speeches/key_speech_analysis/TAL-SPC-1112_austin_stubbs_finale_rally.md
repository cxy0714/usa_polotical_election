## 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That Paxton is not an ideological threat but a social nuisance who has been freeloading on Texans — and that the audience should stop "picking up his tab." The barfly analogy converts an abstract corruption argument into a familiar Texas social type: the loud regular who exploits everyone's goodwill. The implicit policy content of that metaphor — someone else pays for his consumption — maps onto the campaign's earlier claims about public money and donor money.

Paxton 不是意识形态上的威胁，而是一个一直在揩德州人油的社交麻烦——听众应当停止 "picking up his tab"（译：「替他买单」）。「酒吧常客」类比把抽象的反腐论证转化为一种熟悉的德州社会类型：那个嗓门很大、占尽所有人好意的常客。该隐喻隐含的政策内容——由别人为他的消费付账——对应竞选早先关于公款与金主资金的主张。
