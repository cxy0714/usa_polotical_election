## 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That Texas needs a different kind of senator — one who will "stand up and fight for Republican values," "fight for the values of the people of Texas," and "support Donald Trump in the areas that he's focused on." The argument is a substitution claim: Cornyn occupies the seat but does not fight; Paxton, as attorney general, has fought (the campaign's later shorthand: "When they come after Texans, Ken Paxton goes after them").

德州需要另一种参议员——一个 "stand up and fight for Republican values"（译：「站出来为共和党价值观而战」）、"fight for the values of the people of Texas"（译：「为德州人民的价值观而战」）、并 "support Donald Trump in the areas that he's focused on"（译：「在 Donald Trump 关注的领域支持他」）的人。这一论证是替代性主张：Cornyn 占着席位却不战斗；而作为检察总长的 Paxton 一直在战斗（竞选后来的简写："When they come after Texans, Ken Paxton goes after them"（译：「当他们冲着德州人来的时候，Ken Paxton 就会冲着他们去」））。
