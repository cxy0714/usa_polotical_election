## 1. Speech context / 演讲背景

Paxton lost to Talarico after the AP called the race around 22:04; he conceded roughly fifteen minutes later to supporters gathered in Dallas ([SRC-0113](https://www.houstonpublicmedia.org/articles/news/politics/election-2026/2026/11/03/565938/talarico-paxton-senate-race-texas-results/)). This ended a personal streak — Paxton had not lost a race since 2002 — and closed a campaign in which he had never debated his opponent. Audience reaction in the room is documented as raw: supporters shouted "No!" and formed a prayer circle as the result came in ([SRC-0140](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/ken-paxton-supporters-prayer-circle-james-talarico-texas-senate-win-1830078)).

在 AP 于约 22:04 判定该选战结果后，Paxton 输给了 Talarico；他大约十五分钟后向聚集在 Dallas（达拉斯）的支持者承认败选（[SRC-0113](https://www.houstonpublicmedia.org/articles/news/politics/election-2026/2026/11/03/565938/talarico-paxton-senate-race-texas-results/)）。这终结了一段个人连胜——Paxton 自 2002 年以来未输过任何一场选举——并为一场他从未与对手辩论过的竞选画上句号。会场内的听众反应被记录为相当赤裸：支持者高喊 "No!"（译：「不！」），并在结果传来时围成祈祷圈（[SRC-0140](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/ken-paxton-supporters-prayer-circle-james-talarico-texas-senate-win-1830078)）。
