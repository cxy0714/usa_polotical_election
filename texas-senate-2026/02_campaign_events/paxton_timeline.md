The cycle divides cleanly into three parts:

本轮竞选可清晰划分为三部分：

1. **A television-first launch** (2025-04-08) with no rally — the bid was announced on Fox News' *The Ingraham Angle* after a campaign website went live.
2. **A primary and runoff fought mostly through paid media and surrogates**; the only documented discrete events before May are a McKinney appearance (2026-05-19) and a Tyler runoff stop the same day.
3. **A closing bus tour branded "Come and Take It,"** announced 2026-10-14, launched Rockwall 2026-10-17, advertised to end at the Alamo on 2026-11-02 — and then abandoned at the Alamo.

1. **以电视为优先的启动方式**（2025-04-08），没有集会——在竞选网站上线后，其参选通过福克斯新闻的 *The Ingraham Angle* 节目宣布。
2. **初选与决选主要依靠付费媒体与代理人推进**；5 月之前有据可查的独立活动只有 McKinney 的一次露面（2026-05-19）以及同日的一场 Tyler 决选站点。
3. **以「Come and Take It」为品牌的收官巴士巡游**，于 2026-10-14 公布，2026-10-17 在 Rockwall 启动，对外宣称将于 2026-11-02 在 Alamo 收官——随后却在 Alamo 处被放弃。

## 7. Debate record (Paxton side) / 七、辩论记录（Paxton 一侧）

Paxton's only on-record answer came on **2026-10-12** on Fox News' *The Big Weekend with Johnny "Joey" Jones*: "Well, now look, we've been looking on that for months with him and, of course, and we can never quite work out the details because he always wants something we can't do"（译：你看，我们跟他在这件事上已经谈了好几个月，可我们始终无法把细节谈拢，因为他总是提出我们做不到的要求） — then he pivoted to Talarico's two-week flu absence: "two weeks is long time to be away with the flu and is just a very strange time to disappear."（译：因流感离开两周是很长的时间，而且在这个时间点消失实在很奇怪。）

未举行任何辩论。Paxton 唯一有记录的回应出现在 **2026-10-12** 福克斯新闻 *The Big Weekend with Johnny "Joey" Jones* 节目中：「你看，我们跟他在这件事上已经谈了好几个月，可我们始终无法把细节谈拢，因为他总是提出我们做不到的要求」（译）——随后他转向 Talarico 两周因病缺勤一事：「因流感离开两周是很长的时间，而且在这个时间点消失实在很奇怪。」（译）
