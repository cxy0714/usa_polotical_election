# Event Coverage Gaps and Source Conflicts / 竞选活动报道缺口与来源冲突

Scope: [`campaign_events.csv`](campaign_events.csv) (93 rows — **49 TALARICO / 44 PAXTON**, earliest 2025-04-08, latest 2026-11-09), [`talarico_timeline.md`](talarico_timeline.md), [`paxton_timeline.md`](paxton_timeline.md).

范围： [`campaign_events.csv`](campaign_events.csv)（93 行——**49 行 TALARICO / 44 行 PAXTON**，最早 2025-04-08，最晚 2026-11-09）、[`talarico_timeline.md`](talarico_timeline.md)、[`paxton_timeline.md`](paxton_timeline.md)。

**Headline coverage asymmetry:** the Talarico record is *deeper at the level of individual stops* (16 tour stops carry a venue, a time or a post-event report); the Paxton record is *deeper at the level of cancellations and surrogate events* (five cancelled/rescheduled tour stops, eight surrogate events). Neither candidate's record is complete, and the reasons differ — see §1 and §2.

**总体报道不对称性：**Talarico 一侧的记录*在单个站点层面的深度更强*（16 个巡游站点带有场馆、时间或事后报道）；Paxton 一侧的记录*在取消事件与代理助选事件层面的深度更强*（五个被取消或改期的巡游站点、八场代理助选活动）。两位候选人的记录都不完整，但原因不同——见第 1 节与第 2 节。

## 3. Source conflicts (recorded, not resolved) / 来源冲突（已记录，未解决）

### Conflict 1 — Did Trump campaign in person or virtually for Paxton? / 冲突一——特朗普是为 Paxton 亲自到场助选，还是以线上方式助选？

- **In-person record:** Politico photo caption places Trump with Paxton and Ted Cruz at Peterbilt Motors, Denton, 2026-10-01 [SRC-0033]. USA Today Network reports Trump rallies in **Corpus Christi and Lubbock on 2026-10-27** with named guest lists and Paxton among attendees [SRC-0034]. Reuters calls 2026-10-27 Trump's "third Texas trip in a month"（译：「一个月内第三次德州之行」） [SRC-0056]; the NYT live blog headline says "third time this month"（译：「本月第三次」） [SRC-0035]. KLTV documents a **JD Vance** Tyler rally (2026-10-13) [SRC-0012] and The Hill a Vance Weatherford rally (2026-10-28) [SRC-0036].
- **亲自到场的记录：** Politico 的照片说明显示，特朗普于 2026-10-01 在 Denton 的 Peterbilt Motors 与 Paxton、Ted Cruz 同场 [SRC-0033]。USA Today Network 报道特朗普于 **2026-10-27 在 Corpus Christi 与 Lubbock** 举行集会，附有具名嘉宾名单，Paxton 亦在出席者之列 [SRC-0034]。路透社称 2026-10-27 是特朗普「一个月内第三次德州之行」[SRC-0056]；《纽约时报》直播博客标题写作「本月第三次」[SRC-0035]。KLTV 记录了 **JD Vance** 在 Tyler 的集会（2026-10-13）[SRC-0012]，《The Hill》记录了 Vance 在 Weatherford 的集会（2026-10-28）[SRC-0036]。
- **Virtual claim:** the Toronto Star reported Trump would campaign for Paxton virtually rather than in person (Lead-supplied baseline).
- **线上助选的说法：** 《多伦多星报》报道称，特朗普将以线上方式而非亲自到场为 Paxton 助选（由团队负责人提供的基线信息）。
- **Resolution adopted:** these are not mutually exclusive. The virtual-reporting claim may describe a late-race decision or a specific planned appearance; the documented record shows multiple in-person Texas rallies with Paxton in the room. **Both claims are dated in this archive; neither is adopted as the overall characterisation.** A researcher should re-check the Toronto Star date before using it.
- **本档采用的处置：** 两种说法并不互斥。有关线上助选的报道可能描述的是一项临近选举的决定，或某一次具体安排的露面；而有据可查的记录显示多场特朗普亲自出席、Paxton 在场的德州集会。**本档案对两种说法都标注了出处与日期；两者均未被采纳为对整体情况的定性。** 使用前研究者应复核《多伦多星报》的报道日期。
