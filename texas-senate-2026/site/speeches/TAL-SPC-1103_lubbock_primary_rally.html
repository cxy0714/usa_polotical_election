Talarico paired a church sermon with a partisan rally in the same West Texas city during the closing weeks of the Democratic primary. TIME's correspondent, travelling with him, reports the rally audience response and several verbatim passages about the political system, social-media algorithms and a "politics of love" (SRC-0052). 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That the anger and division of U.S. politics is manufactured by an oligopoly that profits from it. In the reported passage, Talarico argues that "it is the system itself," that "a handful of billionaires have redesigned our politics for their own profit," and that social-media algorithms are "professional wrestling… They want us to keep scrolling." The remedy is relational: "Not a politics of fear, not a politics of division, but a politics of love." He then extends an explicit invitation to three groups: the politically disengaged, Democrats tired of folding, and Trump voters fed up with "the extremism and the corruption."

美国政治的愤怒与分裂，是被一个从中获利的寡头结构制造出来的。在已报道段落中，Talarico 主张 "it is the system itself"（译：「问题就在体制本身」），指出 "a handful of billionaires have redesigned our politics for their own profit"（译：「一小撮亿万富翁为自身利润重新设计了我们的政治」），并把社交媒体算法比作 "professional wrestling… They want us to keep scrolling."（译：「职业摔角……他们希望我们一直往下滑。」）处方是关系性的："Not a politics of fear, not a politics of division, but a politics of love."（译：「不是恐惧的政治，不是分裂的政治，而是爱的政治。」）随后他向三类群体发出明确邀请：对政治冷漠者、厌倦一再退让的民主党人，以及厌倦 "the extremism and the corruption"（译：「极端主义与腐败」）的 Trump 选民。
