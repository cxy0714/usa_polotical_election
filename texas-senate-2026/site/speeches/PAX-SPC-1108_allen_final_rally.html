1. Speech context / 演讲背景

The campaign's final scheduled event — 220 miles north of Talarico's Austin finale the same night — held at a Tex-Mex restaurant where Trump had endorsed Paxton in May (SRC-0129, SRC-0049). 2. Main argument / 主要论点

That Texas itself is at risk: "Texas has never been this close to slipping away. We have never been this close to losing everything that we hold here: our jobs, our livelihoods, our way of life in Texas." The conditional closes it: "If Texas falls, we don't have another Texas to move to." The practical ask (documented by Chron) was to canvass contacts "until the last minute."

德州本身处于风险之中："Texas has never been this close to slipping away. We have never been this close to losing everything that we hold here: our jobs, our livelihoods, our way of life in Texas."（译：「德州从未如此接近滑落。我们从未如此接近失去我们在此拥有的一切：我们的工作、我们的生计、我们在德州的生活方式。」）以条件句收束："If Texas falls, we don't have another Texas to move to."（译：「如果德州失守，我们没有另一个德州可搬去。」）实际请求（据 Chron 记录）是扫街联系熟人 "until the last minute"（译：「直到最后一分钟」）。
