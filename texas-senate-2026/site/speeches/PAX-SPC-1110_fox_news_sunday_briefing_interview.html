2. Main argument / 主要论点

Three claims: (a) the polls are partly wrong — "We have our own polling that suggests maybe a little different result"; (b) the race is nonetheless close — "we definitely have a close race," because midterm elections are hard for the party in power; (c) the fundamentals favour him — "the reality is that this is a Republican state. If Republicans get out to vote, we're going to win this race." He adds an attack: Talarico's eight-year record has not been "exposed" because "the media doesn't cover it."

三项主张：（a）民调部分有误——"We have our own polling that suggests maybe a little different result"（译：「我们自己的民调显示，结果或许略有不同」）；（b）选情尽管如此仍然接近——"we definitely have a close race,"（译：「我们确实是一场接近的选战」）因为中期选举对执政党本就艰难；（c）基本面有利于他——"the reality is that this is a Republican state. If Republicans get out to vote, we're going to win this race."（译：「现实是，这是一个共和党州。只要共和党人出来投票，我们就会赢下这场选举。」）他还加了一项攻击：Talarico 八年记录之所以未被 "exposed"（译：「揭露」），是因为 "the media doesn't cover it."（译：「媒体不报道它。」）
