1. Speech context / 演讲背景

East Texas is Paxton's home region and one of the most Republican areas of the state; a Talarico rally in Tyler is therefore a "contested turf" event rather than base maintenance. The rally was announced in advance and tied to the start of early voting (SRC-0120, SRC-0010). A Spanish-language KLTV report describes "hundreds of East Texans waiting for hours" to see him; that is headline-level information only (see §9). The same day Talarico sat for an interview with MS NOW's Katy Tur immediately after a rally, telling her "you had two rallies today — this is the second one" (SRC-0126, [00:00:42]–[00:00:44]).

东德州是 Paxton 的老家，也是全州共和党色彩最浓的地区之一；因此在 Tyler 举行 Talarico 集会，属于「争夺地盘」型活动，而非巩固基本盘。该集会事先公告，并与提前投票启动相绑定（SRC-0120、SRC-0010）。一则西班牙语 KLTV 报道称 "hundreds of East Texans waiting for hours"（译：「数百名东德州人等了好几个小时」）来见他；这仅为标题层面信息（见 §9）。同一天，Talarico 在集会结束后立即接受 MS NOW 的 Katy Tur（凯蒂·特）采访，对她说 "you had two rallies today — this is the second one"（译：「你今天有两场集会——这是第二场」）（SRC-0126，[00:00:42]–[00:00:44]）。
