1. Speech context / 演讲背景

A late addition to the tour schedule: the original Tyler stop had been cancelled because of Vice President JD Vance's visit to Texas, and the campaign re-added it for the final Sunday (SRC-0062, SRC-0011). East Texas is Paxton's home region and one of the state's most Republican areas, so this is base-consolidation rather than persuasion. KLTV also reports protesters outside, including a longtime Republican who said she had changed her vote "up and down the ticket" — an unusually vivid counter-signal for a rally in the candidate's own region.

这是巡游日程的临时增补：原本的 Tyler 一站因副总统 JD Vance 访问德州而被取消，竞选团队为最后一个星期日重新加回（SRC-0062、SRC-0011）。东德州是 Paxton 的家乡地区，也是全州共和党色彩最浓的地区之一，因此此举属巩固基本盘而非说服。KLTV 还报道场外有抗议者，其中包括一位长期共和党人，她说自己已把选票 "up and down the ticket"（译：「从上到下、整张选票」）都改了——对于在候选人本地区的集会而言，这是一个异常鲜明的反向信号。

2. Main argument / 主要论点

That the election is a fork in the road on three concrete dimensions: energy (Texas oil and gas versus "a green new deal that kills the industries that have helped build this state"), the judiciary (a Constitution-following Supreme Court versus "a court packed with radicals"), and Senate control ("not just for two years but for years beyond"). Platform deliverables named: passing the SAVE America Act and making Trump Accounts permanent.

这场选举在三个具体维度上是岔路口：能源（德州石油与天然气，对比 "a green new deal that kills the industries that have helped build this state"（译：「一项扼杀那些帮助建设本州产业的绿色新政」））、司法（遵循宪法的最高法院，对比 "a court packed with radicals"（译：「塞满激进分子的法院」）），以及参议院控制权（"not just for two years but for years beyond"（译：「不只是两年，而是此后多年」））。点名的政纲交付项：通过 SAVE America Act，并使 Trump Accounts 永久化。
