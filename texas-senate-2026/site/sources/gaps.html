Research Gaps / 研究缺口

§14 of the brief requires an explicit register of what could not be located, what is missing, what is uncertain and what conflicts. This file is that register, merged from the Lead's own findings and the five agents' staging registers (_staging/A_gaps.md, B_gaps.md, D_gaps.md, E_gaps.md; no C_gaps.md exists because Agent C produced no output and the Lead completed the visual scope — see §7).

任务书第 14 项要求开列一份明确清单，说明哪些材料未能找到、哪些缺失、哪些不确定、哪些彼此冲突。本文件即该清单，由主导研究者的发现与五条代理线索的阶段登记合并而成（_staging/A_gaps.md、B_gaps.md、D_gaps.md、E_gaps.md；不存在 C_gaps.md，因为负责影像的代理 C 未产出任何结果，其范围由主导研究者接手完成——见第 7 节）。

Nothing here should be read as claiming the campaign was not documented where this file says "not retrieved." The distinction is between absent from the record and absent from what our tools could reach.

本文件任何一处"未能检索到"都不等于"该竞选活动没有记录"。 必须区分两种情形：记录中本就不存在，与*我们的检索工具无法触及*。

2. Election results and electoral data / 选举结果与选举数据

  1. County-level results — county_results.csv is intentionally EMPTY (header only). County and precinct tabulation was published through JavaScript-rendered interactives that the retrieval tool could not execute. The Wikipedia race article contains only county maps (SVG), not a county table. This remains the single largest data gap in the archive. A researcher with a browser, or the Texas Secretary of State county canvass returns, can close it directly.

县级结果——county_results.csv 有意为空（仅表头）。 县与选区级统计通过 JavaScript 渲染的交互页面发布，检索工具无法执行脚本；维基百科条目只有县级*地图*（SVG），没有县级数据表。这是本档案最大的单项数据缺口。有浏览器的研究者，或德州州务卿的县级选举认证回报，可直接补齐。

  1. Certified totals — partially closed. Final figures were obtained from the certified-result aggregate in the Wikipedia race article via its raw wikitext: Talarico 5,447,069 (55.06%), Paxton 4,250,664 (42.97%). These are recorded in election_results.csv as partially verified because the aggregator, not the official canvass, is the source. The Texas Secretary of State canvass has still not been read directly.

认证总票数——部分补齐。 最终数字取自维基百科该选举条目的认证结果汇总（经原始 wikitext 抓取）：塔拉里科 5,447,069 票（55.06%）、帕克斯顿 4,250,664 票（42.97%）。因来源是汇总方而非官方认证，election_results.csv 将其标为 partially verified（部分核实）。德州州务卿的官方认证仍未直接读取。

  1. Certification status. Whether the state canvass had been certified as of 2026-11-10 is unknown. The statutory procedure is documented (SOS canvassing guidance) but no 2026 certification record was retrieved.

认证状态。 截至 2026-11-10 州级认证是否完成，未能查明。法定程序有据可查（州务卿认证指引），但未检索到 2026 年的认证记录。

  1. Minor-party / independent candidates. Not established. The two major-party percentages sum to 98.03%, implying ~2% went elsewhere or was undervoted, but this was not verified; election_results.csv carries an explicit unverified row.

小党与独立候选人。 未能确立。两大党得票率合计 98.03%，意味着约 2% 流向其他候选人或为废票，但未核实；election_results.csv 中有一条明确的 unverified（未核实）行。

  1. Daily early-vote returns. Early voting began 2026-10-19 and "more than 7.7 million" Texans voted early, but no daily county-level series was retrieved.

提前投票逐日数据。 提前投票于 2026-10-19 开始，"超过 770 万"德州人提前投票，但未检索到县级逐日序列数据。

  1. Democratic primary — final figure now known, earlier reporting differed. Final Talarico share 52.4% (certified-result aggregate); an earlier count at 91% reporting had 53%. Crockett's 45.7% is from that same partial count and does not exactly complement 52.4%; treated as approximate.

民主党初选——最终数字已确立，早期报道不同。 塔拉里科最终得票率 52.4%（认证结果汇总）；此前计票 91% 时的报道为 53%。克罗克特的 45.7% 来自同一次部分计票，与 52.4% 并非严格互补；按近似值处理。

  1. GOP primary margin conflict — UNRESOLVED. News From The States (AP, 93% counted) reported Cornyn 41.9% / Paxton 40.8% (1.1-point lead). NY Post reported Cornyn "topped Paxton by 2.5 percentage points." Both recorded; certified margin unknown. Note the aggregate polling going into the primary had Paxton ahead by 3.5–6 points, so the first-round ordering was itself a polling miss.

共和党初选差距冲突——未解决。 News From The States（美联社，计票 93%）报道科宁 41.9%、帕克斯顿 40.8%（领先 1.1 个百分点）。《纽约邮报》称科宁"以 2.5 个百分点领先帕克斯顿"（译）。两者均予记录；认证差距未查明。值得注意的是，初选前的汇总民调显示*帕克斯顿*领先 3.5–6 个百分点，因此首轮排序本身就是一次民调失准。

  1. Runoff — final figure now known. 63.8%, a margin of more than 25 points (certified-result aggregate), replacing the morning-after projection of "exceeding 60 percent." Vote totals were not retrieved.

决选——最终数字已确立。 63.8%，领先超过 25 个百分点（认证结果汇总），取代了选后次日"超过 60%"的预测。得票总数未检索到。

  1. "Drought" framing conflicts. "First Democratic Senate win since 1988" (Lloyd Bentsen; BBC/AP/NYT), "first statewide win since 1994" (San Angelo LIVE), and "three-decade drought" (NYT headline) all appear. The likeliest reconciliation is Senate seat vs any statewide office, but it is not confirmed. Do not use one figure without stating which baseline.

"干旱期"表述冲突。 "自 1988 年以来首位民主党参议员胜选"（劳埃德·本特森；BBC／美联社／《纽约时报》）、"自 1994 年以来首次全州性胜利"（San Angelo LIVE）、"三十年空白"（《纽约时报》标题）三种说法并存。最可能的调和是*参议院席位*与*任何全州公职*之别，但未获确认。引用任一数字时必须说明基线口径。

3. Campaign events / 竞选活动

  1. Tour stop completeness — improved but still short. Paxton's "Come and Take It" tour was announced as 18 days / 30+ stops; 15 of 19 named cities remain schedule-only with no date because stops were posted to Eventbrite and then removed. Talarico's "Clear Eyes, Full Hearts" tour is documented at 12 held stops with evidence out of 20 announced cities, plus 11 schedule-only items (Fort Bend, Galveston, San Antonio, Weslaco etc. have no date at all). Closure route: the campaigns' Eventbrite pages and local outlets.

巡回站点完整性——有改善但仍不完整。 帕克斯顿的 "Come and Take It" 巡回宣传宣布为 18 天／30 余站；19 个具名城市中有 15 个仅有日程安排而无日期，因为站点信息曾发布在 Eventbrite 上随后被撤下。塔拉里科的 "Clear Eyes, Full Hearts" 巡回宣传在 20 个宣布城市中有 12 站有证据证明实际举行，另有 11 条仅有日程记录（Fort Bend、Galveston、San Antonio、Weslaco 等完全没有日期）。补齐路径： 两阵营的 Eventbrite 页面与地方媒体。

  1. No candidate-vs-candidate debate occurred. There is no debate transcript, no joint stage, no head-to-head video. The archive documents the absence and its negotiation chronology (five debate_invitation rows). The CNN–Jubilee event was a solo voter Q&A, not a debate — several outlets mislabelled it.

未发生任何候选人对候选人的辩论。 没有辩论文字稿、没有同台、没有一对一视频。本档案记录的是这一*缺席*及其协商过程（五条 debate_invitation 记录）。CNN–Jubilee 那场活动是单人选民问答，并非辩论——多家媒体误标。

  1. "Three debates scheduled" is FALSE and is preserved only as a caution. Washington Examiner (2026-09-02) reported debates scheduled for Sept 22 / Oct 6 / Oct 13; none happened. Do not place them in any event table (_staging/E_gaps.md §7).

"三场已排定辩论"为不实信息，仅作为警示保留。 《华盛顿观察家报》（2026-09-02）报道辩论排定于 9 月 22 日、10 月 6 日、10 月 13 日；三场均未发生。不得将其写入任何事件表（_staging/E_gaps.md 第 7 节）。

  1. Talarico's two-week absence from the trail (early October, reported as flu) is documented by NYT/NY Post/CNN but the exact start and end dates are not pinned. No events were invented for that period — it is logged as an activity gap.

塔拉里科约两周的活动空窗（10 月初，报道称为流感）由《纽约时报》《纽约邮报》与 CNN 记载，但确切起止日期未能锁定。该期间没有编造任何事件——只作为"活动缺口"记录。

  1. Trump "virtual vs in-person" conflict — effectively resolved against "virtual." Four in-person Texas trips with Paxton present are documented (Dallas September; Denton 2026-10-01; San Antonio 2026-10-07; Corpus Christi + Lubbock 2026-10-27). One announced leg — a Waco 18:00 rally on 10-27 — is logged disputed: announced, execution unconfirmed.

特朗普"线上还是线下"之争——实际已否证"仅线上"说法。 有据可查的四次亲赴德州行程均有帕克斯顿在场（9 月达拉斯；2026-10-01 丹顿；2026-10-07 圣安东尼奥；2026-10-27 科珀斯克里斯蒂与拉伯克）。其中一段宣布的行程——10-27 韦科 18:00 集会——记为 disputed（存疑）：已宣布，执行未证实。

  1. Announcement vs occurrence. Several announced events could not be confirmed as having taken place; marked announced or partially verified, never held.

宣布与举行之别。 若干已宣布活动无法证实确实举行；标为 announced（已宣布）或 partially verified（部分核实），绝不标为 held（已举行）。

  1. Nacogdoches ~2026-05-27 rally attribution is ambiguous — a KWTX preview headline concerns Talarico, so no row was created.

纳科多奇斯 2026-05-27 前后的集会归属不明——KWTX 的预告标题指向塔拉里科，因此未建行。

  1. Midland vs Lubbock same-night sequencing is ambiguous. KOSA says Amarillo and Lubbock were "still to come" while the Lubbock A-J reports Lubbock "Tuesday night"; the Midland row is flagged conflicting sources and may be 10-26 rather than 10-27.

米德兰与拉伯克同夜先后顺序不明。 KOSA 称阿马里洛与拉伯克"尚待前往"，而《拉伯克阿瓦兰奇报》称拉伯克为"周二晚"；米德兰一行标为 conflicting sources（来源冲突），日期可能是 10-26 而非 10-27。

  1. Categories that are empty for BOTH campaigns despite targeted search: HQ openings, closed-door fundraisers, labour events, county-party meetings, podcasts, press conferences, election-day GOTV events. These are gaps in the public record, not evidence the activities did not occur.

双方均为空白的类别（尽管做过定向检索）：竞选总部开幕、闭门筹款会、劳工活动、县党部会议、播客、记者会、选举日催票活动。这些是公开记录中的缺口，不能当作"活动未发生"的证据。

  1. April 2026 has no dated campaign event for either candidate — a shared gap.

2026 年 4 月双方都没有带日期的竞选活动——这是共同的缺口。

5. Money — the discrepancies that must never be silently merged / 资金：绝不可悄然合并的口径差异

  1. Four different Talarico "totals" exist and are not the same quantity: campaign announcement $134.3m cumulative; FEC contributions $140,307,408; FEC total receipts $146,349,912; Texas Tribune figure ~$155m including joint fundraising committees. All four are in campaign_finance.csv as separate rows.

塔拉里科存在四种不同的"总额"，并非同一计量对象： 阵营宣布的累计 1.343 亿美元；FEC 捐款 140,307,408 美元；FEC 总收入 146,349,912 美元；《德州论坛报》所载含联合筹款委员会的 约 1.55 亿美元。四者在 campaign_finance.csv 中分列为不同行。

  1. Paxton Q2/Q3 confusion. Campaign figure $18m for Q3 (principal + two JFCs) vs principal committee $10,249,117; Q2 ≈ $9m. One outlet (El Paso Times) mislabels the $18m as second-quarter money; KWTX/Tribune is unambiguous that it is Q3 and doubled Q2. Row flagged conflicting sources.

帕克斯顿第二／第三季度口径混淆。 阵营口径第三季度 1,800 万美元（主委员会加两个联合筹款委员会），主委员会为 10,249,117 美元；第二季度约 900 万美元。一家媒体（《埃尔帕索时报》）把 1,800 万误作第二季度；KWTX／《德州论坛报》明确其为第三季度且为第二季度的两倍。该行标为 conflicting sources。

  1. THE BIGGEST TRAP — FEC Schedule E vs AdImpact are roughly an order of magnitude apart. Example: "Texans for a Conservative Majority" shows $24.96m in FEC independent expenditures opposing Paxton, while Texas PAC shows $182.1m in AdImpact ad spend. These are different measurement constructs, kept in separate, differently typed rows. Never sum them.

最大的陷阱——FEC Schedule E 与 AdImpact 相差约一个数量级。 例："Texans for a Conservative Majority" 的 FEC 独立支出为 2,496 万美元（反对帕克斯顿），而 Texas PAC 的 AdImpact 广告投放为 1.821 亿美元。二者是不同的计量构造，已分列于类型不同的行中。绝不可相加。

  1. Outside-spending totals do not reconcile.
