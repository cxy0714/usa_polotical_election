Scope. This file reconstructs the press narrative of the race: which storylines organised coverage, when they peaked, who pushed them, and where the two campaigns described the same event in opposite terms. It is the narrative companion to media_coverage.csv (row-level evidence), advertisements.csv (paid media) and 06_campaign_finance/campaign_finance.csv (money).

范围。 本文件重建本场竞选的*新闻叙事*：哪些线索组织了报道，各线索何时达到高峰，由谁推动，以及两场竞选在何处用彼此相反的措辞描述同一事件。它是 media_coverage.csv（逐行证据）、advertisements.csv（付费媒体）与 06_campaign_finance/campaign_finance.csv（资金）的叙事配套文件。

Method and limits. Phases are periodisations imposed by the analyst for readability, not claims about what "really" happened. All citations are to _staging/E_sources.csv ids (E-S###) plus the URL. Where only a headline or search snippet was retrievable, the row says so. Opinion, news reporting, campaign statements and fact-checks are labelled throughout; a headline is never treated as a finding.

Phase 0 — Launch and the long primary (April 2025 – February 2026) / 阶段 0——启动与漫长的初选（2025 年 4 月至 2026 年 2 月）

The race began as two separate contests whose coverage rarely intersected.

本场竞选最初是两个彼此分离的选举过程，两者的报道很少交叉。

  • Paxton's principal committee, KEN PAXTON FOR SENATE (C00901918), was registered 2025-04-08, consistent with reporting that he had begun hiring primary consultants [SRC-0158]. His early money was unremarkable: $2.91m raised in 2025-Q2, $1.30m in 2025-Q3 [SRC-0158]. Paxton 的主要竞选委员会 KEN PAXTON FOR SENATE（C00901918） 于 2025-04-08 完成登记，与关于他已开始聘用初选顾问的报道相符 [SRC-0158]。其早期筹款并不突出：2025-Q2 募得 $2.91m，2025-Q3 募得 $1.30m [SRC-0158]。
  • Talarico entered the Democratic primary in September 2025; his committee TALARICO FOR TEXAS (C00919084) was registered 2025-09-09 [SRC-0157]. In his first 23 days he raised $6.27m — more than Paxton had raised in either of his first two quarters [SRC-0157]. Talarico 于 2025 年 9 月 进入民主党初选；其委员会 TALARICO FOR TEXAS（C00919084） 于 2025-09-09 完成登记 [SRC-0157]。在头 23 天里他募得 $6.27m——多于 Paxton 前两个季度中任一季度的募款额 [SRC-0157]。
  • Coverage of the Democratic primary framed Talarico against Rep. Jasmine Crockett; he won in March 2026 [SRC-0161]. On the Republican side the story was Trump's endorsement of Paxton over four-term Sen. John Cornyn — the storyline CNN later bundled into Trump's "month of GOP revenge" [SRC-0172]. 民主党初选的报道把 Talarico 与 众议员 Jasmine Crockett 相对置；他于 2026 年 3 月胜出 [SRC-0161]。共和党一侧的故事则是 Trump 支持 Paxton 而背弃已连任四届的参议员 John Cornyn——CNN 后来把这一线索并入 Trump 的"month of GOP revenge"（译：共和党复仇之月）[SRC-0172]。
  • Trade and local coverage of the primary air war was episodic and negative-ad-centric; the primary alone accounted for $165.7m of the eventual $573m ad total [SRC-0160]. 行业媒体与地方媒体对初选电视广告战的报道是零散的，且以负面广告为中心；仅初选一项就占了最终 $573m 广告总额中的 $165.7m [SRC-0160]。

Narrative function of this phase: it established the two character frames that later dominated — Talarico as the biography-first, small-donor teacher; Paxton as the scandal-defined Trump acolyte.

本阶段的叙事功能： 它确立了此后占据主导的两套人物框架——Talarico 是以个人生平为先、依靠小额捐款的教师；Paxton 是由丑闻定义、追随 Trump 的附从者。

Phase 1 — The runoff and the pivot to the general (May 2026) / 阶段 1——决选与转向大选（2026 年 5 月）

  • On 2026-05-26 Paxton "resoundingly defeated" Cornyn by more than 27 points, the capstone of Trump's May revenge tour (CNN takeaway piece, analysis) [SRC-0172]. The same piece flagged the Republican fear that had already been voiced privately: that Paxton was the weaker general-election candidate. 在 2026-05-26，Paxton "resoundingly defeated"（译：以压倒性优势击败）Cornyn，领先超过 27 个百分点，这是 Trump 五月复仇之旅的顶点（CNN 要点分析稿）[SRC-0172]。同一篇稿件也点出了此前已在私下表达的共和党担忧：Paxton 是大选中较弱的候选人。
  • Paxton's victory speech supplied the race's most durable insult set — "Tofu Talarico," "Six-gender James," "James Tala-freak-o," "Low-T Talarico" — and the framing line "My opponent is the most extreme radical the Democrats have ever nominated" [SRC-0172]. Paxton 的胜选演说提供了本场竞选流传最久的一组侮辱性绰号——"Tofu Talarico"、"Six-gender James"、"James Tala-freak-o"、"Low-T Talarico"（译：「豆腐 Talarico」「六性别 James」「怪胎 James Tala」「低睾酮 Talarico」）——以及那句框架性表述 "My opponent is the most extreme radical the Democrats have ever nominated"（译：我的对手是民主党有史以来提名过的最极端的激进分子）[SRC-0172]。
  • Talarico's campaign raised $600,000 in the first two hours after Paxton's runoff win, per a campaign source [SRC-0172]. 据竞选团队方面的消息来源，Paxton 赢得决选后的头两小时内，Talarico 的竞选团队募得 $600,000 [SRC-0172]。
  • Talarico's campaign released an ad immediately after the runoff highlighting Paxton's scandals (securities-fraud charges, bribery allegations, the 2023 impeachment over the $3.3m whistleblower settlement, his divorce) [SRC-0172]. This is the moment the general-election frame hardened: Paxton's liabilities were to be the centre of the Democratic message. Talarico 的竞选团队在决选后立即发布了一支广告，突出 Paxton 的丑闻（证券欺诈指控、贿赂指控、因 $3.3m 举报人赔偿和解而引发的 2023 年弹劾，以及他的离婚）[SRC-0172]。正是从这一刻起，大选框架固定下来：Paxton 的种种负担将成为民主党信息的中心。
  • A telling press-side detail: the Senate Republicans' campaign arm appeared to have deleted a statement about Paxton's divorce on runoff night [SRC-0172] — an early sign of the establishment's discomfort with its own nominee. 一个能说明问题的新闻侧细节：决选当晚，参议院共和党竞选机构似乎删除了一份关于 Paxton 离婚的声明 [SRC-0172]——这是建制派对自己提名的人选感到不适的早期迹象。

Phase 2 — The money story becomes the story (June – August 2026) / 阶段 2——资金故事成为主线（2026 年 6 月至 8 月）

Coverage in this phase was dominated not by events but by filings and ad buys.

本阶段的报道并非由事件主导，而是由备案文件与广告投放主导。

  • The Texas Tribune's money tracker framed the race structurally: Talarico's small-donor dominance (about half his money from donors giving under $200; 47% of itemized money from inside Texas) versus Paxton's reliance on joint fundraising committees (about 47% of his haul) and large donors (83%+ of contributions) [SRC-0155]. Texas Tribune 的资金追踪报道从结构上勾勒了本场竞选：Talarico 的小额捐款优势（其资金约一半来自单笔捐款低于 $200 的捐赠者；逐项申报资金中有 47% 来自德州境内），对比 Paxton 对联合筹款委员会（约占其募款额的 47%）与大额捐赠者（占捐款的 83% 以上）的依赖 [SRC-0155]。
  • An important structural storyline entered here and was under-covered relative to its importance: the Supreme Court's decision striking down limits on party-coordinated spending, which the Tribune flagged as a Republican equaliser because party committees can take $44,300 per donor per year versus $3,500 for candidate committees [SRC-0155]. 一条重要的结构性线索在此进入，且相对于其重要性而言报道不足：最高法院推翻政党协调支出上限的裁决；Tribune 将其标记为对共和党有利的平衡因素，因为政党委员会可按每位捐赠者每年 $44,300 收取，而候选人委员会仅为 $3,500 [SRC-0155]。
  • On the outside-money front, the Tribune reported Reid Hoffman's $10m to Lone Star Rising in June — nearly 80% of the super PAC's ~$13m Q2 haul — and recorded Crockett's attack on Talarico for embracing dark money despite his anti-corruption message [SRC-0176]. Talarico's response ("has no control over contributions to any super PAC"; he wants to ban super PACs) became a standing claim-and-response pair reused for the rest of the campaign [SRC-0176]. 在外部资金方面，Tribune 报道 Reid Hoffman 于 6 月向 Lone Star Rising 捐赠 $10m——接近该超级政治行动委员会约 $13m 第二季度募款额的 80%——并记录了 Crockett 对 Talarico 的攻击：尽管他以反腐为信息主轴，却接纳暗钱 [SRC-0176]。Talarico 的回应（"has no control over contributions to any super PAC"（译：对任何超级政治行动委员会收到的捐款均无控制权）；他主张禁止超级政治行动委员会）成为一组长期沿用的「指控—回应」配对，在余下的竞选中反复使用 [SRC-0176]。
  • By 2026-08-25, with polls showing Talarico ahead, his allied super PAC Lone Star Rising opened a seven-figure digital/streaming buy framing Paxton as "the most corrupt politician in Texas" [SRC-0163 / MC-0006]. 到 2026-08-25，在民调显示 Talarico 领先的情况下，与其结盟的超级政治行动委员会 Lone Star Rising 启动了七位数的数字／流媒体投放，把 Paxton 塑造为 "the most corrupt politician in Texas"（译：德州最腐败的政客）[SRC-0163 / MC-0006]。
  • 2026-08-29 is the phase's hinge: Talarico aired his first negative television ad, "Most Corrupt," during the TCU–North Carolina football game, after nearly $29m of almost entirely positive advertising — reported by the Texas Tribune as a deliberate phase change, with the comparison to Allred (early negative) and O'Rourke (mid-October negative) [SRC-0161 / MC-0007]. 2026-08-29 是本阶段的转折点：在投放了近 $29m 几乎全为正面的广告之后，Talarico 在 TCU 对北卡罗来纳的橄榄球赛期间播出了他的首支负面电视广告 "Most Corrupt"（译：最腐败）——Texas Tribune 将其报道为一次有意的阶段性转变，并参照 Allred（较早转负面）与 O'Rourke（10 月中旬转负面）作了比较 [SRC-0161 / MC-0007]。
  • In the same story, the Tribune noted that "several allied GOP groups" had begun TV ads attacking Talarico's vote on a bill restricting Texas land sales to China-linked entities [SRC-0161]. Anti-Talarico outside spending was already running before the big Republican rescue. 在同一篇报道中，Tribune 指出 "several allied GOP groups"（译：数个结盟的共和党团体）已开始投放电视广告，攻击 Talarico 对一项限制向与中国有关联实体出售德州土地的法案所投的票 [SRC-0161]。在共和党的大规模救援行动之前，反 Talarico 的外部支出就已经在运作。

Coverage-volume note: the money story generated more distinct national bylines than any single campaign event in the race. It is the one storyline where a filing, not a rally, drove coverage.

报道量说明： 资金叙事所产生的不同全国性署名报道数量，超过本场竞选中任何单一竞选活动。这是唯一一条由*备案文件*而非集会驱动报道的线索。

Coverage-asymmetry note (conventions §8) / 报道不对称说明（规范第 8 节）

Coverage of Paxton in this dataset is thinner than coverage of Talarico in one specific way: Paxton's campaign creative is largely undocumented at the level of titles, scripts and URLs, because the sources retrieved describe the buys ($20m statewide campaign buy, Texas PAC/LSLF placements) rather than the spots. This is recorded rather than padded: advertisements.csv contains a single campaign-flight row (PAX-AD-0001) with unknown fields, and the substantive pro-Paxton creative is documented under EXT- ids where the sponsor is an outside group. The asymmetry reflects both the reporting available and the fact that most pro-Paxton advertising in the general election was paid for by outside groups rather than by the campaign — AdImpact put outside groups at 92% of Paxton's ad support versus 23% of Talarico's [SRC-0160].

本数据集中对 Paxton 的报道在某一特定方面弱于对 Talarico 的报道：Paxton 的竞选广告创意在标题、脚本与 URL 层面基本无据可查，因为取得的来源描述的是*购买行为*（$20m 全州竞选投放、Texas PAC／LSLF 的投放位置），而非具体广告片。这一点被如实记录而非填充：advertisements.csv 中仅有一行竞选团队投放记录（PAX-AD-0001），且其中多个字段为 unknown，而实质性的支持 Paxton 的创意内容记录在以 EXT- 开头的编号之下，其出资方为外部团体。这一不对称既反映了可获得的报道情况，也反映了一个事实：大选阶段支持 Paxton 的广告绝大部分由外部团体而非其竞选团队支付——AdImpact 把外部团体计为 Paxton 广告支持额的 92%，而 Talarico 为 23% [SRC-0160]。
