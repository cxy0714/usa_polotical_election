Origins, education and family / 出身、教育与家庭

Paxton was born at Minot Air Force Base in North Dakota to an Air Force father; the family moved repeatedly through Florida, New York, North Carolina, California and Oklahoma and at one point lived in a trailer, and he graduated from MacArthur High School in Lawton, Oklahoma [SRC-0099]. He has used that background as an economic credential, and it is the closest analogue on his side to Talarico's family narrative.

帕克斯顿出生于北达科他州的迈诺特空军基地，父亲是空军军人；全家先后辗转佛罗里达、纽约、北卡罗来纳、加利福尼亚与俄克拉何马，一度住在拖车里；他毕业于俄克拉何马州劳顿市（Lawton）的麦克阿瑟高中（MacArthur High School）[SRC-0099]。他把这一背景当作经济资历使用，这也是他这一方最接近塔拉里科家族叙事的对应物。

At twelve he nearly lost an eye when a stick penetrated it during a game of hide-and-seek; a misdiagnosis and a later college re-injury left permanent vision problems, which he now cites when discussing healthcare costs [SRC-0102][SRC-0099]. He earned a BA in psychology in 1985 and an MBA in 1986 at Baylor, where he was student government president, then a JD at the University of Virginia in 1991 [SRC-0099]. He practised at Strasburger and Price in Dallas, worked as in-house counsel at JCPenney in Plano, and opened his own practice in McKinney in 2002 doing estate planning, probate and real estate law [SRC-0099]. That business career is documented only in outline in retrieved sources; no client list, firm revenue or litigation portfolio beyond the outline is available in this file.

十二岁时，一根木棍在捉迷藏游戏中刺入他的眼睛，险些失明；一次误诊与后来在大学期间的二次受伤给他留下了永久性视力问题，他如今在讨论医疗成本时会提及此事 [SRC-0102][SRC-0099]。他于1985年在贝勒大学获得心理学学士学位、1986年获得工商管理硕士学位，并曾任学生会主席；1991年在弗吉尼亚大学获得法学博士学位 [SRC-0099]。他先后在达拉斯的斯特拉斯伯格与普莱斯律师事务所（Strasburger and Price）执业、在普莱诺（Plano）担任JCPenney内部法律顾问，并于2002年在麦金尼（McKinney）开设自己的事务所，从事遗产规划、遗嘱认证与房地产法业务 [SRC-0099]。在已获取的来源中，这段商业职业生涯仅有概要性记录；除概要之外，客户名单、事务所收入或诉讼业务组合在本文中均无从查证。

He married Angela Allen in 1986 and they had four children; the couple were founding members of Stonebriar Community Church in Frisco [SRC-0099]. In July 2025 Angela Paxton, by then a state senator, filed for divorce citing biblical grounds and alleging at-fault serial adultery; the filing stated the couple had not lived together since June 2024 [SRC-0099]. These are allegations in a divorce filing, not adjudicated findings, and are recorded here as such. Reporting also finds his declared net worth rose from under $200,000 on taking office to nearly $8 million, and that he claimed three homes as primary residences on mortgages while claiming homestead exemptions on two at the same time [SRC-0099].

他于1986年与安吉拉·艾伦（Angela Allen）结婚，育有四名子女；二人是弗里斯科石桥社区教会的创始成员 [SRC-0099]。2025年7月，已任州参议员的安吉拉·帕克斯顿以圣经依据提出离婚，并在文件中主张对方存在过错性的持续通奸；文件中称二人自2024年6月起未再共同居住 [SRC-0099]。这些是离婚文件中的指控（allegations），而非经裁定的认定，本文据此记录。报道还发现，其申报净资产从就职时不足20万美元增至近800万美元，且在抵押贷款文件中将三处住宅申报为主要居所，同时对其中两处申报了自住房屋税收豁免（homestead exemption）[SRC-0099]。

Legislative career, 2003 to 2015 / 立法生涯，2003至2015年

He won House District 70 in Collin County in 2002, beating Bill Vitz in a primary runoff with about 64 percent and taking 28,012 votes in the general election [SRC-0099]. He served five terms through January 2013, was re-elected in 2004, 2006 and 2008 and ran unopposed in 2010, and authored anti-abortion legislation during that period [SRC-0099]. In late 2010 he challenged incumbent Speaker Joe Straus and withdrew before the vote [SRC-0099].

他于2002年赢得科林县（Collin County）第70选区众议员席位，在初选决选中以约64%击败比尔·维茨（Bill Vitz），并在普选中获得28,012票 [SRC-0099]。他在众议院任职五个任期至2013年1月，分别于2004、2006、2008年连任，2010年无对手竞选，并在该期间撰写反堕胎立法 [SRC-0099]。2010年末，他挑战现任众议长乔·斯特劳斯（Joe Straus），并在投票前退出 [SRC-0099]。

In 2012 he ran for Senate District 8, finishing first in the March primary with about 44.4 percent and winning the runoff with about 63.6 percent, then beating Democrat Sam Houston in November [SRC-0099]. He served one Senate term, from January 2013 to January 2015 [SRC-0099]. The substantive content of that Senate term is thin in retrieved sources beyond his abortion authorship and his general conservatism; this is a genuine gap in this file.

2012年他竞选第8选区州参议员，在3月初选中以约44.4%居首，并在决选中以约63.6%获胜，随后于11月击败民主党人萨姆·休斯敦（Sam Houston）[SRC-0099]。他担任一个参议院任期，自2013年1月至2015年1月 [SRC-0099]。除反堕胎立法撰写与其总体保守主义立场外，已获取来源中关于该参议院任期实质内容的记录十分单薄；这是本文真实存在的空白。

Attorney General, 2015 to 2026 / 总检察长任期，2015至2026年

He was elected attorney general in 2014, finishing first in a four-way primary with about 44.4 percent, winning the runoff against Dan Branch with about 63.6 percent, and winning the general election without any Texas newspaper endorsement [SRC-0099]. He took office 2015-01-05 and was re-elected in 2018 and 2022 [SRC-0099].

他于2014年当选总检察长：在四人初选中以约44.4%居首，在决选中以约63.6%击败丹·布兰奇（Dan Branch），并在没有任何德克萨斯报纸背书的情况下赢得普选 [SRC-0099]。他于2015-01-05就职，并于2018年与2022年连任 [SRC-0099]。

The distinction that matters analytically: he was charged but never convicted, impeached but never removed, and investigated repeatedly but never sanctioned by a court on the merits. Any characterisation that goes beyond those four facts is an inference. The Dallas Observer's headline-level report that the felony fraud charges were dropped after ten years is corroborative only; the article body was not retrievable [SRC-0108].

从分析角度必须区分的是：他被起诉（charged）但从未被判定有罪（convicted）；被弹劾（impeached）但从未被罢免（removed）；多次受到调查，但从未在实体问题上被法院制裁。任何超出这四项事实的描述都属于推断。达拉斯观察者（Dallas Observer）关于重罪欺诈指控在十年后被撤销的报道仅具标题层面的佐证作用；该文正文无法获取 [SRC-0108]。

Governance style: litigation as policy / 治理风格：以诉讼作为政策工具

Paxton's attorney general record is best read as an instrument of political and policy conflict rather than as conventional law enforcement. The pattern is documented:

帕克斯顿的总检察长记录，最宜解读为政治与政策冲突的工具，而非常规执法。这一模式有以下有据可查的例证：

  • He sued the Biden administration 106 times, winning about one in three, including litigation over immigration enforcement, environmental regulation and labour rules [SRC-0099].
  • 他对拜登（Biden）政府提起诉讼106次，其中约三分之一胜诉，包括涉及移民执法、环境监管与劳工规则的诉讼 [SRC-0099]。
  • He led multi-state challenges to the Clean Power Plan and to Executive Order 13990, arguing the EPA was claiming an "unprecedented expansion of federal authority" [SRC-0099].
  • 他主导多州挑战《清洁电力计划》（Clean Power Plan）与第13990号行政令，主张环保署（EPA）正在主张 "an unprecedented expansion of federal authority"（译：「前所未有的联邦权力扩张」）[SRC-0099]。
  • He intervened to shield ExxonMobil from state climate subpoenas, framing the demands as "the criminalization of speech and thought" [SRC-0099].
  • 他介入以保护埃克森美孚（ExxonMobil）免受州级气候问题传票，将这些要求定性为 "the criminalization of speech and thought"（译：「把言论与思想入罪」）[SRC-0099]。
  • He defended Texas' anti-BDS statute on the argument that "the state of Texas has the right to boycott boycotters in this instance" [SRC-0099].
  • 他为德克萨斯州的反抵制、撤资与制裁法（anti-BDS statute）辩护，论据是 "the state of Texas has the right to boycott boycotters in this instance"（译：「在此情形下，德克萨斯州有权抵制抵制者」）[SRC-0099]。
  • He sued a 26-state coalition's worth of challenges against DAPA and threatened litigation to end DACA [SRC-0099].
  • 他参与了针对DAPA的26州联合挑战，并威胁提起诉讼以终止DACA [SRC-0099]。
  • He used consumer-protection law against the Annunciation House immigrant shelter in 2024 and warned other charities they were on notice [SRC-0099].
  • 他于2024年以消费者保护法对付移民收容机构"报喜之家"（Annunciation House），并警告其他慈善机构已被列入注意名单 [SRC-0099]。
  • He sued school districts whose officials discussed voucher impacts, invoking the election code ban on using public resources to influence elections [SRC-0101].
  • 他起诉了那些其官员讨论过教育券影响的学区，援引选举法中禁止动用公共资源影响选举的条款 [SRC-0101]。
  • In 2026 he halted property-tax increases in 132 municipalities in May and in more than 110 more in August under the 2025 property-tax growth law, after reviewing more than 1,000 [SRC-0099].
  • 2026年，他依据2025年财产税增长法，于5月叫停了132个市镇的财产税上调，在审查逾1,000个市镇后，又于8月叫停逾110个市镇的上调 [SRC-0099]。

The 2026 property-tax enforcement is the clearest example of him converting the office into a campaign instrument: it produced statewide headlines in the exact affordability frame his Senate campaign needed [SRC-0099][SRC-0105].

2026年的财产税执法是他把该职位转化为竞选工具的最清晰例证：它在全州范围内制造了头条新闻，且正好落在其参议院竞选所需的「生活成本可负担性」框架之内 [SRC-0099][SRC-0105]。

Social and cultural positions / 社会与文化议题立场

Paxton's cultural record is consistent across two decades and is the part of his profile most directly opposed to Talarico's.

帕克斯顿的文化议题记录在二十年间保持一致，也是其形象中与塔拉里科最直接对立的方面。

On abortion, his office observes 24 June, the anniversary of the Dobbs decision, as a holiday, and he gave employees a paid day off to mark it [SRC-0106][SRC-0099]. He sued the Biden administration in 2022 over federal guidance requiring hospitals to provide emergency abortions, making Texas the only state blocking that federal position according to the Tribune/19th account [SRC-0106]. He threatened prosecution over Kate Cox's emergency abortion in December 2023, calling the ruling judge an "activist" who was "not medically qualified" and warning hospitals about negligent-credentialing liability [SRC-0099]. In 2024 he sued a New York doctor over mailing abortion pills into Texas, and in 2025 urged Congress to preempt state shield laws [SRC-0099]. Reporting in 2026 records him suggesting openness to further IVF restrictions, which Cornyn used against him in the runoff [SRC-0106].

在堕胎议题上，其办公室将6月24日（多布斯案判决周年）定为假日，并给员工带薪休假以纪念该日 [SRC-0106][SRC-0099]。他于2022年就联邦要求医院提供紧急堕胎的指导意见起诉拜登政府；据《论坛报》／《第十九》（The 19th）的报道，德州因此成为唯一阻止该联邦立场的州 [SRC-0106]。2023年12月，他就凯特·考克斯（Kate Cox）的紧急堕胎威胁提起刑事起诉，称作出裁定的法官是 "activist"（译：「激进派法官」）且 "not medically qualified"（译：「不具备医学资格」），并警告医院可能承担"资质审核过失"（negligent credentialing）责任 [SRC-0099]。2024年他起诉一名纽约医生向德州邮寄堕胎药，2025年又敦促国会优先于各州"庇护法"（shield laws）[SRC-0099]。2026年的报道记录他对进一步限制体外受精（IVF）表现出开放态度，科宁在决选中以此攻击他 [SRC-0106]。

On religion in public life, he issued a September 2025 release stating "Our nation was founded on the rock of Biblical Truth" and that "Twisted, radical liberals want to erase Truth," and encouraged students to recite the Lord's Prayer [SRC-0099]. He intervened in a 2016 Killeen school scripture-poster dispute on the aide's side, and objected in 2017 to a Frisco high school Muslim prayer room - a move the district called "a publicity stunt" designed to politicize a nonissue [SRC-0099]. In August 2025 he opened an investigation into Plano ISD over alleged antisemitic and anti-Israel rhetoric, saying that any teacher or administrator who facilitated such rhetoric "should be fired immediately" [SRC-0099].

在公共生活中的宗教议题上，他于2025年9月发布声明称 "Our nation was founded on the rock of Biblical Truth"（译：「我们国家建立在圣经真理的磐石之上」），并称 "Twisted, radical liberals want to erase Truth"（译：「扭曲的激进自由派想要抹去真理」），同时鼓励学生诵读主祷文 [SRC-0099]。他在2016年基林（Killeen）学校圣经海报争议中站在该助理一方介入，并于2017年反对弗里斯科一所高中的穆斯林祈祷室——学区称此举是 "a publicity stunt"（译：「一场公关作秀」），意在把一个非问题政治化 [SRC-0099]。2025年8月，他就所称的反犹与反以色列言论对普莱诺学区（Plano ISD）展开调查，称任何协助或支持此类言论的教师或管理人员 "should be fired immediately"（译：「应立即被解雇」）[SRC-0099]。

On LGBTQ rights, he declined to implement Obergefell and said he would "do everything I can from this office to be a public voice for those standing in defense of their rights" for clerks refusing licenses [SRC-0099]; he led thirteen states against federal transgender bathroom guidance in 2016, describing it as a "gun to the head" [SRC-0099]; and in October 2025 he described what he called leftist ideology, including "transgenderism and Antifa," as "a cancer on our culture" while announcing undercover operations against what he called leftist terror cells [SRC-0099].

在LGBTQ权利议题上，他拒绝执行"奥贝格费尔诉霍奇斯案"（Obergefell）判决，并表示为拒绝发放同性婚姻执照的书记员 "do everything I can from this office to be a public voice for those standing in defense of their rights"（译：「我会在本职位权限内竭尽所能，为那些捍卫自身权利的人发声」）[SRC-0099]；2016年他率领十三个州反对联邦关于跨性别学生如厕的指导意见，称其为 "gun to the head"（译：「拿枪顶着脑袋」）[SRC-0099]；2025年10月，他将所称的左派意识形态——包括 "transgenderism and Antifa"（译：「跨性别主义与安提法」）——描述为 "a cancer on our culture"（译：「我们文化上的一颗毒瘤」），同时宣布针对所称左派"恐怖细胞"展开卧底行动 [SRC-0099]。

On guns, he defended campus carry against a suit he called frivolous, sued Austin in 2016 to force open carry in City Hall, and was endorsed by the NRA Political Victory Fund in his 2010 House race [SRC-0099].

在枪支议题上，他为校园携枪法辩护，称相关诉讼 "frivolous"（译：「无聊的」）；2016年起诉奥斯汀市以迫使市政厅允许公开携枪；并在2010年州众议员竞选中获得全国步枪协会政治胜利基金（NRA Political Victory Fund）的背书 [SRC-0099]。

On labour, he sued over the 2016 overtime rule, warning it "may lead to disastrous consequences for our economy," and challenged the persuader rule, calling the injunction "a victory for the preservation of the sanctity of attorney-client confidentiality" [SRC-0099].

Assessment / 评估

The record that is actually documented supports three observations. First, Paxton's public career has been continuous since 2003 and his conservatism is unusually stable: the abortion, religious-expression and LGBTQ positions he held as a legislator are the positions his office enforces now. Second, his governance method is litigation and threat of litigation rather than legislation, and he converted that method into a policy record that his base reads as courage and his opponents read as weaponisation - the sources support both readings. Third, the 2026 result is best explained by coalition arithmetic rather than by scandal: he consolidated a Republican plurality in the primary with Trump's endorsement while being badly outspent, then lost a general election in which the affordability frame favoured his opponent and he declined the debate platform.

有据可查的记录支撑三点观察。第一，帕克斯顿的公共生涯自2003年起连续不断，其保守主义立场异常稳定：他作为议员所持的堕胎、宗教表达与LGBTQ立场，正是其办公室如今所执行的立场。第二，其治理方式是诉讼与诉讼威胁，而非立法；他把这种方法转化为一份政策记录，其基本盘将其解读为勇气，其对手将其解读为武器化——来源材料对两种解读都提供了支撑。第三，2026年的结果最好用联盟算术而非丑闻来解释：他凭借特朗普背书、在支出严重落后对手的情况下整合了共和党内的相对多数并赢下初选，随后在一场以"可负担性"为框架、该框架有利于其对手且他本人拒绝辩论平台的普选中落败。
