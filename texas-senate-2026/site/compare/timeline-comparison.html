Parallel campaign chronology / 平行竞选时间线

A phase-by-phase reading of the two campaigns on the same calendar, built from campaign_events.csv (93 rows: Talarico 49, Paxton 44) with the per-candidate narratives in talarico_timeline.md and paxton_timeline.md.

这是一份在同一日历上按阶段解读两场竞选的文本，以 campaign_events.csv（93 行：塔拉里科 49 行，帕克斯顿 44 行）为基础，并结合 talarico_timeline.md 与 paxton_timeline.md 中按候选人分述的叙事。

0. How to read this, and the two cautions that matter / 0. 阅读方式与两项关键告诫

  1. Evidence asymmetry runs through the whole chronology. Paxton has more announced-but-undated activity (14 rows without a usable date, 15 of 19 named tour cities schedule-only), while Talarico has more dated and held activity. A count of rows is not a count of rallies. 证据不对称贯穿整条时间线。 帕克斯顿有更多*已宣布但无日期*的活动（14 行没有可用日期，19 个具名巡回城市中有 15 个仅有日程），而塔拉里科有更多*有日期且已举行*的活动。行数并不等同于集会数。
  2. Phase buckets are not perfectly symmetrical for undated rows. Undated tour stops default to gotv for both candidates, so Paxton's gotv count (21) is inflated relative to Talarico's (5). For any phase comparison, use the dated subset: Talarico 12 dated early_voting rows vs Paxton 4. 对无日期行而言，阶段归类并不完全对称。 两位候选人无日期的巡回停留点均默认归入 gotv，因此帕克斯顿的 gotv 计数（21）相对塔拉里科的（5）被抬高。任何阶段比较都应使用有日期的子集：塔拉里科有 12 行带日期的 early_voting，帕克斯顿为 4 行。

2.2 Democratic primary (2026-02 → 2026-03-03) / 2.2 民主党初选（2026-02 → 2026-03-03）

Talarico's primary phase is documented as an intense, faith-inflected sprint: the Covenant Presbyterian sermon in Lubbock, a Lubbock public rally, a Midland appearance, early voting on 2026-02-17, and the 2026-03-03 win with 52.4%. The polling-to-result reversal is documented: February polls had Crockett ahead by double digits, and Talarico won by ~7 points (07_electoral_context/polling.csv POLL-010; 01_candidates/talarico/biography.md).

塔拉里科的初选阶段被记录为一场紧张、带有信仰色彩的冲刺：拉伯克的Covenant Presbyterian 讲道、拉伯克公开集会、米德兰露面、2026-02-17 的提前投票，以及 2026-03-03 以 52.4% 胜出。民调与结果之间的反转有记录：2 月民调显示 Crockett（克罗克特）领先两位数，而塔拉里科以约 7 个百分点获胜（07_electoral_context/polling.csv POLL-010；01_candidates/talarico/biography.md）。

2.3 Republican primary and runoff (2026-03-03 → 2026-05-26) / 2.3 共和党初选与决选（2026-03-03 → 2026-05-26）

Paxton's phase, documented mostly through surrogate and runoff activity rather than a touring schedule: three runoff-phase rows in May, the 2026-05-19 Trump endorsement (one week out), the 2026-05-26 win at 63.8%, and a Plano victory party with "Thunderstruck" as exit music. The primary's damaging character (attack ads, Cornyn's character attacks, the Paxtons' divorce filing) is reconstructed in 07_electoral_context/election_context.md §3.1.

帕克斯顿的阶段主要通过代理人活动与决选活动来记录，而非通过巡回日程：5 月的三行决选阶段记录、2026-05-19 的特朗普背书（决选前一周）、2026-05-26 以 63.8% 获胜，以及一场以 "Thunderstruck"（译：《雷霆万钧》）作为散场音乐的普莱诺（Plano）胜选派对。初选的破坏性特征（攻击广告、科宁的人身攻击、帕克斯顿夫妇的离婚申请）在 07_electoral_context/election_context.md §3.1 中重建。

Paxton had zero documented campaign events in 2026-04, the month between the primary and the runoff campaign proper; Talarico had zero documented events in that same month as well. Neither campaign is well documented in April — a shared evidence gap, not a shared strategy.

帕克斯顿在 2026-04 没有任何有记录的竞选活动——即初选与决选竞选正式开始之间的那个月；塔拉里科在同一个月也没有任何有记录的活动。两场竞选在 4 月都记录不足——这是共同的证据空缺，而非共同的策略。

4. Geographic overlap / 4. 地理重叠

Both campaigns recorded activity in 14 shared Texas cities (plus "statewide" and undated rows): Amarillo, Austin, Beaumont, Dallas, Denton, El Paso, Fort Worth, Houston, Laredo, Lubbock, San Antonio, Texarkana, Tyler, Waco.

两场竞选都在 14 个共同的得克萨斯城市记录了活动（另有 "statewide"（译：全州）与无日期的行）：阿马里洛、奥斯汀、博蒙特、达拉斯、丹顿、埃尔帕索、沃思堡（Fort Worth）、休斯敦、拉雷多、拉伯克、圣安东尼奥、特克萨卡纳、泰勒、韦科。

Talarico's row set spans 30 distinct city values; Paxton's spans 32. The difference in breadth is small and, given Paxton's 15 undated schedule rows, not a reliable measure of geographic reach. Both campaigns' documented maps are statewide; no county-completeness claim is supportable — and county-level election results were not retrievable at all (07_electoral_context/county_results.csv is intentionally header-only).

塔拉里科的行集覆盖 30 个不同的城市取值；帕克斯顿覆盖 32 个。这一广度差异很小，且鉴于帕克斯顿有 15 行无日期的日程记录，它并非衡量地理覆盖范围的可靠指标。两场竞选有记录的地图都是全州性的；任何关于县级完整性的断言都无法成立——而且县级选举结果完全无法检索（07_electoral_context/county_results.csv 有意仅保留表头）。

5. Documented absences that shape the chronology / 5. 塑造时间线的有记录空缺

  • No debate, no joint appearance. No row in either timeline puts the two candidates on the same stage. The only "joint" event type in the archive is surrogate appearances by Trump and Vance for Paxton. 没有辩论，也没有联合露面。 两份时间线中没有任何一行将两位候选人放在同一舞台上。本档案中唯一的 "joint"（译：联合）活动类型，是特朗普与万斯为帕克斯顿出面的代理人活动。
  • No HQ openings, no closed-door fundraisers, no labour events, no county-party meetings, no podcasts, no press conferences for either campaign, despite targeted searches (event_coverage_gaps.md). 没有总部启用、没有闭门筹款会、没有劳工活动、没有县党会议、没有播客、没有新闻发布会——两场竞选均如此，尽管进行了有针对性的检索（event_coverage_gaps.md）。
