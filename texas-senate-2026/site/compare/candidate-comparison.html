Comparative analysis — the two campaigns side by side / 比较分析——两场竞选并列对照

This document compares what is documented, not who ran a better campaign. It is an inventory of differences, with each difference tied to the dataset that supports it. Where a difference might be an artifact of evidence rather than of campaign conduct, that is said explicitly.

本文件比较的是有据可查的内容，而非对两场竞选孰优孰劣作出评判。它是一份差异清单，每一项差异均对应到支撑它的数据集。若某项差异可能源自证据条件而非竞选行为本身，文中会明确指出。

Companion document: campaign_timeline_comparison.md (phase-by-phase parallel chronology).

2. Structural asymmetry: the twelve-week head start in the general / 2. 结构性不对称：大选阶段十二周的先行起步

The single largest documented structural difference is not rhetorical — it is calendrical. Talarico won his primary outright on 2026-03-03; Paxton was not nominated until the 2026-05-26 runoff. Talarico therefore began the general election roughly twelve weeks before Paxton, while Paxton was still spending money and political capital fighting an incumbent of his own party.

已记录的结构性差异中，最大的一项并不属于修辞层面，而属于日历层面。塔拉里科于 2026-03-03 直接赢得初选；帕克斯顿直到 2026-05-26 的决选才获得提名。因此，塔拉里科比帕克斯顿大约提前 十二周 进入大选阶段，而帕克斯顿当时仍在将资金与政治资本投入到与本党现任参议员的竞争中。

This is documented, not inferred: the runoff is described as "vile" and "brutal," fought almost exclusively with negative advertising (Texas Tribune, 2026-05-14), and the record shows Talarico explicitly shifting to the general election while Republicans were still fighting (07_electoral_context/election_context.md §3.1). Whether that head start caused anything is a question this archive leaves open; the fact itself is solid.

这一点是有据可查的，而非推断：这场决选被描述为 "vile" 与 "brutal"（译：「恶劣」与「残酷」），几乎完全依靠负面广告进行（Texas Tribune, 2026-05-14），并且记录显示，塔拉里科在共和党人尚在争斗之时便已明确转向大选（07_electoral_context/election_context.md §3.1）。这一先行起步是否「导致」了什么，是本档案有意悬置的问题；而事实本身是确凿的。

A second structural difference: Paxton entered the general election having just beaten a sitting senator of his own party, with a party base that had been publicly split. Cornyn said after the runoff that he would vote for Paxton but would not campaign for him (Washington Examiner, 2026-06-09). Lt. Gov. Dan Patrick had warned the runoff could depress Republican turnout (Texas Tribune, 2026-04-08). The archive records these as reported statements by identifiable actors.

第二项结构性差异：帕克斯顿是在刚刚击败本党一位现任参议员之后进入大选的，而其政党基础此前已公开分裂。科宁（Cornyn）在决选后表示，他会投票支持帕克斯顿，但不会为其助选（Washington Examiner, 2026-06-09）。副州长丹·帕特里克（Dan Patrick）此前警告称，决选可能压低共和党投票率（Texas Tribune, 2026-04-08）。本档案将上述内容记为可识别行为者所作的、有报道出处的陈述。

5. Policy and issue coverage by dimension / 5. 按维度统计的政策与议题覆盖

The policy matrix was built identically for both candidates across 12 dimensions: 146 rows — Talarico 80, Paxton 66 (01_candidates/policy_positions.csv).

政策矩阵对两位候选人按完全相同的 12 个维度构建：共 146 行——塔拉里科 80 行，帕克斯顿 66 行（01_candidates/policy_positions.csv）。

The count difference is not a finding about issue emphasis. It is a documented artifact of source availability: Paxton's own campaign website returned no issue literature (the site served site-builder JSON), so his positions are documented through the attorney-general record and press reporting rather than his own pages. Agent B recorded this in _staging/B_gaps.md §5 rather than padding his thinner dimensions. Any statement of the form "Paxton talked less about healthcare" would be unsupported by this dataset.

行数差异并非关于议题强调程度的发现。它是来源可得性造成的、有记录在案的假象：帕克斯顿本人的竞选网站未返回任何议题材料（该网站只提供站点构建器（site-builder）的 JSON），因此他的立场是通过总检察长任内记录与新闻报道来记载的，而非通过他本人的网页。B 号分析员（Agent B）将这一点记入 _staging/B_gaps.md §5，而没有为他行数较少的维度作填充。任何形式如 "Paxton talked less about healthcare"（译：帕克斯顿谈医疗保健较少）的说法，都无法由该数据集支持。

The cancellation record is one-sided and documented. Categories that came back empty for both candidates despite targeted searching — HQ openings, closed-door fundraisers, labour events, county-party meetings, podcasts, press conferences and election-day GOTV events — are listed in event_coverage_gaps.md. 尽管进行了有针对性的检索，两位候选人在以下类别上均无结果——总部启用、闭门筹款会、劳工活动、县党会议、播客、新闻发布会以及选举日 GOTV 活动——这些类别列于 event_coverage_gaps.md。空类别是关于公开记录的发现，而非关于竞选活动的结论。
