The cycle divides cleanly into three parts:

本轮竞选可清晰划分为三部分：

  1. A television-first launch (2025-04-08) with no rally — the bid was announced on Fox News' The Ingraham Angle after a campaign website went live.
  2. A primary and runoff fought mostly through paid media and surrogates; the only documented discrete events before May are a McKinney appearance (2026-05-19) and a Tyler runoff stop the same day.
  3. A closing bus tour branded "Come and Take It," announced 2026-10-14, launched Rockwall 2026-10-17, advertised to end at the Alamo on 2026-11-02 — and then abandoned at the Alamo.
  4. 以电视为优先的启动方式（2025-04-08），没有集会——在竞选网站上线后，其参选通过福克斯新闻的 The Ingraham Angle 节目宣布。
  5. 初选与决选主要依靠付费媒体与代理人推进；5 月之前有据可查的独立活动只有 McKinney 的一次露面（2026-05-19）以及同日的一场 Tyler 决选站点。
  6. 以「Come and Take It」为品牌的收官巴士巡游，于 2026-10-14 公布，2026-10-17 在 Rockwall 启动，对外宣称将于 2026-11-02 在 Alamo 收官——随后却在 Alamo 处被放弃。
