5. The Clear Eyes, Full Hearts tour — stop by stop / 五、Clear Eyes, Full Hearts 巡游——逐站记录

Announced 2026-10-16 [SRC-0005]; 20 cities; opened Plano 2026-10-18; closing rally Austin 2026-11-02.
