2. Method / 方法

  1. Sampling — every 0.5 s for videos ≤ 120 s, every 1.0 s above that (6,939 face crops kept in total).
  2. Detection — OpenCV YuNet (ONNX, 232 KB), score ≥ 0.55, face side ≥ 64 px, up to 3 faces per sampled frame.
  3. Identity — OpenCV SFace (ONNX, 38.7 MB). Embeddings are L2-normalised and compared by cosine against 62 reference embeddings built from the archive's own licensed candidate photographs (04_visual_archive/talarico/campaign/ 7 files, 04_visual_archive/paxton/campaign/ 69 files; 62 usable).
  4. Decision rule — class-balanced best-per-class cosine; a label is assigned only if best ≥ 0.40 and best − second-best ≥ 0.05, otherwise unknown.
  5. Reference sanity check — same-class mean cosine 0.499, cross-class mean 0.123. The two reference pools are cleanly separated.
  6. De-duplication — each surviving candidate frame gets a 64-bit DCT perceptual hash and a torso-region colour signature. Within a video, a frame is dropped when the hash is within 12 bits of an accepted frame (near-identical framing) or when the torso colour matches within 12 RGB units and the hash within 20 bits. Across videos only a near-identical image (≤ 6 bits) counts as a duplicate, so different venues survive. At most 3 frames per video per outfit-colour bucket are kept.
  7. Selection floor — identity_sim ≥ 0.62, sharpness ≥ 40, face width ≥ 80 px; max 8 per video, 2.5 s minimum spacing. Result: 150 candidates → 67 kept frames (31 Talarico / 36 Paxton); 83 redundant frames removed.
  8. Measurements — shot scale from face-width / frame-width ratio; face box in source pixels; Laplacian sharpness; mean grey brightness; mean RGB of the region below the face (a crude torso proxy, not a clothing classification).

Scripts: ../_staging/vision/{extract_faces,recognize_faces,curate}.py; config: ../_staging/vision/videos.json; models cached in ../_staging/vision/models/.

5. First measurable observations / 可先说的量化观察

Stated as measurements, not interpretations:

  • Shot scale: Talarico 22 medium / 9 medium-close / 0 close-up; Paxton 30 medium / 5 medium-close / 0 close-up. The curation floor is a face ≥ 80 px on ≤720p sources, so close-ups are mechanically rare and the difference is partly an artefact of the rule.
  • Face size at selection: Talarico 92–231 px, Paxton 82–258 px.
  • Torso-region mean RGB varies far more between videos than between candidates — venue lighting dominates any colour comparison.
  • Format split: official channels publish very differently — Talarico's tour clip is 640×360 / 75 s (social-first cut), Paxton's Cypress rally is 720p / 20 min (broadcast feed).

5.1 The official mark / 官方标志

assets/talarico_logo.svg is File:James Talarico for Senate logo.svg from Wikimedia Commons, sourced to jamestalarico.com. Parsed as vector data:

PropertyValue
Canvas204 × 52 (ratio ≈ 3.9 : 1, a horizontal wordmark)
Paths18 (glyph outlines converted to paths; plus one fill="none" background)
Coloursblack × 18, white × 1, none × 1 — no hex palette, no gradient, no accent colour
Strokesnone
Typographyoutlined, no live text and no font-family declaration

So the documented mark is a monochrome black wordmark with no colour system of its own: whatever colour the campaign used came from photography and merchandise, not from the logo file. Two small star-shaped paths sit at the lower corners of the canvas.

Paxton side: no comparable mark was located. A Commons namespace search for a Paxton campaign logo returned nothing usable, and the campaign site served no retrievable issue/branding pages. This asymmetry must travel with any "brand-system" comparison.

7. Limits — read before using / 使用前必读

  1. 3 configured videos could not be downloaded: N6PubEh2jZg, sa1vT9B4Ick, DrD_wR6gEU4. They stalled at 0 bytes or at ~5 KB/s during section downloads and were dropped rather than left half-processed. They remain registered in videos.json, so a retry is one command away.
  2. No audio was analysed. Nothing here says anything about delivery, tone or pacing; all prosody fields in the main archive remain unknown.
  3. 67 frames from 26 videos is still a small sample. It cannot support frequency claims about either campaign's imagery.
  4. Mixed production contexts. Official feeds and local-TV packages differ in camera position, lighting and graphics; comparing across them confounds source with candidate.
  5. The torso RGB column is a crude region mean, often capturing background. It is not a garment-colour reading.
  6. No facial geometry measurement was performed beyond the face bounding box. The interocular/face-width protocol in the two visual analyses is still unexecuted.
  7. Rights: these frames are cut from YouTube videos owned by the campaigns and TV stations. They are stored for internal analysis only and are not licensed for republication. Any public use requires a licensed still or link-only treatment.
