Event coverage gaps

What the table covers well

The 35 rows in campaign_events.csv are heavy on endorsement events (17 of 35) and on the final three weeks of rallies. That reflects the sources: union and trade-association releases are dated, published and archived, and late-October rally coverage was abundant.

What is thin

Routine retail politics is almost absent. Neither campaign published a public event calendar, and Ohio political coverage does not report every county fair stop or plant walk. The archive therefore records seven community_visit, small_business_visit or workplace_visit rows against what were certainly dozens of such stops. Absence in this table is an artefact of coverage, not evidence that the candidates stayed home.

Fundraisers are not documented at all. No closed-press fundraiser in Ohio was reported with a date and venue in a retrievable source. There is no fundraiser row, and none should be inferred from the finance totals.

Debates are absent, and this is the single largest gap. No source retrieved for this archive documents a Brown-Husted debate: no date, no venue, no sponsor, no transcript. What the record does contain is evidence of a dispute about debates — a WDTN headline dated 2026-10-30 whose article body could not be retrieved — and a Bowling Green State University/YouGov poll, fielded before debates became an issue, in which 54% of respondents called debates "very important". No debate or debate_invitation row was created, because a headline is not enough to fix a date and venue.

Early-voting events are undercounted. Two early_voting_event rows exist; both campaigns ran turnout operations across all 88 counties for four weeks.

What cannot be recovered at all

Crowd sizes. Where a source printed an estimate ("hundreds", "more than 1,000"), the number is recorded in crowd_size_estimate with the source's own hedging; where no estimate was printed, the cell says unknown. Do not sum these cells.

中文摘要

事件表长于 endorsement（35 行中 17 行）与最后三周的集会，因为工会与行业协会的新闻稿有确定日期 且被存档。日常零售式竞选活动（县集市、工厂走访）几乎没有记录：两个竞选团队都没有公布公开活动 日历。募款活动完全没有记录。最大的缺口是辩论：本档案检索到的材料中没有任何一场布朗—赫斯特德 辩论的日期、场地或主办方；只有一条 2026-10-30 的 WDTN 标题（正文无法获取）显示辩论曾成为争议 议题。因此没有创建 debate 行——一条标题不足以确定日期与场地。人群规模只能照抄来源的估计，未 印出估计的一律记为 unknown，且不可加总。
