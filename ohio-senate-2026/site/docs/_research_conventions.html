Research conventions / 研究规范

1. Retrieval and logging / 抓取与日志

Every URL in 09_sources/sources.csv has a row in _staging/fetch_log.csv with its HTTP status, byte count, timestamp and mirror path. Failures (403, 402, 429, 0) stay in the log and are never cited as content. A source that is not in the log may not appear in sources.csv. Retrieval used urllib with an explicit User-Agent (Mozilla/5.0 (research archive)), one request per URL, no retries against a blocking host.

sources.csv 中每个链接在 _staging/fetch_log.csv 均有状态、字节数、时间与镜像路径记录。 失败请求保留在日志中且绝不作为内容引用。未入日志者不得进入 sources.csv。

2. Source tiers / 来源分层

  • T1 official: FEC, Senate/Commons official portraits, campaign websites, government records.
  • T2 journalism: regional dailies, statehouse bureaus, wire services, pollster releases.
  • T3 synthesis: Wikipedia, aggregators, ratings pages, law-firm newsletters.

T3 is used for the spine (dates, tables) but any T3-only fact is marked partially verified. When T2 and T3 disagree, both values are recorded.

T1 官方；T2 新闻；T3 综合（维基、聚合站、评级页）。仅有T3支撑的事实标注 partially verified； T2与T3冲突时两者并记。

3. Dates / 日期

event_date is the date the event happened, not the publication date; when a report says "Wednesday" or "Monday", the date is derived from the publication date and the derivation is stated in analyst_notes. Husted's incumbency is always dated: announcement 2025-01-17, oath 2025-01-21. unknown is written where no record fixes the date.

event_date 记事件发生日而非发稿日；由“周三/周一”推算的日期在 analyst_notes 中说明推算过程。 赫斯特德的在任起点一律标注：2025-01-17 宣布、2025-01-21 宣誓。

4. Quoting / 引语

Quotation marks mean the words appeared inside quotation marks in the retrieved source. Paraphrase is never placed inside quotation marks. Where a campaign speaks through a spokesperson, the row says so and the quote is attributed to the spokesperson. No quote is reconstructed from memory or from a search-result snippet.

引号内文字必须是检索到的原文引号内容；转述绝不加引号；由发言人代述时明确标明。不得凭记忆或搜索摘要 重构引语。

5. unknown and placeholders / unknown 与占位符

unknown is a legitimate value and is used whenever the record is silent. TBD, TODO, N/A, NA, null, None and - are forbidden by the validator and do not appear.

凡记录未载明即填 unknown；禁用占位符。

6. Verification vocabulary / 校验语汇

  • verified — an official record or two independent T2 sources agree.
  • partially verified — one T2 source, or T3 corroborated by one T2.
  • conflicting sources — two retrieved sources disagree; both are reported.
  • unverified — a single non-independent claim, e.g. a campaign's own attendance or jobs figure.

7. Analytic writing / 分析写作

analyst_interpretation and analyst_notes are clearly separated from observed facts. The archive does not infer crowd size, enthusiasm, or venue from a photograph, and it does not treat a headline as a body fact.

analyst_interpretation 与 analyst_notes 与事实严格分离；不从照片推断人数、气氛或场地；不把标题当作正文事实。

8. Bilingual structure / 双语结构

Every ## section carries an English block followed by its Chinese counterpart. English governs where the two differ. Chinese text is written for a Chinese-reading researcher, not machine-translated word for word.

每个 ## 小节先英文后中文；两版不一致时以英文为准。

9. What this archive refuses to do / 本档案拒绝做的事

No composite quotes, no invented venues, no interpolated crowd sizes, no synthesised "typical" rally description, no imputed policy position from party platform alone. Where a position is inferred from endorsements rather than a statement, the row says so.

不合成引语、不虚构场地、不插值人数、不臆测立场；仅由 endorsement 推断的立场会明确标注。

Every CSV row carries SRC-####|https://...; unknown is a value, not a gap.

每个 CSV 行均带 SRC-####|https://...；unknown 是取值，不是缺失。
