Jon Husted / 乔恩·赫斯特德

1. Who he is / 人物概述

Jon Allen Husted, born 1967-08-25 in Royal Oak, Michigan and raised in northwest Ohio, is a Republican who served in the Ohio House (2001-2009, Speaker 2005-2009), the Ohio Senate (2009-2011), as Ohio secretary of state (2011-2019) and as lieutenant governor (2019-2025) before being appointed to the U.S. Senate (SRC-1008). He lost the 2026 special election to Sherrod Brown, 2,080,064 votes (45.17%) to 2,302,669 (50.01%) (SRC-1006).

乔恩·艾伦·赫斯特德，1967年8月25日生于密歇根州皇家橡树，在俄亥俄西北部成长。历任州众议员（2001–2009， 2005–2009任议长）、州参议员（2009–2011）、州务卿（2011–2019）、副州长（2019–2025），后获任命进入联邦参议院。 2026年特别选举以2,080,064票（45.17%）败于布朗。

2. The appointment — the fact that defines the candidacy / 任命：定义其候选人身份的事实

Vance resigned the seat effective 2025-01-10 (SRC-1096). Gov. Mike DeWine announced Husted's appointment on 2025-01-17, saying "I wanted someone who knew Ohio"; Husted said "we have worked to make Ohio great again" (SRC-1016). He took the oath on 2025-01-21, administered by Vance himself; the Commons photograph of that moment is dated 2025-01-21 (SRC-1178). Any statement in this archive describing Husted as "the incumbent" is dated to those two days. The task brief's alternative dates (appointed 2025-01-10, sworn in 2025-01-14) are not supported by any retrieved record and are corrected here.

范斯于2025年1月10日辞去席位。州长德万于2025年1月17日宣布任命赫斯特德，称“我需要了解俄亥俄的人”； 赫斯特德于2025年1月21日宣誓，由范斯本人主持，Commons 照片日期为2025年1月21日。本档案中任何称其为 “现任参议员”的表述均以这两天为时间基准。 简报所给另一组日期（1月10日任命、1月14日宣誓）无任何记录支持， 此处予以更正。

3. State record / 州级履历

As Speaker he led what the encyclopedia calls the largest income-tax cut in Ohio history at the time and expanded school choice (SRC-1008). As secretary of state (2011-2019) he was Ohio's chief elections officer — the credential he used in 2026 to argue for the SAVE America Act and a standalone photo-ID bill (SRC-1081). As lieutenant governor he recruited data centers; the Plain Dealer called him Ohio's "Face of Data Centers", a nickname Brown turned into the centre of his closing argument (SRC-1008, SRC-1028).

任议长期间推动当时俄亥俄史上最大规模所得税减税并扩张择校；任州务卿（2011–2019）为州首席选举官员，这一资历成为他 2026年主张《SAVE America Act》与单独 Photo ID 法案的依据；任副州长期间招引数据中心，《老实人报》称其为俄亥俄 “数据中心门面”，此绰号被布朗用作收尾论证核心。

4. The 2026 campaign / 2026年竞选

Husted launched on 2025-04-09, the same day Trump endorsed him (SRC-1095, SRC-1094). He was unopposed in the 2026-05-05 primary (736,413 votes). His coalition included Republican statewide officeholders (Yost, Sprague, Faber), most of the congressional delegation, 75 county sheriffs, the state FOP, the Operating Engineers, the Boilermakers, the Cleveland Building Trades, ABC of Ohio and the U.S. Chamber (SRC-1070, SRC-1063, SRC-1126).

He also carried liabilities the archive can date: testimony in the FirstEnergy corruption trial on 2026-03-11 after being unable to appear in person two days earlier (SRC-1044); reporting that a political committee received nearly $500,000 from a bank he had worked for; that a top Senate aide kept working for an Ohio lobbying firm; and that he took gifts from JobsOhio while lieutenant governor (SRC-1046, SRC-1047, SRC-1049). His campaign's closing message was tenure: "he's been in office for over 50 years" (SRC-1074).

2025年4月9日启动竞选，同日获特朗普背书；2026年5月5日初选无对手（736,413票）。联盟包括州级共和党官员、 多数国会议员、75名县治安官、州警察协会、工程师工会、锅炉工工会、克利夫兰建筑工会、ABC of Ohio 与美国商会。 同时背负可系日的负债：2026年3月11日就 FirstEnergy 腐败案作证（两天前未能亲自出庭）；有报道称其政治委员会从他曾任职的 银行获得近50万美元；一名高级参议院助手仍在为俄亥俄游说公司工作；任副州长期间收受 JobsOhio 礼品。收尾讯息是任期： “他从政已超过50年”。

5. Personal / 个人

Married Tina Zwayer in 2006; three children. In the January 2026 Senate HELP Committee hearing on the abortion pill he cited his own adoption story, saying his mother could have chosen abortion (SRC-1037, SRC-1008).

2006年与蒂娜·兹韦尔结婚，三名子女。2026年1月参议院 HELP 委员会关于堕胎药的听证会上，他讲述自己被收养的经历， 称母亲本可选择堕胎。
