2026 Ohio U.S. Senate special election / 2026年俄亥俄州联邦参议员特别选举

1. What this archive covers / 本档案覆盖范围

This case covers the 2026 special election to fill the U.S. Senate seat vacated by JD Vance when he became vice president. The seat was filled by gubernatorial appointment on 2025-01-17 and the appointee, Jon Husted, defended it in a special election held concurrently with the 2026 general election on 2026-11-03. Sherrod Brown, who had lost this same seat in 2024, won it back. The archive holds 15 CSVs (candidates, events, speeches, visuals, campaign media, finance, electoral context) holding 418 data rows in total, 195 catalogued sources, 21 bilingual documents, and 15 downloaded image files. Every retrieval is logged in _staging/fetch_log.csv with its HTTP status and byte count.

本案例研究2026年俄亥俄州联邦参议员特别选举：JD·范斯就任副总统后留下的参议院席位先由州长 任命填补（2025年1月17日宣布），被任命者乔恩·赫斯特德在2026年11月3日与中期选举同日举行的 特别选举中捍卫该席位。2024年失去该席位的谢罗德·布朗重新赢回。档案包含15个CSV数据集与中英 双语分析文档。

3. Structural facts this archive establishes / 本档案确立的结构性事实

1. The appointment, not the election, created the incumbency. Vance resigned effective 2025-01-10; Gov. Mike DeWine announced Husted's appointment on 2025-01-17 and Husted took the oath on 2025-01-21 (SRC-1016, SRC-1178). Every "incumbent" label in this archive is dated to those two days. The task brief's "appointed 2025-01-10 / sworn in 2025-01-14" matches neither the resignation date nor any retrieved record, and is corrected here.

2. Money ran one way and the polls did not. Through 2026-09-30 Brown had raised $68,409,200 to Husted's $19,894,786 (SRC-1006), yet the race was rated a toss-up all year and the final margin was 4.84 points. Total spending in the race passed $500 million (SRC-1074), of which outside groups accounted for more than $325 million by AdImpact's count (SRC-1026), so candidate receipts were a minority of the money in the race.

3. Data centers became the sleeper issue. A leaked NRSC memo called data centers "the anchor hanging around Husted's neck" and the race "a dead heat" (SRC-1028). Brown built his closing argument on Husted's recruitment of them — despite having praised an Amazon data center in a 2015 release (SRC-1081).

4. Labour split rather than labour consolidation. Brown held the UAW, Steelworkers, IUPAT, IFPTE, CWA, the Ohio Federation of Teachers and the Ohio AFL-CIO; Husted took the Operating Engineers, Boilermakers, Cleveland Building Trades, ABC of Ohio and the state FOP (SRC-1054, SRC-1101, SRC-1062). A Republican carrying building trades and the Boilermakers is the unusual part.

5. A split-ticket state. Brown won 50.01% in a state Trump carried three times, and Democrats swept the Ohio governor, secretary of state and treasurer races in the same election (SRC-1006). The Senate result was part of a state-level Democratic wave, not an isolated personal comeback.

5. What is strong vs thin / 强项与薄弱环节

Strong: the result arithmetic and its internal inconsistencies; the appointment chronology; the finance asymmetry; the issue positions, because a Columbus Dispatch issue survey carried by Yahoo News was retrieved in full and quotes both candidates at length on affordability, tariffs, Medicaid, data centers, immigration and the SAVE Act (SRC-1081); the union and county-party endorsement record.

Thin: county-level results (no table exists in the article — only two SVG maps, and the Ohio Secretary of State canvass spreadsheets returned 403, so county_results.csv is header-only); any joint debate (nothing in the retrieved corpus documents one); crowd sizes (almost never reported); speech transcripts (no campaign publishes them, and the video pages that would carry them returned 403); television advertising (no ad video URLs were retrievable except one 2026-10-29 vertical video, so seven of the eight ad rows are documented through reporting, a fact-check or a sponsor's own release rather than from the creative).

强项：结果算术及其内部矛盾、任命时间线、筹款不对称、议题立场（两阵营均回答了结构化议题问卷且全文可获取）、 工会与县党 endorsement 记录。薄弱：县级结果（条目仅有地图SVG、无表格）、联合辩论（现有语料无任何记载）、 现场人数（几乎无报道）、演讲全文（无竞选发布，视频页403）、电视广告（广告链接不可得，8条中6条依据报道而非广告本身）。
