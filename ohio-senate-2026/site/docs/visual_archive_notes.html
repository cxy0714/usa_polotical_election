Visual archive notes

What is here

image_archive.csv holds 18 records: 11 for Brown, 7 for Husted. Fifteen corresponding files are downloaded into brown/ and husted/, each under 2 MB. Three records are catalogue-only and say so: the 2018 lieutenant-governor portrait (7.0 MB original), a 2017 photograph (5.6 MB) and the 2026 vertical campaign video (19 MB), all above the size ceiling.

Licensing

Every file is public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, read from the Wikimedia Commons API's imageinfo response, and the licence string in the CSV is the API's own value. Attribution requirements are recorded in photographer; two files (the 2016 DNC photograph under CC BY-SA 3.0 and the 2016 City Club screen capture under CC BY 3.0) require credit when reused.

Deliberate unknowns

clothing_notes, camera_angle, face_visibility and body_visibility are unknown for every row. This build has no image-reading step: nothing in the pipeline opened the pixels, and no retrieved caption describes what either candidate is wearing. Filling those cells from imagination would have produced the most confident-looking and least defensible columns in the archive. framing is limited to what the Commons file title or description states (for example "official portrait" or "screen capture from video"), and image_resolution is either the API's pixel size or, for downloaded files, the measured pixel size.

What the images can and cannot establish

They establish likeness, dates and licensing. They do not establish crowd size, mood or setting. Only two records can be tied to a dated campaign event: the oath photograph (2025-01-21) and the 2026-10-29 video, and in both cases the date comes from the Commons metadata rather than from inspection.

Portraits for comparison

Three public-domain portraits satisfy the contract's portrait requirement: Brown 2021 (official, 117th Congress), Brown 2009 (earlier official photo) and Husted 2025 (official, 119th Congress). The two 2025 Husted portraits are the fairest pair for side-by-side comparison, taken within weeks of each other and both official.
