Jon Husted — political positions / 政治立场

Fourteen rows carry Husted. A large share of his issue answers arrived through spokesperson Amy Natoce in the structured questionnaire (SRC-1081); those rows attribute the statement to the spokesperson and are marked accordingly. Where only the encyclopedia records a position — AI, education, Russia sanctions — the row is partially verified (SRC-1008).

立场表14行。相当部分议题答复由发言人 Amy Natoce 在结构化问卷中给出，相关行明确标注为发言人表述。 仅由百科全书记载的立场（人工智能、教育、对俄制裁）标注 partially verified。

2. Data centers — the defining position / 数据中心：定义性立场

As lieutenant governor Husted recruited data centers and called them jobs and money for rural Ohio; the Plain Dealer called him the state's "Face of Data Centers" (SRC-1008, SRC-1028). By 2026 he was sponsoring a bill requiring state regulators to consider a policy blueprint making large-load data centers pay for their power — consider, not adopt (SRC-1081). The NRSC memo called data centers "the anchor hanging around Husted's neck"; the campaign's answer was a considered-not-mandated bill, which is the weakest possible form of the position.

任副州长期间招引数据中心，称其为俄亥俄乡村的就业与资金，被《老实人报》称为“数据中心门面”。到2026年，他提出 要求州监管机构考虑（而非采纳）大型负载数据中心自付电力的政策蓝图的法案。共和党参议院委员会备忘录称数据中心是 “挂在赫斯特德脖子上的锚”；其回应是“仅需考虑”的法案，属该立场最弱形式。

3. Economy, taxes, trade / 经济、税收、贸易

He defends the 2025 tax law (no tax on tips or overtime, higher child tax credit, work requirements) and sponsors a "benefits cliff" pilot bill (SRC-1081). On tariffs he does not repudiate them: "Ohio relies on the relationship with trading partners like Canada, but it's got to be fair... they want predictability, but they also don't want to have China using countries like Canada to import products into Canada and then moving them into America" (SRC-1081). He credits tariff protection with Kenworth adding 1,000 jobs in Chillicothe: "protecting American manufacturing is the America First agenda" (SRC-1074).

为2025年税法辩护并提出“福利悬崖”试点法案。对关税不予否定，而以“公平、可预期、反转运”表述； 将 Chillicothe 的 Kenworth 新增1000个岗位归功于关税保护。

4. Health care / 医疗

He introduced a bill extending enhanced ACA tax credits for two years with a $5 minimum monthly payment aimed at fraud reduction, and claims the $50 billion rural-hospital fund as a win (SRC-1081). Documented change: in December 2025 he wanted to "freeze" the subsidies (SRC-1038); by October 2026 he sponsored an extension. The row records position_changed: partial with both dates.

提出将强化版《平价医疗法案》税收抵免延长两年、并设每月5美元最低缴费以打击欺诈的法案，并将500亿美元乡村医院 基金视为政绩。已记录的变化： 2025年12月他主张“冻结”补贴，2026年10月转而提出延长法案。

5. Abortion / 堕胎

At the Senate HELP Committee hearing on 2026-01-22 he spoke against the abortion pill and cited his own adoption story (SRC-1037). Ohio voters had protected abortion in the state constitution in 2023 (57%-43%), which makes this the position with the clearest electoral cost in the archive (SRC-1012).

2026年1月22日在 HELP 委员会听证会上反对堕胎药，并讲述自身被收养经历。俄亥俄选民2023年以57%对43%将堕胎权写入 州宪，这使其成为本档案中选举成本最清晰的立场。

6. Immigration and elections / 移民与选举

Immigration: supports the Trump agenda and says immigration law "should be implemented as it was intended", including TPS and birthright citizenship; his spokesperson frames the choice as safety versus "dangerous criminals" (SRC-1081). Elections: a staunch SAVE America Act supporter who pushed a standalone photo-ID measure, and recorded Senate floor remarks on photo ID on 2026-07-27 (SRC-1081, SRC-1179).

移民：支持特朗普议程，称移民法“应按原意执行”，包括临时保护身份与出生公民权；发言人将选择框定为安全对 “危险罪犯”。选举：坚定的《SAVE America Act》支持者，推动单独 Photo ID 法案，并于2026年7月27日发表参议院 发言（有公开领域录音）。

7. Labour, AI, foreign policy / 劳工、人工智能、外交

Labour: an unusual Republican labour footprint — the Operating Engineers, Boilermakers, Cleveland Building Trades, ABC of Ohio, the state FOP and the Teamsters-adjacent building trades (SRC-1101, SRC-1106). AI: the encyclopedia records that in the 2026 campaign he "favored progressively replacing human jobs with AI" (SRC-1008) — a single-sentence claim whose underlying Plain Dealer article was not retrieved, hence partially verified. Foreign policy: supported Russia sanctions in 2025 unless Moscow pursued a lasting peace, and voted to continue the Iran war as characterised by Brown (SRC-1008, SRC-1074).

劳工：共和党少见的劳工版图；人工智能：条目称其在2026年竞选中主张“以人工智能逐步替代人工岗位”，该单句说法 所依据的《老实人报》原文未获取，故标注 partially verified；外交：2025年支持对俄制裁（除非实现持久和平）， 并按布朗的定性投票支持继续伊朗战争。
