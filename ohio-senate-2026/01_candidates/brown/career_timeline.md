## 2. Why the gaps matter / 空白为何重要

The timeline is continuous from 1975 to 2025 with no gap longer than the two months between
leaving the Senate and launching the institute. That continuity is precisely Husted's attack line
("in office for over 50 years") and the archive's own check on it: 1975-01-03 to 2026-11-03 is
51 years and 10 months of near-continuous officeholding (SRC-1074).

时间线自1975年至2025年连续，除离开参议院到创办研究所的两个月外无空档。这一连续性正是赫斯特德“从政超过50年”
攻击的根据，也是本档案的核对：1975年1月3日至2026年11月3日为51年10个月的近乎连续任职。

## 3. Turning points / 转折点

- **2023-02-16 / 2023-03-01** — East Palestine visit and the Railway Safety Act with JD Vance: the
  moment his Senate record became a campaign story he could tell about accountability
  (SRC-1011, SRC-1013).
- **2024-11-05** — loss to Bernie Moreno (SRC-1009).
- **2025-08-18** — the comeback launch, framed against an appointed incumbent.
- **2026-05-05** — 712,106 votes (89.40%) in a primary he was never going to lose.
- **2026-11-03** — 2,302,669 votes (50.01%).
