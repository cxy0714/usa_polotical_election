## 2. Economy and cost of living / 经济与生活成本

The affordability frame is the campaign's spine: "Ohio workers are more productive than ever, but
no matter how hard they work, their pay doesn't keep up" (SRC-1081). He opposes the
tariffs in force, tying them to farm input costs — "farmers can't afford a new tractor or a new
combine... they in many cases can't even afford spare parts because of the tariffs"
(SRC-1078). He wants to restore the expanded child tax credit and calls the 2025 tax law
tilted to "billionaires and corporations" (SRC-1083, SRC-1081).

**Documented inconsistency.** Brown is a long-standing protectionist — he voted against NAFTA,
backed the 2018 washing-machine tariffs and supported USMCA — yet campaigned in 2026 to end the
tariffs in force (SRC-1007, SRC-1078). The row records `position_changed: partial`
and states both sides rather than resolving them.

生活成本是主轴：生产率上升而工资未同步；反对现行关税并联系农场投入成本；主张恢复扩大的儿童税收抵免，
称2025年税法是向“亿万富翁与大公司”倾斜。**已记录的不一致：** 布朗长期是保护主义者（反对北美自贸协定、
支持2018年洗衣机关税与美墨加协定），却在2026年主张结束现行关税。立场行记录 `partial` 并并列两面。

## 3. Energy and data centers / 能源与数据中心

Brown's closing argument is that Husted recruited data centers that raise household bills:
"Husted's been the front guy on all these data centers, 200 in Ohio, getting huge over-the-top tax
breaks, and raising utility rates" (SRC-1028).
He wants data centers to pay for their own power and opposes "out-of-control tax giveaways"
(SRC-1081). The NRSC's own leaked memo called data centers the race's "sleeper
issue", which is evidence the attack landed inside the Republican operation.

**Documented inconsistency.** In a 2015 release Brown applauded Amazon's central-Ohio data center
(SRC-1081). The archive records the earlier position.

布朗的收尾论证是赫斯特德招引的数据中心推高居民电费，主张数据中心自付电力成本、反对“失控的税收馈赠”。
共和党参议院委员会自身泄露的备忘录称数据中心是本次选举的“潜议题”，说明该攻击在共和党内部被认为有效。
**已记录的不一致：** 2015年他曾赞许亚马逊在俄亥俄中部建设数据中心。

## 4. Labour and rail safety / 劳工与铁路安全

The labour record is the most verifiable part of his profile: the Railway Safety Act co-sponsored
with JD Vance after the 2023-02-03 East Palestine derailment, pension protection for Teamsters
retirees, and a pledge to co-sponsor the Faster Labor Contracts Act
(SRC-1111, SRC-1013). On the
derailment he was on the ground within days: "I want to see rail safety legislation passed in the
next few weeks" (SRC-1011).

劳工记录最可核实：与范斯共同提出铁路安全法案、保护卡车司机退休金、承诺共同提出《加速劳动合同法案》；
脱轨事故后数日内到场。

## 5. Immigration, democracy, guns / 移民、民主、枪支

Immigration: deport people convicted of serious crimes, extend TPS for Springfield's Haitian
community, and a path for long-resident taxpayers — "I, of course, support deportation of those
people who have committed a serious crime... Beyond that, I want to see us work out a real policy
on immigration" (SRC-1081). Democracy: opposes the proof-of-citizenship and photo-ID
push and defends early, mail and Election Day voting (SRC-1081). Guns: supports
restricting access for people with criminal records or mental-health issues; endorsed by Giffords
(SRC-1083, SRC-1115).

移民：驱逐重罪犯、延长海地人临时保护身份、为长期居住纳税人提供路径；民主：反对公民身份证明与 Photo ID 强制，
捍卫提前投票、邮寄投票与选举日投票；枪支：支持限制有犯罪记录或精神健康问题者购枪，获 Giffords 背书。

## 6. Foreign policy / 外交

Brown campaigned against the war with Iran as a price issue — "ending the war in Iran and
eliminating tariffs would help lower prices" (SRC-1083) — and criticises Husted's votes
to continue it (SRC-1074). OCJ's March 2026 survey found no Ohio Senate candidate gave the
war unqualified support, Brown included (SRC-1041).

布朗以物价为由反对伊朗战争，并批评赫斯特德支持继续战争的投票。OCJ 2026年3月的梳理发现无一位俄亥俄参议员候选人
无条件支持该战争，布朗亦然。
