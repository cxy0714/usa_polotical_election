# Sherrod Brown / 谢罗德·布朗

## 1. Who he is / 人物概述

Sherrod Campbell Brown, born 1952-11-09 in Mansfield, Ohio, is a Democratic politician who has
held elective office in Ohio almost continuously since 1975: state House (1975-1983), secretary
of state (1983-1991), U.S. House (1993-2007) and U.S. Senate (2007-2025). He lost the Senate seat
to Bernie Moreno in 2024 and won it back in the 2026 special election with 2,302,669 votes
(50.01%) against appointed Republican Sen. Jon Husted (SRC-1007, SRC-1006).

谢罗德·坎贝尔·布朗，1952年11月9日生于俄亥俄州曼斯菲尔德，自1975年起几乎连续担任俄亥俄州民选公职：
州众议院（1975–1983）、州务卿（1983–1991）、联邦众议院（1993–2007）、联邦参议院（2007–2025）。
2024年将参议院席位输给伯尼·莫雷诺，2026年特别选举中以2,302,669票（50.01%）赢回该席位。

## 2. Political identity / 政治身份

Brown's public identity is built on two things the archive can document rather than assert:
labour alignment and a populist economic argument. He chaired the Senate Banking Committee from
2021-02-03 to 2025-01-03 — the post that made him a Wall Street critic with a legislative record
rather than only a rhetorical one (SRC-1007, SRC-1088). His post-Senate
vehicle, the Dignity of Work Institute, launched 2025-03-24 with the argument that "corporate
profits have soared, executive compensation has exploded, and productivity keeps going up and up,
but wages aren't rising along with it" (SRC-1018).

His 2026 campaign translated that into three testable claims: tariffs raise Ohio farmers' input
costs; data centers raise household electricity bills; and the 2025 tax law favours high earners
(SRC-1078, SRC-1081).

布朗的公共身份由两条可被文献证实的线索构成：劳工立场与民粹式经济论述。2021年2月至2025年1月任参议院
银行委员会主席，使其“华尔街批评者”形象有立法记录支撑。离开参议院后于2025年3月24日创办“劳动尊严研究所”，
以“企业利润飙升、高管薪酬爆炸、生产率持续上升而工资未同步增长”为核心论点。2026年竞选将其转化为三项可检验
主张：关税推高农场成本、数据中心推高居民电费、2025年税法是向高收入者倾斜。

## 3. The 2024 defeat and the comeback / 2024年失败与2026年回归

Brown lost in 2024 to Bernie Moreno, ending 18 years in the Senate; the 2024 result also gave Ohio
two Republican senators for the first time in his tenure (SRC-1009). He announced the
comeback on 2025-08-18 (SRC-1071). The article asserts he is the first
senator since Slade Gorton in 1988 to return to the Senate after losing a re-election bid; this
archive reports the claim as the encyclopedia's, not as an independently verified fact.

2024年败给莫雷诺，结束18年参议员生涯，也使俄亥俄首次在其任内出现两位共和党参议员。2025年8月18日宣布复出。
条目称他是1988年斯莱德·戈顿以来首位在连任失败后重返参议院的参议员；本档案将此作为条目的说法记录，
而非独立核实的事实。

## 5. Personal / 个人

Married Larke Recchie 1979-1987 (two children); later married Connie Schultz, a Cleveland Plain
Dealer columnist (SRC-1007). The encyclopedia also records a 1987 restraining order obtained
by Recchie during divorce proceedings, which she later walked back; it is noted here because it is
part of the public record the article carries, not because the 2026 campaign turned on it.

1979–1987年与拉克·雷奇结婚（两名子女），后与《克利夫兰老实人报》专栏作家康妮·舒尔茨结婚。条目亦记载1987年
离婚期间雷奇获得的限制令（其后她撤回相关说法）；此处记录因其属于条目所载公开记录，而非因2026年竞选围绕此事。
