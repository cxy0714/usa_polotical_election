# Jon Husted / 乔恩·赫斯特德

## 1. Who he is / 人物概述

Jon Allen Husted, born 1967-08-25 in Royal Oak, Michigan and raised in northwest Ohio, is a
Republican who served in the Ohio House (2001-2009, Speaker 2005-2009), the Ohio Senate
(2009-2011), as Ohio secretary of state (2011-2019) and as lieutenant governor (2019-2025) before
being appointed to the U.S. Senate (SRC-1008). He lost the 2026 special election to
Sherrod Brown, 2,080,064 votes (45.17%) to 2,302,669 (50.01%) (SRC-1006).

乔恩·艾伦·赫斯特德，1967年8月25日生于密歇根州皇家橡树，在俄亥俄西北部成长。历任州众议员（2001–2009，
2005–2009任议长）、州参议员（2009–2011）、州务卿（2011–2019）、副州长（2019–2025），后获任命进入联邦参议院。
2026年特别选举以2,080,064票（45.17%）败于布朗。

## 3. State record / 州级履历

As Speaker he led what the encyclopedia calls the largest income-tax cut in Ohio history at the
time and expanded school choice (SRC-1008). As secretary of state (2011-2019) he was Ohio's
chief elections officer — the credential he used in 2026 to argue for the SAVE America Act and a
standalone photo-ID bill (SRC-1081). As lieutenant governor he recruited data
centers; the Plain Dealer called him Ohio's "Face of Data Centers", a nickname Brown turned into
the centre of his closing argument (SRC-1008, SRC-1028).

任议长期间推动当时俄亥俄史上最大规模所得税减税并扩张择校；任州务卿（2011–2019）为州首席选举官员，这一资历成为他
2026年主张《SAVE America Act》与单独 Photo ID 法案的依据；任副州长期间招引数据中心，《老实人报》称其为俄亥俄
“数据中心门面”，此绰号被布朗用作收尾论证核心。

## 5. Personal / 个人

Married Tina Zwayer in 2006; three children. In the January 2026 Senate HELP Committee hearing on
the abortion pill he cited his own adoption story, saying his mother could have chosen abortion
(SRC-1037, SRC-1008).

2006年与蒂娜·兹韦尔结婚，三名子女。2026年1月参议院 HELP 委员会关于堕胎药的听证会上，他讲述自己被收养的经历，
称母亲本可选择堕胎。
